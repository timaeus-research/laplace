# Tide 102 consult: stationary autocovariance and long-run variance of the ULA-sampled anchored energy (laplace seabed, Lean 4 + Mathlib; E5)

Seabed (tides 95–101): the tilted Gaussian `tiltedExpectation P v φ = (∫ φ·exp(−½uᵀPu + v·u))/Z` with mean `tiltMean P v = P⁻¹v`; the mixed Wick covariance `Cov_{P,v}(½uᵀHu, ½uᵀBu + b·u) = ½tr(HΣBΣ) + (Hm)ᵀΣ(Bm) + (Hm)ᵀΣb` (`tiltedCov_quadForm_quadProbe`, `Σ = P⁻¹`); the ULA law on the anchored Gaussian `N(m, P⁻¹)` after `k ≥ 1` steps from `x₀` as the tilted Gaussian with covariance `Σ_k = Σ(1 − A^{2k})`, `A = 1 − hP`, mean `m + A^k(x₀ − m)`, and its energy `⟨½uᵀHu⟩_k = ½∑λᵢσᵢ²(1 − ρᵢ^{2k}) + ½∑λᵢ(m̂ᵢ + ρᵢ^k(x̂₀ᵢ − m̂ᵢ))²` in any orthogonal frame `U` diagonalising `P` (`p`) and `H` (`λ`) (`burnInAnch_energy_frame`); `Σ = ulaCov P h = U diag(σᵢ²) Uᵀ`, `σᵢ² = 1/(pᵢκᵢ)`, `κᵢ = 1 − hpᵢ/2`, `ρᵢ = 1 − hpᵢ`, `|ρᵢ| < 1`; the k→∞ stationary variance `½∑λᵢ²σᵢ⁴ + ∑λᵢ²σᵢ²m̂ᵢ²` (tide 100). There is no Markov-process object in the seabed: laws are Gaussians, conditional laws are the burn-in Gaussians from a start.

Candidates v1 (Claude):
A. Define `condEnergy ℓ x₀ := ⟨½uᵀHu⟩_{ℓ-step law from x₀}` and the lag-ℓ autocovariance `ulaAutoCov ℓ := Cov_{N(m,Σ)}(½uᵀHu, condEnergy ℓ ·)` (covariance under the stationary law between the energy and its ℓ-step conditional mean; by the tower property this is `Cov(Y₀, Y_ℓ)` of the stationary chain `Y_k = ½x_kᵀHx_k`). Claim: `condEnergy ℓ x₀ = ½x₀ᵀB_ℓx₀ + b_ℓ·x₀ + c_ℓ` with `B_ℓ = A^ℓHA^ℓ = U diag(λᵢρᵢ^{2ℓ}) Uᵀ`, `b_ℓ = U diag(λᵢρᵢ^ℓ(1−ρᵢ^ℓ)) Uᵀ m`, and for `ℓ ≥ 1`
   `ulaAutoCov ℓ = ½∑ᵢλᵢ²σᵢ⁴ρᵢ^{2ℓ} + ∑ᵢλᵢ²σᵢ²m̂ᵢ²ρᵢ^ℓ`.
B. Stationary variance under the stationary law directly (`ulaStatVar = ½∑λᵢ²σᵢ⁴ + ∑λᵢ²σᵢ²m̂ᵢ²`, via tide 96's `Var(uᵀHu) = 2tr(HΣHΣ) + 4(Hm)ᵀΣ(Hm)`), and the long-run variance
   `τ² := ulaStatVar + 2∑'_{ℓ≥0} ulaAutoCov(ℓ+1) = ½∑ᵢλᵢ²σᵢ⁴(1+ρᵢ²)/(1−ρᵢ²) + ∑ᵢλᵢ²σᵢ²m̂ᵢ²(1+ρᵢ)/(1−ρᵢ)`
   `= ∑ᵢλᵢ²(1+ρᵢ²)/(4hpᵢ³κᵢ³) + 2∑ᵢλᵢ²m̂ᵢ²/(hpᵢ²)` in sampler variables (`1 − ρᵢ² = 2hpᵢκᵢ`, `(1+ρᵢ)/(1−ρᵢ) = 2κᵢ/(hpᵢ)`). Integrated autocorrelation times `(1+ρᵢ²)/(1−ρᵢ²)` (quadratic part) and `(1+ρᵢ)/(1−ρᵢ)` (mean part); with `h = η/t`, `pᵢ ≈ tλᵢ`: `→ (1+(1−ηλᵢ)²)/(ηλᵢ(2−ηλᵢ))` and `(2−ηλᵢ)/(ηλᵢ)`, t-independent steps per effective sample.
C. Exact finite-n variance of the chain average as an identity of the lag function: `(1/n²)(n·ulaStatVar + 2∑_{j<n}(n−(j+1))·ulaAutoCov(j+1)) = (1/n²)∑ᵢ[½λᵢ²σᵢ⁴(n + 2G_n(ρᵢ²)) + λᵢ²σᵢ²m̂ᵢ²(n + 2G_n(ρᵢ))]`, `G_n(r) = ∑_{j<n}(n−(j+1))r^{j+1} = r(n(1−r) − (1−rⁿ))/(1−r)²` (induction: `G_{n+1} = G_n + r(1−rⁿ)/(1−r)`).
D (optional). β-scaled limit `t²τ² → ∑ᵢ½(1+(1−ηλᵢ)²)/(ηλᵢ(2−ηλᵢ)(1−ηλᵢ/2)²)` as t→∞ (the mean part is O(1/t)).

Numerical check (2D anchored Gaussian, t=20, g=0.7, h=0.3/t, rotated frame, 4·10⁶ chains): the closed form in A equals the exact tilted-Gaussian covariance of the probe to 1e-16 and matches Monte Carlo autocovariances at lags 0–8; the conditional mean equals probe + constant and tide 99's frame form; B's series and closed form agree (0.0082909); C's lag-sum, closed form and Monte Carlo agree (3.21e-4 vs MC 3.20e-4, τ²/n = 3.32e-4 at n = 25).

Questions:
1. Are A–C correct as stated? In particular the sign/exponent structure `ρᵢ^{2ℓ}` (quadratic part) vs `ρᵢ^ℓ` (mean part), and the identity `λ²σ²m̂²ρ^{2ℓ} + λ²σ²m̂²ρ^ℓ(1−ρ^ℓ) = λ²σ²m̂²ρ^ℓ` merging the cross and linear Wick terms.
2. Is "covariance under the stationary law between the observable and its ℓ-step conditional mean" the right formal proxy for the process autocovariance `Cov(Y₀,Y_ℓ)` given the seabed has no process object? Any subtlety (e.g. the ℓ-step burn-in Gaussian *is* the conditional law of x_ℓ given x₀ under the ULA kernel, so the tower property is the only unformalised step)? How should the note phrase this?
3. E5 reading: the LLC estimator averages `t·Y_k` over `n` stationary steps; `Var(t·Ȳ_n) ≈ t²τ²/n`. With `h = η/t` the per-mode integrated autocorrelation times are t-independent, so the number of steps for a given precision does not grow with `t` (unlike the burn-in and the minibatch bias). Is this the right E5 statement? Should the note contrast with independent replicates (variance `t²c₀/n`, tide 100's `d/2 + …`)? For the quadratic part `(1+ρ²)/(1−ρ²) ≈ 1/(ηλ)` vs the mean part `≈ 2/(ηλ)` — is the factor 2 the usual AR(1) vs squared-AR(1) autocorrelation (ρ vs ρ²)?
