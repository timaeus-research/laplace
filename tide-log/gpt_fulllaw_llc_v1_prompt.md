# Tide 107 consult: the LLC mean under the full (state-dependent) minibatch covariance law (laplace seabed, Lean 4 + Mathlib; E8)

Seabed: minibatch SGLD on the quadratic model has gradient noise whose covariance depends on the state; the note (E8) writes the stationary covariance as the solution of the *full law* `Σ = AΣAᵀ + N + c∑ᵢDᵢΣDᵢᵀ`, `Dᵢ = Hᵢ − H` (per-sample Hessian deviations), `c = h²t²(1−m/n)/(m(n−1))` (`minibatchCoeff`, from Cochran's finite-population correction, `minibatch_hessian_term`). `FullStep.lean` treats it as an affine contraction in the ℓ∞ operator norm: when `fullLipschitz A D c = ‖A‖‖Aᵀ‖ + c∑‖Dᵢ‖‖Dᵢᵀ‖ < 1` there is a unique solution `fullFixed`, PSD, with `fullFixed − S ⪰ 0` and `fullFixed − S − c·stateTerm D S ⪰ 0` for the PSD fixed point `S` of the additive law `covStep A N`. Tides 101/104/105/106: the additive law's solution `Σ^{mb} = lyapunovVia U ρ N ≻ 0`, `(t/2)tr(HΣ^{mb}) = (t/2)∑λᵢ(1 + ht²Ĉᵢᵢ/2)/(pᵢκᵢ)`, variances, long-run variance, scaling. Tide 101's consult said the constant-C law is an approximation and the full law's Hessian term is needed for exactness of the mean.

Candidates v1 (Claude):
A. Frame trace order: `X ⪰ 0 ⇒ 0 ≤ tr(HX)` for `H = U diag(λ) Uᵀ`, `λ ≥ 0` (`tr(HX) = ∑λᵢ(UᵀXU)ᵢᵢ`); `Y − X ⪰ 0 ⇒ tr(HX) ≤ tr(HY)`.
B. `tr(HΣ^{mb}) + c·tr(H·stateTerm D Σ^{mb}) ≤ tr(HΣ_full)` and `0 ≤ tr(H·stateTerm D Σ^{mb})`, hence the LLC chain `(t/2)∑λᵢ/(pᵢκᵢ) ≤ (t/2)∑λᵢ(1 + ht²Ĉᵢᵢ/2)/(pᵢκᵢ) ≤ (t/2)∑λᵢ(1 + ht²Ĉᵢᵢ/2)/(pᵢκᵢ) + (t/2)c·tr(H·stateTerm D Σ^{mb}) ≤ (t/2)tr(HΣ_full)`: the state-dependent noise inflates the LLC mean further, by at least the first-order Hessian-fluctuation term.
C. `tr(H∑ᵢDᵢSDᵢᵀ) = ∑ᵢ∑ⱼλⱼ∑ₖₗ(UᵀDᵢU)ⱼₖ(UᵀSU)ₖₗ(UᵀDᵢU)ⱼₗ` in the frame.
D (not formalised). The energy mean `E[½uᵀHu] = ½tr(HΣ) + ½mᵀHm` holds for any law with covariance `Σ`, Gaussian or not; the full law's stationary law is not Gaussian, so the identification of `(t/2)tr(HΣ_full)` with the LLC mean under the full law rests on this second-moment identity (prose).

Numerical check (3D, 5 random PSD per-sample Hessians, `m = 2`, `t = 20`, `h = 0.1/t`): Lipschitz below 1; `Σ_full − Σ^{mb} ⪰ 0`, `Σ_full − Σ^{mb} − c·stateTerm ⪰ 0`; tide 101's closed form and the frame formula match.
