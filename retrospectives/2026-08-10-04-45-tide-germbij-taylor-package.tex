\section{Setting}

The smooth-germ programme (C2--C5) is the last gap between the
formalised polynomial recovery core and germbij Theorem~3.1: the
note's own ``Justification'' paragraph, made formal. Its scoping
consult (archived in the tide log, with the note attached as context)
settled three design questions before any Lean: the fixed moving
split $|x| \le t^{-2/5}$ suffices at every finite order
\emph{provided} the Taylor power is integrated rather than sup-ed
over the inner region; Taylor degree $D = R+2$ suffices; and the
stabilizer $d\,x^M$ needs $M > R + 2$ --- not merely $M > R$, since
its scaled contribution first appears at rung $M - 2$ --- with an
elementary coefficient-wise formula for $d$.

\section{The thing formalised}

Three theorems in \texttt{Laplace/OneD/TaylorPackage.lean}, sorry-free
with zero warnings. The centerpiece is the stabilizer
envelope\footnote{\texttt{exists\_stabilizer\_envelope}.}: for
$a > 0$, higher coefficients at degrees $3, \dots, R'+2$, and even
$M$ above every degree present, an explicit
$d = \bigl(\sum_i |c_i| \rho^{3+i}\bigr)/\rho^M$ with
$\rho = \min(1, a/(2(B+1)))$ gives
\[
\frac{a}{2}\,x^2 \;\le\; a\,x^2 + \sum_i c_i\,x^{3+i} + d\,x^M
\qquad \text{for all } x,
\]
by a case split at $|x| = \rho$: inside, the cubic-and-higher tail is
absorbed by half the quadratic; outside, it is absorbed by the
stabilizer. No suprema, no compactness. Supporting it: the exact
scaling $\int |x|^k e^{-t a x^2} = q^{k+1} \int |y|^k e^{-a y^2}$
under $t q^2 = 1$\footnote{\texttt{abs\_moment\_scaling}.}, and the
tail bound\footnote{\texttt{tail\_integral\_le}.}
\[
\int_{|x| \ge r} |x|^s e^{-t K}
\;\le\; e^{-\frac{t}{2}\rho_K r^2}
\int_{\mathbb{R}} |x|^s e^{-\frac{t}{2}\rho_K x^2}
\]
for any envelope-dominated potential --- the superpolynomial
prefactor of the moving split, since $t r^2 = q^{-2/5}$ at
$r = q^{4/5}$.

One deliberate re-scope: the partition \emph{lower} bound requires an
upper envelope near the minimum, an admissibility hypothesis that
belongs with C3's setup for the smooth loss; it moves there, and the
moment upper bounds it would have carried are subsumed by scaling
plus domination.

\section{Honesty note}

Two process events worth the record. First, this tide's numerical
check could not certify the tail rate directly: the superpolynomial
regime $e^{-c q^{-2/5}} \ll q^N$ sets in only below
$q \sim 10^{-6}$, so a raw check at accessible $q$ shows
\emph{growing} values (the pre-asymptotic regime, not a
counterexample); the check was replaced by its inequality-level
driver, verified pointwise. Second, the first draft of the check
section was written in the same shell command as the check itself and
carried invented values --- the session's documented failure mode
recurring --- caught and corrected against the actual output before
any commit, with the rule sharpened: the check runs in one command,
the log text is written in a later one, quoting.

\section{What the next tide inherits}

Taylor comparison
$\langle x^s\rangle^{K_1} - \langle x^s\rangle^{K_2} = o(q^{s+D-2})$
for potentials with $K_1 - K_2 = o(|x|^D)$ at $0$, from the secant
bound, this tide's tail estimate on the moving split, the inner
integral with the Taylor power kept inside, and the quotient rule.
It should fix the admissibility package (including the re-scoped
partition lower bound) as its first act.
