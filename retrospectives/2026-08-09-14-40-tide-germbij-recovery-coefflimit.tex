\section{Setting}

The all-order expansion
tide\footnote{\tideref{2026-08-09-13-40-tide-germbij-recovery-allorder}.}
deliberated three candidates and deferred the third, the \texttt{Tendsto}
packaging, until the expansion API stabilized. It has, so this tide
closes it on the inherited deliberation: no fresh consult, the route was
agreed in the recorded round.

\section{The thing formalised}

One theorem in
\texttt{Laplace/OneD/RecoveryCoeffLimit.lean}\footnote{\texttt{quartic\_expansion\_coefficient\_limit}.},
sorry-free with zero warnings: for $b \geq 0$ and every $n$,
\[
\Bigl( Z_b(t) - \sqrt{2\pi}\sum_{j=0}^{n}
\frac{(-b)^j (4j-1)!!}{j!}\, t^{-(j+1/2)} \Bigr)\, t^{\,n+3/2}
\;\longrightarrow\;
\sqrt{2\pi}\,\frac{(-b)^{n+1}(4n+3)!!}{(n+1)!}
\qquad (t \to \infty).
\]
Reading down the ladder, every coefficient of the expansion is
recovered as a limit of partition-function values: the note's
``the expansion determines each coefficient'' in the form a consumer of
the data would actually use, and the analytic counterpart of the
archimedean recovery theorems earlier in the thread.

\section{Proof strategy}

Exactly the deferred plan: apply the all-order expansion at order
$n+1$; split the last term of the truncation with
\texttt{Finset.sum\_range\_succ}, whose rescaling collapses to the
constant coefficient (one \texttt{rpow} cancellation); the remaining
order-$(n+1)$ remainder rescales to at most $C/t$, squeezed to zero by
\texttt{squeeze\_zero\_norm'} against $C t^{-1} \to 0$; conclude by
\texttt{Tendsto.add} and congruence on the eventually-positive ray.

\section{Roadblocks and resolutions}

One: a bare \texttt{simp} could not close the \texttt{rpow} exponent
cancellation $t^{-(a)} \cdot t^{a} = 1$ after \texttt{rpow\_add}
(it normalized the exponent without recognising the sum as zero);
the explicit \texttt{show ... = 0 by ring} then \texttt{rpow\_zero}
chain is deterministic. Second build was clean.

\section{What was Mathlib, what was new}

Mathlib: the squeeze lemma, \texttt{tendsto\_inv\_atTop\_zero}, filter
congruence. Seabed: the all-order expansion, consumed at two adjacent
orders simultaneously, which is the whole trick. New: nothing beyond
the packaging; that is the point of a deferred-candidate tide.

\section{Lessons}

Deferring a candidate until its dependency stabilizes is cheap when the
deliberation is recorded: this tide needed no new decisions, only
execution. Explicit \texttt{show ... by ring} beats \texttt{simp} for
\texttt{rpow} exponent arithmetic, consistently across the whole
thread.
