\section{Setting}

The programme's summit. Stages 1--3 built, in order: the signed
exponential remainder, the second-order rescaled integral expansions,
and the Gibbs moment rates. This tide assembles them into the limit the
whole programme exists to prove.

\section{The thing formalised}

Three declarations in \texttt{Laplace/OneD/Kappa4Asymptotic.lean},
sorry-free with zero warnings: two private squeeze converters (an
order-two rate $|f - L - C/t| \leq K/(t\sqrt t)$ gives
$t(f - L) \to C$; a $\sqrt t$-rate gives a plain limit) and the
theorem\footnote{\texttt{kappa4\_anharmonic\_asymptotic}.}: for the
anharmonic potential with the standing hypotheses, writing $\mu_r$ for
the Gibbs expectation of $x^r$,
\[
t^3\bigl(\mu_4 - 4\mu_3\mu_1 - 3\mu_2^2 + 12\mu_2\mu_1^2 - 6\mu_1^4\bigr)
\;\longrightarrow\;
\frac{3\alpha^2}{\lambda^5} - \frac{\gamma}{\lambda^4}
\qquad (t \to \infty).
\]
The moment combination is the fourth cumulant; the limit was pinned
numerically at programme scoping and its cancellation table verified
symbolically before stage 1.

\section{Proof strategy}

Pure limit algebra over the stage-3 rates. The split
\[
t^3\kappa_4 = t\bigl(t^2\mu_4 - \tfrac{3}{\lambda^2}\bigr)
- 3\,t\bigl((t\mu_2)^2 - \tfrac{1}{\lambda^2}\bigr)
- 4\,(t^2\mu_3)(t\mu_1) + 12\,(t\mu_2)(t\mu_1)^2
- 6\,\frac{(t\mu_1)^4}{t}
\]
is one \texttt{ring}; the first piece converges to $C_4$ by the squeeze,
the second factors as a difference of squares and converges to
$2C_2/\lambda$, the third and fourth are products of first-order limits
(the seabed's mean asymptotic supplying $t\mu_1$), and the last
vanishes. The $t^{-2}$ Gaussian contributions cancel structurally
between $\mu_4$ and $3\mu_2^2$; the constant evaluation
($C_4 - 6C_2/\lambda - 180A^2/\lambda^2 + 108A^2/\lambda^2 =
3\alpha^2/\lambda^5 - \gamma/\lambda^4$) is the radical-atom
substitution and \texttt{ring}.

\section{Roadblocks and resolutions}

Six build iterations, every one a documented catalogue class:
\texttt{squeeze\_zero\_norm'} takes positional arguments;
\texttt{Real.sqrt\_atTop} is spelled \texttt{Real.tendsto\_sqrt\_atTop};
two dead \texttt{ring}s after \texttt{field\_simp}; one
\texttt{field\_simp} needing its explicit nonzero hypothesis; and
\texttt{tendsto\_const\_nhds} needing the type-ascribed anchor --- the
catalogue's own entry, which cost one iteration instead of a debugging
session.

\section{What was Mathlib, what was new}

Mathlib: \texttt{Tendsto} algebra and the squeeze. Seabed: everything
--- four stages of this programme and the original Stage-2 mean
asymptotic, composed and consumed. New: only the assembly.

\section{Lessons}

A programme scoped with a numerically pinned target and a symbolically
verified route runs its summit tide as pure bookkeeping: nothing
mathematical moved between the scoping consult and this proof. The
squeeze converters are the reusable shape for turning any of the
seabed's rate bounds into limits.
