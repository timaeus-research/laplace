\begin{abstract}
An optimization tide on the laplace seabed: no new mathematics, only
faster compilation of what is already proven. A forced rebuild of the
repo's own modules took 402\,s of wall time on a 96-core machine, 98\%
of it on a single serial module chain ending in the 19\,400-line
\texttt{Multi/CovarianceExplicit} (299\,s alone). Two acts. First,
profiling attributed the bulk of that file to one pathology:
\texttt{nlinarith} calls with small explicit hint lists, sitting in
proofs whose local contexts hold dozens of polynomial hypotheses.
Because \texttt{nlinarith} preprocesses the \emph{entire} context and
forms pairwise products, goals as trivial as $\surd t \cdot t \le t^2$
cost 65\,s each; converting 36 \texttt{nlinarith} and 20
\texttt{linarith} sites to their \texttt{only [\dots]} forms took the
module from 299\,s to 54\,s and the rebuild to 177\,s. Second, with the
covariance chain tamed, the critical path became a 24-hop chain of
small modules, each paying the $\sim$4.6\,s cost of loading the full
\texttt{import Mathlib} closure; a \texttt{shake}-driven import
narrowing (30 umbrella imports replaced, several chain edges cut, 8
false positives repaired and pinned in \texttt{noshake.json}) brought
the rebuild to 137\,s. Total: $402 \to 137$\,s wall ($-66\%$), CPU
$1215 \to 905$\,s, with the public signature fingerprint
byte-identical, 0 sorry/axiom, and
\texttt{\#print axioms} on the headline theorem unchanged.
\end{abstract}

\section{Setting}

The laplace seabed (commit \texttt{0224a74}) carries the Susceptibility
Primer's Laplace-asymptotics formalisation; its heaviest module,
\texttt{Laplace/Multi/CovarianceExplicit.lean}, proves the
explicit-coefficient covariance expansion
\texttt{gibbsCov\_first\_order\_rate\_explicit} through roughly 180
private bounding lemmas. A closing-tactic squeeze earlier the same day
(a gated \texttt{tryAtEachStep} sweep) had already established that
mechanical tactic replacement buys only $\sim$1\% here, and that the
module's cost is elaboration of a handful of monster proofs. This tide
went after those monsters directly, gated on statement invariance: the
sorted fingerprint of all non-\texttt{private} declaration signatures
must be byte-identical before and after, alongside the usual
0-sorry/0-axiom audit.

\section{Where the time went}

Baseline, measured with \texttt{lakeprof} on a forced rebuild of the
repo's own modules (dependency packages cached, Lake's artifact cache
disabled, page cache warmed by a discarded first build): 402\,s wall,
1215\,s CPU, and \emph{flat} simulated build times beyond 16 cores ---
the wall is one serial import chain,
\begin{gather*}
\texttt{Defs} \to \texttt{Basic} \to \texttt{QuadraticApprox} \to
\texttt{GaussianIBP} \to \texttt{RescaledIntegrals} \to {}\\
\texttt{Covariance} \to \texttt{CovarianceSharp} \to
\texttt{CovarianceExplicit},
\end{gather*}
with the last hop worth 299\,s (74\% of the total).

Inside that module, \texttt{-Dtrace.profiler} attribution showed Lean's
asynchronous proof elaboration already active, so the module wall is
(serial frontend) $+$ (longest proof tails). The frontend --- parsing
plus statement elaboration, measured directly with
\texttt{-Ddebug.byAsSorry=true} --- is only 15\,s. The tails were the
story: \texttt{abs\_bulkErr\_local\_le} at $\sim$323\,s CPU,
\texttt{abs\_bulkErr\_tail\_le} at 133\,s, \texttt{expNumErr₄\_bound} at
91\,s, then a dozen lemmas in the 20--40\,s band.

Drilling into the 323\,s proof found five sequential \texttt{h\_piece}
blocks, each dominated by a single \texttt{nlinarith} on a trivial
goal: $\surd t \cdot t \le t^2 \cdot 1$ given $\surd t \le t$, or $1
\cdot t \le t^2 \cdot 1$ given $1 \le t$, each costing $\sim$65\,s.
The hint lists were fine; the context was the problem. Default
\texttt{nlinarith} preprocesses every hypothesis in scope and forms
pairwise products before calling \texttt{linarith}, and these proofs
carry 40--60 polynomial hypotheses (\texttt{set} constants, Taylor
bounds, norm bounds), so each call paid a quadratic context tax
unrelated to its goal. This is triage case 1 of the skill's
\texttt{nlinarith} reference (``context-choke''), at five sites in one
proof.

\section{What changed}

All edits are in \texttt{Laplace/Multi/CovarianceExplicit.lean}; no
statement, name, or visibility changed anywhere.

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2em]
\item \textbf{All 34 hinted \texttt{nlinarith [\dots]} became
\texttt{nlinarith only [\dots]}.} The \texttt{only} form restricts
preprocessing to the listed terms. A blanket conversion compiled with
just two failures, each fixed by naming one context fact the original
call had used silently (\texttt{hsqrt\_inv\_le},
\texttt{h\_norm\_pow}). Effect: \texttt{abs\_bulkErr\_local\_le}
$323 \to 55$\,s; the module $299 \to 88$\,s.
\item \textbf{The two hot \emph{bare} \texttt{nlinarith} in
\texttt{expNumErr₄\_bound}} (19.8\,s and 10\,s) got explicit
\texttt{only} hint sets read off their goals ($D$-monotonicity and an
$8bD \le 8b(D + 1/\delta^2)$ padding step). Effect: $55 \to 33$\,s.
\item \textbf{Twenty \texttt{linarith} sites inside
\texttt{abs\_bulkErr\_local\_le} became \texttt{linarith only
[\dots]}.} \texttt{linarith} pays the same (linear) context tax; twelve
calls above 1\,s summed to 14\,s. The per-site hypothesis lists were
formulaic --- each piece closes from its own \texttt{h\_step},
\texttt{h\_eq}, \texttt{h\_final} --- so all twenty landed in one
compile. Effect: $55 \to 36$\,s.
\item \textbf{Import narrowing with Mathlib's \texttt{shake}} (after
the above landed, the critical path had shifted to a 24-hop chain of
4--16\,s modules --- mostly the forward/recovery arc --- where each hop
pays the full-Mathlib olean load before doing a few seconds of work).
\texttt{lake exe shake --fix} rewrote 55 files: 30 \texttt{import
Mathlib} umbrellas became targeted import lists, and several intra-repo
chain edges were cut with compensating direct imports downstream
(\texttt{ExpGraded} no longer waits for \texttt{ForwardDomain};
\texttt{Dilation} no longer waits for \texttt{Basic}; and so on).
Shake's analysis misses notation and names used only via transitivity:
8 files came out broken and were repaired --- one by re-adding the
notation-bearing import (\texttt{Gibbs} needs
\texttt{Integral.Bochner.Basic} for `$\int$'), the rest by restoring
their original imports --- and all are pinned in
\texttt{scripts/noshake.json} so future shake runs leave them alone.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Lessons}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=2em]
\item \texttt{nlinarith [hints]} in a fat context is an invisible
$40\times$ tax: the hints being small says nothing about the cost,
because the context is preprocessed regardless. \texttt{only} is a
three-character fix, and a blanket file-wide conversion is safe to
attempt --- the failures are loud, few, and each names exactly the
missing fact.
\item Attribute before restructuring. The obvious structural plays
(splitting the 19k-line file, per-piece private lemmas) were all
inferior to the tactic-flag fix, and some are blocked by the
statement-invariance gate (moving \texttt{private} helpers across
files would change the public surface).
\item \texttt{-Ddebug.byAsSorry=true} cleanly separates serial frontend
from proof tails; with async elaboration on, ``module wall $=$ frontend
$+$ longest tail from its spawn point'' predicted every measurement
here within a few seconds.
\item Measurement traps re-confirmed: warm the page cache before the
baseline recording, and remember \texttt{enableArtifactCache = true} in
the lakefile overrides \texttt{LAKE\_ARTIFACT\_CACHE=false}.
\end{itemize}

\section{Follow-ups}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=2em]
\item The next $\sim$25\,s of module wall sits in the five diffuse
tails; the mechanical option is hoisting their \texttt{have hpt}
pointwise-bound sub-proofs into \texttt{private} lemmas so they
elaborate in parallel. Estimated gain 10--15\,s, moderately invasive.
\item \texttt{Covariance} (32\,s) and \texttt{CovarianceSharp} (35\,s)
each carry one diffuse monster (27\,s / 30\,s) with the same hoisting
option.
\item Every module in the chain pays $\sim$4.6\,s to load the full
\texttt{import Mathlib} closure; targeted imports across the eight
chain files would recover an estimated 12--16\,s of critical path.
\item The \texttt{nlinarith only} pattern almost certainly recurs in
other seabeds' bound-heavy files; the blanket-convert-then-repair
recipe transfers directly.
\end{itemize}
