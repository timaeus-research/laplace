\begin{abstract}
Companion to the just-landed kthkth-moment-asymptotic tide; closes
the second of the two strict-improvement follow-ups named in the
kthkth-partition-asymptotic retrospective. Lifts the V1 (2D
unnormalised numerator) machinery from $(k_1, k_2) = (2, 3)$ to

\section{Setting}

This tide is the natural pair to the kthkth-moment-asymptotic tide.
The kthkth-partition retrospective (this morning) named two
follow-ups: the moment analogue (Z2 generic) and the numerator
analogue (Z1 generic). The moment tide landed earlier in this
session; the numerator tide closes the pair.

\section{The thing formalised}

For $k_1, k_2 \ge 1$, $j_1, j_2 : \mathbb{N}$, and $t > 0$:
\[
  \int\!\!\!\int x^{2j_1} y^{2j_2}\,e^{-t\,L(x,y)}\,dx\,dy
    \;=\; C'(k_1, k_2, j_1, j_2)\,
            t^{-(2j_1+1)/(2k_1) - (2j_2+1)/(2k_2)}
\]
on the 2D separable monomial potential, where $C'$ is the natural
generalisation of the
quartic-sextic numerator prefactor.

Plus the rescaled-\texttt{Tendsto} and
$\texttt{Asymptotics.IsEquivalent}$ packagings.

\section{Proof strategy}

Identical to the kthkth-moment tide's, with one difference: the 2D
integral here is the unnormalised numerator, so
$\texttt{integral\_separable\_addSeparable}$ supplies the
factorisation directly (no Gibbs normalisation). The 1D building
block is then $\texttt{kth\_moment\_even}$ (not the Gibbs-normalised
$\texttt{gibbsExpectation\_kthPotential\_even}$), and the rest is
$\texttt{Real.rpow}$ algebraic rearrangement.

\section{Roadblocks and lessons}

No roadblocks. Build clean first-try.

\textbf{Lesson:} the moment / numerator pair of mirror-shape lifts
land symmetrically when both have a corresponding 1D base case in
the seabed.\footnote{The moment tide uses
\texttt{gibbsExpectation\_kthPotential\_even}; this tide uses the
1D unnormalised numerator \texttt{kth\_moment\_even}.} Both lift
cleanly to 2D via the separable factorisation machinery from
\texttt{TwoD/AddSeparable.lean}.
