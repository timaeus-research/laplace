\begin{abstract}
\noindent The analytic heart of the J5 stage: on a fixed ball inside
both integration domains,
\[
\int \mathbf{1}_{B_\rho}(qx)\, P(x)\,
\frac{e^{-a_1(q,x)} - e^{-a_2(q,x)}}{q^{k-2}}\, dx
\;\longrightarrow\;
-\int P\, Q\, K_H,
\]
where $a_j$ are the rescaled losses and $Q$ is the degree-$k$
diagonal difference. The `HigherLaplaceDomain` structure extends the
H4 package with the shape consult's engineering choice --- a direct
order-$k$ Taylor remainder bound on a fixed ball --- and the matched
lower jets telescope the pairwise difference down to the two
remainders, giving the exact $q^{k-2}$ cancellation in the
dominator. The pointwise input composes three earlier tides: H3b's
rescaling limit for the exponents, J4's pairwise limit for the rate,
and J5a's scalar secant limit for the exponential difference.
\end{abstract}

\section{How the proof works}

The dominator is the chain
\[
\Bigl|P\,\tfrac{e^{-a_1}-e^{-a_2}}{q^{k-2}}\Bigr|
\le |P|\,\tfrac{|a_1-a_2|}{q^{k-2}}\max(e^{-a_1},e^{-a_2})
\le (C_1{+}C_2)\,|P|\,\|x\|^k e^{-c\|x\|^2},
\]
with the middle step J5a's secant bound, the difference bound from
the telescoped Taylor remainders (the $q^k$ from $\|qx\|^k$ splits
as $q^2 \cdot q^{k-2}$, feeding the rescaling denominator and the
rate exactly), and the Gaussian factor from both packages' rescaled
lower bounds at the minimum coercivity constant. Integrability is
the J5a general-rate layer. The pointwise limit is one application
of J5a's scalar theorem with exponents converging by H3b and the
rate converging by J4.
