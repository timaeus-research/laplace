\begin{abstract}
Unit 209 opens Astra~\#22's programme~A2 (general-$d$ stochastic transfer). Transferring
Headline~XIII to random phases needs remainder control along varying inputs; the mechanism is an
eventual equi-Lipschitz estimate for the normalised chart integral $F_N(\xi,\eta)=I_N(\xi,\eta)/S_N$.
Pointwise, $|e^{-\beta s^2+\beta as}-e^{-\beta s^2+\beta a's}|\le\beta s|a-a'|e^{-\beta s^2+\beta Ms}$
for $|a|,|a'|\le M$, $s\ge0$; integrating,
\begin{align*}
|I_N(\xi,\eta)-I_N(\xi',\eta')|&\le\|\eta-\eta'\|_\infty G_0(N)+\beta A\|\xi-\xi'\|_\infty G_1(N),\\
G_0&=\int u^he^{-\beta s^2+\beta Ms},\qquad G_1=\int u^h\,s\,e^{-\beta s^2+\beta Ms},
\end{align*}
with $s=Nu^k$ and $|\eta'|\le A$. Both $G_0/S_N$ and $G_1/S_N$ converge (Headline~XIII at constant
phase $M$, exponents $h$ and $h+k$; $G_1=N\cdot I^{h+k}$), so there is $L=L(h,k,\beta,M,A)$ with
$|F_N(\xi,\eta)-F_N(\xi',\eta')|\le L(\|\xi-\xi'\|_\infty+\|\eta-\eta'\|_\infty)$ for all large $N$
and all continuous inputs bounded by $M$ and $A$ on the cube
(\texttt{normalised\_lipschitz\_eventually}). Review v11 (units 206--208) was clean; its
documentation should-fixes are applied. Zero \texttt{sorry}/\texttt{axiom}; 9086 jobs.
\end{abstract}

\section{Mathematics}
The decomposition $\eta K-\eta'K'=(\eta-\eta')K+\eta'(K-K')$, the monotonicity $K_a\le K_M$ for
$a\le M$, and $|K_a-K_{a'}|\le\beta s|a-a'|K_M$ (from $|e^x-e^y|\le|x-y|e^{\max(x,y)}$, already in
\texttt{MixedBlockEnvelope}) give the integrand bound; \texttt{norm\_integral\_le\_of\_norm\_le}
integrates it. The factor $s=Nu^k$ in $G_1$ is exactly the exponent shift $h\mapsto h+k$
(\texttt{prod\_pow\_phaseShift} with $j=1$), so $G_1/S_N$ converges by Headline~XIII at
$(h+k,\lambda+\tfrac12)$ after the same $N^{-2(\lambda+1/2)}=N^{-2\lambda}N^{-1}$ bookkeeping as in
unit~200. The constant $L=(|C_0|+1)+\beta|A|(|C_1|+1)$ with $C_0,C_1$ the two limits is uniform in
the inputs because $G_0$, $G_1$ do not depend on them.

\section{Lean notes}
\begin{itemize}
\item \texttt{rw [integral\_const\_mul]} unifies up to beta, so a constant written as
  \texttt{(fun \_ => 1) u} still matches \texttt{?c * ?f}; when two constant-multiple integrals are
  present, give the constant explicitly (\texttt{integral\_const\_mul (β * A * δξ)}) to steer which
  one is rewritten.
\item \texttt{Real.rpow\_neg\_one} does exist on this pin (contrary to the CLAUDE.md note about
  \texttt{Real.rpow\_neg\_one} being missing) --- it rewrote $N^{-1}=N^{-1}$ here without complaint.
  Keep the CLAUDE.md caveat in mind but test.
\item Deriving $0\le A$, $0\le\delta$ from the bounds at the point $0\in[0,1]^d$ avoids adding
  nonnegativity hypotheses.
\end{itemize}
