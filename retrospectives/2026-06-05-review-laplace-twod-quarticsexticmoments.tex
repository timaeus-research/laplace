\begin{abstract}
A soundness review of \texttt{Laplace/TwoD/QuarticSexticMoments.lean}: the single closed-form
theorem $\langle x^{2j}y^{2k}\rangle_{2D}=\big((24/t)^{j/2}\Gamma(\tfrac{2j+1}{4})/\Gamma(\tfrac14)\big)
\big((720/t)^{k/3}\Gamma(\tfrac{2k+1}{6})/\Gamma(\tfrac16)\big)$ --- the ``T1'' factorisation that
the moment-asymptotic file consumes. Result: \textbf{a clean fidelity pass with no edits.} The file
has no \texttt{open} statements and no dead code; the reliably-safe bucket was empty.
\end{abstract}

\section{Setting}
This is the closed-form moment companion to the
\reviewref[QuarticSextic core]{2026-06-05-review-laplace-twod-quarticsextic}: it instantiates the
core's \texttt{gibbsExpectation\_quarticSextic\_factor} at $f=x^{2j}$, $g=y^{2k}$ and substitutes
the 1D quartic/sextic even-moment closed forms. Its single theorem is exactly the
\texttt{gibbsExpectation\_quarticSextic\_pow\_pow\_eq} (T1) consumed by the
\reviewref[moment-asymptotic specialisation]{2026-06-05-review-laplace-twod-quarticsexticmoment}.

\section{What was reviewed}
One public theorem, \texttt{gibbsExpectation\_quarticSextic\_pow\_pow\_eq}.

\section{Fidelity / soundness findings}
\textbf{Sound; no mismatch.} The product closed form is exactly the two 1D even-moment closed forms
(\texttt{quartic\_expected\_value\_even}: $(24/t)^{j/2}\Gamma(\tfrac{2j+1}{4})/\Gamma(\tfrac14)$;
\texttt{sextic\_expected\_value\_even}: $(720/t)^{k/3}\Gamma(\tfrac{2k+1}{6})/\Gamma(\tfrac16)$),
the $t>0$ hypothesis is present, and the docstring is appropriately scoped --- it states the closed
form for $t>0$, attributes it to factorisation plus the 1D forms, and does not overclaim.

\section{Simplifications applied}
\textbf{None --- the bucket was empty.} The file carries no \texttt{open} statements (so no dead
open to trim), its single import is load-bearing, and the proof is two genuinely-working
\texttt{rw} steps. The reviewer's one suggestion --- merging the two consecutive \texttt{rw} calls
into a single \texttt{rw [\dots]} block --- is cosmetic proof-golf, not a dead-code or
warning-clearing change, and was declined. No worktree was created.

\section{Disagreements and open questions}
None.

\section{Lessons}
\begin{itemize}[noitemsep]
  \item \textbf{A two-\texttt{rw} closed-form file is the minimal clean-pass shape.} With no opens,
    one load-bearing import, and a proof that is exactly ``apply the factorisation, substitute the
    two 1D forms'', there is nothing safe to land --- and the cosmetic \texttt{rw}-merge is not it.
    The empty bucket is the result, recorded as such.
\end{itemize}
