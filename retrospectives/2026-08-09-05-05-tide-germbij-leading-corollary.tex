\section{Setting}

Tenth tide of the germbij arc, making its two chains structurally parallel.
The 1D chain runs analyticity $\to$ growth bound $\to$ sector bound $\to$
composite $\to$ composed corollary; the multivariate chain had every link
except the last. This tide composes the leading-part
instantiation\footnote{\tideref{2026-08-09-04-35-tide-germbij-leading-part}.}
with the multivariate composite, mirroring the 1D analytic
corollary.\footnote{\tideref{2026-08-09-03-25-tide-germbij-analytic-corollary}.}

\section{The thing formalised}

One theorem in \texttt{Laplace/Multi/LeadingIdentifiability.lean}:\\
\texttt{leading\_part\_pencil\_difference\_lower\_bound}, sorry-free
with zero warnings. For $L_1, L_2 \geq 0$ on `$\iota \to \mathbb{R}$' whose
difference has a continuous homogeneous leading part $P$ of degree $m$
(nonzero at a direction of norm $3/2$, remainder $O(\|x\|^{m+1})$), with
$L_1 + L_2 \leq C_0 \|w\|^2$ on the ball of radius $R$ and a bump $\psi$
equal to $1$ there: there exist $\kappa > 0$ and
$T_0 = \max(4/R^2,\, u_0^{-2})$ such that for all $t \geq T_0$, under the
per-$t$ integrability premises,
\[
\kappa \cdot t \cdot t^{-m - d/2}
\;\leq\;
\int (L_2 - L_1)\, \psi\, \bigl( e^{-t L_1} - e^{-t L_2} \bigr)\, dw .
\]
The packaged constant, positive by \texttt{ENNReal.toReal\_pos}, is
$\kappa = \mathrm{vol}(S) \cdot c^2 e^{-4 C_0}$ with $S, c$ from the
instantiation.

\section{Proof strategy}

Pure composition plus one scale calculation: the threshold $T_0$ makes both
the window condition $4 \leq R^2 t$ and the instantiation's scale condition
$(\sqrt t)^{-1} \leq u_0$ hold simultaneously; the latter converts through
$u_0^{-1} \leq \sqrt t$ via \texttt{Real.sqrt\_sq},
\texttt{Real.sqrt\_le\_sqrt}, and the inverse-comparison lemma. The final
step is the multivariate composite applied verbatim, with one \texttt{ring}
re-association of the packaged constant.

\section{Roadblocks and resolutions}

One naming iteration: \texttt{inv\_le\_inv\_of\_le} no longer exists in
current Mathlib; the stable idiom for ``$a^{-1} \leq b$ iff $b^{-1} \leq a$''
is \texttt{inv\_le\_comm}$_0$. Otherwise the composition compiled
directly.
Operationally this tide again ran concurrently with the parallel session's
tides on the repo, without interference.

\section{What was Mathlib, what was new}

Mathlib: square-root and inverse comparison lemmas,
\texttt{ENNReal.toReal\_pos}. New: the threshold bookkeeping and the
packaging. As with the 1D corollary, the mathematical content was already
merged; the value is that the multivariate chain now has the same
user-facing shape as the 1D one.

\section{Lessons}

Renamed order-of-operations lemmas (\texttt{inv\_le\_inv\_of\_le} and kin)
are the most common single-error class in this arc; when one fails, look
for the \texttt{\_comm}$_0$ iff-form, which has been stable.
