\begin{abstract}
Second and final unit of the signed-reflection extension. Combining the exact reduction of unit~196
with the continuous-amplitude theorem of unit~186: the symmetric-box dressed normal moment,
normalised by $N^{-\lambda}(\log N)^{|J|-1}$, converges to the face coefficient of the signed
symmetrisation $\eta_{\rm sym}$ (\texttt{symmetric\_amplitude\_tendsto}); for equal ratios the limit is
$\eta(0)\prod_i(1+(-1)^{h_i})\cdot\Gamma(\lambda)\beta^{-\lambda}/(m!\prod_i2k_i)$
(\texttt{symmetric\_amplitude\_equal}), so \textbf{an odd exponent annihilates the equal-ratio leading
corner coefficient} (\texttt{symmetric\_amplitude\_odd}): the integral is then
$o(N^{-\lambda}(\log N)^m)$; it does not vanish, and no next-order rate is claimed. Headline~XI
wrappers restate this on the Bochner integrals. Zero \texttt{sorry}/\texttt{axiom}.
\end{abstract}

\section{Paper-facing meaning}
Interior normal coordinates with a continuous amplitude are now covered: the leading functional is
the face functional of the signed symmetrisation, and the paper's parity mechanism appears as the
factor $\prod_i(1+(-1)^{h_i})$ on the corner value in the equal-ratio case. Astra~\#18's caution is
formalised exactly: an odd exponent kills the corner coefficient (hence little-$o$ of the bare
scale), not the integral, and no faster rate follows.

\section{Lean notes}
Both theorems are one-line compositions of \texttt{integral\_symBox\_eq\_symAmp},
\texttt{amplitude\_tendsto}, \texttt{amplitudeCoeff\_equal} and \texttt{symAmp\_zero}; the odd case is
\texttt{Finset.prod\_eq\_zero} with \texttt{Odd.neg\_one\_pow}. The only friction was the usual
beta-redex in a \texttt{Tendsto.congr'} goal.
