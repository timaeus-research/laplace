\section{Setting}

Second tide of the germbij arc, following
\tideref{2026-08-08-19-11-tide-germbij-pencil}, which landed the pencil
identity for pairs of potentials. The identifiability theorem of the germbij
note (``What expectation values know about the loss landscape'', SRI,
August 2026, Theorem 7.3) needs one more quantitative ingredient: a
polynomial-in-$t$ lower bound for $\int a^2 \psi\, e^{-tK}$ when $K \geq 0$
vanishes at a point with at most quadratic growth and $a$ has finite
vanishing order there. This tide formalises that bound in one dimension, with
the analytic-germ input factored into the hypothesis $c\,w^m \leq |a(w)|$ on
$[0, r_0]$ (which is what analyticity supplies after shrinking the
neighborhood and choosing the side of the sign). The candidate was drafted
and deliberated during the previous tide's consult (candidate B there), so
this tide proceeded on the deliberation of record without a fresh consult.

\section{The thing formalised}

Two statements in \texttt{Laplace/Sector.lean}, both sorry-free. The
scale-window lemma: for $0 < u$, $2u \leq r_0$, $0 \leq c$, $0 \leq C_0$,
$0 \leq t$, with $K \leq C_0 w^2$ and $c\,w^m \leq |a(w)|$ on $[0, r_0]$ and
the integrand continuous on the window,
\[
u \cdot c^2 u^{2m} e^{-4 C_0 t u^2}
\;\leq\; \int_{[u,\, 2u]} a(w)^2\, e^{-t K(w)}\, dw .
\]
The Laplace-scale corollary (\texttt{sector\_lower\_bound}): substituting
$u = (\sqrt{t})^{-1}$, under $4 \leq r_0^2\, t$,
\[
c^2\, e^{-4 C_0}\, t^{-m - 1/2}
\;\leq\; \int_{[(\sqrt t)^{-1},\, 2 (\sqrt t)^{-1}]} a(w)^2\, e^{-t K(w)}\, dw ,
\]
with the exponent as a real power $t^{\,-(m : \mathbb{R}) - 1/2}$. This is
germbij Lemma~7.2 in dimension one. The numerical check (previous tide's
log): at $t = 100$, $K = w^2$, $a = w^2$, $r_0 = 1$, the integral is
$4.6 \times 10^{-6}$ against the bound $1.8 \times 10^{-7}$.

\section{Proof strategy}

The window lemma is a constant lower bound integrated over an interval: on
$[u, 2u]$ the amplitude factor satisfies $a^2 \geq c^2 u^{2m}$ (monotonicity
of powers plus $c\,w^m \leq |a|$ and $|a|^2 = a^2$) and the Boltzmann factor
satisfies $e^{-tK} \geq e^{-4 C_0 t u^2}$ (from $K \leq C_0 w^2 \leq
4 C_0 u^2$), so the pointwise product bound holds; the set-integral
monotonicity lemma against a constant\footnote{%
\texttt{MeasureTheory.setIntegral\_ge\_of\_const\_le}, with
\texttt{ContinuousOn.integrableOn\_compact} supplying integrability and
\texttt{Real.volume\_real\_Icc\_of\_le} evaluating the window length to $u$.}
finishes. The corollary is pure algebra of the substitution
$u = (\sqrt t)^{-1}$: positivity of $t$ from $4 \leq r_0^2 t$ (by
contradiction), $2 \leq r_0 \sqrt t$ from squaring, $t u^2 = 1$, $u^{2m} =
(t^m)^{-1}$, and the real-power identity $t^{-m - 1/2} = (t^m)^{-1} u$ via
\texttt{Real.rpow\_add}, \texttt{Real.rpow\_neg},
\texttt{Real.rpow\_natCast}, and \texttt{Real.sqrt\_eq\_rpow}.

\section{Roadblocks and resolutions}

None of substance: the file compiled on the first serious attempt, with two
lint-level fixes. One mathematical simplification surfaced by the linter: the
deliberated statement carried the hypothesis $0 \leq K$, but the proof never
uses it (only the upper bound on $K$ enters a lower bound on the Boltzmann
factor), so the landed statement is slightly stronger than the deliberated
one. The rpow/sqrt bookkeeping that the deliberation flagged as the main
Lean cost was real but contained: a five-rewrite chain covers it. The
\texttt{nlinarith} calls needed the usual explicit product hints
(\texttt{sq\_nonneg} of the right expressions).

\section{What was Mathlib, what was new}

All Mathlib lookup: set-integral monotonicity, interval volume, power monotonicity\footnote{\texttt{pow\_le\_pow\_left}$_0$.}, \texttt{Real.sq\_sqrt} and the
rpow API. New content: the two statements and the choice of hypothesis
factoring (the analytic input as $c\,w^m \leq |a|$), which is what makes the
statement elementary while remaining exactly the germbij Lemma~7.2 shape.

\section{Lessons}

When a deliberated statement carries a hypothesis the proof does not use,
drop it and note the strengthening; the linter catches this reliably. The
window-then-substitute decomposition (prove the bound on $[u, 2u]$ with $u$
free, then specialise $u = (\sqrt t)^{-1}$) cleanly separates measure theory
from rpow algebra and is worth reusing whenever a Laplace-scale bound is
needed.

\section{Follow-ups}

The composite tide: germbij Theorem 7.3 in dimension one, combining the
integrated pencil identity and comparison lemma (landed in the pencil
tide) with the sector bound of this tide, into: if $L_1, L_2
\geq 0$ vanish at $0$ with $L_1 + L_2 \leq C_0 w^2$ and $g = L_2 - L_1$
satisfies $c\, w^m \leq |g|$ on $[0, r_0]$, then for a bump $\psi \geq 0$
equal to $1$ near $0$, the observable $\phi = g \psi$ gives
$\int \phi\,(e^{-tL_1} - e^{-tL_2}) \geq c^2 e^{-4C_0}\, t^{1 - m - 1/2}$ for
$t \geq 4/r_0^2$: the quantity that contradicts $o(t^{-\infty})$ and proves
1D identifiability. The glue steps are restricting the integral to the
window (nonnegativity of the integrand via \texttt{exp\_pencil\_ge}) and the
pencil average.
