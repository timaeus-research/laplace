\begin{abstract}
The last two stages of the scoping consult's plan for the germbij
note's constructive class (c), and its designated endpoint. Stage C
is covariance injectivity: a continuous observable with vanishing
self-covariance under a positive reference density is almost
everywhere its mean --- the $L^2$ argument
$\int (Q - m)^2 e^{-P} = Z \cdot \operatorname{Cov}_P(Q,Q) = 0$ ---
hence constant by continuity against the open-positive volume, and a
vanishing value at the origin kills the constant; finite combinations
of nonconstant monomials need only the covariances against their own
support. Stage D composes this with the one-grade difference limit:
when the normalized moment differences of two families vanish at the
grade rate for every support monomial while the difference limits
equal the negative covariances, uniqueness of limits forces the grade
difference to vanish identically. With the exact moment-ratio
recovery for principal quasi-homogeneous models, the consult's
endpoint is realized: principal-model moment ratios are recoverable,
and conditional on a common recovered principal part, each finite
weighted correction grade is recoverable from normalized moment
rates.
\end{abstract}
