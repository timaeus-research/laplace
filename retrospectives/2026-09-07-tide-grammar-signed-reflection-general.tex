\begin{abstract}
Unit 216 (Astra~\#23 programme~D2, step~1). Unit~196 reduced the zero-phase dressed integral over the
symmetric box $(-1,1]^d$ to the unit box by reflecting coordinates; with a phase the kernel is no
longer a function of $x^{2k}$ alone, so the reduction is needed for an arbitrary continuous integrand:
\[
\int_{(-1,1]^d}F(x)\,dx=\sum_{\sigma\in\{\pm1\}^d}\int_{(0,1]^d}F(\sigma\cdot u)\,du
\qquad(\texttt{integral\_symBox\_eq\_sum\_reflect}).
\]
Proof by induction on $d$ with the Bochner box peel, the one-coordinate symmetrisation
$\int_{-1}^1g=\int_0^1(g(a)+g(-a))$, and the splitting of the sign sum along the first coordinate
(\texttt{Fin.consEquiv}). Zero \texttt{sorry}/\texttt{axiom}; 9093 jobs.
\end{abstract}

theorem integral_symBox_eq_sum_reflect : ∀ (d : ℕ) (F : (Fin d → ℝ) → ℝ), Continuous F →
    ∫ x in symBox d, F x = ∑ σ : Fin d → Bool, ∫ u in unitBox d, F (reflect σ u)

\section{Mathematics}
For $d+1$ coordinates: peel the first, $\int_{-1}^1\!\int_{(-1,1]^d}F(a,b)\,db\,da$; the inner
integral is $\sum_\tau\int_{(0,1]^d}F(a,\tau\cdot v)\,dv$ by induction; symmetrise $a$:
$\int_0^1\sum_\tau[\int F(a,\tau v)+\int F(-a,\tau v)]\,da$; on the right,
$\sum_{\sigma=(b,\tau)}\int_{(0,1]^{d+1}}F(\sigma\cdot u)$ peels to
$\sum_b\sum_\tau\int_0^1\!\int F(\operatorname{sgn}(b)a,\tau v)$ by
\texttt{reflect\_cons}; the two agree with $\operatorname{sgn}(\mathrm{true})=-1$,
$\operatorname{sgn}(\mathrm{false})=1$. The marginal integrabilities come from
\texttt{integrableOn\_pi\_box\_marginal} applied to $F\circ\mathrm{reflect}$.
