\begin{abstract}
A soundness review of \texttt{Laplace/OneD/JFunctionQuartic.lean}: the leading-order asymptotic of the
$J$-function for the quartic potential, $t^{1/4}\!\int_{\mathbb R}e^{-t\,w^4/24}\,\pi(w)\,dw\to
\pi(0)\cdot\tfrac12\cdot24^{1/4}\,\Gamma(1/4)$, via a substitute-then-dominated-convergence argument. Result:
\textbf{a clean fidelity pass, no edits} --- a genuinely substantive result (unlike the copy-paste quartic /
sextic specialisations), with the one ``dead open'' flag rejected as a repeat of an earlier over-flag.
\end{abstract}

\section{Setting}
This is Step~2 of the grammar-paper precursor sequence: a single-component blow-up at the degenerate quartic.
It consumes the \reviewref[pure-quartic moments]{2026-06-05-review-laplace-oned-quartic} (the partition closed
form and the integrability) but the argument itself --- a change of variables to a $t$-independent integrand
followed by the dominated-convergence theorem --- is new content, not a specialisation.

\section{What was reviewed}
Four declarations: a private substitution lemma ($u=t^{1/4}w$ reshapes the centred integral), a private
dominator-integrability lemma, the headline centred DCT theorem
(\texttt{quartic\_jfunction\_centered\_tendsto\_zero}: the prior-perturbation $\pi(w)-\pi(0)$ is negligible
against the concentrating Gibbs weight), and the user-facing asymptotic corollary.

\section{Fidelity / soundness findings}
\textbf{Sound; no mismatch.} The substitution is correct ($a:=t^{1/4}$, $a^4=t$, $(w a)^4=t w^4$, $(wa)/a=w$,
$\int g(wa)\,dw=|a^{-1}|\int g$ with $|a^{-1}|=1/a$ cancelling the outer $a$). The DCT step uses the right
envelope $2M\,e^{-u^4/24}$ (from $|\pi(u/a)-\pi(0)|\le 2M$) and the right pointwise limit ($u/t^{1/4}\to0$,
continuity, so $\pi(u/t^{1/4})\to\pi(0)$). The corollary splits $\pi=(\pi-\pi(0))+\pi(0)$, applies the
partition closed form to the constant piece, and uses $t^{1/4}(24/t)^{1/4}=24^{1/4}$ to land the constant
$\pi(0)\cdot\tfrac12\cdot24^{1/4}\,\Gamma(1/4)$.

One report-only note: the theorems assume \texttt{Continuous prior} (global), while the docstring speaks of a
prior ``continuous (at 0)''. This is not a defect --- global continuity is genuinely used to get
\texttt{AEStronglyMeasurable} of the integrand (the pointwise limit alone needs only continuity at $0$, but
measurability of a merely-continuous-at-$0$ prior is not free). The docstring captures the mathematical
essence (continuity at $0$ is what drives the limit); the global hypothesis is a sound formalisation choice.
A forward tide could weaken it to \texttt{ContinuousAt}~$+$~\texttt{AEMeasurable}; it is not a review-tide
edit.

\section{Simplifications applied}
\textbf{None.} The reviewer's only structural suggestion --- that \texttt{open Topology} ``looks unused'' ---
was \textbf{rejected}: the neighbourhood-filter notation $\mathcal N$ is the \texttt{Topology} scope's, and it
appears eight times in the file, \emph{including the two public theorem statements} ($\ldots\,\mathtt{atTop}\,
(\mathcal N\,0)$). Removing the open would break the build. This is the same over-flag the
\reviewref[third-cumulant review]{2026-06-05-review-laplace-anharmonic-third-cumulant} caught earlier in the
sweep --- GPT mislabels a load-bearing \texttt{open Topology} because every $\mathcal N$ is written with the
notation rather than a long-form name. The remaining suggestions (a duplicated $\le 2M$ estimate, two helper
\texttt{have}s) are tactic-golf and were declined. All three imports are genuinely used (including
\texttt{Haar.NormedSpace}, for the change-of-variables \texttt{integral\_comp\_mul\_right}); the file carries
no cruft.

\section{Disagreements and open questions}
None on the mathematics. The hypothesis note is surfaced for the user, not escalated.

\section{Lessons}
\begin{itemize}[noitemsep]
  \item \textbf{The \texttt{open Topology}/$\mathcal N$ over-flag is now a recognised recurring false positive.}
    Twice in this sweep the reviewer has called \texttt{open Topology} dead while $\mathcal N$ --- its scoped
    notation --- was load-bearing, here even in the public signatures. The standing rule holds: a dead-open
    claim is checked by grepping for the \emph{notation} the open provides ($\mathcal N$, $\int$, \dots), not
    only for long-form names, and the build is the final arbiter. When $\mathcal N$ appears, \texttt{open
    Topology} stays.
  \item \textbf{Not every file in a churned area is cruft-laden.} After a run of specialisation files thick
    with copy-pasted dead imports and dead tactics, this substantive J-function result was clean --- all
    imports used, all opens load-bearing, no warning. The audit value here was the fidelity confirmation of a
    non-trivial DCT argument, not a cleanup.
\end{itemize}
