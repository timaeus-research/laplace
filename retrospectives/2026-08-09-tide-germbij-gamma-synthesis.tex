\begin{abstract}
\noindent
Seven tides carried the seabed's anharmonic machinery from first to
second order and cashed the result as a recovery theorem: for the
potential $\tfrac{\lambda}{2}x^2 + \tfrac{\alpha}{6}x^3 +
\tfrac{\gamma}{24}x^4$, the fourth cumulant of the Gibbs measure
satisfies $t^3\kappa_4 \to 3\alpha^2/\lambda^5 - \gamma/\lambda^4$, and
consequently the complete jet $(\lambda, \alpha, \gamma)$ is determined
by observable data --- the germbij note's Theorem~3.1 triangular
induction, formalised through its third rung. The arc is a case study
in programme-level scoping: the target was pinned numerically and its
cancellation table verified symbolically before any Lean, and neither
moved in seven tides.
\end{abstract}

\section{The mathematics}

The germbij note's regular case (Theorem~3.1) recovers the Taylor
coefficients of a loss at a nondegenerate minimum from susceptibilities,
one order at a time. The seabed's Stage-2 programme (May 2026) had the
first two rungs' inputs: leading asymptotics for the mean and
covariance susceptibilities, from which $(\lambda, \alpha)$ recovery was
read off earlier in the recovery thread. The third rung needs
$\gamma$, which first appears in the \emph{fourth cumulant} at order
$t^{-3}$ --- behind an exact cancellation of the Gaussian $t^{-2}$
terms, so the leading-order machinery cannot see it. The programme
therefore had to deepen the entire analytic stack by one order:

\emph{Stage 1} --- the exponential Taylor remainder for signed
arguments, $|e^{-s} - \sum_{j<n}(-s)^j/j!| \leq \tfrac{|s|^n}{n!}
\max(1, e^{-s})$, because the rescaled perturbation
$s_t(u) = Au^3/\sqrt t + Bu^4/t$ changes sign.

\emph{Stage 2} --- the cubic-order integral remainder
($O(1/(t\sqrt t))$, reusing the seabed's two-branch Gaussian decay
verbatim) and the quadratised $J_n$ expansion
$J_n = m_n - \tfrac{A}{\sqrt t}m_{n+3} - \tfrac{B}{t}m_{n+4} +
\tfrac{A^2}{2t}m_{n+6} + O(1/(t\sqrt t))$, whose $A^2$ coefficient is
the payload.

\emph{Stage 3} --- second-order rates for the Gibbs moments themselves,
each riding an exact $1/t$ cancellation in
$J_r - (\text{leading} + \text{coeff}/t)J_0$ verified by \texttt{ring}
on explicit coefficients.

\emph{Stages 4--5} --- the limit: $t^3\kappa_4$ splits by one
\texttt{ring} identity into five rate-convertible pieces; the constants
sum to $108A^2 - 24B$ over $\lambda^2$, which is
$3\alpha^2/\lambda^5 - \gamma/\lambda^4$.

\emph{Stage 6} --- the recovery: composing with the merged
$(\lambda, \alpha)$ recovery, uniqueness of limits pins $\gamma$.
Fifteen lines, first-attempt clean.

\section{What made it work}

\emph{Scope before stage 1.} The scoping consult received the seabed
inventory and a numerically pinned target (high-precision quadrature
with a spike breakpoint --- the adaptive integrator misses the
$O(t^{-1/2})$-width peak without one), verified the full symbolic route
including the fourth-cumulant cancellation table, fixed a six-stage
plan, and named the risks. Both named risks were already discharged:
the coercivity risk by the seabed's standing discriminant hypothesis,
the domination risk by the existing \texttt{ScalarBound} layer. Nothing
in the plan was revised during execution.

\emph{The catalogue compounds.} Across seven tides, every build failure
belonged to a documented class --- and twice the failure was one this
very programme had documented days earlier (the radical-atom rewrite,
the type-ascribed constant witness). New entries added: the
$\sqrt t$-atom substitution for mixed $t$/$\sqrt t$ identities, the
\texttt{Pi.sub} member of the \texttt{Pi}-form family,
\texttt{set\_option} placement relative to docstrings, and
\texttt{gcongr}'s self-sufficiency with context hypotheses. Two tides
built first-try, including the finale.

\emph{Checkpointed WIP commits.} The two largest files (the moment
rates at $\sim$700 lines, stage 2's layers) were built layer by layer
with building, sorry-free commits at each seam, so no session boundary
or error ever implicated more than the newest layer.

\emph{Exact algebra over estimates.} At every level --- the $J_n$
terms, the moment ratios, the cumulant combination --- the design keeps
closed forms exact until a single explicitly bounded remainder, so
\texttt{ring} carries the mathematical content and the analysis reduces
to one squeeze per level.

\section{What it cost}

Seven tides (stage 2 split at its natural seam), roughly 1800 lines of
new Lean, five consults (one programme-scoping, the rest inherited),
and two numerical checks executed before their Lean. The de-privatised
seabed lemmas (\texttt{integrable\_J\_n},
\texttt{J\_0\_eventually\_bounded}) and the promoted catalogue entries
are the reusable residue.

\section{What remains}

The rungs above $\gamma$ (fifth cumulant onward) are structurally
identical ladders over the same machinery, one Taylor order deeper per
rung; a general-order Theorem~3.1 would want the pattern abstracted
rather than iterated. The multivariate regular case and the note's
remaining programmes (Proposition~4.1's flat counterexample,
multivariate Section~7.4) are separate commissions.
