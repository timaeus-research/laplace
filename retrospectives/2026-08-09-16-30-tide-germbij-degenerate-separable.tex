\begin{abstract}
Everything recovered from posterior moments so far lived at a
nondegenerate minimum, or in dimension one. This tide opens the
degenerate multivariate direction of the germbij note (\S 7.4(b))
with its exact separable germ: for
$L(w) = \sum_i a_i\, w_i^{2k_i}/(2k_i)!$ on a finite product of
lines, the Gibbs measure factorizes, each normalized coordinate
second moment is exactly its one-dimensional counterpart --- an exact
power law $C(k_i, a_i)\, t^{-1/k_i}$ --- and matched coordinate
second-moment data on a temperature ray recovers every degree $k_i$
and every scale $a_i$. The degrees are the note's anisotropic
weights $q_i = 1/(2k_i)$, read off exponents; the scales come from
leading constants. All analytic content is merged one-dimensional
material; the new work is the product bridge and the statement
design, which is exactly why this class was the right first step.
\end{abstract}

\section{Setting}

The germbij note's \S 7.4 asks when the recovery direction of the
correspondence survives degeneracy: at a singular minimum there is no
single scale, the expansion is graded by resolution data, and the
nondegenerate proof's isotropic rescaling is unavailable. The note's
constructive classes are dimension one (fully formalised), weighted
quasi-homogeneous germs (weights recoverable from moment exponents),
and Morse--Bott strata. The scoping consult for this tide confirmed
the separable weighted-monomial class as the minimal genuine germ of
the quasi-homogeneous story: assigning $q_i = 1/(2k_i)$ makes every
term weighted-degree one, so the class is degenerate, genuinely
multidimensional, anisotropic with unknown weights, and --- the
decisive property --- fully reducible to the merged one-dimensional
theory through the product structure. The consult explicitly rejected
the tempting non-separable example $x^4 + x^2y^2 + y^4$ as a first
step: its weights are common rather than anisotropic, its integrals
do not factor, and it abandons the strongest available seabed.

\section{The thing formalised}

On $\iota \to \R$ ($\iota$ finite) define
\code{separableMonomial}~$a$~$k$ as
$L(w) = \sum_i a_i \cdot L_{k_i}(w_i)$ with
$L_k(x) = x^{2k}/(2k)!$ the seabed's monomial potential, $a_i > 0$,
$k_i \ge 1$. Three layers:

\emph{Factorization.} The Boltzmann factor is a product of
one-dimensional Boltzmann factors, so the partition function
factorizes (\code{partitionFunction\_separableMonomial}), by the
unconditional finite-product Fubini --- no integrability side
conditions at all.

\emph{Coordinate reduction.} The normalized coordinate second moment
loses its spectator factors
(\code{gibbsExpectation\_coordSq\_separableMonomial}): the numerator
factorizes with a one-hot observable, the common factors cancel
against the denominator (each one-dimensional partition function is
strictly positive), and what remains is exactly the one-dimensional
normalized moment at coordinate $i$. On the one-dimensional side the
temperature absorbs the scale ($\langle x^2\rangle_{aL_k, t} =
\langle x^2\rangle_{L_k, ta}$), giving the exact power law
\[
  \bigl\langle w_i^2 \bigr\rangle_t \;=\;
  \left(\frac{(2k_i)!}{a_i}\right)^{1/k_i}
  \frac{\Gamma\!\left(\tfrac{3}{2k_i}\right)}
       {\Gamma\!\left(\tfrac{1}{2k_i}\right)}
  \; t^{-1/k_i}.
\]

\emph{Recovery.} \code{separableMonomial\_recovery}: if two separable
weighted-monomial potentials have equal normalized coordinate second
moments for every coordinate and all $t$ beyond some threshold, then
$k_1 = k_2$ and $a_1 = a_2$. Per coordinate, the seabed's power-law
rigidity (\code{eventual\_power\_eq}) forces the exponents
$-1/k_i$ equal, hence the degrees; cancelling the positive Gamma
ratio and applying \code{rpow} injectivity forces the scales. The
numerical check in the tide log confirms the closed form, the
coordinate reduction, and the recovery exponent to ten digits.

\section{Where progress stalled}

One process failure worth its lesson: the tide's worktree was created
while the canonical clone's \code{main} was stale (the previous
tide's merges had not been pulled), so the new file's first build was
\emph{vacuous} --- the import line had silently landed in a
\code{Laplace.lean} predating the file's dependencies, and
\code{lake build} reported success without compiling anything new.
The repository's own gate (verify the import line \emph{and} the
fresh \code{.olean}) caught it; the fix was a rebase onto
\code{origin/main}. The lesson is now in the log:
\code{tide-worktree create} bases on the canonical clone's local
\code{main}, so pull the canonical clone before opening a tide meant
to sit on the latest merges.

Lean-side friction was minor and familiar: a replace-all that
clobbered a theorem name of which the target was a prefix; a
rewrite under an integral binder that had to become
\code{congr}~+~\code{funext} first (the bound variable appears in
the \code{ite}); and the usual \code{rpow} bookkeeping for
$t^{-1/k}$, contained to one helper exactly as the consult
predicted for the separable route.

\section{Mathlib lookup versus new mathematics}

Lookup: \code{Real.exp\_sum}; the finite-product Fubini
\code{integral\_fintype\_prod\_volume\_eq\_prod}; the one-hot
collapse via \code{Finset.prod\_ite\_eq'} and
\code{Finset.mul\_prod\_erase}; \code{rpow} algebra; and the
seabed's one-dimensional closed forms and rigidity. New: the
statement design --- in particular taking the data per coordinate
(the entire large-$t$ moment function, exponents plus leading
constants; a single scalar at one temperature cannot determine both
the discrete $k_i$ and the continuous $a_i$) and phrasing the
analytic core on the plain pi type, leaving Euclidean wrappers for a
later layer, per the consult.

\section{What the next tide inherits}

The consult's roadmap continues: the full even multi-index
factorization and the exact exponent-affinity statement (normalized
$\ell(\alpha) = \sum_i \alpha_i q_i$, linear with zero intercept ---
the note's $(\ell(2e_i) - \ell(0))/2$ formula is the unnormalized
convention); then the genuinely non-separable step, an abstract
anisotropic scaling lemma ($P(\delta_s x) = sP(x)$ gives
$M_\alpha(t) = t^{-\sum \alpha_i q_i} M_\alpha(1)$ by diagonal change
of variables), which recovers weights without separability and
covers mixed examples like $x^4 + x^2y^2 + y^4$; and only then the
mixed-coefficient/Gelfand--Leray identification. Each is one tide,
in that order.
