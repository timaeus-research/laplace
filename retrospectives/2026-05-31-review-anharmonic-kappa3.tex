\begin{abstract}
The second Tide-review, and the first selected automatically by
\texttt{tide-review-pick}. The target was \texttt{Laplace/OneD/AnharmonicKappa3.lean}
--- the cross-repo bridge that derives the leading-order third cumulant of the 1D
anharmonic Gibbs measure, $t^2\,\kappa_3(x,x,x)\to-\alpha/\lambda^3$, from the
abstract \texttt{Threepoint.kappa3} framework and the concrete Laplace asymptotics.
GPT-5.5 Pro reviewed it against the Susceptibility Primer's $\backslash$leanref-tagged input
asymptotics and the file's retrospective, and returned a \emph{clean fidelity pass}:
it independently re-derived the historically error-prone limit constants
($-\alpha/\lambda^3$ and $-5\alpha/2\lambda^3$, the $3/2$-vs-$5/2$ coefficient) and
confirmed the cumulant identity, the trilinear $abc$ factor, and the signs. One
signature-preserving simplification was implemented under the review discipline
(extracting a duplicated $\langle x\rangle_t\to0$ derivation into a shared private
helper), affirmed by a second GPT pass, and merged. One genuine
\emph{terminology} finding --- the \texttt{*\_affine\_*} theorems handle scalar
multiples $b\cdot x$, not affine maps $c\cdot x+d$ --- was escalated to the human as
a future-tide rename rather than touched here.
\end{abstract}

\section{Setting}

This review continues the inward-facing arc opened by the pure-quartic
review.\footnote{\reviewref{2026-05-31-review-2d-pure-quartic}.} It is the first
review whose \emph{target} was chosen by the new \texttt{tide-review-pick} skill,
which surveys the seabeds for never-reviewed (or drifted-since-review) files and
ranks them by the richness of their informal anchor --- the principle being that an
independent fidelity check is most valuable where there is documented ground truth
to check against. The top recommendation was this file, on the strength of its
anchor: the Susceptibility Primer states its three input asymptotics with
$\backslash$leanref tags, so the reviewer had the paper's own constants to compare the Lean
against.

The reviewed file sits at \texttt{laplace} commit \texttt{b7188f4}. It is a
cross-repo bridge: it imports \texttt{Threepoint.kappa3} (the abstract third-cumulant
framework) and the concrete anharmonic asymptotics from
\texttt{IntegralRemainder.lean}, and assembles
\[
  t^2\,\kappa_3(x,x,x)\;\to\;-\frac{\alpha}{\lambda^3}
\]
for $L(x)=\tfrac\lambda2 x^2+\tfrac\alpha6 x^3+\tfrac\gamma{24}x^4$ with
$\lambda,\gamma>0$, $\alpha^2<3\lambda\gamma$.

\section{What was reviewed}

The file's eight declarations (three private helpers, five public theorems) build to
the headline κ₃ asymptotic and its corollaries (the third-moment asymptotic and the
affine / scalar-multiple lift).\footnote{Public theorems:
\texttt{secondMoment\_anharmonic\_asymptotic},
\texttt{kappa3\_anharmonic\_id\_id\_id\_asymptotic},
\texttt{thirdMoment\_anharmonic\_asymptotic}, \texttt{kappa3\_affine\_id\_id\_id\_eq},
\texttt{kappa3\_anharmonic\_affine\_asymptotic}.} The
informal corpus: the file's module docstring, the file's own
retrospective,\footnote{\tideref{2026-05-06-tide-anharmonic-kappa3}.} and --- the
load-bearing anchor --- three primer equations, each $\backslash$leanref-tagged to this seabed:
$\langle x\rangle_t=-\alpha/(2\lambda^2 t)+O(t^{-2})$,
$\Cov_t[x,x]=1/(\lambda t)+O(t^{-2})$, and
$\Cov_t[x^2,x]=-2\alpha/(\lambda^3 t^2)+O(t^{-3})$. The file's potential and
hypothesis triple match the primer verbatim.

\section{Fidelity findings}

\textbf{None --- a clean pass, with the arithmetic independently re-derived.} The
review's highest value here was checking the \emph{limit constants}, which combine
three input asymptotics through a cumulant identity and are exactly the kind of
coefficient bookkeeping that is easy to get wrong. GPT-5.5 Pro verified each step
from first principles:

\begin{itemize}[nosep]
\item the cumulant expansion $\kappa_3(x,x,x)=\langle x^3\rangle-3\langle x^2\rangle
  \langle x\rangle+2\langle x\rangle^3$ (coefficients $1,-3,+2$);
\item the headline $-\dfrac{2\alpha}{\lambda^3}-2\cdot\dfrac1\lambda\cdot
  \Big(-\dfrac{\alpha}{2\lambda^2}\Big)+0=-\dfrac{\alpha}{\lambda^3}$;
\item the third moment $-\dfrac{2\alpha}{\lambda^3}+\dfrac1\lambda\Big(-\dfrac{\alpha}
  {2\lambda^2}\Big)=-\dfrac{5\alpha}{2\lambda^3}$ --- explicitly flagging and
  confirming the historically error-prone $3/2$-vs-$5/2$ constant;
\item the trilinear $abc$ factor and the sign on $-(abc)\alpha/\lambda^3$.
\end{itemize}

\gptbox{I do not see a mismatch in any of the public theorem statements or limit
constants relative to the attached ground truth. The coefficient arithmetic that
was historically error-prone ($3/2$ vs $5/2$) is consistent in the final public
results here.}

\paragraph{A terminology finding (escalated, not fixed).} The
\texttt{*\_affine\_*} theorems --- and the docstrings calling them ``affine
multilinearity'' --- actually handle \emph{scalar multiples} $b\cdot x$, not affine
maps $c\cdot x+d$. No statement is wrong against its own target (the target was
exactly $(b\cdot x,a\cdot x,c\cdot x)$), so this is not a fidelity defect; but the
name and prose overclaim. Because renaming changes the public API, it is recorded as
a future forward-tide item, not touched in this review. This is the review
discipline working as intended: the reviewer surfaces a real imprecision, and the
fix that would change a signature is escalated rather than slipped into a
``simplification''.

\section{Simplification applied}

Of GPT's five proof-body suggestions, one was implemented; four were deferred to
keep the run bounded.

\paragraph{Landed.} Both the second-moment and the headline κ₃ asymptotic proofs
contained a near-identical
$\sim$20-line derivation of $\langle x\rangle_t\to0$ (as
$(1/t)\cdot(t\langle x\rangle_t)$, with $1/t\to0$). This was hoisted into a single
private \texttt{mean\_anharmonic\_tendsto\_zero} lemma that both proofs now call in
one line, and the now-unused local \texttt{h\_inv\_t} witnesses were removed. The
new lemma is \emph{private}: the file's five \emph{public} theorem signatures are
byte-identical before and after, which is the review discipline's hard invariant.
Full library build green, no \texttt{sorry}/\texttt{axiom}; the file drops from 488
to 479 lines with the duplication gone.

\paragraph{Deferred.} Replacing the square lambda-collapse \texttt{have}s with
\texttt{simp [pow\_two]} (the mixed-product ones genuinely need \texttt{ring}); a few
\texttt{.const\_mul} tidies; a \texttt{let L :=} local abbreviation. All
signature-preserving, all lower-yield; left for a later pass.

A second GPT-5.5 Pro pass on the diff affirmed the change (``a faithful realization
\dots\ preserves the public surface \dots\ overall verdict: accept'') and agreed both
the deferrals and the escalate-not-fix call on the naming.

\section{Disagreements and open questions}

None requiring adjudication: the fidelity pass was clean and the affirmation pass
accepted the implemented change. The only item handed to the human is the
\texttt{*\_affine\_*} naming, below.

\section{Lessons}

\begin{itemize}
\item \emph{The arithmetic check is the point.} For a file whose headline is a
  constant assembled from several asymptotics through an algebraic identity, the
  single most valuable thing an independent reviewer does is re-derive that constant
  from scratch. The clean pass here is real evidence, from a second model, that the
  $-5\alpha/2\lambda^3$ did not pick up the classic off-by-a-half.
\item \emph{Naming is a fidelity surface too.} ``Affine'' that is really
  ``scalar-multiple'' is exactly the sort of informal/formal gap a review should
  catch --- and exactly the sort whose fix (a rename) belongs in a forward tide, not
  a review. The two-bucket discipline routed it correctly without prompting.
\item \emph{Private-helper extraction is the safe simplification.} Hoisting shared
  proof code into a \emph{private} lemma keeps the public surface byte-identical by
  construction, so it sails through the statement-invariance gate. It is becoming the
  workhorse review simplification (cf.\ the \texttt{num\_zero} helper in the
  pure-quartic review).
\end{itemize}
