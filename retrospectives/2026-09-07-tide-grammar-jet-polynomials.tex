\begin{abstract}
For $\xi(u)=\sum_{i<R}x_iu^i+O(u^R)$ and $g=\xi-x_0$, the $u$-jet of $e^{\beta sg(u)}$ to order $R$
has coefficients $E_n(s)=\sum_{m<R}\frac{(\beta s)^m}{m!}[u^n](g_R(u)^m)$, finite sums of coefficients
of powers of the Taylor polynomial $g_R$. We prove the exponential jet remainder
$|e^{\beta sg(u)}-\sum_{n<R}E_n(s)u^n|\le Cu^R(1+s)^Re^{\beta L_1bs}$ on $[0,b]$ from the Taylor
remainder of $\xi$ and the Lipschitz bound $|g(u)|\le L_1u$, using the exponential tail bound, the
identity $a^m-b^m=(a-b)\sum a^ib^{m-1-i}$, and the truncation error of a polynomial on $[0,b]$. This
is the first half of Astra~\#3's target~2b (finite compatible jets as the engine of the all-order
theory). Zero \texttt{sorry}/\texttt{axiom}.
\end{abstract}

\section{Proof strategy}
$e^{\beta sg}-\sum_nE_nu^n=\big(e^{\beta sg}-\sum_{m<R}\tfrac{(\beta sg)^m}{m!}\big)
+\sum_{m<R}\tfrac{(\beta s)^m}{m!}\big(g^m-\mathrm{trunc}_R(g_R^m)\big)$. The first term is the
exponential tail $\le|\beta sg|^Re^{|\beta sg|}/R!$; for the second, $|g^m-g_R^m|\le m|g-g_R|(L_3b)^{m-1}$
by the geometric-sum identity and $|g_R^m-\mathrm{trunc}_R(g_R^m)|\le u^R\,\mathrm{truncConst}$ by the
polynomial truncation lemma. Powers $s^m$, $m\le R$, are absorbed into $(1+s)^R$.

\section{Roadblocks and resolutions}
\begin{itemize}
\item \texttt{Σ} is notation and cannot appear in an identifier (\texttt{hΣ}).
\item A placeholder inside \texttt{mul\_comm (|\_|)} leaves the type of the absolute value
undetermined and stalls instance search; restructure the bound as a separate \texttt{have}.
\item Distribute sums (\texttt{simp\_rw [Finset.sum\_mul]}) before \texttt{Finset.sum\_comm}.
\end{itemize}
