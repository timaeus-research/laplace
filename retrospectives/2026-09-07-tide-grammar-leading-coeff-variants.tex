\begin{abstract}
Following the audit in the second Astra consult, the leading theorem is exposed in coefficient-based
form: the asymptotic equivalence $Z\sim CN^{-p}\log^{d_0}N$ needs only $C\ne0$, and $C>0$ holds as soon
as $\eta(0,\cdot)\ge0$ on the noncritical box and $\eta(0,v_0)>0$ at one interior point, since the
positive kernel and weights make the coefficient a positive integral over an open set of positive
measure contained in the support. Zero \texttt{sorry}/\texttt{axiom}.
\end{abstract}

\section{Proof strategy}
\texttt{integral\_pos\_iff\_support\_of\_nonneg\_ae} reduces positivity to a positive measure of the
support; the open set $\{e>0\}\cap(0,b)^{d'}$ contains $v_0$, hence has positive Lebesgue measure
(\texttt{IsOpen.measure\_pos}), lies in the closed box, and lies in the support because every other
factor of the density is positive there.
