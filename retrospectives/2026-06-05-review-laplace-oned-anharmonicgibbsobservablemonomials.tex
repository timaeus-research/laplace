\begin{abstract}
A soundness review of \texttt{Laplace/OneD/AnharmonicGibbsObservableMonomials.lean}: the file that
discharges the \texttt{Threepoint.GibbsObservable} side conditions for the anharmonic monomial
observables $x,x^2,x^3$ (the witnesses the anharmonic FDT review consumed), plus the
$|x|^k e^{-tL}$ integrability lemma the partition-derivative base uses. Result: \textbf{a clean
fidelity pass with one dead-import removal} --- a redundant \texttt{IntegralRemainder} import that a
full build confirmed inert.
\end{abstract}

\section{Setting}
This is the witness factory below the anharmonic FDT/cumulant files: it proves, by dominated
differentiation under the integral sign, that the monomial observables satisfy the abstract
\texttt{GibbsObservable} predicate, so that the
\reviewref[FDT identities]{2026-06-05-review-laplace-oned-anharmonicfdt} become unconditional in
those side conditions. It also proves the $|x|^k e^{-tL}$ integrability lemma reused by the
\reviewref[partition-derivative base]{2026-06-05-review-laplace-oned-anharmonicpartitionhigherderiv}.

\section{What was reviewed}
\texttt{integrable\_\allowbreak abs\_\allowbreak pow\_\allowbreak mul\_\allowbreak exp\_\allowbreak neg\_\allowbreak t\_\allowbreak anharmonic}, the general
\texttt{Threepoint.\allowbreak anharmonic\_\allowbreak id\_\allowbreak gibbsObservable\_\allowbreak pow}, and the $k=1,2,3$ specialisations
(\texttt{\_id}, \texttt{\_mul\_self}, \texttt{\_mul\_mul\_self}).

\section{Fidelity / soundness findings}
\textbf{Sound; no defects.} The integrability lemma is the standard coercivity-to-Gaussian
domination: $c x^2\le L$ gives $e^{-tL}\le e^{-tcx^2}$, times $|x|^m$, integrable by the
Gaussian-times-polynomial lemma. The general \texttt{gibbsObservable\_pow} supplies the full
dominated-differentiation package --- $h=0$ numerator identity and integrability (reduced to the
integrability lemma), AE measurability by continuity, the dominator
$t\,e^{t/(2c)}|x|^{k+1}e^{-(t/2)L}$ (the $k=0$ pointwise bound times $|x|^k$), its integrability
(the lemma at exponent $k+1$, temperature $t/2$), the pointwise \texttt{HasDerivAt}, and the
\texttt{hasDerivAt\_integral\_of\_dominated\_loc\_of\_deriv\_le} finish. The three specialisations
are function-extensional transports along $x^1=x$, $x^2=x\,x$, $x^3=x\,x\,x$. The reviewer confirmed
all four.

\section{Simplifications applied}
\textbf{One dead import.} \texttt{import Laplace.OneD.IntegralRemainder} is referenced by no
symbol --- \texttt{integrable\_abs\_pow\_mul\_exp\_neg\_t\_anharmonic} is \emph{defined} here, not
imported from it. The reviewer flagged this as ``build-arbitrated only'', worrying that the Mathlib
lemma \texttt{integrable\_abs\_pow\_mul\_exp\_neg\_mul\_sq} used in the integrability proof might
come \emph{through} \texttt{IntegralRemainder}. A full \texttt{lake build} ($8306$ jobs) stayed green
with the import removed, settling it: that lemma is in scope via the kept imports, and no consumer
relies on the transitive dependency. \texttt{open MeasureTheory} and the other two imports are
load-bearing. Signatures byte-identical. GPT affirmed.

\section{Disagreements and open questions}
None. The reviewer's optional \texttt{simpa} compressions were declined as proof-golf.

\section{Lessons}
\begin{itemize}[noitemsep]
  \item \textbf{The reviewer's ``maybe this lemma comes from that import'' is exactly the
    transitive-load-bearing question --- and the full build answers it.} GPT correctly refused to
    call \texttt{IntegralRemainder} dead from the text alone, since
    \texttt{integrable\_abs\_pow\_mul\_exp\_neg\_mul\_sq} \emph{could} have lived there. The green
    full build is the adjudication: it does not. This is the same discipline as the
    \reviewref[AddSeparable]{2026-06-05-review-laplace-twod-addseparable} case (where the build went
    the \emph{other} way and kept a symbol-less import) --- ``no symbol referenced'' is a hypothesis
    the build tests, in both directions.
\end{itemize}
