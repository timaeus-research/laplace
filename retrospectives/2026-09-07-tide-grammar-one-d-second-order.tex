\begin{abstract}
For $Z(N)=\int_0^bu^h\eta(u)e^{-\beta(Nu^k)^2+\beta Nu^k\xi(u)}\,du$ with $\xi,\eta$ admitting
first-order Taylor expansions at $0$ with quadratic remainders, the density-transfer theorem (unit~83)
applied to the exact density $\rho(r,s)=k^{-1}r^{p-1}\eta(r^{1/k})e^{\beta s\xi(r^{1/k})}$ and the
kernel Taylor bound (unit~84) give, for $N\ge1$,
\begin{align*}
\big|Z(N)-C_0N^{-p}-C_1N^{-q}\big|&\le K'N^{-(h+3)/k},\\
C_0=\tfrac1k\eta_0S_p(a),\qquad C_1&=\tfrac1k\big(\eta_1S_q(a)+\beta\eta_0a_1S_{q+1}(a)\big),
\end{align*}
with $p=(h+1)/k$, $q=(h+2)/k$, $a=\xi(0)$, $\eta_0=\eta(0)$, $\eta_1=\eta'(0)$, $a_1=\xi'(0)$ and
$S_\lambda(a)=\int_0^\infty s^{\lambda-1}e^{-\beta s^2+\beta as}\,ds$. This is the two-term case of
\texttt{thm:TaylorTree} at $d=1$ with the coefficient at the second candidate exponent identified.
Zero \texttt{sorry}/\texttt{axiom}.
\end{abstract}

\section{Proof strategy}
The substitution $r=u^k$ (unit~44's \texttt{integral\_Ioc\_pow\_mul\_comp\_pow} with
$(u^k)^{1/k}=u$) writes the chart integral as a transfer integral with the exact density. The
density expansion has two terms indexed by \texttt{Fin 2}: $r^{p-1}c_0(s)$ and $r^{q-1}c_1(s)$ with
$c_0=k^{-1}\eta_0e^{\beta sa}$, $c_1=k^{-1}e^{\beta sa}(\eta_1+\beta s\eta_0a_1)$, and remainder
$r^{p+2/k-1}H(s)$, $H=Ck^{-1}(1+s)^2e^{3\beta Ls}$, read off from the kernel Taylor bound at
$u=r^{1/k}$. The weighted moments are finite because every coefficient is bounded by
$e^{\beta sa'}(A+Bs+Ds^2)$, i.e.\ by sums of weighted quadratic kernels; the log moments at degree~0
are the fluctuation masses $S_\lambda(a)$ by \texttt{weightedMass}.

\section{Roadblocks and resolutions}
\begin{itemize}
\item The subtracted sum in the \texttt{rem} field is $A-(B+C)$ after \texttt{Fin.sum\_univ\_two};
state the algebraic identity in that shape.
\item \texttt{rw [Fin.sum\_univ\_two]} rewrites only the first sum; use \texttt{simp only} to expand
both sides before matching against the transferred bound.
\item After \texttt{fin\_cases} on \texttt{Fin 2} the vector entries are already reduced; the
\texttt{Matrix.cons\_val\_*} simp arguments are then unused (linter).
\end{itemize}

\section{Outcome}
The first nonconstant-$\xi,\eta$ coefficient beyond the leading term is formalised, in the explicit
form predicted by Astra's consult ($C_1=k^{-1}[\eta'(0)S_q(a)+\beta\eta(0)\xi'(0)S_{q+1}(a)]$).
Next: the $d=2$ equal-exponent anchored subtraction (the coefficient $B$ seeing both axes) via the same
transfer theorem, and the face-parameter version of the second-order theorem under the strict gap
$q<\min_ip_i$.
