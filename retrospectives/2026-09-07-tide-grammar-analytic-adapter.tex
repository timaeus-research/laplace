\begin{abstract}
An amplitude given on the box by a double power series with a radius margin,
$\Phi(u,v,s)=\sum c_{ij}(s)u^iv^j$ with $|c_{ij}(s)|\rho^{i+j}\le H(s)$ and $b<\rho$, is packaged
into the data of the $d=2$ cutoff theorem (Astra~\#6, route~D): the jointly continuous amplitude
\texttt{anaAmp} (coordinates clamped to $[0,b]$ so that the series converges everywhere), the
compatible faces \texttt{anaFaceU}, \texttt{anaFaceV} of unit~113, the face remainders with envelope
$H(s)\rho^{-i-M}/(1-b/\rho)$, and the mixed remainder bound
$H(s)\rho^{-M_1-M_2}/(1-b/\rho)^2\cdot u^{M_1}v^{M_2}$. Continuity in $s$ comes from a locally uniform
bound (\texttt{continuous\_tsum\_of\_locally\_bounded}), which needs only continuity of the coefficient
functions and of $H$. Zero \texttt{sorry}/\texttt{axiom}.
\end{abstract}

\section{Mathematics}

The series $\sum_{ij}c_{ij}(s)u^iv^j$ converges absolutely on $[0,b]^2$ with the $s$-dependent bound
$H(s)\theta^{i+j}$, $\theta=b/\rho$; a global uniform bound is unavailable (the envelope grows with
$s$), but on every closed ball $H$ is bounded, and \texttt{continuousOn\_tsum} on the ball plus
\texttt{ContinuousOn.continuousAt} gives continuity at every point. Clamping the coordinates to
$[0,b]$ makes the amplitude and faces globally defined continuous functions agreeing with the series on
the box, which is all the cutoff theorem uses. The remainder bounds are unit~113's with
$1-v/\rho\ge1-b/\rho$ on $(0,b]$, and the $v$-face statements follow from the $u$-face ones for the
swapped coefficient array.

\section{Paper-facing meaning}

With this adapter, the $d=2$ equal-exponent cutoff expansion needs no compatibility assumption on
face jets and no symmetry of mixed partial derivatives: analytic data with a radius margin, the
paper's standing hypothesis, produce compatible faces by construction. The next unit discharges the
\texttt{FaceMoments} through \texttt{faceMoments\_of\_envelope} (all coefficients are bounded by
$C_0\rho^{-i-j}(1+s)^De^{\beta sL}$ when $H$ is) and states the analytic $d=2$ cutoff theorem; the
weighted-$\ell^1$ coefficient closure for $\eta e^{\beta s\xi}$ then supplies the series hypothesis
from those of $\xi,\eta$.

\section{Lean notes}

\begin{itemize}
\item \texttt{continuous\_tsum} needs a global bound; for envelopes growing in a parameter, prove
continuity at each point from \texttt{continuousOn\_tsum} on \texttt{Metric.closedBall x₀ 1}
(compact in a \texttt{ProperSpace}) and \texttt{IsCompact.exists\_bound\_of\_continuousOn}.
\item Clamping with \texttt{max 0 (min b x)} is simpler than \texttt{Set.projIcc} (no subtype
coercions); \texttt{clampB\_of\_mem} restores the identity on the box.
\item \texttt{field\_simp} cannot cancel $1-b/\rho$ without the normalised fact $\rho-b\ne0$; a
targeted \texttt{div\_mul\_eq\_mul\_div, div\_mul\_cancel₀ \_ hne} is more robust.
\item \texttt{gcongr} side goals of the form \texttt{0 ≤ v \^{} M} need \texttt{0 < v} as a bare
hypothesis in context (\texttt{have hv0 : 0 < v := hv.1}).
\item Symmetric statements for the two faces: define the swapped array once (\texttt{swapC}) and
transport the bound with \texttt{add\_comm}.
\end{itemize}
