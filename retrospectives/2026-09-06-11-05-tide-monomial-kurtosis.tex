\section{Setting}
The 1D monomial track of the laplace formalisation computes moments of $x$ against the symmetric
reference weight $\exp(-t\,x^{2k}/(2k)!)$ in Gamma closed form. The second cumulant --- the variance
--- was already landed as \texttt{monomial\_variance\_even}. This tide adds the fourth cumulant, the
connected four-point function, which for a symmetric weight (mean zero) is $\langle x^4\rangle -
3\langle x^2\rangle^2$. It is the minimal good next step: a self-contained closed form that reuses
only machinery already present, and mirrors an already-proved lemma structurally. It also opens the
cumulant ladder past the variance on the monomial track, complementing the anharmonic track's
$\kappa_3,\kappa_4$ work.
\section{The thing formalised}
For $k \ge 1$ and $t > 0$,
\[
  \langle x^4\rangle - 3\langle x^2\rangle^2
  = \Big(\tfrac{(2k)!}{t}\Big)^{2/k}
    \left(\frac{\Gamma(5/(2k))}{\Gamma(1/(2k))}
      - 3\left(\frac{\Gamma(3/(2k))}{\Gamma(1/(2k))}\right)^{2}\right).
\]
The Lean statement is \texttt{monomial\_excess\_kurtosis}, phrased with the raw
\texttt{gibbsExpectation} of $x^4$ and $x^2$. For the Gaussian reference $k=1$ the bracket is
$\tfrac34 - 3\cdot(\tfrac12)^2 = 0$: a Gaussian has zero excess kurtosis, the sanity check the tide
began from.
\section{Proof strategy}
The proof is a near-verbatim structural copy of \texttt{monomial\_variance\_even}. Rewrite $x^4$ and
$x^2$ into the even form $x^{2\cdot 2}$, $x^{2\cdot 1}$ (definitionally equal, so \texttt{rfl}), then
substitute the even-moment closed form \texttt{gibbsExpectation\_kthPotential\_even} at $j=2$ and
$j=1$. The two terms then share the power $\big((2k)!/t\big)^{2/k}$, once the square of the
$j=1$ term's power $\big((2k)!/t\big)^{1/k}$ is folded in via
$\big((2k)!/t\big)^{2/k} = \big(\big((2k)!/t\big)^{1/k}\big)^2$, an instance of \texttt{Real.rpow\_add}.
After a \texttt{push\_cast} to align the natural-number casts in the exponents and Gamma arguments,
that single power rewrite plus \texttt{ring} closes the goal --- the Gamma ratios ride along as
opaque atoms.
\section{Roadblocks and resolutions}
None. The tide compiled on the first attempt; the mirror discipline (find the closest landed lemma,
copy its skeleton, change the arithmetic) made it a mechanical exercise once the survey had picked
the target. The only care needed was ordering \texttt{push\_cast} before the power rewrite so the
$\mathbb{N}$-cast $2$ in the substituted exponent matches the $\mathbb{R}$-literal $2$ in the
\texttt{Real.rpow\_add} lemma.
\section{What was Mathlib, what was new}
Everything analytic was already in the seabed: the closed-form even moments, the integrability
inputs behind them, and the \texttt{Real.rpow} algebra. New here is the fourth-cumulant closed form
itself, and the observation that it vanishes at $k=1$. No GPT consult was needed.
