\begin{abstract}
Paper-facing wrappers for the mixed-ratio general-$d$ monomial milestone (units 177--181), stated on
the Bochner box integral $M(N)=\int_{(0,1]^{m+1}}\prod_ix_i^{h_i}e^{-\beta N\prod_ix_i^{2k_i}}\,dx$:
the exact weighted reduction, the dominated-coordinate transfer principle, the mixed-ratio
asymptotic with the explicit Gamma/residue constant
$\Gamma(\lambda)\beta^{-\lambda}/(|J|-1)!\cdot\prod_{i\in J}\frac1{2k_i}\prod_{i\notin J}\frac1{h_i+1-2k_i\lambda}$,
and its form at the computed minimal ratio. The mirror \texttt{grammar\_lean.tex} gains the labelled
equation \texttt{eq:normal\_moment\_mixed\_lean} inside \texttt{rem:normal\_moment\_lean} with a
\texttt{\textbackslash leanrefL} dot, and the pinned commit is bumped to \texttt{ef76bda}. Zero
\texttt{sorry}/\texttt{axiom}.
\end{abstract}

\section{Paper-facing meaning}
For the deterministic positive-orthant unit-cutoff normal moment integral, the paper's zeta-function
prediction is now formalised in full generality of exponent pattern and dimension: decay exponent
equal to the minimal ratio, logarithmic degree one less than its multiplicity, and the Laurent
coefficient $a_{-|J|}$ of eq.~\texttt{a\_minus\_m\_explicit} (at $b=1$) times the Laplace--Tauberian
factor $\Gamma(\lambda)/(|J|-1)!$. Excluded: cutoffs $b\neq1$, interior parity assembly, variable
amplitudes, stochastic phase terms, the full Taylor-tree expansion.
