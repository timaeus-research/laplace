\begin{abstract}
For the equal-exponent block and an amplitude given on the box by a double power series with a radius
margin, $\Phi(u,v,s)=\sum c_{ij}(s)u^iv^j$ with $|c_{ij}(s)|\rho^{i+j}\le H(s)\le C_0(1+s)^De^{\beta sL}$
and $b<\rho$, the reduced cutoff expansion of units~107--109 holds with the faces
$a_i=\sum_jc_{ij}v^j$, $b_j=\sum_ic_{ij}u^i$ and the corner jets $c_{ij}$, and no further hypotheses:
\[
Z_\Phi(N)-\mathrm{reducedSum}(N)=O\bigl(N^{-(p+\min(M_1/k_1,M_2/k_2))}(1+\log N)\bigr).
\]
All face remainders, the mixed remainder and the moment hypotheses are discharged by units~110 and~114.
This is the paper-facing higher-order $d=2$ theorem under the paper's own analyticity assumption, in the
Schwarz-free form recommended in Astra~\#6. Zero \texttt{sorry}/\texttt{axiom}.
\end{abstract}

\section{Mathematics}

The theorem is an instantiation of \texttt{twoD\_cutoff\_reduced\_isBigO}. Continuity of the amplitude
and faces is unit~114's; the face remainders are $H(s)\rho^{-i-M_2}/(1-b/\rho)\cdot v^{M_2}$ and the
mixed remainder $H(s)\rho^{-M_1-M_2}/(1-b/\rho)^2\cdot u^{M_1}v^{M_2}$, both of the required envelope
form once $H\le C_0(1+s)^De^{\beta sL}$. The \texttt{FaceMoments} bundles come from unit~110's
constructor: every coefficient $c_{im}$ with $m<M$ is bounded by
$C_0\rho^{-i}(1+\rho^{-1})^M(1+s)^De^{\beta sL}$, the face envelopes by the corresponding constants,
and the corner data by $C_0\rho^{-i-j}$ with a zero remainder. The resonance conditions
$\gamma<M$ for the $u$-faces, $v$-faces and corners are the same arithmetic as in unit~107, now
extracted as lemmas.

\section{Paper-facing meaning}

Under the paper's standing hypothesis (analytic data near the exceptional divisor, here in the form of
a convergent double series on a larger polydisc), the $d=2$ equal-exponent block has the complete
finite-cutoff expansion: exponents $p+i/k_1$ and $p+j/k_2$, logarithms exactly at collisions, constant
coefficients given by the weighted finite parts of the face series, remainder
$O(N^{-(p+\tau)}(1+\log N))$ with $\tau=\min(M_1/k_1,M_2/k_2)$. No compatibility or moment hypothesis
remains. What the theorem does not yet do: produce the double series of $\eta e^{\beta s\xi}$ from those of
$\xi,\eta$ (the weighted-$\ell^1$ coefficient closure, Astra~\#6 rank~2) and merge the three log
families into one copy per collision.

\section{Lean notes}

\begin{itemize}
\item Symmetric hypotheses are discharged by symmetric lemmas: \texttt{vface\_gamma\_lt} is
\texttt{uface\_gamma\_lt} with the coordinates swapped, and the $v$-face envelopes come from
\texttt{anaCoeff\_env} applied to \texttt{swapC c} followed by \texttt{simpa [swapC]}.
\item A two-factor inequality inside \texttt{calc} is \texttt{mul\_le\_mul} with two
\texttt{positivity} side goals; \texttt{gcongr} may close the main goal and leave a side goal in
an unexpected slot.
\item Long theorem applications with many \texttt{?\_} placeholders are more legible as
\texttt{refine … ?\_ ?\_} followed by bullet proofs than as inline lambdas.
\end{itemize}
