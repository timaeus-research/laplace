\begin{abstract}
A soundness review of \texttt{Laplace/TwoD/QuarticSexticPartitionAsymptotic.lean}: the
$(k_1,k_2)=(2,3)$ specialisation of the 2D separable partition asymptotic, $Z_{2D}(t)=C''\cdot
t^{-1/4-1/6}$ for $L(x,y)=x^4/24+y^6/720$. Result: \textbf{a clean fidelity pass with two dead-open
removals} --- both \texttt{open Real} \emph{and} \texttt{open MeasureTheory}, the latter dead here
(unlike its generic sibling) because the specialisation consumes the closed form directly and
carries no integrability blocks.
\end{abstract}

\section{Setting}
This is the concrete $(2,3)$ instance --- the ``first instance'' per the seabed \texttt{CLAUDE.md}
--- of the partition leg whose generic-$(k_1,k_2)$ form was reviewed at
\reviewref[KthKthPartitionAsymptotic]{2026-06-05-review-laplace-twod-kthkthpartition}. It does not
re-run the lift machinery: it takes the seabed closed form
\texttt{partitionFunction\_quarticSextic\_eq} (from \texttt{TwoD/QuarticSextic.lean}) and repackages
it as a \texttt{const}$\cdot t^{(\dots)}$ identity, a rescaled \texttt{Tendsto}, and an
\texttt{IsEquivalent}.

\section{What was reviewed}
Three public theorems plus the constant
$C''=\tfrac16\,24^{1/4}\,720^{1/6}\,\Gamma(1/4)\,\Gamma(1/6)$. The constant is exactly the $(2,3)$
instance of the generic \texttt{kthKthPartitionConst} ($4!=24$, $6!=720$, $\tfrac12\cdot\tfrac13
=\tfrac16$), and the exponent $-1/4-1/6$ matches $-1/(2k_1)-1/(2k_2)$.

\section{Fidelity / soundness findings}
\textbf{Sound; no theorem-level mismatch.} The constant and exponent are correct, the closed-form
combination $(1/2)(1/3)=1/6$ is right, and the exact theorem carries \texttt{ht : 0 < t} where
needed. One report-only prose note: the module docstring states the closed-form identity without
restating $t>0$ --- terse-but-true narrative (the $t$-power superscript carries the parameter, and
the theorems require it), the same recurring elision seen across the \texttt{TwoD/} module
docstrings; left as is, consistent with the
\reviewref[KthKthOddMoments]{2026-06-05-review-laplace-twod-kthkthoddmoments} and
\reviewref[AddSeparable]{2026-06-05-review-laplace-twod-addseparable} reviews.

\section{Simplifications applied}
\textbf{Two dead opens.} \texttt{open Real} is dead (all \texttt{Real.*} qualified, bare
$t\,\hat{}\,(\text{real})$ is \texttt{rpow}). And --- the distinguishing feature of this file ---
\texttt{open MeasureTheory} is \emph{also} dead: unlike the generic sibling (and the
numerator/moment legs), this specialisation has no \texttt{have hU/hV : Integrable \dots} blocks, so
there is no bare \texttt{Integrable} or \texttt{$\int$}; \texttt{partitionFunction}/%
\texttt{addSeparable} are used as project defs. With the open line reduced to
\texttt{open Filter Asymptotics} (plus scoped \texttt{Topology}), the module is green at $2702$
jobs, no warnings, signatures byte-identical. GPT affirmed (and had flagged the
\texttt{MeasureTheory} removal as ``if the build confirms'' --- it did).

\section{Disagreements and open questions}
None. The reviewer's other suggestions (\texttt{show \dots\ by ring} $\to$ named-\texttt{have};
dropping the terminal \texttt{exact} wrappers) were declined as proof-golf.

\section{Lessons}
\begin{itemize}[noitemsep]
  \item \textbf{A specialisation can be \emph{more} trimmable than the generic it instantiates.} The
    generic legs keep \texttt{open MeasureTheory} load-bearing through their inline integrability
    proofs; this $(2,3)$ file delegates the closed form to a seabed lemma and never touches
    \texttt{Integrable}/\texttt{$\int$}, so a second open dies. The dead-open verdict is per-file
    even within a copy-paste family --- the build is the arbiter, and ``if the build confirms'' is
    the honest way to flag a second open.
  \item \textbf{The \texttt{t>0} module-prose elision is a family-wide pattern, not a per-file
    drift.} Several \texttt{TwoD/} module docstrings state the $t$-dependent identity without
    restating $t>0$. Fixing them one at a time is noise; a single batched hygiene tide is the right
    granularity.
\end{itemize}

\section{Follow-ups}
\begin{itemize}[noitemsep]
  \item The remaining \texttt{QuarticSextic*} files (\texttt{Numerator}/\texttt{Moment}%
    \texttt{Asymptotic}, \texttt{QuarticSextic}, \texttt{QuarticSexticMoments},
    \texttt{QuarticSexticOddMoments}) --- check each for the \emph{second} dead open
    (\texttt{MeasureTheory}) as well as the first, since the asymptotic-packaging ones likely share
    this file's no-integrability shape.
  \item Batched hygiene tide: add ``for $t>0$'' to the \texttt{TwoD/} module docstrings that state
    a $t$-dependent closed form; plus the \texttt{show}$\to$\texttt{have} proof-golf.
\end{itemize}
