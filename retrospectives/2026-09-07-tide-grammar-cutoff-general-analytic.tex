\begin{abstract}
The analytic adapter of unit~115 generalised to independent starting exponents
$p_1=(h_1+1)/k_1$, $p_2=(h_2+1)/k_2$: for an $s$-parametrised double series with envelope
$|c_{ij}(s)|\rho^{i+j}\le H(s)\le C_0(1+s)^De^{\beta sL}$ and the shifted compatibilities, the merged
expansion holds with remainder $O(N^{-\min(p_1+M_1/k_1,\,p_2+M_2/k_2)}(1+\log N))$
(\texttt{twoD\_cutoff\_general\_analytic}), and hence for the chart amplitude $\eta e^{\beta s\xi}$
of two analytic functions (\texttt{twoD\_cutoff\_general\_amplitude}). The proof was generated
mechanically from the equal-start proof by replacing the three admissibility bounds and the final
assembly theorem: the equal-exponent hypothesis entered nowhere else. Zero
\texttt{sorry}/\texttt{axiom}.
\end{abstract}

\section{Mathematics}

Nothing new beyond unit~127: the face envelopes $H(s)\rho^{-i-M_2}/(1-b/\rho)$, the mixed
remainder constant $C_0\rho^{-M_1-M_2}/(1-b/\rho)^2$ and the \texttt{FaceMoments} constructions
from the envelope are exactly as in the equal case; only the three admissibility inequalities
$\gamma_i<M_2$, $\gamma'_j<M_1$, $\gamma_{ij}<M_1+k_1M_2$ now come from the shifted compatibilities
(the last via $\gamma_{ij}<M_1$, Astra~\#9).

\section{Paper-facing meaning}

Together with unit~127 this is the $d=2$ cutoff theorem of \texttt{thm:TaylorTree} for arbitrary
$(h,k)$, under the paper's analyticity hypothesis alone, in merged normal form. The regrouping by
exponent and the ``for every $T$'' statement for unequal starts follow in the next unit with the
cutoffs $q=\max(2T,p_1,p_2)+1$, $M_\ell=\lceil k_\ell(q-p_\ell)\rceil$.
