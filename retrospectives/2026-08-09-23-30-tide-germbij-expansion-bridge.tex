\begin{abstract}
An audit of the nondegenerate half of the germbij note against what
is actually merged found a language gap: the recovery theorems
consume internal rate hypotheses --- pairwise rescaled moments
matching to $o(q^{k-2})$ --- while the note's Theorem~3.1 speaks of
equal asymptotic expansion families, moment functions of the
temperature agreeing beyond all orders. The audit consult ranked
closing this gap as the highest-value immediate target, ahead of
location recovery, the analytic germ corollary, and the (still
missing) forward direction. This tide closes it. The normalized
localized moment at temperature $t = q^{-2}$ equals $q^k$ times the
machinery's rescaled moment for a degree-$k$ homogeneous observable
(the dilation prefactors cancel in the quotient), and a
superpolynomially small difference of temperature-level moment
functions transports through the substitution and a fixed-power
division to every $o(q^r)$ rate. The headline theorem then reads
exactly like the note: monomial moment families with the same
asymptotics force equal derivative tensors at every order.
\end{abstract}

\section{Setting}

The user's direction fixed the priority: the core is recovery of all
coefficients and the main theorem in the nondegenerate case. The
audit (consult archived with the tide log) found four gaps between
the merged corpus and the literal Theorem~3.1: this data-language
bridge (B), recovery of the location $w^*$ (D), the analytic germ
corollary (C), and the forward direction --- existence of the
arbitrary-order expansion with jet-determined coefficients (A), the
remaining substantive half. Its explicit caveat is worth repeating:
B together with C would \emph{not} justify claiming Theorem~3.1
formalised; B+D+C completes the inverse half only. The strategic
order is B, D, A, C, E, and this tide is B.

\section{The thing formalised}

Three layers. The temperature-level object:
\code{posteriorMoment}~$A$~$f$~$q$ is the quotient of localized
posterior integrals at scale $q$, and \code{posteriorMomentT} is its
reparametrization by $t$ through $q = (\sqrt t)^{-1}$; the merged
dilation identity cancels the prefactors $q^d e^{-L(0)/q^2}$ in the
quotient, and a degree-$k$ homogeneous observable satisfies
\[
  \langle P \rangle_{t = q^{-2}}
  \;=\; q^{k} \cdot \mathrm{rescaledMoment}(P, q).
\]
The rate transport (\code{isLittleO\_pow\_of\_superPoly}): if $f$ is
beyond all orders in $t$, then for every $m$ and $r$
\[
  q \;\longmapsto\; \frac{f(q^{-2})}{q^m}
  \quad\text{is}\quad o(q^r) \text{ at } 0^+
\]
--- compose the little-$o$ along $t = q^{-2}$
(\code{tendsto\_inv\_sq\_atTop}), convert the power through
\code{rpow} algebra, and absorb the division by choosing the
order $N$ with $2N \geq m + r$. The headline is\\
\code{smooth\_jet\_recovery\_of\_superPoly\_moments}: if for every
degree $k > 2$ and every monomial word the two losses' localized
moment functions differ by a \code{SuperPoly} function of $t$ ---
the note's own convention for ``the same asymptotics'' --- then,
with the usual packages and base matching, every derivative
tensor agrees. The proof is the transport at $(m, r) = (k, k-2)$
composed with the merged monomial recovery theorem.

\section{Where progress stalled}

Nothing structural; three catalogued-class iterations. The notable
one: rewriting with $2N = r + (2N - m - r) + m$ cascaded into the
occurrence of $2N$ \emph{inside} the exponent $2N - m - r$ on the
other side of the equation --- the catalogue's cascading-rewrite
trap in a fresh costume --- fixed by a calc step against the
reversed equation, which rewrites the composite exponent as a whole.
The others: \code{Set.Ioi} membership needing explicit conversion to
$0 < q$ before \code{pow\_pos}, and the current Mathlib name
\code{Ioo\_mem\_nhdsGT} for one-sided interval membership.

\section{Mathlib lookup versus new mathematics}

Lookup: little-$o$ composition along a map
(\code{IsLittleO.comp\_tendsto}), the one-sided inverse limit
\code{tendsto\_inv\_nhdsGT\_zero}, \code{rpow} algebra, and the
merged dilation identity. New: only the two definitions and the
observation that they make the merged machinery speak the note's
language --- which is the point of the tide. The mathematics was
finished in the recovery programmes; this is the statement finally
matching the paper.

\section{What the next tide inherits}

The audit's remaining inverse-half items: (D) location recovery ---
$\langle w_i \rangle_t \to w_i^*$, nearly free from the merged
first-moment limit, plus superpolynomially matched first moments
forcing $w_1^* = w_2^*$; then (C) the analytic germ corollary via
the identity theorem. After those, the substantive remaining half of
Theorem~3.1 is (A), the forward direction, staged by the audit
consult as a six-stage programme (rescale/localize, exponent Taylor
packaging, numerator expansion, quotient division, coefficient/jet
packaging, return to $t$).
