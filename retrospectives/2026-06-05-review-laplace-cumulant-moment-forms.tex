\begin{abstract}
A soundness review of \texttt{Laplace/OneD/AnharmonicCumulantMomentForms.lean}: the third and fourth
connected cumulants of $u=-(tx)$ re-expressed in physically transparent moment form,
$\kappa_3=\langle u^3\rangle_0-3\langle u^2\rangle_0\langle u\rangle_0+2\langle u\rangle_0^3$ and the
analogous five-term $\kappa_4$, with $\langle u^k\rangle_0$ the unperturbed Gibbs expectation. Result: \textbf{a
clean fidelity pass, no edits}. This is the last unreviewed file of the one-dimensional anharmonic arc, so it
closes a nine-file review sweep.
\end{abstract}

\section{Setting}
The arc proved $\kappa_3,\kappa_4$ first as rational closed forms in the partition values $G_n(0)$ (the form
the asymptotic analysis needs), and then --- in the file reviewed here --- in the moment form a physicist
reads off directly, $\kappa_n=$ (raw-moment expansion of $\langle u^k\rangle_0$). The two forms are the same
object dressed differently; this file is the bridge, and the $\kappa_2$ moment form lives in its sibling
\texttt{anharmonic\_susceptibility\_eq\_connected\_variance}
(\reviewref[$\kappa_2$ review]{2026-06-05-review-laplace-anharmonic-second-cumulant}).

\section{What was reviewed}
Two public theorems: \texttt{anharmonic\_third\_cumulant\_moment\_form} and
\texttt{anharmonic\_fourth\_cumulant\_moment\_form}, each equating $\mathrm{iteratedDeriv}\,(n{-}1)$ of the
mean $M=G_1/G_0$ at $0$ (the same LHS object as the rational cumulant theorems) with the raw-moment expansion
in $\langle u^k\rangle_0=\mathrm{gibbsExp}\dots(\lambda x.\,(-(tx))^k)$.

\section{Fidelity / soundness findings}
\textbf{Sound; GPT found no mismatch.} The coefficients were the focus and are the standard raw-moment
cumulant expansions:
\begin{itemize}[noitemsep]
  \item $\kappa_3$: $m_3-3m_2m_1+2m_1^3$, coefficients $(1,-3,+2)$.
  \item $\kappa_4$: $m_4-4m_3m_1-3m_2^2+12m_2m_1^2-6m_1^4$, coefficients $(1,-4,-3,+12,-6)$.
\end{itemize}
Both close the same way: the helper $\langle u^k\rangle_0=G_k(0)/G_0(0)$ (moment-normalisation composed with
$\mathrm{iteratedDeriv}\,k\,(G_0)\,0=G_k(0)$) rewrites every Gibbs expectation to a partition ratio, the
rational cumulant theorem rewrites the LHS, and \texttt{field\_simp} clears the $G_0(0)$ denominators ---
recovering, for $\kappa_3$, $G_3G_0^2-3G_2G_1G_0+2G_1^3$ over $G_0^3$, byte-identical to the reviewed
rational form, and analogously for $\kappa_4$. The observable is written uniformly as $(-(tx))^k$ (including
the $k=1$ case as $(-(tx))^1$ rather than $-(tx)$) --- a presentation choice, faithful.

\section{Simplifications applied}
None. GPT's six proposals were all proof-refactoring or golf --- factor the duplicated \texttt{hmom} and
\texttt{hne} locals into private helpers, \texttt{simp\_rw [hmom]} instead of the explicit \texttt{hmom 4 \dots
hmom 1} list, \texttt{norm\_num}$\to$\texttt{simp} on a trivial side goal, \texttt{field\_simp}$\to$\texttt{field\_simp
[hne]}, and a descriptive rename of \texttt{hmom}. None removes dead code, subsumes a hand-rolled argument
with a Mathlib lemma, or drops a hypothesis; helper-extraction and golf are the declined classes. The file was
left byte-identical.

Two of GPT's \emph{non}-findings are worth recording, because they are the right calls: \texttt{open
MeasureTheory} is \textbf{load-bearing} here (the statements write \texttt{(volume : Measure $\mathbb R$)}
with unqualified \texttt{Measure}/\texttt{volume}, thirteen times) and \texttt{hne} is \textbf{not dead} (it is
the $G_0(0)\ne0$ side condition \texttt{field\_simp} consumes from context). The contrast with the immediately
preceding review is sharp: there \texttt{open MeasureTheory} was genuinely dead and removed; here the same open
is essential. The discriminator is, as always, whether any name or notation the open provides is actually
used --- and here \texttt{Measure}/\texttt{volume} are right there in the statements.

\section{Disagreements and open questions}
None on the mathematics.

\section{Lessons}
\begin{itemize}[noitemsep]
  \item \textbf{Two adjacent files, same open, opposite verdict --- and that is fine.} A dead-open finding is
    per-file, never per-name-globally: \texttt{open MeasureTheory} was dead in
    \reviewref[the cumulants-from-log-partition file]{2026-06-05-review-laplace-cumulants-logpartition} (no
    \texttt{Measure}/\texttt{volume}/\texttt{$\int$} anywhere) and load-bearing here (both names in the
    statements). The grep is the test, and it must be run against \emph{this} file's text, not a habit carried
    from the last one.
  \item \textbf{Closing an arc-level sweep is itself a result.} With this file the entire one-dimensional
    anharmonic arc --- nine files, from the moment-normalisation foundation through the FDT asymptotics, the
    $\kappa_2/\kappa_3/\kappa_4$ ladder, the cumulant generating function, and now the moment forms --- has
    been independently reviewed end-to-end. Eight clean-no-edit passes and one dead-open removal; zero
    mathematical errors found across the arc. That uniformity is the headline, not any single file.
\end{itemize}
