\section{Setting}

The smooth-germ programme's named trap is that replacing a smooth
loss by its Taylor polynomial produces \emph{order-controlled}
moment errors, never exact equality --- so the polynomial recovery
theorems, whose public forms take eventually-equal data, cannot be
invoked directly. The scoping consult prescribed stage C1: a theorem
interface in which every data hypothesis is a vanishing limit at the
coefficient-sensitive scale.

\section{The thing formalised}

Two theorems in \texttt{Laplace/OneD/StableRecovery.lean}, sorry-free
with zero warnings on the first
build\footnote{\texttt{jet\_recovery\_stable} and
\texttt{nondegenerateJet\_recovery\_stable}.}: variable-base recovery
from pure \texttt{Tendsto} data --- the second-moment difference
tends to $0$, and each rung's scaled moment difference
$q^{-r}(F^1_{2k+r} - F^2_{2k+r})$ tends to $0$ --- concluding
$a_1 = a_2$ and $c^1 = c^2$; and its $k = 1$ form in the note's
$\lambda/2$ normalisation.

\section{Why it was nearly free}

The consult predicted the existing proofs would ``adapt''; in fact no
adaptation was needed at all. The programme's internal theorems were
already limit-based --- the strong-induction recovery takes scaled
\texttt{Tendsto} hypotheses, and the base recovery was built with an
\texttt{\_of\_tendsto} variant precisely as the bridge --- so C1 is
eleven lines of composition. Only the outermost public packaging had
hardened its data to eventual equality (for the $t$-transfer, where
exact equality is natural). The moral, worth keeping: maintaining the
limit-based interface alongside the equality interface at every layer
is what makes a stability programme cheap later.

\section{Deliberation and check}

Prior-deliberation path (C1 is specified verbatim in the archived
scoping consult); no distinct numerical check is feasible or needed
--- the statement is an interface weakening of theorems whose
analytic content was checked in the two preceding tides, and it adds
no closed form.

\section{What the next tide inherits}

Stages C2--C6 are the genuinely analytic remainder: two-sided
quadratic control near the minimum and domain splitting (C2),
rescaled Taylor-remainder bounds on the moving inner region (C3),
normalized-quotient and cutoff transfer (C4), the finite-order smooth
recovery (C5), and full-jet quantifier packaging (C6). These need a
dedicated scoping consult with the note's ``Justification'' paragraph
as the primary source; C2's outer estimate in particular must decide
what global hypothesis stands in for the note's quadratic bound.
