\begin{abstract}
The paper's bare normal moment integral lives on $[0,b]^{|I|}$ with an arbitrary cutoff $b>0$; the
monomial milestones were stated on the unit box. The substitution $u=bv$ on $\mathbb R^d$ gives the
exact identity $M_b(N)=b^{\sum_i(h_i+1)}M_1(Nb^{2\sum_ik_i})$ (\texttt{monomialBoxRealCutoff\_eq}),
and hence the mixed-ratio asymptotic on $(0,b]^d$ with the extra factor
$b^{\sum_i(h_i+1)-2\lambda\sum_ik_i}=\prod_{i\notin J}b^{h_i+1-2k_i\lambda}$ --- exactly the
$b$-dependence of the paper's Laurent coefficient $a_{-|J|}$ --- and no $b$-dependence at all in the
equal-ratio case. Zero \texttt{sorry}/\texttt{axiom}.
\end{abstract}

\section{Mathematics}
$(0,b]^d=b\cdot(0,1]^d$ and Mathlib's \texttt{setIntegral\_comp\_smul\_of\_pos} on the
$d$-dimensional space give $\int_{b\cdot S}f=b^d\int_Sf(bv)\,dv$; the monomial factor contributes
$b^{\sum h_i}$ and the exponent absorbs $b^{2\sum k_i}$ into $N$. The asymptotic transfer is a product
of two limits: the unit-box ratio along $N\mapsto Nb^{2\sum k_i}$ and $(\log(Nc)/\log N)^r\to1$; the
scale factors combine as $b^{\sum(h_i+1)}\,c^{-\lambda}=b^{\sum(h_i+1)-2\lambda\sum k_i}$.

\section{Paper-facing meaning}
Item~(iii) of Astra~\#15's list: the cutoff dependence of the leading coefficient matches
eq.~\texttt{a\_minus\_m\_explicit} ($\prod_{i\notin J}b^{h_i+1-2k_i\lambda}$), and is absent when all
ratios are equal. Astra~\#16 (received during this unit) ranks the continuous-amplitude milestone
next and records a leading-coefficient qualification for mixed ratios (the amplitude is integrated
over the nonminimal normal directions, not evaluated at the origin).
