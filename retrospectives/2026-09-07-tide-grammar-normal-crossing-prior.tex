\section{Mathematics}
Continuity gives $\rho\ge\rho(0)/2$ on a sup-norm ball $B_\delta$ around the origin, $\delta\le1$, which
lies in the box; there the kernel is at least $e^{-N^2/2-|Nz|}$ since $|x_0x_1|\le1$. So
$\int_{(-1,1]^2}\rho K\ge\int_{B_\delta}\rho K\ge\tfrac{\rho(0)}2e^{-N^2/2-|Nz|}\,|B_\delta|>0$,
using nonnegativity of the integrand on the whole box for the monotonicity in the domain. The truth's
likelihood factor is a positive constant.
