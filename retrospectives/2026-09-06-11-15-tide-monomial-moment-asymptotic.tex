\section{Setting}
The laplace seabed's 2D separable-monomial track proves the mixed moment
$\langle x^{2j_1} y^{2j_2}\rangle_t$ is asymptotically equivalent to a pure power of $t$
(\texttt{KthKthMomentAsymptotic}), via a four-step pattern: exact factorisation into a product of
1D moments, rewriting the $t$-dependence as $C\cdot t^{-\ldots}$, a rescaled \texttt{Tendsto} to the
constant, and the \texttt{IsEquivalent} packaging. That 2D result quietly leans on the corresponding
one-dimensional statement, which existed nowhere as a standalone lemma. This tide supplies it.
\section{The thing formalised}
For the symmetric weight $\exp(-t\,x^{2k}/(2k)!)$ with $k\ge 1$, and every $t>0$,
\[
  \langle x^{2j}\rangle_t = C(k,j)\cdot t^{-j/k},\qquad
  C(k,j) := (2k)!^{\,j/k}\,\frac{\Gamma((2j+1)/(2k))}{\Gamma(1/(2k))},
\]
and hence $\langle x^{2j}\rangle_t \sim C(k,j)\,t^{-j/k}$ at $\mathrm{atTop}$. The Lean file gives
all four rungs (\texttt{kthMomentConst}, \texttt{\_eq\_const\_mul\_rpow},
\texttt{\_rescaled\_tendsto}, \texttt{\_isEquivalent\_rpow}), the single-factor mirror of the 2D
trio. The equivalence is, in fact, an eventual equality: the moment is exactly the power law for
every positive $t$, so there is no asymptotic remainder to control --- the content is entirely in the
closed form the earlier tides already established.
\section{Proof strategy}
Each rung copies its 2D counterpart with one factor instead of two. The closed form
$\langle x^{2j}\rangle_t = ((2k)!/t)^{j/k}\,\Gamma((2j+1)/(2k))/\Gamma(1/(2k))$
splits by \texttt{Real.div\_rpow} into $(2k)!^{j/k}\,t^{-j/k}$ times the Gamma ratio, and
\texttt{ring} finishes once $t^{-j/k}$ is written as $(t^{j/k})^{-1}$ by \texttt{Real.rpow\_neg}. The
rescaled \texttt{Tendsto} is the constant sequence in disguise: multiplying by $t^{j/k}$ and
combining the powers with \texttt{Real.rpow\_add} and \texttt{add\_neg\_cancel} collapses it to
$C(k,j)$, so \texttt{tendsto\_const\_nhds.congr'} applies. The equivalence is
\texttt{IsEquivalent.refl.congr\_left} against the closed form, valid eventually via
\texttt{eventually\_gt\_atTop}.
\section{Roadblocks and resolutions}
None; the tide compiled on the first attempt. The four-step pattern is documented in the seabed's
own notes, and the single-factor specialisation is mechanical once the closest 2D file is read.
\section{What was Mathlib, what was new}
Mathlib supplied only the \texttt{Real.rpow} algebra and the \texttt{IsEquivalent}/\texttt{Tendsto}
API. Everything else --- the closed-form even moment and its integrability inputs --- was already in
the seabed. New here is the packaged 1D asymptotic itself, the base the 2D lift had been assuming.
No GPT consult was needed.
