\section{Setting}

Stage 2a built the cubic-order remainder layer; this tide completes
stage 2 with the exact algebra and the headline. Together they upgrade
the seabed's rescaled integral asymptotics to second order, which is
the analytic heart of the gamma-rung programme: the $A^2/(2t)$
coefficient made visible here is what survives into the fourth
cumulant and carries $\alpha^2/\lambda^5$ against $\gamma/\lambda^4$.

\section{The thing formalised}

Three declarations in \texttt{Laplace/OneD/JnSecondOrder.lean},
sorry-free with zero warnings, plus a one-word de-privatisation of the
seabed's $J_n$-integrand integrability. The exact
decomposition\footnote{\texttt{quadratised\_integral\_decomposition}.}:
for $t > 0$, with $A, B$ the cubic and quartic scales and
$m_k = \int u^k e^{-u^2/2}$,
\[
\int u^n e^{-u^2/2}\Bigl(1 - s_t + \tfrac{s_t^2}{2}\Bigr)
= m_n - \frac{A}{\sqrt t} m_{n+3} - \frac{B}{t} m_{n+4}
+ \frac{A^2}{2t} m_{n+6}
+ \frac{AB}{t\sqrt t} m_{n+7} + \frac{B^2}{2t^2} m_{n+8},
\]
and the stage headline\footnote{\texttt{J\_n\_asymptotic\_order2}.}:
\[
J_n(t) = m_n - \frac{A}{\sqrt t} m_{n+3} - \frac{B}{t} m_{n+4}
+ \frac{A^2}{2t} m_{n+6} + O\Bigl(\frac{1}{t\sqrt t}\Bigr)
\qquad (t \geq 1),
\]
with the $AB$ and $B^2$ cross-terms absorbed into the explicit error
constant. The numerical check (executed, archived) confirms the error
scale with room at $n = 2$.

\section{Proof strategy}

The pointwise six-term identity is where the new difficulty lived: the
square $s_t^2$ mixes $1/\sqrt t$ and $1/t$ denominators, and
\texttt{ring} does not know $(\sqrt t)^2 = t$. The resolution is the
$\sqrt t$-\emph{atom substitution}: after \texttt{set st := Real.sqrt t}
folds every $\sqrt t$ in the goal into the opaque local \texttt{st},
the remaining bare $t$'s are rewritten by $t = \texttt{st} \cdot
\texttt{st}$ --- safe precisely because the substitution target no
longer contains $\sqrt t$ syntactically --- and the identity becomes
rational in \texttt{st}, closed by \texttt{field\_simp} and
\texttt{ring}. The integral split is a six-fold nested
\texttt{integral\_add} with type-ascribed cumulative witnesses per the
repo's \texttt{Pi.add} discipline; the headline then splits $J_n$ into
the quadratised part plus the stage-2a remainder and routes the two
cross-terms into the constant via $t^2 \geq t\sqrt t$.

\section{Roadblocks and resolutions}

Four build iterations. The one instructive failure: an
\texttt{Integrable.congr} whose pointwise goal contained an unapplied
\texttt{Pi.sub} --- the fourth member of the repo's \texttt{Pi}-form
family (\texttt{add}, \texttt{div}, derivative \texttt{sub}/\texttt{sum},
and now integrand \texttt{sub}), with the same one-line repair. Two
further iterations removed tactics that \texttt{gcongr} had already
made redundant.

\section{What was Mathlib, what was new}

Mathlib: integral linearity, \texttt{gcongr}, the triangle inequality.
Seabed: the linearised decomposition supplied the assembly pattern; the
Gaussian-moment integrability family and stage 2a supplied every
analytic input. New: the $\sqrt t$-atom substitution, promoted to the
repo \texttt{CLAUDE.md} as the standard way to prove rescaled-time
polynomial identities.

\section{Lessons}

When an algebraic identity mixes $t$ and $\sqrt t$, fold the radical
into an atom \emph{first} and rewrite $t$ afterwards; the substitution
is only unsafe while the radical is still spelled with $t$ inside.
First-attempt success on both the identity and the six-fold split shows
the catalogue disciplines have compounding value: this tide's genuinely
new content compiled clean, and every failure was a known class.
