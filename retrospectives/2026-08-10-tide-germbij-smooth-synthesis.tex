\begin{abstract}
\noindent Five stages (PRs \#60--\#63) carried the germbij note's
Theorem~3.1 from its polynomial algebraic core to the smooth setting:
two admissible $C^{R+2}$ losses whose normalized moments at
$x^{2+r}$ differ by $o(q^{2+2r})$ for every $r \le R$ have the same
Taylor data through degree $R+2$
(\texttt{smooth\_jet\_recovery}), with nondegeneracy $\lambda > 0$
derived from the admissibility envelope rather than assumed. The
programme's central design constraint --- Taylor replacement produces
order-controlled errors, never exact data --- shaped every interface:
recovery theorems with pure \texttt{Tendsto} hypotheses, an
epsilon-radius jet form, and a comparison theorem whose split radius
$\sqrt{q}$ keeps \texttt{rpow} out of the entire development.
\end{abstract}

\section{What was proven}

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{StableRecovery} (PR \#60): the recovery interface with
all data hypotheses as vanishing limits at the coefficient-sensitive
scales --- eleven lines of composition, because the weighted-jet
programme's internal theorems were already limit-based.
\item \textbf{TaylorPackage} (PR \#61): the stabilizer envelope
(coefficient-wise $d$, case split at $|x| = \rho$, no suprema), exact
Gaussian scaling in $q$-space, and the envelope tail bound with the
superpolynomial prefactor.
\item \textbf{TaylorCompare} (PR \#62): the admissibility package
(partition lower bound $2\delta e^{-\kappa\delta^2} q$, moment bound
$C_s q^{s+1}$) and the programme's hardest step --- the local Taylor
comparison, unnormalized at $o(q^{s+D-1})$ and normalized at
$o(q^{s+D-2})$, by the $\sqrt{q}$ split, the exponential secant
bound with the Taylor power kept inside the Gaussian, and a
\texttt{Metric.tendsto\_nhds} epsilon-of-room assembly whose
quantifier order matches the theorem's.
\item \textbf{SmoothRecovery} (PR \#63): the Taylor adapter ---
the Peano remainder from Mathlib, the derived minimum facts, the
Taylor polynomial in jet shape, and the stabilized jet's joint
certificate of admissibility and profile --- plus the headline
composition.
\end{itemize}

\section{Design decisions that carried the programme}

Three consult-driven choices did disproportionate work. The
\emph{uniformly positive profile} envelope (from the weighted-jet
programme) survived unchanged into the smooth setting: the stabilized
Taylor polynomial inherits it by construction, with the stabilizer
placed above the Taylor degree so its scaled contribution is
invisible at every recovered rung. The \emph{$\sqrt{q}$ split} made
the outer region's superpolynomial factor $e^{-c/q}$ elementary and
banished \texttt{Real.rpow} from five files of asymptotics. And the
\emph{limit-based interfaces} maintained at every layer meant the
``asymptotically stable'' variants the smooth setting requires were
compositions, not reproofs.

\section{The composition-time discovery}

The one structural surprise arrived exactly where the original
scoping consult pointed (``full-jet quantifier management''): the
recovery induction quantifies over every rung of its coefficient
vector, but the stabilized jet is deliberately longer than the data
reaches. The honest statement is the induction truncated at rung $R$
(\texttt{jet\_recovery\_stable\_partial}); the full-length theorem is
simply inapplicable, since the data genuinely says nothing about the
stabilizer coefficient. Interface mismatches of this kind seem to be
the characteristic failure mode of composition stages, worth
budgeting for in future programme scoping.

\section{Process}

Five tides, each with the consult-or-prior-deliberation record,
executed numerical checks (with one caught fabrication near-miss,
documented in the C2 log, and the rule sharpened: the check runs in
one command, the log text quotes its output in a later one), and the
post-incident build gate (import line plus fresh \texttt{.olean})
verified at every checkpoint. The composition plan for the final
stage was written into the tide log \emph{before} the Lean, which let
the assembly proceed mechanically across several hundred lines.

\section{What remains}

The germbij nondegenerate arc is now formalised end to end. The
note's open commissions: the multivariate Theorem~3.1 (the
Susceptibility Primer's missing Stage~3 --- Wick machinery on
$\mathbb{R}^d$, a foundations programme), and the full-jet quantifier
packaging (C6), which would upgrade the finite-order theorem to
equality of whole Taylor series. Both need fresh scoping consults.
