\begin{abstract}
A soundness review of \texttt{Laplace/OneD/JFunctionMonomial.lean}: the generic-$k$ J-function asymptotic
$t^{1/(2k)}J_k(t)\to\pi(0)\cdot\tfrac1k\cdot((2k)!)^{1/(2k)}\,\Gamma(1/(2k))$ that folds the quartic and sextic
special cases into one parametric theorem. Result: \textbf{a clean fidelity pass with five cleanups} --- a
\texttt{show}$\to$\texttt{change}, two redundant imports, and two docstring inaccuracies (a hypothesis the
header over-stated, and a lemma-usage miscount).
\end{abstract}

\section{Setting}
This is the abstraction the \reviewref[sextic J-function]{2026-06-05-review-laplace-oned-jfunction-sextic}
specialises (and the quartic one should). Its design goal was the \emph{minimal} hypothesis class --- the prior
need only be \texttt{AEMeasurable}, \texttt{ContinuousAt 0}, and globally bounded --- and the review's most
interesting finding was that the module docstring did not reflect that achievement.

\section{What was reviewed}
Seven declarations: the universal inequality $x^2\le1+x^{2k}$, monomial-Gibbs integrability, the substitution
lemma, the dominator integrability, the centred DCT theorem, the closed-form partition function, and the
asymptotic corollary.

\section{Fidelity / soundness findings}
\textbf{Sound; no mismatch.} The partition closed form $(1/k)((2k)!/t)^{1/(2k)}\Gamma(1/(2k))$ is correct (the
$2\cdot(1/p_R)=2/(2k)=1/k$ step), the substitution ($a^{2k}=t$, $|a^{-1}|=1/a$ cancelling the outer $a$) is
right, and the limit constant matches. The load-bearing check was the DCT \emph{measurability} step: with only
\texttt{AEMeasurable} on the prior, the file gets \texttt{AEStronglyMeasurable} of $u\mapsto\pi(u/t^\alpha)$ by
composing with the quasi-measure-preserving scaling $u\mapsto(t^\alpha)^{-1}u$ --- so \emph{no} global
continuity is needed, and only \texttt{ContinuousAt 0} is used later for the pointwise limit. GPT confirmed this
works as claimed; it is exactly why the sextic specialisation could inherit the weak hypotheses.

\section{Simplifications applied}
\textbf{Five.}
\begin{itemize}[noitemsep]
  \item A \texttt{show}$\to$\texttt{change} on the reshaping goal in the measurability branch (cleared a
    standing \texttt{linter.style.show} warning --- the same one flagged from the sextic review).
  \item Two redundant imports: \texttt{Laplace.Gibbs} (the file references no \texttt{partitionFunction}/%
    \texttt{gibbsExpectation}/\texttt{gibbsCov} --- \texttt{kth\_partition} is stated as a raw integral) and
    \texttt{Gamma.Basic} (\texttt{Real.Gamma} arrives transitively via \texttt{Integral.Gamma}).
  \item Two docstring accuracy fixes. The module header advertised a ``continuous, globally bounded prior'',
    but the theorems take the weaker \texttt{AEMeasurable} $+$ \texttt{ContinuousAt 0} $+$ bound --- the
    \emph{whole point} of the refactor that produced this file. (The per-theorem docstring was already precise;
    only the module header lagged.) And the strategy prose claimed the master Gamma-integral lemma was ``used
    twice'', when in fact it is used once (the partition) and integrability is a separate
    \texttt{integrable\_exp\_neg\_mul\_sq} argument.
\end{itemize}
Foundation file (two consumers); arbitrated by a \textbf{full project build}, green at $8306$ jobs with both
consumers rebuilt, three public signatures byte-identical, file now warning-free; GPT affirmed all five.

\section{Disagreements and open questions}
None.

\section{Lessons}
\begin{itemize}[noitemsep]
  \item \textbf{A module header can lag the very refactor that file embodies.} This file exists to provide the
    minimal-hypothesis J-function, yet its top docstring still described the old ``continuous'' hypothesis ---
    while the per-theorem docstrings, written later, were accurate. When a file's purpose is a hypothesis
    weakening, the summary at the top is a prime spot for stale over-statement; check it against the actual
    signatures.
  \item \textbf{``Used twice'' is a checkable claim.} Prose that counts how often a lemma is invoked, or names
    which lemma does the work, is verifiable by reading the proof --- and here it was wrong (the master rpow
    lemma once, Gaussian comparison separately). Strategy prose that names mechanisms should be audited like a
    theorem statement, not skimmed.
\end{itemize}

\section{Follow-ups}
\begin{itemize}[noitemsep]
  \item \textbf{Forward tide (carried from the sextic review):} migrate \texttt{JFunctionQuartic} to specialise
    this generic file, weakening its hypothesis from global \texttt{Continuous} to
    \texttt{AEMeasurable}~$+$~\texttt{ContinuousAt 0}.
  \item \textbf{Next review:} \texttt{JFunctionAsymptoticEquivalence.lean} (the remaining J-function file, and
    this file's other consumer).
\end{itemize}
