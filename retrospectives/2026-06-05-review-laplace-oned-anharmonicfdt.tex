\begin{abstract}
A soundness review of \texttt{Laplace/OneD/AnharmonicFDT.lean}: the two conditional
fluctuation--dissipation / three-point identities for the anharmonic Gibbs measure ---
$\partial_h\langle x\rangle_h|_0=-t\,\mathrm{Var}_0(x)$ and
$\partial_h\mathrm{Cov}_h(x,x)|_0=-t\,\kappa_3(x,x,x)$. Result: \textbf{a clean fidelity pass with
five edits} --- two anticipatory dead imports and three docstring over-claims. The compositions are
correct; the docstring had simply been written ahead of the code.
\end{abstract}

\section{Setting}
The anharmonic analogue of the
\reviewref[harmonic cross-susceptibility capstone]{2026-06-05-review-laplace-oned-harmoniccrosssuscderiv}:
the same Threepoint abstract identities (\texttt{gibbsExp\_deriv\_eq\_neg\_t\_cov},
\texttt{gibbsCov\_deriv\_eq\_neg\_t\_kappa3}), specialised at $A=B=\varphi=\mathrm{id}$ to the
anharmonic potential and made unconditional in the observability side conditions by the anharmonic
monomial witnesses. Where the harmonic $\kappa_3$ vanished by parity, here the cubic term keeps it.

\section{What was reviewed}
Two theorems: the first-cumulant FDT and the cross-susceptibility three-point identity.

\section{Fidelity / soundness findings}
\textbf{The compositions are sound.} The FDT specialises \texttt{gibbsExp\_deriv\_eq\_neg\_t\_cov}
with the regularity witness and observable witnesses for $\varphi=x$ and $\varphi\cdot A=x^2$; the
three-point identity fills the six \texttt{GibbsObservable} slots of
\texttt{gibbsCov\_deriv\_eq\_neg\_t\_kappa3} as \texttt{hx hx hxx hxx hxx hxxx} (two $x$, three
$x^2$, one $x^3$, all coinciding under $A=B=\varphi=\mathrm{id}$). The reviewer confirmed the
multiplicities; no witness mismatch.

\textbf{Three docstring over-claims, fixed.} (1) The \texttt{\#\# Headlines} section advertised
four headlines, but only two exist as theorems --- the two \texttt{*\_asymptotic} capstones are an
unwritten TODO. Split into ``present in this file'' and ``Planned (TODO)''. (2) ``the two headline
identities are therefore \textbf{unconditional}'' was too strong: they discharge the
\texttt{GibbsObservable} side conditions but still require $\lambda,\gamma>0$, $\alpha^2<3\lambda
\gamma$, $t>0$. Qualified. (3) ``the cubic term breaks the parity argument'' over-claims, since the
theorem does not assume $\alpha\ne0$ --- at $\alpha=0$ the potential is even and $\kappa_3$ may
still vanish. Softened to ``no parity simplification is built in''.

\section{Simplifications applied}
\textbf{Two dead imports.} \texttt{Laplace.OneD.AnharmonicKappa3} and
\texttt{Laplace.OneD.IntegralRemainder} are referenced by no symbol --- only named in the TODO
prose for the unwritten asymptotic capstones. A full \texttt{lake build} ($8306$ jobs) stayed green
with both removed, confirming they are not transitively load-bearing. \texttt{open MeasureTheory}
(bare \texttt{Measure $\mathbb R$}/\texttt{volume}) and the three used imports stay. Signatures
byte-identical, no warnings. GPT affirmed.

\section{Disagreements and open questions}
None. The reviewer's trailing-\texttt{exact} removal was declined: the proofs are tactic blocks
with preceding \texttt{have}s, so the closing \texttt{exact <application>} is not cleanly
convertible to a term --- proof-golf, out of scope.

\section{Lessons}
\begin{itemize}[noitemsep]
  \item \textbf{A ``Headlines'' list can run ahead of the declarations.} The module doc advertised
    two \texttt{*\_asymptotic} theorems that are still a TODO --- a reader scanning the headlines
    would expect them to exist. When a file documents planned work inline, keep the headline list to
    what actually type-checks, and quarantine the rest under a ``Planned'' heading.
  \item \textbf{Anticipatory imports are dead imports until the anticipated code lands.} The two
    imports were placed for capstones not yet written; until then they are removable dead code (the
    future tide re-imports as it needs). The build, not the intent, decides --- and here the build
    confirmed them inert.
  \item \textbf{``Unconditional'' needs a scope.} The identities are unconditional \emph{in the
    observability side conditions}, not in the model parameters; an unqualified ``unconditional''
    misleads about $\lambda,\gamma,t$ and the discriminant.
\end{itemize}
