\begin{abstract}
\noindent The shape consult's genuinely load-bearing J5 checkpoint:
for two losses carrying the higher-order domain package with matched
derivative tensors below order $k$,
\[
\frac{I_1(P,q) - I_2(P,q)}{q^{k-2}}
\;\longrightarrow\;
-\int P\, Q\, K_H,
\]
where the $I_j$ are the posterior integrands of the dominated
convergence stage and $Q$ the degree-$k$ diagonal difference. This is
the integrated form of the germbij higher-order recovery input:
once it exists, normalization (J5e) is quotient algebra, and the
recovery stages J6--J7 are compositions with the rigidity and
polarization engines.
\end{abstract}

\section{How the proof works}

Each posterior integrand splits, pointwise by membership cases, into
its slice over the common ball (radius the minimum of the two Taylor
radii) plus a retreating domain tail. The ball-slice difference is
exactly $q^{k-2}$ times the local rate-DCT integrand of the previous
tide, so its integral converges after division; each tail vanishes
at the rate by squeeze against the retreating-tail lemma, with the
coercive Gaussian bound valid on the whole domain. The per-$q$
bookkeeping (splitting integrals, moving the rate through) is
guarded by slice-integrability lemmas, and the final algebra folds
the six integral values into scalar atoms before touching them.

\section{Where it stalled and how it moved}

Two additions to the error catalogue. First, \texttt{field\_simp} on
an equation between integrals rewrites \emph{under the integral
binders}, silently reassociating the integrand and splitting what
\texttt{linarith} needs to see as a single atom; the robust pattern
is to \texttt{set} each integral value as a scalar first --- the
inverse of the usual set-folding hazard, safe precisely because
scalars carry no binder. Second, a squeeze against a previously
proven \texttt{Tendsto} must reuse its raw statement: massaging it
with \texttt{simpa using h.norm} renormalizes signs inside binders
and the syntactic match dies. The rest was catalogued classes
(beta-unreduced congruence bodies, anonymous-constructor membership
in difference sets).

\section{What this opens}

J5e (normalization: two applications of this theorem, at $P$ and at
$\mathbf{1}$, against the H4/H5 ordinary limits and eventual
denominator positivity), then J6 (single-degree recovery: uniqueness
of limits kills the covariance, rigidity kills $Q$, polarization
upgrades to the tensor) and J7 (the induction). The end of the
tensor programme's analytic work is in sight.
