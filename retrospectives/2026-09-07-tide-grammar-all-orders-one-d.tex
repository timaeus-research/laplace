\begin{abstract}
For a chart amplitude $\eta(u)e^{\beta s\xi(u)}$ with $\xi,\eta$ admitting order-$R$ Taylor
expansions at $u=0$ on $[0,b]$ and $\xi$ Lipschitz at $0$, the scaled integral
$\int_0^bu^he^{-\beta(Nu^k)^2}\eta(u)e^{\beta(Nu^k)\xi(u)}\,du$ equals
$\sum_{j<R}c_jN^{-(p+j/k)}+O(N^{-(p+R/k)})$ with $p=(h+1)/k$ and
\begin{gather*}
c_j=\frac1k\sum_{m<R}\frac{\beta^m}{m!}\,e_{j,m}\,S_{p+j/k+m}(x_0),\qquad
e_{j,m}=\sum_{\ell\le j}y_\ell\,[u^{j-\ell}](g_R^m),\\
S_\lambda(a)=\int_0^\infty s^{\lambda-1}e^{-\beta s^2+\beta as}\,ds.
\end{gather*}
This assembles the abstract kernel-jet transfer (unit~92) with the amplitude jet remainder
(unit~95): it is the all-order form of the one-dimensional case of \texttt{thm:TaylorTree}, with
every coefficient an explicit finite combination of the paper's $S_\lambda$ functions. Zero
\texttt{sorry}/\texttt{axiom}.
\end{abstract}

\section{Mathematics}

\texttt{oneDKernel\_jet\_expansion} (unit~92) turns a finite $u$-jet of an amplitude $F(u,s)$ with
remainder $u^RH_R(s)$ into an expansion in $N^{-(p+j/k)}$, given moment integrability of
$s^{p+j/k-1}e^{-\beta s^2}|F_j(s)|$ and of the remainder envelope. Unit~95 supplies, for
$F=\eta e^{\beta s\xi}$, the jet $F_j=e^{\beta sx_0}P_j$ and
$H_R(s)=C(1+s)^Re^{\beta s(x_0+L_1b)}$. Two points remain and are settled here.

\emph{Moment integrability.} The envelope $A(1+s)^Re^{\beta sa'}$ is expanded by the binomial
theorem into $\sum_mA\binom Rm s^me^{\beta sa'}$, and each term times $s^\gamma e^{-\beta s^2}$ is a
shifted weighted kernel $s^{\gamma+m}\,\mathrm{quadKernel}(\beta,a',s)$, integrable for $\gamma>-1$
by \texttt{logPowShift\_weightedKernel\_integrableOn}. The $(1+|\log s|)^j$ factor is carried along
for later use with log-weighted terms although the present application has $j=0$.

\emph{Coefficients.} $P_j(s)=\sum_\ell y_\ell\sum_m\frac{(\beta s)^m}{m!}[u^{j-\ell}](g_R^m)$ is a
polynomial in $s$, so $c_j=k^{-1}\int s^{p+j/k-1}e^{-\beta s^2}e^{\beta sx_0}P_j(s)\,ds$ integrates
termwise (each term is a weighted kernel with exponent $p+j/k+m-1>-1$) into the finite sum of
$S_\lambda(x_0)$ values displayed in the abstract. In the paper's notation
$S_\lambda(a)=\mathtt{weightedMass}\,\beta\,a\,(\lambda-1)$.

\emph{Domain of the envelope.} The abstract transfer requires $H_R\ge0$ on all of $\mathbb R$; the
envelope is therefore defined with $(1+|s|)^R$ and reduced to $(1+s)^R$ on $s>0$ where it is used.

\section{Paper-facing meaning}

This is the one-dimensional case of the all-order statement of \texttt{thm:TaylorTree} with an
explicit remainder: the exponents $p+j/k$ are the poles of the tree, and the coefficients are finite
combinations of $S_\lambda(x_0)$ with universal polynomial weights in $\beta$ and the Taylor data
$(x_i,y_i)$. The inverse-$N$ series obtained by letting $R\to\infty$ is not claimed to converge; the
theorem is a finite-$R$ statement with an explicit $O(N^{-(p+R/k)})$ remainder, which is exactly
the level of rigour of the paper's Lemma on the fluctuation expansion.

Follow-ups in the same line: (i) derive the Taylor-remainder hypotheses from $C^R$ regularity via
\texttt{taylor\_mean\_remainder\_bound} (a $C^\infty$ corollary with hypotheses only on $\xi,\eta$);
(ii) the $d=2$ block with noncritical face parameters (Astra~\#3 target~3); (iii) the weighted
finite-part axis lemma (target~4).

\section{Lean notes}

\begin{itemize}
\item \texttt{rw [add\_pow]} chose $(1+|\log s|)^j$ before $(s+1)^R$; pin the instance,
\texttt{add\_pow s 1 R}.
\item \texttt{rw [← mul\_assoc]} reassociated the wrong product in
$s^\gamma\cdot(E\cdot(X\cdot\Sigma))$; pass the first factor,
\texttt{← mul\_assoc (Real.exp (-β * s \^{} 2))}.
\item \texttt{positivity} cannot prove $0\le(1+s)^R$ for unconstrained real $s$; define the
envelope with $|s|$ when a global nonnegativity hypothesis is demanded.
\item \texttt{Measurable.mul\_const} takes the constant explicitly; \texttt{fun\_prop} proves
measurability of these exponential-polynomial expressions directly.
\item \texttt{IntegrableOn.congr\_fun} pointwise goals are beta-redexes: \texttt{beta\_reduce}
before \texttt{rw}.
\item \texttt{MeasureTheory.integral\_finset\_sum} is deprecated for \texttt{integral\_finsetSum}.
\end{itemize}
