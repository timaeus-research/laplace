\begin{abstract}
Astra~\#13 unit u157. If $(U_n,V_n)\Rightarrow(U_0,V_0)$ in $\mathbb R^2$ and $V_0>0$ almost surely,
then $U_n/V_n\Rightarrow U_0/V_0$ (\texttt{tendstoInDistribution\_div\_of\_pos}), with Lean's
totalised division and no finite-$n$ nonvanishing hypothesis. Division is not continuous at $v=0$;
we divide instead by $\max(v,c)$, which is continuous and agrees with division on $\{v\ge c\}$. By
portmanteau on the closed half-line $\{v\le c\}$ the disagreement probability is eventually at most
$\mu'\{V_0\le c\}$, which is small for small $c$; the approximation lemma of unit~156 finishes.
Zero \texttt{sorry}/\texttt{axiom}.
\end{abstract}

\section{Mathematics}
Given $\eta>0$ choose $c=1/(m+1)$ with $\mu'\{V_0\le c\}<\eta/2$ (the sets $\{V_0\le1/(m+1)\}$
decrease to $\{V_0\le0\}$, a null set). The marginal $V_n\Rightarrow V_0$ follows from the joint
convergence by projection, and portmanteau on the closed set $(-\infty,c]$ gives
$\limsup_n\mu\{V_n\le c\}\le\mu'\{V_0\le c\}<\eta$, hence eventually $\mu\{V_n\le c\}\le\eta$. On
$\{V_n>c\}$ the clipped quotient equals the true quotient, so the two disagree with probability at
most $\eta$; the same holds for the limits. Since the clipped quotients converge by the continuous
mapping theorem, unit~156's approximation lemma yields convergence of the quotients.

\section{Paper-facing meaning}
This is the division step of cor:empirical\_expectation at chart level: the posterior expectation
$Z_n[\varphi]/Z_n$ is the quotient of two jointly convergent normalised chart integrals whose limiting
denominator is a.s.\ positive (unit~154). Astra~\#13 recommended exactly this route (clipped division
plus portmanteau on the denominator marginal) over a general a.e.-continuous mapping theorem or a
ball-localisation of the input, and noted that Lean's totalised division removes any need for
finite-sample nonvanishing of the chart normaliser.
