\begin{abstract}
Unit 212 completes programme~A2 (Astra~\#22). For random inputs $X_n:\Omega\to C([0,1]^d)^2$
(phase, amplitude) converging in distribution to $Z$ along a countably generated filter, and
deterministic scales $N_n\to\infty$, the normalised chart integrals converge in distribution to the
limit functional of the limiting input:
\[
F_{N_n}(X_n)\;\Rightarrow\;F(Z),\qquad
F_N=\frac{\int\eta\,u^he^{-\beta N^2u^{2k}+\beta Nu^k\xi}}{N^{-p}(\log N)^{m-1}},\quad F=\mathrm{phaseCoeff}
\]
(\texttt{tendstoInDistribution\_normChart}). Proof: $F$ is continuous (Lipschitz on balls), so
$F(X_n)\Rightarrow F(Z)$ by continuous mapping; and $F_{N_n}(X_n)-F(X_n)\to0$ in probability, because
the limit law is tight in the Polish space $C([0,1]^d)^2$ (Mathlib's
\texttt{isTightMeasureSet\_singleton}), portmanteau puts most of $X_n$ in the open $\delta$-thickening
of a compact $K$ carrying most of $Z$, and on that thickening the uniform convergence on $K$
(unit~211) extends by the Lipschitz bounds (units 209--210). Mathlib's Slutsky lemma
\texttt{TendstoInDistribution.add\_of\_tendstoInMeasure\_const} finishes. Zero
\texttt{sorry}/\texttt{axiom}; 9089 jobs.
\end{abstract}

\section{Mathematics}
The key point, anticipated in Astra~\#21/\#22, is that no tightness of the \emph{sequence} $X_n$ is
needed: only the limit law is tight (automatic on a Polish space), and the passage from the compact
$K$ to its open $\delta$-thickening $U$ uses (i) portmanteau for the closed set $U^c$
($\limsup\mu(X_n\in U^c)\le\mu'(Z\in U^c)\le\mu'(Z\notin K)$), and (ii) the equi-Lipschitz bounds on
the ball of radius $R+1\supseteq U$: for $x\in U$ with $d(x,y)<\delta$, $y\in K$,
$|F_N(x)-F(x)|\le L\delta+\sup_K|F_N-F|+L'\delta<\varepsilon$ once $(L+L')\delta\le\varepsilon/3$ and
$N$ is large. The random inputs enter only through their sup-norm topology; the deterministic
Headline~XIII does the analysis.
