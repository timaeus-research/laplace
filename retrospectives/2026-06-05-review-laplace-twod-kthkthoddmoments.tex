\begin{abstract}
A soundness review of \texttt{Laplace/TwoD/KthKthOddMoments.lean}: the two parity-completion
theorems stating that a mixed 2D moment $\langle x^a y^b\rangle_{2D}$ on the separable monomial
potential vanishes whenever at least one of $a,b$ is odd. Result: \textbf{a clean fidelity pass with
no edits} --- the file carries no dead \texttt{open}, no dead import, and no no-op tactic. The
reliably-safe simplification bucket was empty, and that is the result.
\end{abstract}

\section{Setting}
This is the odd-moment companion to the kthkth lift trio
(\reviewref[partition]{2026-06-05-review-laplace-twod-kthkthpartition},
\reviewref[numerator]{2026-06-05-review-laplace-twod-kthkthnumerator},
\reviewref[moment]{2026-06-05-review-laplace-twod-kthkthmoment}): where the trio computes the
\emph{even}-moment asymptotics, this file records that the \emph{odd} ones vanish identically. It
sits on the same factorisation root
(\reviewref[AddSeparable]{2026-06-05-review-laplace-twod-addseparable}) and 1D base
(\reviewref[MonomialPotential]{2026-06-05-review-laplace-oned-monomialpotential}).

\section{What was reviewed}
Two theorems: \texttt{gibbsExpectation\_kthKth\_odd\_pow\_pow\_eq\_zero}
($\langle x^{2j_1+1}y^n\rangle_{2D}=0$ for any $n$) and
\texttt{gibbsExpectation\_kthKth\_pow\_odd\_pow\_eq\_zero}
($\langle x^m y^{2j_2+1}\rangle_{2D}=0$ for any $m$). Each factorises the 2D expectation into a
product of two 1D ones and kills the odd factor via
\texttt{gibbsExpectation\_kthPotential\_odd}; the product is zero by \texttt{zero\_mul}/%
\texttt{mul\_zero}.

\section{Fidelity / soundness findings}
\textbf{Sound; no mismatch.} The load-bearing check is that the \emph{non-odd} factor needs only
\emph{integrability} --- supplied by \texttt{kth\_integrable\_pow\_pot} for \emph{every} natural
exponent, not just even ones --- so each theorem genuinely holds for arbitrary $n$ (resp.\ $m$),
and the two together cover every ``at least one odd'' case, including odd--odd (apply whichever
side's exponent is odd; if both, either). The per-theorem docstrings' ``regardless of the parity of
$n$/$m$, including odd--odd'' is exactly justified.

One report-only prose nit: the module-level docstring states the vanishing without restating the
$t>0$ hypothesis that the formal theorems carry (and the proofs use, via partition positivity). The
$\langle\cdot\rangle_{2D,t}$ notation carries the $t$, and the result \emph{is} true for all $t>0$,
so this is terse rather than wrong --- left as is, consistent with the line drawn on the
\reviewref[AddSeparable]{2026-06-05-review-laplace-twod-addseparable} docstring-completeness notes.

\section{Simplifications applied}
\textbf{None --- the bucket was empty.} Unlike its three trio siblings, this file opens \emph{only}
\texttt{MeasureTheory} (load-bearing for bare \texttt{Integrable}); there is no dead \texttt{open
Real} to trim, both imports are used, the (correctly absent) Asymptotics import is not present to
remove, and the final \texttt{simp} does real work (\texttt{zero\_mul}/\texttt{mul\_zero}). The
reviewer's only suggestions were proof-golf (\texttt{simpa}-shorten the $n=0$ integrability blocks;
compress the closing \texttt{rw}\,;\,\texttt{simp}), declined as out of the signature-preserving
dead-code safe class. No worktree was created.

\section{Lessons}
\begin{itemize}[noitemsep]
  \item \textbf{An empty reliably-safe bucket is a valid --- and common --- outcome.} A file written
    without the accreted dead \texttt{open Real} of its siblings yields a clean fidelity pass and
    nothing to land; manufacturing a mutation to ``show work'' would violate the review's safety
    contract. The result is the clean pass plus the report-only prose nit.
  \item \textbf{The sibling-prediction cuts both ways.} The trio legs all carried a dead
    \texttt{open Real}; this companion did not. A copy-paste family tends to share dead code, but
    membership is not destiny --- the per-file check (here: the open line literally reads
    \texttt{open MeasureTheory}) still governs.
\end{itemize}
