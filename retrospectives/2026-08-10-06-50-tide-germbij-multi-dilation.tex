\section{Setting}

With the one-dimensional Theorem~3.1 complete at every order, the
note's remaining commission is the multivariate statement --- also
the Susceptibility Primer's missing Stage~3. The scoping consult
(archived in the tide log, with the note attached) made four rulings
that shape the whole programme: recover the Hessian first, before any
jet package, since $H$-recovery alone forces every new foundation;
represent the ambient space as
$\mathrm{EuclideanSpace}\ \mathbb{R}\ (\mathrm{Fin}\ d)$; build
\emph{one} project-local dilation wrapper so downstream proofs never
touch Mathlib's change-of-variables surface; and --- the structural
prize --- handle the eventual degree-$k$ tensor injectivity by
covariance-Gram positive-definiteness from the Gaussian's full
support, the same open-support argument as one dimension, avoiding
Wick/Isserlis entirely.

\section{The thing formalised}

Three declarations in
\texttt{Laplace/Multi/Dilation.lean}\footnote{\texttt{EuclidD},
\texttt{integral\_dilation}, \texttt{setIntegral\_dilation}.}: the
ambient abbreviation, the dilation wrapper
$\int f = q^d \int f(q \cdot x)$ for $q > 0$, and its indicator form
for the expanding domains of the later dominated-convergence stage.
The consult's named trap --- the pushforward carries $q^{-d}$ while
the substitution carries $q^d$ --- is fixed here once, and the
orientation was checked numerically first (the $d = 2$
Gaussian-with-cubic integral and a box indicator, both matching to
the printed precision).
