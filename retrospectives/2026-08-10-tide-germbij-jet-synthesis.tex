\begin{abstract}
\noindent Four tides carried the germbij note's \S7.4(a) inductive
recovery from scoping to a sorry-free comparison theorem: two finite
jets $a\,x^{2k} + \sum_{r=1}^R c_r x^{2k+r}$ with uniformly positive
profiles whose Gibbs moments at the matching observables
$x^{2k+r}$ eventually agree have equal coefficients
(\texttt{polynomialJet\_recovery}, PRs \#53--\#56). The programme was
scoped against three alternatives, rerouted once mid-course --- the
pairwise-difference argument replacing the planned multi-index
expansion machinery at roughly half the formal cost --- and survived
one process failure worth remembering: a merged PR whose build gate
had been silently vacuous. This synthesis records the mathematics,
the two consults that shaped it, and the incident.
\end{abstract}

\section{What was proven}

The headline chain, all in \texttt{Laplace/OneD/}:

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{MonomialVariance} (PR \#53): Gamma closed forms and
strict positivity of $\mathrm{Var}(x^n)$ against the reference weight
$e^{-t\,x^{2k}/(2k)!}$ --- the injectivity input, formalising the
note's own ``$\mathrm{Var}[Q] = 0$ forces $Q$ constant'' argument via
the open nonempty support of the centered integrand.
\item \textbf{JetScaling} (PR \#54): the jet potential factors
through its profile, $L_q(u) = u^{2k} P(qu)$, so the correct
integrability envelope is a uniformly positive profile
($\rho \le P$ on all of $\mathbb{R}$), giving
$\rho u^{2k} \le L_q(u)$ for \emph{every} real $q$; the exact scaling
identity $\langle x^s \rangle_t = q^s \cdot$ (normalized jet moment)
under $t q^{2k} = 1$; and the $R = 2$ discriminant certificate
$c_1^2 < 4 a c_2$, admitting unrestricted odd $c_1$.
\item \textbf{JetDifference} (PR \#55, repaired in \#56): for jets
agreeing below rung $r$, the difference factors as
$L^1_q - L^2_q = q^r g(q)$ with
$g(0) = \delta_r u^{2k+r}$; the exponential secant bound and the
$\min(\rho_1,\rho_2)$ shared envelope give a $q$-free majorant, and
dominated convergence along $q \to 0^+$ yields
$q^{-r}(J^1_s - J^2_s) \to -\delta_r \int u^{s+2k+r} e^{-a u^{2k}}$.
\item \textbf{JetRecovery} (PR \#56): the quotient-difference lemma
turns this into the normalized covariance limit; at $s = 2k+r$ the
covariance is the stage-1 variance, forcing $\delta_r = 0$; strong
induction and the scaling-identity transfer give
\texttt{polynomialJet\_recovery}.
\end{itemize}

With the pure-monomial recovery of $(k, a)$ from the earlier recovery
thread, every coefficient of a finite-jet potential is pinned by
moment asymptotics, each at its own graded order --- the note's
\S7.4(a), now marked at its covariance-pairing sentence.

\section{The two consults}

Both consults contributed mathematics, not just tactics, and both
survived contact with the numerics executed before formalisation.

The \emph{envelope consult} rejected the drafted
``even-positive-top'' integrability condition with a counterexample:
$P(y) = 1 - 3y + y^2$ has positive even top yet $P(1) = -1$, so the
Gibbs weight keeps a remote peak $e^{q^{-2k}}$ at $u \sim 1/q$ ---
integrable at every $q$, never close to the reference measure. Since
$L_q(u) = u^{2k}P(qu)$ and $qu$ ranges over all of $\mathbb{R}$
regardless of $q$, no smallness of $q$ can substitute for profile
positivity. This reshaped the programme's foundations before a line
of Lean depended on them.

The \emph{route consult} replaced its own earlier plan. The original
stages 3--5 built the full weighted exponential expansion
(multi-index coefficient polynomials, triangular decomposition,
quotient recurrences). The challenge observation --- recovery is a
comparison statement, so each rung needs only the \emph{first-order
difference} of two Gibbs weights --- was endorsed as ``the shortest
route'', with two load-bearing corrections: the secant bound
$|e^{-x} - e^{-y}| \le |x-y|\max(e^{-x}, e^{-y})$ in place of a
Taylor bound whose $e^{|D|}$ factor no envelope controls, and the
observation that $\min(\rho_1, \rho_2)$ envelopes the entire segment
between the two potentials. The eliminated machinery would only be
needed if explicit expansion coefficients were wanted for their own
sake.

\section{The incident}

PR \#55 merged an uncompiled file. Its tide never added the file to
\texttt{Laplace.lean}'s imports, so \texttt{lake build} succeeded
vacuously, the audit script (which greps text) saw nothing, and CI
passed for the same reason. The tide's log and retrospective claimed
a clean build with zero warnings; the claim was false, discovered
only when the next tide imported the file and hit fifteen ordinary
first-build errors. All were repaired in PR \#56; the mathematics was
untouched; the false retrospective now carries an erratum. Two
durable outputs: the repository rule that a build result may be
reported only after verifying the import line (and, for files
compiled in the current worktree, the fresh \texttt{.olean}), and a
transitive import-closure audit which confirmed no other file in the
repository shares the exposure. The general lesson for the tide
loop: gates fail open exactly when a step is forgotten, so the gate's
own preconditions belong in the close-out checklist.

\section{Process observations}

Six tide-sized steps against the consult's original eight-stage plan,
with the reroute cutting the middle three stages to two. The
stacked-worktree chain (\#55's branch carrying \#56's tide) meant the
incident was caught one tide after introduction rather than at some
distant integration point --- linear chains double as an integration
test of the previous link. The numerical checks continued to pay:
the response-slope check validated the covariance structure before
the route consult returned, and the machine-precision check of the
exact scaling identity was effectively free.

\section{What the next programme inherits}

The normalized-series and quotient-difference handling here was named
by the scoping consult as the de-risking prerequisite for the
general-order nondegenerate induction (germbij Theorem~3.1 at all
orders); the multivariate first rung remains the other open
commission, strategically valuable as the Susceptibility Primer's
missing Stage 3. Both need fresh scoping consults. The weighted-jet
machinery itself is reusable: the profile envelope and the pairwise
difference limit are stated for arbitrary finite jets, not just the
recovery use case.
