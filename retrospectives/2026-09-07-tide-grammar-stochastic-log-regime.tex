\begin{abstract}
First unit of Astra~\#19's next programme, and its feasibility gate. For a corner-vanishing
observable ($y_{\varphi,00}=0$) with a random phase $X_n\Rightarrow Z$ in the coefficient-pair space,
the chart posterior ratio decays like $1/\log N$ with a \emph{random} limiting constant:
\[
\log N\cdot\frac{Z_N[\varphi](X_n)}{Z_N[1](X_n)}\;\Rightarrow\;\frac{B^\varphi_p(Z)}{A^1_p(Z)}
\]
(\texttt{tendstoInDistribution\_log\_posterior}). The gate passed with the existing infrastructure:
the numerator's leading log coefficient vanishes identically for every phase, so its B-slot
normalised remainder is exactly $Z_N[\varphi]/N^{-p}$; the B-slot error tends to zero in probability
along the same random input as the denominator's A-slot error, giving joint convergence; the
positive-denominator quotient theorem and the exact identity
$\mathrm{normB}^\varphi/\mathrm{normA}^1=(Z[\varphi]/Z[1])\log N$ finish. Zero
\texttt{sorry}/\texttt{axiom}.
\end{abstract}

\section{Mathematics}
Astra~\#19's gate obligations: (1) cancellation for the whole stochastic phase family ---
$A^\varphi_p=y_{\varphi,00}J_p(x_{00})/(k_1k_2)=0$ for every phase; (2) the correct scale of the
numerator remainder --- with $a_N=N^{-p}\log N$ the B-slot theorem gives
$Z_N[\varphi]-N^{-p}B^\varphi_p=o_{\mathbb P}(N^{-p})=o_{\mathbb P}(a_N/\log N)$ exactly as required;
(3) joint convergence from the common input --- both errors are functions of $X_n$ and the pair of
limiting coefficients is a continuous image of $Z$; (4) almost-sure positivity of $A^1_p(Z)$ from
$y_{1,00}>0$. Everything reduces to units 148--159 (envelope bounds on balls, tightness, the
approximation lemma, the clipped-division quotient theorem).

\section{Paper-facing meaning}
This is the stochastic form of the $1/\log N$ regime recorded in \texttt{rem:empirical\_expectation\_lean}:
for an observable vanishing at the corner, the empirical posterior expectation decays like
$1/\log N$ with a random coefficient $B^\varphi_p(Z)/A^1_p(Z)$ determined by the limiting
coefficient array; when $B^\varphi_p(Z)=0$ the limit is $0$ and no faster rate is claimed.

\section{Lean notes}
The unit is a near-verbatim B-slot mirror of \texttt{FixedAmplitudeJointLimit.lean} and
\texttt{ChartPosteriorLimit.lean}: \texttt{normB\_sub\_le}/\texttt{tendsto\_errB} replace the A-slot
bounds, \texttt{measurable\_normB\_comp} and \texttt{continuous\_coeffB} need the extra hypothesis

identity for $N>1$. It compiled at the first attempt.
