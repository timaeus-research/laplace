\begin{abstract}
An abstract, finite, quantitative form of ``a power-log expansion of the state density transfers to a
power-log asymptotic expansion of the partition function''. For
$Z(N)=\int_0^Re^{-\beta(Nr)^2}\rho(r,Nr)\,dr$ with a two-variable density satisfying
$\rho(r,s)=\sum_ir^{\alpha_i-1}\log^{j_i}(1/r)\,c_i(s)+E(r,s)$, $|E|\le r^{a-1}(1+|\log r|)^JH(s)$,
$\alpha_i<a$, $j_i\le J$, and finite weighted moments,
\begin{align*}
&\Big|Z(N)-\sum_iN^{-\alpha_i}\sum_{m\le j_i}\binom{j_i}{m}(\log N)^m(-1)^{j_i-m}M_{i,j_i-m}\Big|\\
&\qquad\le N^{-a}(1+\log N)^J\Big(M_H+\sum_iR^{\alpha_i-a}T_i\Big)\qquad(N\ge1),
\end{align*}
with $M_{i,\ell}=\int_0^\infty s^{\alpha_i-1}(\log s)^\ell e^{-\beta s^2}c_i(s)\,ds$. This is Astra's
target~7 in the form recommended in the second consult. Zero \texttt{sorry}/\texttt{axiom}.
\end{abstract}

\section{Proof strategy}
Three independent mechanisms, each a lemma. Scaling: $\int_0^RF(Nr)\,dr=N^{-1}\int_0^{NR}F$
(\texttt{intervalIntegral.integral\_comp\_mul\_left}), and integrability transports the same way
(\texttt{IntervalIntegrable.comp\_mul\_left}). Binomial expansion:
$(\log N-\log s)^j=\sum_m\binom jm(\log N)^m(-\log s)^{j-m}$ (\texttt{add\_pow}), so the truncated
term is a combination of truncated log moments. Tail replacement: on $s\ge NR$,
$s^{\alpha-1}\le(NR)^{\alpha-a}s^{a-1}$ and $|\log s|^\ell\le(1+|\log s|)^j$, whence the tail costs
$N^{-a}R^{\alpha-a}T$ times $\sum_m\binom jm(\log N)^m=(1+\log N)^j\le(1+\log N)^J$. The remainder is
dominated pointwise after scaling via $1+|\log s-\log N|\le(1+\log N)(1+|\log s|)$.

\section{Roadblocks and resolutions}
\begin{itemize}
\item \texttt{IntervalIntegrable.comp\_mul\_left} takes the scale as a named implicit (\texttt{(c := N)})
after two autoParams.
\item \texttt{sub\_eq\_add\_neg} rewrites the first subtraction it finds (the exponent $\alpha-1$),
not the intended $\log N-\log s$; rewrite the specific term with \texttt{show}.
\item A helper name (\texttt{abs\_log\_pow\_le}) clashed with an existing declaration in another file
of the namespace; the clash surfaces only in the full \texttt{lake build} of \texttt{Laplace.lean}.
\textbf{Gate lesson}: a shell pipeline that greps ``error'' returns success; gate on the build's exit
code, not on the grep.
\item \texttt{Measurable.abs} does not exist; use \texttt{continuous\_abs.measurable.comp}.
\end{itemize}

\section{Outcome}
The transfer theorem separates analytic estimates from the future combinatorial construction of the
density expansion. Next: the parameterised first/second-order Taylor kernel lemma for
$\eta(u,v)e^{\beta s\xi(u,v)}$ and the $d=2$ anchored-subtraction density formula, whose output plugs
into \texttt{densityTransfer\_bound} to give the first complete nonconstant-$\xi,\eta$ log polynomial.
