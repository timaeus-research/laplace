\begin{abstract}
First geometric bridge after the normal block (Astra~\#17). For a continuous amplitude
$\eta(v,u)$ on tangential$\times$normal variables, a compact tangential set $K$ and an integrable
density $q$ on $K$, the tangential average $\bar\eta(u)=\int_Kq(v)\eta(v,u)\,dv$ is continuous
(\texttt{continuous\_tangentialAvg}), Fubini identifies the tangentially integrated dressed normal
moment with the dressed moment of $\bar\eta$ (\texttt{integral\_tangential\_swap}), and hence
\[
\frac{\int_Kq(v)\int_{(0,1]^{d}}\eta(v,u)\,u^he^{-\beta Nu^{2k}}\,du\,dv}{N^{-\lambda}(\log N)^{|J|-1}}
\longrightarrow\int_Kq(v)\,\operatorname{amplitudeCoeff}(\eta(v,\cdot))\,dv
\]
(\texttt{tangential\_amplitude\_tendsto}). Signed densities and amplitudes are allowed. Zero
\texttt{sorry}/\texttt{axiom}.
\end{abstract}

\section{The things formalised}
{\small
\begin{verbatim}
def tangentialAvg (K) (q) (η : (Fin t → ℝ) × (Fin r → ℝ) → ℝ) (u) : ℝ :=
  ∫ v in K, q v * η (v, u)
theorem continuous_tangentialAvg (K) (hKc : IsCompact K) (hK) (q) (hq : IntegrableOn q K)
    (η) (hη : Continuous η) : Continuous (tangentialAvg K q η)
theorem integrable_tangential_prod ... :
    Integrable (fun z => (q z.1 * w z.2) * η (z.1, φ z.2))
      ((volume.restrict K).prod (volume.restrict S))
theorem integral_tangential_swap ... :
    ∫ v in K, q v * ∫ u in S, η (v, φ u) * w u
      = ∫ u in S, (∫ v in K, q v * η (v, φ u)) * w u
theorem tangential_amplitude_tendsto (d) (h k) (hk) (l β) (hl) (hβ) (hmin) (hatt)
    (K) (hKc) (hK) (q) (hq) (η : (Fin t → ℝ) × (Fin (d+1) → ℝ) → ℝ) (hη : Continuous η) :
    Tendsto (fun N => (∫ v in K, q v * ∫ x in unitBox (d + 1),
        η (v, x) * ((∏ i, x i ^ h i) * exp (-(β * N * ∏ i, x i ^ (2 * k i)))))
      / (N ^ (-l) * log N ^ (multCount (ratioExp h k) l - 1))) atTop
      (𝓝 (∫ v in K, q v * amplitudeCoeff h k l β (fun u => η (v, u))))
\end{verbatim}
}

\section{Mathematics}
Astra~\#17's ``average first'' route: the weight $u^he^{-\beta Nu^{2k}}$ does not depend on $v$, so
Fubini moves the tangential integral inside, the amplitude theorem of unit~186 applies once to
$\bar\eta$, and a second Fubini (with the face projection as the cube-valued map) turns
$\operatorname{amplitudeCoeff}(\bar\eta)$ into $\int_Kq\,\operatorname{amplitudeCoeff}(\eta(v,\cdot))$.
Continuity of $\bar\eta$ is dominated continuity: near $u_0$ the amplitude is bounded on
$K\times\overline B(u_0,1)$, so $C|q|$ dominates. Joint integrability on $K\times S$ uses the product
integrability of $q(v)w(u)$ and the same compactness bound.

\section{Paper-facing meaning}
This is the deterministic chart-level precursor of the paper's tangential integration of the normal
block against the density $c_0$ and the observable's normal data: for equal ratios the limit reduces
to $\Gamma(\lambda)\beta^{-\lambda}/((d-1)!\prod2k_i)\int_Kq(v)\eta(v,0)\,dv$ (next unit), and for
mixed ratios it retains the face integral in the nonminimal normal directions. The identification of
$\int_Kq(v)\,\cdot\,dv$ with $\int_{S_I}c_0|dv|$ requires the chart/partition-of-unity bridge, which
remains outside the formalised boundary.

\section{Lean notes}
\begin{itemize}
\item \texttt{continuousAt\_of\_dominated} wants \texttt{∀ᶠ x in 𝓝 x₀} measurability and bound;
\texttt{Metric.closedBall\_mem\_nhds} supplies the neighbourhood on which the compact bound holds.
\item For the product integrability, put the integrable factor first:
\texttt{(hq.mul\_prod hw).aestronglyMeasurable.mul (continuous∘measurable)} avoids any
\texttt{AEStronglyMeasurable.fst} lemma.
\item \texttt{Measure.prod\_restrict} converts the product of restricted measures into the restriction

\texttt{(fun u => ...) (faceProj u)}: \texttt{unfold} then \texttt{beta\_reduce} before \texttt{rw}.
\end{itemize}
