\begin{abstract}
A soundness review of \texttt{Laplace/TwoD/KthKthNumeratorAsymptotic.lean}: the generic-%
$(k_1,k_2)$ asymptotic of the unnormalised 2D separable monomial numerator
$\int x^{2j_1}y^{2j_2}e^{-tL}$, the second leg of the ``2D lift'' trio. Result: \textbf{a clean
fidelity pass with one dead-code removal} --- the dead \texttt{open Real} predicted by the
\reviewref[partition-leg review]{2026-06-05-review-laplace-twod-kthkthpartition}, confirmed.
\end{abstract}

\section{Setting}
This is the numerator analogue of the partition leg reviewed immediately before it: same
four-step lift pattern, same factorisation root
(\reviewref[AddSeparable]{2026-06-05-review-laplace-twod-addseparable}, here via
\texttt{integral\_separable\_addSeparable}), same 1D base
(\reviewref[MonomialPotential]{2026-06-05-review-laplace-oned-monomialpotential}, here via
\texttt{kth\_moment\_even}). The partition-leg retrospective predicted the same dead
\texttt{open Real} would recur in the sibling files; this review confirms it.

\section{What was reviewed}
Three public theorems and the composite constant
$C'(k_1,k_2,j_1,j_2)=\tfrac1{k_1k_2}(2k_1)!^{(2j_1+1)/(2k_1)}(2k_2)!^{(2j_2+1)/(2k_2)}%
\Gamma(\tfrac{2j_1+1}{2k_1})\Gamma(\tfrac{2j_2+1}{2k_2})$: the exact closed form
$C'\cdot t^{-(2j_1+1)/(2k_1)-(2j_2+1)/(2k_2)}$, the rescaled limit $\to C'$, and the asymptotic
equivalence.

\section{Fidelity / soundness findings}
\textbf{Sound; no mismatch.} The composite constant is exactly the product of the two 1D
\texttt{kth\_moment\_even} constants, and the exponent $-(2j_1+1)/(2k_1)-(2j_2+1)/(2k_2)$ is correct
throughout (with the positive mirror in the rescaling). The hypotheses sit where they should
($t>0$ for the exact form, only $k_i\ge1$ for the \texttt{atTop} statements). Unlike the partition
leg --- whose module bullet had conflated two theorems and called a fractional power-law a
``polynomial'' --- this file's docstrings match their statements exactly; the reviewer found no
over- or under-claim.

\section{Simplifications applied}
\textbf{One dead \texttt{open}.} Every \texttt{Real.exp}/\texttt{Real.Gamma}/%
\texttt{Real.div\_rpow}/\texttt{Real.rpow\_*} is qualified and the bare $t\,\hat{}\,(\text{real})$
is the \texttt{rpow} instance, so \texttt{open Real} is dead; \texttt{MeasureTheory} (bare
$\int$), \texttt{Filter}, \texttt{Asymptotics}, and scoped \texttt{Topology} stay. Module green at
$2700$ jobs, no warnings, signatures byte-identical. GPT affirmed.

\section{Disagreements and open questions}
None. The reviewer's other suggestions (replace the inline \texttt{show \dots\ by ring} equality
proofs inside \texttt{rw} with \texttt{change}/named haves; factor the twin \texttt{h\_k1}/%
\texttt{h\_k2} rewrites into a local helper) were declined as proof-style/structural refactors ---
and, as in the partition leg, \texttt{change} cannot replace an equality \emph{proof term} passed to
\texttt{rw}, so that one is not even warning-clearing.

\section{Lessons}
\begin{itemize}[noitemsep]
  \item \textbf{The sibling-prediction pattern holds again.} A dead-code finding in one instance of
    a copy-paste lift family is a near-certain finding in the rest --- the dead \texttt{open Real}
    crossed cleanly from the partition leg to the numerator leg, as the
    \reviewref[Quartic$\to$Sextic]{2026-06-05-review-laplace-oned-sextic} prediction first
    established. The cheap confirmation is one rebuild.
  \item \textbf{A clean docstring is the more common case.} The partition leg's mislabelled bullet
    was the exception, not the rule; this sibling's terse bullets were accurate. Catching the one
    drift does not mean the family is riddled with it.
\end{itemize}
