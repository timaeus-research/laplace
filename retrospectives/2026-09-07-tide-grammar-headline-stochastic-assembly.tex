\begin{abstract}
Unit 215 closes Astra~\#23's programme~G: the paper's leading-order stochastic expectation
expansion, conditional on the chart decomposition. For finitely many charts $a\in\kappa$ with their
own dimensions and exponents, deterministic continuous observables $\varphi_a$ and densities $c_a>0$,
a common $\beta$, jointly convergent random phases $X_n\Rightarrow Z$ in $\prod_aC([0,1]^{d_a+1})$ and
deterministic $N_n\to\infty$ ($N_n>1$),
\[
\frac{\sum_a\int\varphi_ac_a\,u^{h_a}e^{-\beta N_n^2u^{2k_a}+\beta N_nu^{k_a}X_n^a}}
     {\sum_a\int c_a\,u^{h_a}e^{-\beta N_n^2u^{2k_a}+\beta N_nu^{k_a}X_n^a}}
\;\Rightarrow\;
\frac{\sum_{a\in S}\mathrm{phaseCoeff}_a(Z^a,\varphi_ac_a)}{\sum_{a\in S}\mathrm{phaseCoeff}_a(Z^a,c_a)},
\]
$S$ the charts with minimal exponent $p_a=2\lambda_a$ and, among those, maximal multiplicity
(\texttt{headline\_stochastic\_assembled\_posterior}). It is a ratio of sums, not a sum of chart
ratios; the selected limiting denominator is a.s.\ (indeed surely) positive. Zero
\texttt{sorry}/\texttt{axiom}; 9092 jobs.
\end{abstract}

\section{Mathematics}
Per chart, $\sum_aI_a/S_*=\sum_a(S_a/S_*)(I_a/S_a)$ with $S_a=N^{-p_a}(\log N)^{m_a-1}$ and
$S_*$ the selected normalisation. The vector $(I_a^{\varphi c}/S_a,\,I_a^{c}/S_a)_a$ converges jointly
in distribution (joint pair-vector Headline~XVI: common phase vector, errors to $0$ in probability
collected by \texttt{tendstoInMeasure\_pi\_zero}); the deterministic ratios $S_a/S_*\to e_a\in\{0,1\}$
(\texttt{scale\_ratio\_tendsto}); Slutsky pairs the two; the continuous map
$(v,w)\mapsto(\sum_aw_av_a^{(1)},\sum_aw_av_a^{(2)})$ gives the assembled normalised numerator and
denominator jointly; the selected denominator $\sum_{a\in S}\mathrm{phaseCoeff}_a(Z^a,c_a)$ is a
finite sum of strictly positive terms over a nonempty selection; the quotient theorem of unit~157 and
the exact cancellation of $S_*$ (all $N_n>1$) finish. As Astra~\#23 insisted, this is the honest
random object --- the denominator weights are random and do not cancel chart by chart.
