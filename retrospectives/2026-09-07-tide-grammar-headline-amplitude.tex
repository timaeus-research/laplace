\begin{abstract}
Paper-facing wrappers for the continuous-amplitude milestone (units 184--186). For the dressed
normal moment $I_\eta(N)=\int_{(0,1]^{m+1}}\eta(x)x^he^{-\beta Nx^{2k}}\,dx$ with continuous $\eta$:
the face-supported leading functional spelled out on the Bochner integral
(\texttt{headline\_normal\_moment\_amplitude}); the constant-amplitude consistency check
(\texttt{amplitudeCoeff\_const}); the equal-ratio case, where the coefficient is $\eta(0)$ times the
bare constant (\texttt{headline\_normal\_moment\_amplitude\_equal}); the face-vanishing case with zero
coefficient (\texttt{amplitudeCoeff\_eq\_zero\_of\_face}); and the equivalence form under a nonzero
coefficient (\texttt{headline\_normal\_moment\_amplitude\_equiv}). Zero \texttt{sorry}/\texttt{axiom}.
\end{abstract}

\section{Paper-facing meaning}
This closes the deterministic one-block programme at the level Astra~\#16 called ``one normal block
complete'': arbitrary mixed ratios, continuous amplitudes, an explicit face-supported leading
functional, cutoff scaling, and an explicit treatment of a possibly vanishing leading coefficient
(the asymptotic equivalence is asserted only when the coefficient is nonzero). Origin evaluation of
the amplitude is verified to be correct exactly in the equal-ratio case.
