\begin{abstract}
Astra~\#9's R5 target. For every $s$-parametrised double series $c$ with the COMMON envelope
$|c_{ij}(s)|\rho^{i+j}\le H(s)\le C_0(1+s)^De^{\beta sL}$ and every $N\ge1$,
\[
|Z_c(N)-S_c(N)|\le\mathrm{uniformConst}\cdot N^{-E}(1+\log N),\qquad
E=\min\Bigl(p_1+\frac{M_1}{k_1},\,p_2+\frac{M_2}{k_2}\Bigr),
\]
where $\mathrm{uniformConst}$ depends only on $(\beta,b,\rho,C_0,L,D,h,k,M)$: the constant is chosen
before the coefficient family (\texttt{twoD\_cutoff\_uniform}). It is the explicit cutoff constant
of unit~131 with every face constant replaced by its envelope bound (unit~132) at the face envelopes
of the analytic adapter, plus the explicit mixed constant of unit~130. Zero
\texttt{sorry}/\texttt{axiom}.
\end{abstract}

\section{Mathematics}

The analytic adapter supplies, for the $u$-face $i$, the Taylor-data envelope
$|c_{im}(s)|\le C_0\rho^{-i}(1+\rho^{-1})^{M_2}(1+s)^De^{\beta sL}$ ($m<M_2$) and the remainder
envelope $H_i(s)=H(s)\rho^{-i-M_2}/(1-b/\rho)\le C_0\rho^{-i-M_2}(1-b/\rho)^{-1}(1+s)^De^{\beta sL}$;
for the corners the constant Taylor datum $c_{ij}$ has envelope $C_0\rho^{-i-j}(1+s)^De^{\beta sL}$
and zero remainder. Unit~132 converts each face constant into $\mathrm{faceEnvConst}$ times one
Gaussian envelope moment at the face's remainder exponent; the mixed constant is already
envelope-only. Summing termwise gives $\mathrm{cutoffConst}\le\mathrm{uniformConst}$, and unit~131
gives the bound for every $N\ge1$.

\section{Paper-facing meaning}

This is the uniformity Astra~\#9 asked for: a single remainder constant for the whole family of
chart amplitudes $\eta_we^{\beta s\xi_w}$ whose coefficient arrays share a weighted bound, i.e.\ for
$\xi,\eta$ analytic on a common neighbourhood of the closed box times the support of the partition
of unity in the noncritical coordinates $w$. With $K(w)\equiv\mathrm{uniformConst}$ measurable and
bounded, the per-stratum integral over $w$ can be fed to \texttt{param\_integration} (unit~99) once
the leading-order corollary is extracted and the coefficient functions $A(w),B(w)$ are shown
measurable; that is the noncritical-coordinate adapter of Astra~\#6 rank~4.
