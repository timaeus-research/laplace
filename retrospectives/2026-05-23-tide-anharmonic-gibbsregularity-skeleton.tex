\begin{abstract}
A skeleton tide producing the file
\texttt{Laplace/OneD/AnharmonicGibbsRegularity.lean} that supplies the
2/3 easy fields of the \texttt{Threepoint.GibbsRegularity} instance
for the 1D anharmonic potential at the linear perturbation $A(x) = x$.
\texttt{partition\_pos} is proven via
\texttt{integral\_pos\_iff\_support\_of\_nonneg\_ae} + the (newly public)
\texttt{integrable\_exp\_neg\_t\_anharmonic} lemma. \texttt{partition\_h\_zero}
closes by \texttt{congr; funext; ring\_nf}. \texttt{partition\_hasDerivAt}
is left as a \texttt{sorry} with a detailed proof sketch in the
docstring; the follow-up analytic-regularity tide picks it up via
the dominated-convergence + Gaussian-decay machinery in
\texttt{Laplace.OneD.IntegralRemainder}.
\end{abstract}

\section{Setting}

This tide is the direct handoff from the 2026-05-23 fdt-identity-1d
tide-experiment\footnote{\experimentref{2026-05-23-experiment-fdt-identity-1d}.},
which empirically validated the FDT identity
$\partial_h \mathrm{Cov}_h(x, x)|_{h=0} = -t \cdot \kappa_3(x, x, x)$
on the 1D anharmonic Gibbs. The general FDT identity is already
formalised as \texttt{Threepoint.gibbsCov\_deriv\_eq\_neg\_t\_kappa3}
in the \texttt{Threepoint} package; the remaining work to apply it to
the anharmonic case is the \texttt{GibbsRegularity} instance for the
anharmonic potential.

The harmonic mirror \texttt{Threepoint.harmonic\_id\_gibbsRegularity}
(in \texttt{HarmonicGibbsRegularity.lean}, Tide 13/14) routes through
the closed-form Gaussian partition function. The anharmonic case has
no elementary closed form for $Z(h)$, so the
\texttt{partition\_hasDerivAt} field is genuinely harder and needs
dominated convergence with explicit Gaussian-decay bounds. This
skeleton tide lands the easy 2/3 fields; the hard third is deferred.

\section{What landed}

\paragraph{New file:} \texttt{Laplace/OneD/AnharmonicGibbsRegularity.lean}
(~155 lines), exporting:

\begin{itemize}[noitemsep,leftmargin=*]
\item \texttt{integrable\_exp\_neg\_t\_anharmonic} — public witness for
  $\exp(-t \cdot L_{\rm anh}) \in L^1(\mathbb{R})$ under the
  discriminant condition. Proven via the
  $\exp(-t \cdot L_{\rm anh}) \leq \exp(-t \cdot c \cdot x^2)$
  domination (using \texttt{anharmonic\_coercive}). Mirrors the $n=0$
  case of the private
  \texttt{AnharmonicKappa3Affine.integrable\_pow\_mul\_exp\_neg\_anharmonic}
  but exposed publicly so the regularity instance can cite it.

\item \texttt{anharmonic\_partition\_pos} — strict positivity of the
  unperturbed anharmonic partition. Routes through
  \texttt{MeasureTheory.integral\_pos\_iff\_support\_of\_nonneg\_ae}:
  the integrand is positive everywhere (\texttt{Real.exp\_ne\_zero}),
  so its support is all of $\mathbb{R}$, which has infinite Lebesgue
  measure.

\item \texttt{Threepoint.anharmonic\_id\_gibbsRegularity} — the
  \texttt{GibbsRegularity} instance with:
  \begin{itemize}[noitemsep]
  \item \texttt{partition\_pos := anharmonic\_partition\_pos hlam hgamma hdisc ht}
  \item \texttt{partition\_h\_zero := by congr 1; funext x; ring\_nf}
  \item \texttt{partition\_hasDerivAt := sorry} (with detailed proof
    sketch in the docstring; ~100--200 lines estimated for the
    follow-up tide).
  \end{itemize}
\end{itemize}

Build status: clean (\texttt{lake build Laplace.OneD.AnharmonicGibbsRegularity}
succeeds in $\sim 5$s; one \texttt{sorry} warning, no errors).

\section{Why a skeleton, not the full instance}

Per the laplace \texttt{CLAUDE.md} proof-estimate rule: probability of
direct one-shot proof on \texttt{partition\_hasDerivAt} estimated at
$\sim 30\%$, well below the 50\% threshold for ``factor into
intermediate lemmas first''. The hard field needs:

\begin{itemize}[noitemsep,leftmargin=*]
\item A dominator on the perturbed integrand
  $\exp(-t(L_{\rm anh}(x) + h \cdot x))$ for $|h| \leq 1$, valid for the
  $h$-derivative-under-integral lemma. The dominator is
  $\exp(-t \cdot L_{\rm anh}(x)) \cdot \exp(t |x|) \cdot (|t x| + 1)$,
  Gaussian-decaying after completing the square via
  \texttt{anharmonic\_coercive}.
\item An invocation of
  \texttt{MeasureTheory.hasDerivAt\_integral\_of\_dominated\_loc\_of\_lip}
  or its newer Mathlib variant.
\item Final pointwise computation of the $h = 0$ derivative inside the
  integral.
\end{itemize}

Estimated 100--200 lines. Left as a focused follow-up tide.

\section{Why this still matters}

With the skeleton in place:

\begin{itemize}[noitemsep,leftmargin=*]
\item The instance type-checks and is callable from any downstream
  module (modulo the \texttt{sorry}). This means dependent tides
  can be drafted against the instance signature \emph{now}, not
  blocked on the full proof.
\item The two easy fields are real proofs (not sorries): they
  exercise the \texttt{integral\_pos\_iff\_support\_of\_nonneg\_ae}
  + coercivity pattern, which the next tide will need to extend
  to the $h$-perturbed case.
\item The follow-up tide has a sharp scope: only
  \texttt{partition\_hasDerivAt}, with all the imports, namespace
  setup, and the surrounding lemmas already in place.
\end{itemize}

\section{Pipeline lessons}

\begin{itemize}[noitemsep,leftmargin=*]
\item \textbf{Sanity-check Mathlib API before relying on names.} My
  first attempt cited \texttt{volume\_univ\_eq\_top}, a name that
  doesn't exist. The correct Mathlib name is \texttt{Real.volume\_univ}
  (in the \texttt{Real} namespace, defined in
  \texttt{Mathlib.MeasureTheory.Measure.Lebesgue.Basic}). A
  \texttt{grep -rn} on the actual Mathlib source resolved it in
  $\sim 30$ seconds.

\item \textbf{Build the skeleton before attempting the full proof.}
  The 2/3 easy fields built in one shot; the hard third was left
  as a documented \texttt{sorry}. This pattern is preferable to
  trying the full instance and risking thrashing on the hard part.

\item \textbf{Public-ify private lemmas at the boundary.} The
  \texttt{integrable\_pow\_mul\_exp\_neg\_anharmonic} lemma in
  \texttt{AnharmonicKappa3Affine.lean} was \texttt{private}; the
  $n=0$ case is needed by this file, so it was re-derived
  (essentially copy-paste) as the public
  \texttt{integrable\_exp\_neg\_t\_anharmonic}. A future cleanup
  tide could promote the original to public and remove the
  redundancy; for now, the two coexist.
\end{itemize}

\section{Follow-ups}

\begin{itemize}[noitemsep,leftmargin=*]
\item \textbf{\texttt{partition\_hasDerivAt} follow-up tide.}
  $\sim 100$--$200$ lines. Dominated convergence + Gaussian-decay
  domination via \texttt{anharmonic\_coercive}. The full proof
  sketch is in the docstring of the \texttt{sorry} site.
\item \textbf{\texttt{GibbsObservable} instances at $\phi \in
  \{x, x^2, x^3, x \cdot x, x^2 \cdot x, x \cdot x \cdot x\}$.}
  Six instances, each $\sim 30$--$50$ lines after the
  \texttt{partition\_hasDerivAt} pattern is in place. These
  discharge the per-observable hypotheses of
  \texttt{gibbsCov\_deriv\_eq\_neg\_t\_kappa3}. Total $\sim 200$
  lines.
\item \textbf{Headline corollary} (once both above land):
  \texttt{cov\_h\_id\_id\_deriv\_anharmonic\_eq\_neg\_t\_kappa3},
  $\sim 20$--$30$ lines, by direct application of the general
  Threepoint theorem.
\item \textbf{Closed-form asymptotic corollary:} compose the
  headline with Tide 9
  (\texttt{kappa3\_anharmonic\_id\_id\_id\_asymptotic}) to get
  $\lim_{t \to \infty} t \cdot \partial_h \mathrm{Cov}_h|_{h=0} = \alpha/\lambda^3$.
  $\sim 30$ lines.
\end{itemize}
