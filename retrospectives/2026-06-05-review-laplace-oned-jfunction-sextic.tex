\begin{abstract}
A soundness review of \texttt{Laplace/OneD/JFunctionSextic.lean}: the $J$-function asymptotic for the sextic,
$t^{1/6}\!\int e^{-t\,w^6/720}\pi(w)\,dw\to\pi(0)\cdot\tfrac13\cdot720^{1/6}\,\Gamma(1/6)$, now a thin $k=3$
specialisation of the generic \texttt{kth\_jfunction\_*} family. Result: \textbf{a clean fidelity pass with
three small cleanups} --- a dead private bridge lemma, a dead \texttt{open Real}, and a \texttt{show}$\to$%
\texttt{change} style fix --- plus a report-only observation that the quartic twin was never migrated to the
same refactor.
\end{abstract}

\section{Setting}
Unlike the \reviewref[quartic J-function]{2026-06-05-review-laplace-oned-jfunction-quartic} (still a standalone
substitute-then-DCT proof), the sextic file was refactored (its ``L3'' tide) to be a five-line specialisation of
the generic-$k$ J-function abstraction, inheriting the mathematically-minimal hypothesis class
(\texttt{AEMeasurable} $+$ \texttt{ContinuousAt 0} $+$ global bound). It is the cleanest member of the family to
review --- the content lives upstream, and the file is just the $k=3$ constant bridging.

\section{What was reviewed}
Two public theorems (the centred DCT core and the asymptotic corollary), each a $k=3$ instance of the generic
theorems, plus three private constant-bridge lemmas ($2\cdot3=6$, $6!=720$, $1/((2\cdot3{:}\mathbb N){:}\mathbb R)=1/6$).

\section{Fidelity / soundness findings}
\textbf{Sound; no mismatch.} The bridges are correct ($6!=720$, $1/6$), the scaling exponent is $t^{1/6}$, the
kernel is $e^{-tw^6/720}$, and the limit $\pi(0)\cdot\tfrac13\cdot720^{1/6}\,\Gamma(1/6)$ is exactly the
parametric $\pi(0)\cdot(1/k)\cdot((2k)!)^{1/(2k)}\,\Gamma(1/(2k))$ at $k=3$ (the prefactor is $1/k=1/3$, not
$1/(2k)$). The weakened hypotheses are carried through faithfully --- no global \texttt{Continuous} is
reintroduced.

One report-only observation: the \reviewref[quartic J-function file]{2026-06-05-review-laplace-oned-jfunction-quartic}
still uses its original standalone proofs with the \emph{stronger} \texttt{Continuous prior} hypothesis and was
\emph{not} migrated to specialise the generic family the way the sextic was --- even though the family's L1
retrospective explicitly anticipated ``both existing files become 5-line specialisations''. So the two siblings
are now inconsistent: sextic on the minimal hypotheses, quartic on the global one. That is a forward-tide
follow-up (migrate the quartic), not a review edit, and it corroborates the hypothesis note raised in the
quartic review.

\section{Simplifications applied}
\textbf{Three.} A dead private lemma \texttt{sextic\_kth\_eq\_const\_six}~($(2\cdot3{:}\mathbb N)=6$) --- unused,
because the $2\cdot3\to6$ passage is handled definitionally rather than through the named lemma; a dead
\texttt{open Real} (every \texttt{Real.exp}/\texttt{Real.Gamma} is qualified, and \texttt{open
MeasureTheory}/\texttt{Filter}/\texttt{Topology} carry the load --- the last via $\mathcal N$); and a
\texttt{show}$\to$\texttt{change} on the factorial-bridge lemma, clearing a standing \texttt{linter.style.show}
warning per the seabed's ``\texttt{change}, not \texttt{show}'' rule. Rebuild-arbitrated green, file now
warning-free, both public signatures byte-identical, GPT-affirmed. (A residual \texttt{show}-style warning lives
in the upstream \texttt{JFunctionMonomial.lean} --- out of scope here, flagged for that file's review.)

\section{Disagreements and open questions}
None on the mathematics.

\section{Lessons}
\begin{itemize}[noitemsep]
  \item \textbf{A refactor can migrate one sibling and silently strand the other.} The J-function family's
    hypothesis-weakening refactor reached the sextic but not the quartic, leaving two files that prove the
    ``same'' kind of theorem under different hypotheses. A review of either alone would miss it; reviewing them
    in succession surfaced the asymmetry. When a file advertises itself as ``the $k=c$ specialisation'' of a
    generic, check whether its siblings made the same move.
  \item \textbf{Three small file-local cleanups, three independent arbiters.} The dead private lemma (grep:
    unused), the dead open (grep: no notation used), and the \texttt{show}/\texttt{change} (linter: style
    warning) are each confirmed by a different mechanical check, and the single rebuild settles all three at
    once with no new warnings.
\end{itemize}

\section{Follow-ups}
\begin{itemize}[noitemsep]
  \item \textbf{Forward tide:} migrate \texttt{JFunctionQuartic} to specialise the generic
    \texttt{kth\_jfunction\_*} (as the sextic does), weakening its hypothesis from \texttt{Continuous} to
    \texttt{AEMeasurable}~$+$~\texttt{ContinuousAt 0}.
  \item \textbf{Next review:} the generic \texttt{JFunctionMonomial.lean} (the N1 abstraction both J-function
    files rest on) --- it also carries a \texttt{show}-style warning to address. Then
    \texttt{JFunctionAsymptoticEquivalence}.
\end{itemize}
