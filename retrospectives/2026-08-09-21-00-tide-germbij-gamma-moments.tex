\section{Setting}

Stage 2 gave the second-order $J_n$ expansions; this stage converts them
into rates for the Gibbs moments themselves, the direct inputs of the
fourth-cumulant assembly. The scoping consult's worked expansions fixed
every target coefficient in advance, and all three were confirmed
numerically before any Lean was written.

\section{The thing formalised}

Ten declarations in \texttt{Laplace/OneD/MomentSecondOrder.lean},
sorry-free with zero warnings: three bridge identities
($t\langle x^2\rangle = J_2/(\lambda J_0)$ and the $x^3$, $x^4$
analogues, from the seabed's substitution relation), four $J$-deltas
with the Gaussian constants evaluated, and the three public rates. With
$A, B$ the cubic and quartic scales, eventually in $t$:
\[
\Bigl| t\langle x^2\rangle - \frac{1}{\lambda}
- \frac{45A^2 - 12B}{\lambda\,t} \Bigr| \leq \frac{K}{t\sqrt t},
\qquad
\Bigl| t^2\langle x^4\rangle - \frac{3}{\lambda^2}
- \frac{450A^2 - 96B}{\lambda^2\,t} \Bigr| \leq \frac{K}{t\sqrt t},
\]
\[
\Bigl| t^2\langle x^3\rangle + \frac{15A}{\sqrt\lambda^{\,3}} \Bigr|
\leq \frac{K}{\sqrt t}.
\]
Together with the seabed's existing mean rate, these are exactly the
inputs the $\kappa_4$ cancellation table consumes.

\section{Proof strategy}

Each rate is a fraction analysis over the $J$-form bridge: combine the
difference into a single fraction with numerator
$N = J_r - (\text{leading} + \text{coeff}/t)\,J_0$; split $N$ against
the two deltas, where the $1/t$ terms cancel \emph{exactly}
($q_2 - q_0 = c_2$ and $p_4 - 3q_0 = c_4'$, verified by \texttt{ring} on
explicit coefficients); bound the three pieces by the triangle
inequality; and control the denominator by the $J_0$ lower bound. The
third moment is the same shape one order earlier, with odd Gaussian
moments annihilating everything but the single $A$-term.

\section{Roadblocks and resolutions}

Built across four checkpointed commits (bridges, deltas, first
assembly, remaining two); the deltas layer compiled on the first
attempt. Every iteration was a documented catalogue class ---
including, satisfyingly, one instance of the unsafe
$t$-inside-radical rewrite that this programme's own stage-2b
\texttt{CLAUDE.md} entry warns about, caught immediately from the goal
display ($\sqrt{\sqrt t^2}$) and repaired with the radical-atom
pattern. New micro-entries: \texttt{div\_le\_div\_iff} and
\texttt{le\_div\_iff} are now the $_0$-suffixed variants, and
\texttt{set\_option} must precede a declaration's docstring.

\section{What was Mathlib, what was new}

Mathlib: field and absolute-value algebra, \texttt{gcongr}. Seabed: the
substitution relation, the $J_0$ bounds, and the whole stage-2 layer.
New: the exact-cancellation fraction pattern, now used three times and
likely the template for any future order-deepening.

\section{Lessons}

Checkpointed WIP commits (each building, sorry-free) made a
four-hundred-line file safe to build across session boundaries; the
per-layer validation (bridges, then deltas, then assemblies) meant no
error ever implicated more than the newest layer. When a catalogue
entry exists, the failure it describes costs one iteration; the only
multi-iteration fights in this stage were in the first assembly, whose
structure the other two then reused verbatim.
