\begin{abstract}
For an amplitude $\Psi(u,s)$ depending on the first coordinate only, Lipschitz in $u$ at $0$, the
one-variable density of unit~86 expands as
$\rho_\Psi(r,s)=r^{p-1}\log(1/r)\,\tfrac{\Psi(0,s)}{k_1k_2}+r^{p-1}\big(\tfrac{\log R\,\Psi(0,s)}{k_1k_2}
+\tfrac{I_\Psi(s)}{k_2}\big)+O\big(r^{p-1+1/k_1}(1+s)e^{\beta Ls}\big)$ with the axis integral
$I_\Psi(s)=\int_0^b(\Psi(u,s)-\Psi(0,s))/u\,du$, and the density-transfer theorem gives
$Z_\Psi(N)=N^{-p}(A_\Psi\log N+B_\Psi)+O(N^{-(p+1/k_1)}(1+\log N))$ for $N\ge1$. The constant $B_\Psi$
sees the whole axis $\{v=0\}$, not only the corner. Zero \texttt{sorry}/\texttt{axiom}.
\end{abstract}

\section{Proof strategy}
Split $u^{-1}\Psi(u,s)=u^{-1}\Psi(0,s)+u^{-1}(\Psi(u,s)-\Psi(0,s))$ on $[m,b]$,
$m=(r/b^{k_2})^{1/k_1}$: the first part integrates to $\Psi(0,s)\log(b/m)$ with
$\log(b/m)=(\log R+\log(1/r))/k_1$, the second is $I_\Psi(s)$ minus the tail over $(0,m]$, bounded by
$m\,C_1(1+s)e^{\beta Ls}$ since the integrand is bounded by $C_1(1+s)e^{\beta Ls}$. Hence the density
expansion with a degree-one log term and remainder $r^{p-1+1/k_1}$. The moments are finite by the
log-power version of the polynomial-exponential bound lemma, and the transferred terms are
$N^{-p}(\log N\cdot M_0-M_1)$ (degree~1) and $N^{-p}M_0$ (degree~0), which rearrange to
$A_\Psi\log N+B_\Psi$.

\section{Roadblocks and resolutions}
\begin{itemize}
\item Joint measurability of $(r,s)\mapsto\int_{m(r)}^b u^{-1}\Psi(u,s)\,du$: write the set integral as
an integral of an indicator over $\mathbb R$ and use
\texttt{StronglyMeasurable.integral\_prod\_right'} with the indicator function named explicitly
(otherwise the typeclass search for the codomain is stuck).
\item \texttt{Integrable.const\_mul} of an \texttt{IntegrableOn} returns an \texttt{Integrable} on the
restricted measure; ascribe the \texttt{IntegrableOn} type before \texttt{.congr\_fun}.
\item \texttt{logMoment\_const\_mul} rewrites also through eta-reduced $\Psi\,0$; state the
coefficients with \texttt{(Ψ 0)} rather than \texttt{fun s => Ψ 0 s}.
\item The envelope must be nonnegative for all $s$ (structure field): use $(1+|s|)$.
\end{itemize}

\section{Outcome}
The axis contribution of the $d=2$ theorem is formalised. Remaining for the milestone: the anchored
decomposition $F=F(u,0)+F(0,v)-F(0,0)+uvG$ with the mixed-term bound via a shifted constant-kernel
block integral, and the assembly $Z(N)=N^{-p}(A\log N+B)+O(N^{-(p+\delta)}(1+\log N))$.
