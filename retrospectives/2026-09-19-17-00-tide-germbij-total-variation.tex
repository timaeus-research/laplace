\begin{abstract}
If two smooth nonnegative losses have Laplace families that agree
beyond all orders on every smooth compactly supported observable, then
the local total variation of the two Boltzmann weights is beyond all
orders: $\int\eta^2\,|e^{-tL_2} - e^{-tL_1}| = o(t^{-\infty})$ for every
smooth bump $\eta$, and $\int_K|e^{-tL_2} - e^{-tL_1}| = o(t^{-\infty})$
for every compact $K$. Consequently every bounded measurable compactly
supported observable, in particular every continuous one, sees the two
families agree beyond all orders, and normalized expectations of such
observables agree once the partition values carry the anchor. This
closes the observable-class gap left open by the day's two previous
tides, whose transfer theorem was stated for the tested class
$C_c^\infty$ only. The mechanism is two pointwise inequalities,
$D(a - b) \ge 0$ and $|a - b| \le t|D|$ for $a = e^{-tL_1}$, $b =
e^{-tL_2}$, $D = L_2 - L_1$, tested at the smooth observable $\eta^2 D$,
and an AM--GM step with the free parameter $t^{-N}$ that does the work
of Cauchy--Schwarz with no square roots and no H\"older inequality. Six
hundred and fifty lines, four LSP rounds, one whnf timeout bisected to a
compact-support unification.
\end{abstract}

\section{Setting}

The projective-closure tide
(\tideref[2026-09-19-15-40]{2026-09-19-15-40-tide-germbij-projective-closure})
ended with a transfer theorem,
\begin{center}\code{superPoly\_difference\_of\_projective}.\end{center}
It says that projective
agreement plus local equality near one zero gives exact agreement
$I_2(\phi) - I_1(\phi) = o(t^{-\infty})$ for every $\phi$ in the tested
class $C_c^\infty$. Its consult flagged the natural question, whether
the conclusion extends to continuous observables, and sketched the
answer: control the local total variation of the weights through the
smooth observable $\eta^2 D$. This tide formalises that sketch. The
deliberation (tide log \code{2026-09-19-17-00-tide-germbij-total-variation.md}
in \code{tide-log/}, consult in \code{gpt\_germbij\_tv\_v1.md}) fixed the plan at the level
of statements and settled the one design choice that mattered for Lean:
replace the Cauchy--Schwarz step by an AM--GM bound with a free
parameter, so that nothing about $L^2$ norms or square roots enters.
Both parties voted for the full package.

\section{The thing formalised}

Write $a = e^{-tL_1}$, $b = e^{-tL_2}$, $D = L_2 - L_1$, and
$I_i(\phi) = \int\phi\,e^{-tL_i}$.

\begin{quote}\itshape
Let $L_1, L_2 \ge 0$ be smooth and suppose $I_2(\phi) - I_1(\phi) =
o(t^{-\infty})$ for every $\phi \in C_c^\infty$. Then:
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(V)] $\int\eta^2\,|b - a| = o(t^{-\infty})$ for every smooth
compactly supported $\eta$, and $\int_K|b - a| = o(t^{-\infty})$ for
every compact $K$.
\item[(U)] $I_2(\phi) - I_1(\phi) = o(t^{-\infty})$ for every bounded
measurable compactly supported $\phi$, in particular for every
continuous compactly supported $\phi$.
\item[(N)] If moreover $Z_1, Z_2 > 0$ carry polynomial lower bounds and
$Z_2/Z_1 = 1 + o(t^{-\infty})$, then $I_2(\phi)/Z_2 - I_1(\phi)/Z_1 =
o(t^{-\infty})$ for every such $\phi$.
\end{enumerate}
\end{quote}

\noindent The Lean signature of (V):
\begin{verbatim}
theorem superPoly_integral_sq_mul_abs_exp_sub {L1 L2 : (iota -> R) -> R}
    (h1 : ContDiff R infty L1) (h2 : ContDiff R infty L2)
    (hL1 : forall w, 0 <= L1 w) (hL2 : forall w, 0 <= L2 w)
    (hexact : forall phi, ContDiff R infty phi -> HasCompactSupport phi ->
      SuperPoly fun t => (integral phi * exp (-(t * L2)))
        - integral phi * exp (-(t * L1)))
    {eta : (iota -> R) -> R} (heta : ContDiff R infty eta)
    (hetas : HasCompactSupport eta) :
    SuperPoly fun t =>
      integral (eta ^ 2 * |exp (-(t * L2)) - exp (-(t * L1))|)
\end{verbatim}
(integrals abbreviated). The other declarations:
\begin{itemize}
\item the compact-set form, \code{superPoly\_setIntegral\_abs\_exp\_sub};
\item (U): \code{superPoly\_difference\_of\_bounded} and
\code{superPoly\_difference\_of\_continuous};
\item (N): \code{superPoly\_normalized\_difference\_of\_bounded};
\item the closure wiring,
\code{superPoly\_difference\_of\_projective\_bounded};
\item the pointwise lemmas \code{abs\_exp\_neg\_sub\_exp\_neg\_le},
\code{weightDiff\_mul\_nonneg} and \code{sq\_exp\_sub\_le\_mul};
\item the helper \code{superPoly\_of\_forall\_eventually\_le};
\item the helpers \code{SuperPoly.of\_abs\_le} and
\code{SuperPoly.polyBounded\_mul}.
\end{itemize}

Smoothness of both losses is used exactly once, to put $\eta^2 D$ in the
tested class; the consult's remark that smoothness of $D$ alone would
do is recorded but not formalised. The conclusions are for compactly
supported observables; nothing is claimed about tails, and the local
$L^\infty$ statement the consult showed to be true (via a gradient
bound and interpolation) is left for a later tide.

\section{Proof strategy}

\emph{Pointwise.} For $x, y \ge 0$, $|e^{-x} - e^{-y}| \le |x - y|$:
in the ordered case $e^{-x} - e^{-y} = e^{-x}(1 - e^{-(y-x)}) \le
y - x$ from $e^{-x} \le 1$ and $1 - u \le e^{-u}$
(\code{Real.add\_one\_le\_exp}). The weight is antitone, so $D(a - b)
\ge 0$ for $t \ge 0$, and then $|a - b|^2 \le |a - b|\,t|D| = t\,D(a - b)$.

\emph{Energy.} $E(t) = \int\eta^2 D(a - b) = I_1(\eta^2 D) -
I_2(\eta^2 D)$ is beyond all orders by hypothesis at the smooth
observable $\eta^2 D$, and nonnegative pointwise.

\emph{AM--GM with a free parameter.} For $\varepsilon = t^{-N}$,
$|h| \le \varepsilon/2 + h^2/(2\varepsilon)$, so $|a - b| \le t^{-N}/2 +
t^{N+1} D(a - b)/2$ and
\[
\int\eta^2|a - b| \;\le\; \tfrac12\Big(\int\eta^2\Big)t^{-N} +
\tfrac12\,t^{N+1}E(t) \;\le\; C_N\,t^{-N}
\]
eventually, using $E(t) \le t^{-(2N+1)}$. Since this holds for every
$N$, the integral is beyond all orders
(\code{superPoly\_of\_forall\_eventually\_le}, which turns an $O(t^{-N})$
bound for all $N$ into $o(t^{-N})$ for all $N$ by shifting the index).

\emph{Bounded tests.} A \code{ContDiffBump} at the origin equal to $1$
on a closed ball containing the support of $\phi$ gives $\eta^2 = 1$ on
$\operatorname{supp}\phi$, so $|I_2(\phi) - I_1(\phi)| \le \int|\phi|\,
|b - a| \le M\int\eta^2|b - a|$. Integrability of $\phi\,e^{-tL_i}$ for
$t \ge 0$ comes from a small lemma,
\code{integrable\_of\_bounded\_of\_hasCompactSupport}, via domination by
the indicator of the support. Continuous $\phi$ is bounded by
\code{HasCompactSupport.exists\_bound\_of\_continuous}.

\emph{Normalized tests.} $I_2/Z_2 - I_1/Z_1 = (I_2 - I_1)/Z_1 +
(I_2/Z_2)(1 - Z_2/Z_1)$; the first term is (U) times a polynomially
bounded $1/Z_1$, the second is $Z_2/Z_1 - 1$ times a polynomially bounded
factor ($|I_2| \le M\,\mathrm{vol}(\operatorname{supp}\phi)$ and $1/Z_2
= O(t^n)$), both by \code{SuperPoly.polyBounded\_mul}.

\section{Roadblocks and resolutions}

Four LSP rounds. Three were ordinary: an \code{isLittleO\_one\_iff} that
needs its normed type passed explicitly; an absolute-value orientation;
a parenthesisation slip where an integral written as
\code{integral w, eta w \^{} 2 + ...} swallowed the rest of the line into
the integrand (the pretty-printer shows the same
text either way, so read integrals with a following \code{+} twice); a
sign lemma I had first stated without $t \ge 0$, which is false for
$t < 0$ and which \code{linarith} rightly refused; and the
\code{Measure.real} versus \code{toReal} spelling of
\code{setIntegral\_const}.

The fourth was a deterministic whnf timeout attributed to the whole
compact-set lemma, with every \code{have} checking. Bisection by
inserting \code{sorry} at successive points located it to one term,
\begin{center}
\code{((heta.continuous.pow 2).mul hc).integrable\_of\_hasCompactSupport}
\code{(heta2s.mul\_right)}
\end{center}
where \code{mul\_right} produces \code{HasCompactSupport (f * g)} in
\code{Pi} form and the expected type is a lambda; the unifier tried to
unfold its way through. Naming the compact-support fact first with the
lambda type ascribed (\code{have hsupp : HasCompactSupport fun w => ...}
followed by \code{:= heta2s.mul\_right}) and passing \code{hsupp} elaborated instantly. The
identical construction had worked two lemmas earlier by luck of the
elaboration order. This is the \code{Pi.add} gotcha of \code{CLAUDE.md}
in a new coat.

\emph{The consult}, before writing, settled the design and scoped the
claims. On the AM--GM route:
\gptbox{The AM--GM route loses nothing at the SuperPoly level. [\ldots]
The one bookkeeping point: to conclude little-$o$ at exponent $N$, use
the big-$O$ estimate at exponent $N + 1$. Your proposed helper is
exactly right.}
On regularity:
\gptbox{Both losses being smooth is sufficient, but not genuinely
necessary. What this proof needs for the test is $\eta^2(L_2 - L_1) \in
C_c^\infty$. [\ldots] I would not promote that into a necessity theorem.}
And on the scope of the normalized corollary:
\gptbox{(U) covers bounded measurable compactly supported $\varphi$. It
does not establish this normalized conclusion for arbitrary globally
bounded measurable tests. Partition-function agreement alone does not
control total variation in remote tails.}

\section{Lessons}

\begin{itemize}
\item For \code{CLAUDE.md}: a whnf timeout attributed to a whole
declaration is bisected fastest by inserting \code{sorry} at successive
tactic positions and re-checking; three runs located this one. And
compact-support facts built by \code{mul\_right} should be named with
their lambda type before use.
\item For the tide skill: when the consult of the previous tide names
a follow-up with a proof sketch, the next tide can go straight to a
statement-level consult; this one needed one round and no revision.
\item For the paper: the observable class of the closure theorems is now
$C_c^0$, indeed bounded measurable with compact support. The
normalized/unnormalized distinction of the note collapses, modulo
$o(t^{-\infty})$, on that class as soon as one common zero anchors the
gauge.
\end{itemize}

\section{Follow-ups}

\begin{itemize}
\item \emph{Local $L^\infty$.} Under smoothness, $\|\nabla(a - b)\| \le
Ct$ on a fixed ball, so $\sup_K|a - b| \le C_K\,(t^d\int_{K'}|a -
b|)^{1/(d+1)}$: local total variation beyond all orders implies local
uniform agreement beyond all orders. Needs a Lipschitz-peak
interpolation argument.
\item \emph{Smooth difference only.} Restate (V) with $L_1$ merely
continuous and $D = L_2 - L_1$ smooth.
\item \emph{$t$-dependent tests.} The compact-set form is uniform over
$|\phi_t| \le M$ supported in $K$; state it.
\item \emph{Paper.} Fold (U) into the closure theorem's statement as the
final observable class; one sentence on the AM--GM mechanism.
\end{itemize}
