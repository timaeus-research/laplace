\begin{abstract}
Mirror-shape follow-up to the kthkth-partition-asymptotic tide (also
21 May), explicitly named in that tide's Follow-ups. Lifts the V2
(2D moment) machinery from the
$(k_1, k_2) = (2, 3)$ quartic-sextic case to parametric $(k_1, k_2)$
with $k_1, k_2 \ge 1$. The four-step packaging
(factorisation $\to$ const-mul-rpow $\to$ rescaled tendsto $\to$
\texttt{IsEquivalent}) mirrors the partition-asymptotic tide
line-for-line; the only material difference is the 2D
factorisation step, which now uses
$\texttt{gibbsExpectation\_separable\_addSeparable}$ to factor the
mixed-even moment into a product of two 1D moments. Build clean
first-try; 155 lines.
\end{abstract}

\section{Setting}

The kthkth-partition tide (\texttt{tide/kthkth-partition-asymptotic},
landed earlier in the same day) lifted the Z2 partition asymptotic
from the quartic-sextic specialisation to parametric $(k_1, k_2)$.
Its retrospective named two strict-improvement follow-ups: the
generic numerator asymptotic and the generic moment asymptotic. This
tide closes the latter. The numerator analogue is left for a future
mechanical-extension session.

\section{The thing formalised}

For $k_1, k_2 \ge 1$, $j_1, j_2 : \mathbb{N}$, and $t > 0$, on the
2D separable monomial potential with the standard
even-monomial-potentials in each coordinate,
the mixed even moment satisfies
\[
  \bigl\langle x^{2j_1}\, y^{2j_2}\bigr\rangle_{2D, t}
    \;=\; C(k_1, k_2, j_1, j_2) \cdot t^{-j_1/k_1 - j_2/k_2}
\]
where $C(k_1, k_2, j_1, j_2)$ is the composite constant
\[
  (2k_1)!^{j_1/k_1} \cdot (2k_2)!^{j_2/k_2}
    \cdot \frac{\Gamma((2j_1+1)/(2k_1))}{\Gamma(1/(2k_1))}
    \cdot \frac{\Gamma((2j_2+1)/(2k_2))}{\Gamma(1/(2k_2))}.
\]

Plus the rescaled-\texttt{Tendsto} and
$\texttt{Asymptotics.IsEquivalent}$ packagings on top.

\section{Proof strategy}

Step 1 (factorisation): the 2D mixed-even moment factors as a
product of 1D moments via the separable-observable factorisation
lemma.\footnote{\texttt{Laplace.TwoD.gibbsExpectation\_separable\_addSeparable}.}
The four integrability premises specialise directly from
$\texttt{kth\_integrable\_pow\_pot}$ (the Boltzmann weight is the
$n = 0$ case).

Steps 2-4 mirror the partition-asymptotic tide. Substitute the 1D
moment closed form on each factor; peel off the
$t$-dependence;\footnote{Mathlib chain: \texttt{Real.div\_rpow},
\texttt{Real.rpow\_add}, \texttt{Real.rpow\_neg}.} lift to
$\texttt{Tendsto}$ via $\texttt{tendsto\_const\_nhds.congr'}$; lift
to $\texttt{IsEquivalent}$ via the standard
$\texttt{IsEquivalent.refl.congr\_left}$ pattern.

\section{Roadblocks and lessons}

No roadblocks. Build clean first-try, 155 lines. The mirror-shape
template from the partition-asymptotic tide carried over
unchanged.

\textbf{Lesson:} the partition $\to$ moment lift via
$\texttt{gibbsExpectation\_separable\_addSeparable}$ is reusable.
A future numerator-asymptotic tide will compose the same way
(separable factorisation + 1D numerator closed form).

\section{What was Mathlib, what was new}

Mathlib supplied the analytical primitives,\footnote{Lemmas:
\texttt{Real.div\_rpow}, \texttt{Real.rpow\_add},
\texttt{Real.rpow\_neg}, \texttt{Asymptotics.IsEquivalent.refl},
\texttt{tendsto\_const\_nhds.congr'},
\texttt{Filter.eventually\_gt\_atTop}.} and the seabed's previously
formalised facts supplied everything domain-specific.\footnote{1D
moment closed form: \texttt{gibbsExpectation\_kthPotential\_even}.
1D Boltzmann integrability: \texttt{kth\_integrable\_pow\_pot}.
2D separable-observable factorisation:
\texttt{gibbsExpectation\_separable\_addSeparable}.}

What was new: the
$\texttt{kthKthMomentConst}$ definition, the four packaged
theorems, and the parametric Boltzmann-integrability assembly.
