\begin{abstract}
A soundness review of \texttt{Laplace/OneD/AnharmonicFDTAsymptotic.lean}: the large-$t$ asymptote
$t\cdot\partial_h\mathrm{Cov}_h(x,x)|_0\to\alpha/\lambda^3$, obtained by composing the FDT identity
$\partial_h\mathrm{Cov}=-t\,\kappa_3$ with the third-cumulant asymptotic $t^2\kappa_3\to-\alpha/\lambda^3$.
Result: \textbf{a clean fidelity pass, no edits} --- the sign chain and limit value check out, matching the
HMC-validated $\partial_h\mathrm{Cov}\sim\alpha/(\lambda^3 t)$.
\end{abstract}

\section{Setting}
The FDT capstone proved the unconditional identity $\partial_h\mathrm{Cov}_h(x,x)|_0=-t\,\kappa_3(x,x,x)$ for
the anharmonic Gibbs measure and deferred its $t\to\infty$ asymptote. This file lands it by composing with the
reviewed third-cumulant asymptotic, recovering the closed-form value validated empirically (HMC) in the 1D
FDT-identity experiment.

\section{What was reviewed}
The single public theorem \texttt{gibbsCov\_deriv\_anharmonic\_asymptotic}.

\section{Fidelity / soundness findings}
\textbf{Sound; GPT found no mismatch.} The proof is a two-fact composition with careful sign-tracking:
\begin{itemize}[noitemsep]
  \item \textbf{Sign chain}: $t^2\kappa_3\to-\alpha/\lambda^3$ (\texttt{kappa3\_anharmonic\_id\_id\_id\_asymptotic});
    \texttt{.neg} gives $-(t^2\kappa_3)\to-(-\alpha/\lambda^3)$, and the local
    \texttt{heq}$:-(-\alpha/\lambda^3)=\alpha/\lambda^3$ delivers the limit $\alpha/\lambda^3$ (not
    $-\alpha/\lambda^3$).
  \item \textbf{Composition}: on $t>0$, the FDT identity's \texttt{.deriv} rewrites
    $\partial_h\mathrm{Cov}|_0=-t\,\kappa_3$, so $t\cdot\partial_h\mathrm{Cov}=t(-t\kappa_3)=-(t^2\kappa_3)$ ---
    exactly the function in the limit, so \texttt{.congr'} closes it.
  \item \textbf{Value and power}: $\alpha/\lambda^3$, the rescaled FDT asymptote $\partial_h\mathrm{Cov}\sim\alpha/(\lambda^3 t)$.
\end{itemize}
The only filter fact is eventual $t>0$; the $\lambda,\gamma>0$, $\alpha^2<3\lambda\gamma$ hypotheses are
consumed by the two reviewed dependencies.

\section{Lessons}
\begin{itemize}[noitemsep]
  \item \textbf{For an asymptote-composition file, the fidelity surface is the sign and the rescaling power.}
    The mathematical content is one \texttt{.neg} (does $-(-\alpha/\lambda^3)=\alpha/\lambda^3$?) and one
    multiplication ($t\cdot(-t\kappa_3)=-(t^2\kappa_3)$, matching the rescaled $t^2$ in the cumulant
    asymptotic). Both are one-line checks; the limit's \emph{sign} (here, the asymptote is $+\alpha/\lambda^3$
    because $\kappa_3$ itself $\to-\alpha/\lambda^3$) is the single thing most worth an independent eye.
\end{itemize}
