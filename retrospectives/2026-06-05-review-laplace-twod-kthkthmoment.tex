\begin{abstract}
A soundness review of \texttt{Laplace/TwoD/KthKthMomentAsymptotic.lean}: the generic-$(k_1,k_2)$
asymptotic of the 2D separable mixed-even Gibbs moment
$\langle x^{2j_1}y^{2j_2}\rangle_{2D}$, the third and final leg of the ``2D lift'' trio. Result:
\textbf{a clean fidelity pass with one dead-code removal} (the dead \texttt{open Real}). With this,
the trio --- partition, numerator, moment --- and their factorisation root are all reviewed.
\end{abstract}

\section{Setting}
This is the Gibbs-moment leg of the lift pattern: it factorises the 2D mixed moment as a product of
two 1D even moments (via
\reviewref[AddSeparable]{2026-06-05-review-laplace-twod-addseparable}'s
\texttt{gibbsExpectation\_separable\_addSeparable}), substitutes the 1D closed form
\texttt{gibbsExpectation\_kthPotential\_even}, and packages the limit. It is the moment quotient
of the two legs reviewed just before it
(\reviewref[partition]{2026-06-05-review-laplace-twod-kthkthpartition},
\reviewref[numerator]{2026-06-05-review-laplace-twod-kthkthnumerator}).

\section{What was reviewed}
Four public theorems and the composite constant
$C(k_1,k_2,j_1,j_2)=(2k_1)!^{j_1/k_1}(2k_2)!^{j_2/k_2}\,
\frac{\Gamma((2j_1+1)/(2k_1))}{\Gamma(1/(2k_1))}\frac{\Gamma((2j_2+1)/(2k_2))}{\Gamma(1/(2k_2))}$:
the factorisation $\langle\cdot\rangle_{2D}=\langle\cdot\rangle_U\langle\cdot\rangle_V$, the closed
form $C\cdot t^{-j_1/k_1-j_2/k_2}$, the rescaled limit $\to C$, and the asymptotic equivalence.

\section{Fidelity / soundness findings}
\textbf{Sound; no mismatch.} The constant is the product of the two 1D
\texttt{gibbsExpectation\_kthPotential\_even} constants, and the per-coordinate exponent $-j/k$ is
exactly the moment relation: numerator exponent $-(2j+1)/(2k)$ minus partition exponent $-1/(2k)$.
The factorisation discharges the four integrability obligations and both $Z\ne0$ conditions from the
1D infrastructure. Hypotheses sit correctly ($t>0$ exact, $k_i\ge1$ for the limit). Docstrings match
the statements --- like the numerator leg, and unlike the partition leg whose summary bullet had
mislabelled a theorem.

\section{Simplifications applied}
\textbf{One dead \texttt{open}.} \texttt{open Real} is dead (all \texttt{Real.*} qualified, bare
$t\,\hat{}\,(\text{real})$ is \texttt{rpow}); \texttt{MeasureTheory} (bare \texttt{Integrable}),
\texttt{Filter}, \texttt{Asymptotics}, scoped \texttt{Topology} stay. Module green at $2700$ jobs,
no warnings, signatures byte-identical. GPT affirmed.

\section{Disagreements and open questions}
None. The reviewer's other suggestions (collapse the $n=0$ integrability sub-proofs to
\texttt{simpa}; replace the inline \texttt{show \dots\ by ring} equality proofs inside \texttt{rw})
were declined as proof-style refactors, consistent with the two sibling legs.

\section{Lessons}
\begin{itemize}[noitemsep]
  \item \textbf{An end-to-end family review converges fast.} Reviewing the four files
    --- factorisation root, then partition, numerator, moment legs --- in dependency order made
    each fidelity check incremental: only the per-file constant and exponent were new, and the dead
    \texttt{open Real} was found in three of the three legs (the root used \texttt{open Real
    MeasureTheory} too, also trimmed). One genuine docstring drift surfaced across the four
    (the partition bullet), and the rebuild settled the one wrong simplification (the root's
    transitively-load-bearing \texttt{SemiDegenerate} import).
  \item \textbf{The moment exponent is a checkable arithmetic relation.} $-j/k =
    \big(-(2j+1)/(2k)\big)-\big(-1/(2k)\big)$ ties the moment leg's exponent to the numerator and
    partition legs already reviewed --- a free cross-check that the three constants are mutually
    consistent.
\end{itemize}
