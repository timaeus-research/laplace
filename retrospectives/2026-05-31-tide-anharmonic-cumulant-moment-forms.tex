\begin{abstract}
A polish tide closing the presentation of the anharmonic cumulant arc: the
third and fourth connected cumulants of $u=-(tx)$, proved earlier as rational
forms in the partition values $G_n(0)$, re-expressed in the physically
transparent unperturbed-Gibbs moment form,
\[
  \kappa_3 = \langle u^3\rangle_0 - 3\langle u^2\rangle_0\langle u\rangle_0 + 2\langle u\rangle_0^3,
\]
\[
  \kappa_4 = \langle u^4\rangle_0 - 4\langle u^3\rangle_0\langle u\rangle_0 - 3\langle u^2\rangle_0^2 + 12\langle u^2\rangle_0\langle u\rangle_0^2 - 6\langle u\rangle_0^4,
\]
with $\langle u^k\rangle_0 = \texttt{gibbsExp}\,\cdots\,(\cdot^k)$. New file
\texttt{AnharmonicCumulantMomentForms.lean}; builds clean, no
\texttt{sorry}/\texttt{axiom}/\texttt{native\_decide}.
\end{abstract}

\section{Setting}

The anharmonic cumulant arc proved $\kappa_2,\kappa_3,\kappa_4$ as derivatives of
the perturbed mean and identified them with $\partial^n\log Z$. The cumulant
\emph{values}, however, were stated as rational expressions in the bare
partition integrals $G_n(0)$ --- correct but opaque. This tide re-states
$\kappa_3,\kappa_4$ in the form a reader of the Susceptibility Primer expects:
combinations of the raw moments $\langle u^k\rangle_0$ under the unperturbed
Gibbs measure. ($\kappa_2$'s moment form,
\texttt{anharmonic\_susceptibility\_eq\_connected\_variance}, already existed.)
It was the lowest-risk available step: warm seabed, zero race, a definitional
re-expression of already-proved theorems.

\section{The thing formalised}

\begin{quote}\itshape
For the anharmonic potential, $\kappa_3$ and $\kappa_4$ (as the second and third
iterated derivatives of the mean $G_1/G_0$ at $0$) equal the moments-to-cumulants
combinations displayed above, with
$\langle u^k\rangle_0 = \texttt{gibbsExp}\,\mathrm{volume}\,L\,\mathrm{id}\,t\,0\,(x\mapsto(-(tx))^k)$.
\end{quote}

\section{Proof strategy}

A single helper does the work: moment-normalisation gives
$\langle u^k\rangle_0 = G_k(0)/G_0(0)$ for every $k$ (compose
\texttt{iteratedDeriv\_partition\_div\_eq\_gibbsExp} with the
\texttt{iteratedDeriv} identity). Rewriting every $\langle u^k\rangle_0$ on the
right-hand side to $G_k(0)/G_0(0)$, and the cumulant on the left to its proved
$G_n(0)$-rational form, leaves a pure scalar identity over the common
denominator $G_0(0)$, which \texttt{field\_simp} (clearing $G_0(0)\neq0$) closes.

\section{Roadblocks and resolutions}

Only one, trivial: after \texttt{field\_simp} the goal was already closed, so the
\texttt{ring} I had appended reported ``no goals''. \texttt{field\_simp} fully
normalises and discharges these moment identities on its own; the \texttt{ring}
was simply removed. (Contrast the $\kappa_4$ derivative tide, where
\texttt{field\_simp} did \emph{not} close and \texttt{ring} was load-bearing ---
the behaviour is identity-dependent.)

\section{Lessons}

\begin{itemize}[itemsep=3pt]
\item \textbf{State the headline in the reader's basis.} The $G_n(0)$-rational
  forms are what the proofs produce; the $\langle u^k\rangle_0$ forms are what a
  physicist reads. A one-helper moment-normalisation rewrite bridges them.
\item \textbf{Probe whether \texttt{field\_simp} closes before appending
  \texttt{ring}.} Its terminal behaviour is identity-dependent; a stray
  \texttt{ring} on an already-closed goal is an error, not a no-op.
\end{itemize}
