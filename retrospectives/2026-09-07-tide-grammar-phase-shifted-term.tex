\begin{abstract}
Unit 200 (programme~A, step~2). The $j$-th Taylor term of the phase factor $e^{\beta Nu^k\xi(u)}$
is $(\beta N)^j/j!\cdot\xi(u)^ju^{jk}$, which turns the dressed normal integral with exponents $h$
into one with exponents $h+jk$ and amplitude $\xi^j\eta$. We record that the shift
$(h,\lambda)\mapsto(h+jk,\lambda+j/2)$ leaves the entire face structure invariant --- the minimiser
set $J$, the multiplicity $m$, the face projection and the residual weight
$\prod_{i\notin J}u_i^{h_i-2k_i\lambda}$ --- and only replaces $\Gamma(\lambda)\beta^{-\lambda}$
by $\Gamma(\lambda+j/2)\beta^{-\lambda-j/2}$ in the face constant. The $j$-th term is then packaged
as a limit in the chart variable $N$ (monomial parameter $N^2$):
\[
\frac{N^j\int_{(0,1]^d}\eta(u)\,u^{h+jk}e^{-\beta N^2u^{2k}}\,du}{N^{-2\lambda}(\log N)^{m-1}}
\longrightarrow 2^{m-1}\,\mathrm{amplitudeCoeff}(h+jk,k,\lambda+j/2,\beta)(\eta),
\]
by the continuous-amplitude theorem of unit~186 and the square-parameter transport of unit~193.
Zero \texttt{sorry}/\texttt{axiom}; 9077 jobs.
\end{abstract}

\section{Mathematics}
$\mathrm{ratioExp}(h+jk,k,i)=\frac{h_i+jk_i+1}{2k_i}=\frac{h_i+1}{2k_i}+\frac j2$, so
$\mathrm{ratioExp}(h+jk,k,i)=\lambda+j/2\iff\mathrm{ratioExp}(h,k,i)=\lambda$: the minimisers, hence
$J$, $m$, the face projection and the indicator structure of every face object, are unchanged. The
residual exponents $h_i+jk_i-2k_i(\lambda+j/2)=h_i-2k_i\lambda$ are literally unchanged. The
bookkeeping $N^j\,(N^2)^{-(\lambda+j/2)}=N^{-2\lambda}$ and $(\log N^2)^{m-1}=2^{m-1}(\log N)^{m-1}$
gives the $2^{m-1}$; the $\beta^j/j!$ of the Taylor coefficient is left to the assembly unit.
