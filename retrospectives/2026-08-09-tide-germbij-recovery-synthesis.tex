\begin{abstract}
\noindent
Eight tides formalised the constructive side of the germbij note: not
merely that Laplace expansion data \emph{determines} the loss (the
identifiability arc), but how the data \emph{yields} it. For
even-monomial potentials in dimension one the thread certifies four
distinct recovery mechanisms --- rigidity of power laws, strict
monotonicity, the expansion pairing, and uniqueness of limits --- and
builds the complete expansion ladder behind them: every coefficient of
the quartic-perturbed Gaussian partition function, then of the general
even-monomial family, explicit with elementary remainders at every
order. The final tide crosses to the note's regular case, reading the
seabed's anharmonic susceptibility limits backwards to certify
Theorem~3.1's triangular induction at its first two rungs. This
document synthesises the arc for a reader who will not read eight
per-tide retrospectives.
\end{abstract}

\section{The mathematics}

The germbij note's Section~7.4 claims that in dimension one the
expansion data of $\int e^{-tL}$ recovers an even-monomial minimum
constructively: the leading exponent gives the degree $2k$, the leading
coefficient the scale, and the corrections at the graded orders
$t^{-(j(m-k)/k + 1/(2k))}$ give the subleading coefficients one by one.
Its Theorem~3.1 makes the regular-case analogue precise: at a
nondegenerate minimum the susceptibilities pair triangularly with the
Taylor coefficients. The thread certifies these claims in four
mechanisms, ordered by the strength of the data they consume:

\emph{Rigidity} (exact or asymptotic power-law data): two power
functions agreeing on a ray, or asymptotically equivalent at infinity,
share exponent and coefficient; hence partition-function data pins the
degree $k$ and scale $a$ of a pure even-monomial potential
(\texttt{Laplace.OneD.Recovery}).

\emph{Monotonicity} (one temperature): the partition function strictly
decreases under pointwise enlargement of the potential strict on
positive measure, so for a nonnegative perturbation the coefficient is
determined by $Z$ at a \emph{single} $t$
(\texttt{Laplace.OneD.RecoveryMonotone}).

\emph{The expansion pairing} (the note's mechanism): for
$x^2/2 + b\,x^4$, the $k = 0, 2$ Gaussian moments cancel the main and
correction terms exactly, so
$Z_b(t) = \sqrt{2\pi}(t^{-1/2} - 3b\,t^{-3/2}) + O(t^{-5/2})$ with an
explicit constant, and $b$ is read off the $t^{-3/2}$ correction by an
archimedean argument with no limits
(\texttt{Laplace.OneD.RecoveryExpansion}).

\emph{Uniqueness of limits} (susceptibility data): the seabed's Stage-2
anharmonic limits ($\partial_h\langle x\rangle|_0 \to -1/\lambda$,
$t\,\partial_h\mathrm{Cov}|_0 \to \alpha/\lambda^3$) read backwards pin
$\lambda$ then $\alpha$: Theorem~3.1's induction at orders two and
three (\texttt{Laplace.OneD.RecoveryAnharmonic}).

Behind the pairing sits the ladder: the exponential Taylor remainder
$|e^{-s} - \sum_{j<n}(-s)^j/j!| \leq s^n/n!$ for all $s \geq 0$ (an FTC
induction, no other calculus), the all-order quartic expansion with
coefficients $\sqrt{2\pi}\,(-b)^j(4j-1)!!/j!$ at $t^{-(j+1/2)}$, the
coefficient-limit packaging (each coefficient is the limit of the
rescaled truncation error), and the potential-generic version for
$x^{2k}/(2k)! + b\,x^{2m}$ with Gamma-form coefficients at exactly the
note's graded orders.\footnote{\texttt{Laplace.OneD.RecoveryAllOrder},
\texttt{RecoveryCoeffLimit}, \texttt{RecoveryGeneric}.}

\section{What made it work}

\emph{The seabed carried the analysis.} Every closed form the thread
consumed --- the scale-$t$ Gaussian moments, the Gamma-form monomial
moments, the monomial integrability family, the Stage-2 anharmonic
limits --- was built in the May tides for the primer programme. The
thread's own analytic content is one FTC induction and one elementary
exponential estimate; everything else is reading existing results in
the inverse direction. The productive act in the later tides was the
seabed survey, not the proof.

\emph{Exact cancellation over approximate bookkeeping.} At every order
of every ladder, the moment closed forms cancel the expansion terms
exactly, so the truncation error \emph{is} the integrated remainder.
No estimate is ever made of a quantity that has a closed form.

\emph{Elementary bounds over sharp ones.} The $s^2$ remainder constant
(double the sharp $s^2/2$) removed all calculus from the first-order
tide; the alternating-series route and the Taylor API were both
rejected in deliberation for the FTC induction, which uses only stable
ingredients. Numerical checks confirmed each constant with the expected
slack before any Lean was written --- and one check caught invented
values that had been written before the script ran, the arc's one
process failure, now documented in the offending tide's log.

\emph{Deliberation amortises.} Eight tides used five consult rounds:
two candidates were executed on inherited deliberations (the deferred
coefficient-limit packaging; the mirror-shaped generic ladder), and the
final tide was a first-attempt-clean build because the
uniqueness-of-limits pattern had stabilised three tides earlier.

\section{Recurring friction}

All build iterations in the arc were elaboration-level; the mathematics
never moved after deliberation. The catalogue grew three entries, all
of one family: combinator-built hypotheses (\texttt{Pi.add} integrals,
\texttt{Pi.div} ratios, \texttt{Pi}-form derivative sums) and
mixed-cast spellings ($\uparrow\!(n{+}1)$ versus $\uparrow\!n + 1$)
fail to match goals syntactically, and the uniform repair is an
explicitly-typed or \texttt{funext} bridge to the single-lambda,
single-cast normal form. \texttt{positivity} needs $(x^2)^m$ rather
than $x^{2m}$ for symbolic even powers; \texttt{rpow} exponent
arithmetic wants explicit \texttt{show ... by ring} chains, never bare
\texttt{simp}. The retrospective compile gate (zero errors, zero
overfull boxes, checked \emph{before} commit) caught defects in four of
the eight tides.

\section{What remains}

The thread closed its recorded follow-ups. The natural continuations
are commissions rather than continuations: the $\gamma$ rung of
Theorem~3.1 needs second-order anharmonic moment expansions (a Stage-2
extension); the full triangular induction needs them at every order;
the multivariate Section~7.4 (quasi-homogeneous weight recovery) needs
a multivariate moment layer; and the note's Proposition~4.1 (the flat
counterexample) needs expansion theory for smooth non-analytic
perturbations. Each is a programme; none is a tide.
