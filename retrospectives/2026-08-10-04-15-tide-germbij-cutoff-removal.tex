\begin{abstract}
The third and final perimeter tide from the germbij fidelity
review\footnote{\reviewref{2026-08-10-01-29-review-germbij-new-content};
predecessors: \tideref[the $k=2$
bridge]{2026-08-10-02-25-tide-germbij-hessian-bridge} and \tideref[the
base-case discharge]{2026-08-10-03-05-tide-germbij-hbase-discharge}.} closes
finding F3: the note's Theorem~3.1 premise quantifies over observables
$\varphi \in C_c^\infty(U)$, while the merged recovery machinery consumed
bare monomial moment families, and the implication between the two was only
semantic. It is now a theorem. The mechanism is the loss gap: with $\chi$ a
smooth bump equal to $1$ near the minimum, the un-cut part of any
polynomial-growth moment sits where $L - L(0) \geq c r^2$, so it is
$O(e^{-\delta t})$ relative to the partition function --- smaller than every
power of the temperature. Consequently superPoly agreement on smooth
compactly supported tests in the common localization region implies
superPoly agreement for every smooth polynomial-growth observable
(\texttt{superPoly\_moment\_of\_ccData}), and composing with the previous
tide's base-case-free headline yields
\texttt{smooth\_positive\_jet\_recovery\_of\_ccData} and
\texttt{analytic\_germ\_recovery\_of\_ccData}: germbij Theorem~3.1 and
Corollary~3.2, inverse directions, from the note's own kind of data. With
this, all three review findings are closed.
\end{abstract}

\section{Setting}

The fidelity review's finding F3 was the quiet one: not a wrong statement,
but a missing lemma. The note's $\Phi_L$ is defined on $C_c^\infty(U)$; the
Lean data lived on bare monomials against the $U$-restricted Gibbs measure.
The gap ``the difference is exponentially small by the loss gap'' was
asserted in the review and believed by everyone --- and was exactly the kind
of believed-but-unformalised step this perimeter arc exists to eliminate.
The deliberation settled three design points: the tail estimate splits the
rescaled exponent as $G \geq \tfrac12 c\|x\|^2 + \tfrac12 c r^2/q^2$ (both
lower bounds hold on the tail support, so their average does); linearity
happens at the \emph{rescaled} level, where the merged integrability
certificates already live; and the data premise quantifies over tests
supported in the \emph{common} localization region of the two packages ---
faithful to the note (which has one $U$) without pretending the two Lean
packages share theirs.

\section{The thing formalised}

Three layers. First, a small missing vocabulary item: an eventual bound
$|f(t)| \leq K e^{-\delta t}$ with $\delta > 0$ makes $f$ superpolynomially
small (\texttt{superPoly\_of\_eventually\_abs\_le\_exp}) --- the
quantitative form of the note's ``exponentially small terms lie below every
element of the scale''. Second, the tail lemma
(\texttt{posteriorMoment\_cutoff\_tail}): for continuous polynomial-growth
$P$ and continuous $0 \leq \chi \leq 1$ with $\chi \equiv 1$ on a ball of
radius $r$ around the minimum,
\[
t \;\longmapsto\; \langle P \rangle_t - \langle P\chi \rangle_t
\quad\text{is smaller than every power of } t^{-1}.
\]
Third, the bridge and the composed corollaries: if the moment families of
two localized losses agree beyond all orders on every smooth compactly
supported test in the common localization region, then they agree on every
smooth polynomial-growth observable; feeding the second-moment and monomial
instances into the base-case-free headline gives positive-order jet equality
and, for analytic losses, germ equality modulo the additive constant --- the
note-literal inverse of Theorem~3.1, with no base-case assumptions, no
shared package matrix, and the note's own observable class.

\section{Proof strategy}

The tail estimate works at the rescaled level, where the seabed keeps its
integrability. At scale $q$ the moment difference is a quotient of rescaled
integrals with the dilation prefactors cancelled exactly
(\texttt{posteriorMoment\_eq\_integrand\_div}). The numerator's integrand is
supported where $q \cdot x \in U$ and $\|q \cdot x\| \geq r$ (inside the
bump's plateau it vanishes); there the coercivity field gives both
$G \geq c\|x\|^2$ and $G \geq c r^2/q^2$ for the rescaled exponent $G$, so
\[
e^{-G} \;\leq\; e^{-\frac{c r^2}{2q^2}}\, e^{-\frac{c}{2}\|x\|^2},
\]
and with $|P(q \cdot x)| \leq C(1 + \|x\|^n)$ for $q \leq 1$ the whole
numerator is dominated by $C M e^{-\frac{c r^2}{2q^2}}$ with $M$ a fixed
polynomial-Gaussian integral. The denominator tends to the positive Gaussian
mass (the merged \texttt{tendsto\_integral\_rescaled\_one}), so it is
eventually at least half of it, and the quotient bound survives the
$t = q^{-2}$ substitution as $K e^{-\delta t}$. The bridge picks a
\texttt{ContDiffBump} with plateau $\rho/2$ and support $3\rho/4$ inside
both packages' balls ($\rho$ the smaller of the two radii), splits
$m_A(P) - m_B(P)$ into two tails and one data term at $\varphi = P\chi$
(smooth times smooth, compactly supported, supported in both $U$'s), and
closes with superPoly additivity. The corollaries instantiate at
$w_iw_j$ and \texttt{monomialTest} --- coordinate projections are continuous
linear maps, so smoothness is \texttt{contDiff\_prod} over them.

\section{Roadblocks and resolutions}

All Lean-mechanical, in three families. The catalogued un-beta-reduced-lambda
class struck twice more (after \texttt{integral\_const\_mul} and inside the
indicator case split); \texttt{beta\_reduce} and \texttt{show} dispatch it.
\texttt{set}-bound locals are opaque to \texttt{positivity} (the bump
constructor's side conditions needed \texttt{linarith} against the recorded
positivity fact instead). And two elaboration-order items: the exponential
constant $K$ of the superPoly source must be passed explicitly at
\texttt{refine} time (it is not determined until the eventual bound is
supplied), and a chained
$\mathrm{closedBall} \subseteq \mathrm{ball} \subseteq \mathrm{ball}$
inclusion left its middle radius unsynthesizable --- one direct
\texttt{closedBall\_subset\_ball} step with the end radius was both simpler
and elaborable. Four build iterations to green.

\section{What was Mathlib, what was new}

Mathlib lookup: the \texttt{ContDiffBump} API (plateau, support, compact
support, smoothness; the inner-product-space existence instance),
\texttt{tendsto\_pow\_mul\_exp\_neg\_atTop\_nhds\_zero},
\texttt{norm\_integral\_le\_of\_norm\_le}, \texttt{contDiff\_prod},
\texttt{EuclideanSpace.proj}, \texttt{tsupport}/\texttt{support} plumbing.
Seabed reuse: the coercivity field at $q = 1$, the dilation identity, the
rescaled integrability certificate, the rescaled partition limit, and the
previous tides' freed headline. New: the exponential-to-superPoly packaging,
the tail estimate, the cutoff bridge, and the note-literal corollaries. The
superPoly \texttt{add}/\texttt{sub} helpers and
\texttt{HasPolynomialGrowth.comp\_smul} are small general-purpose additions
the seabed had been implicitly re-deriving.

\section{Lessons}

The review-to-tide pipeline closed a full loop here: a fidelity finding of
the form ``this implication is believed but not formalised'' converts
directly into a tide whose statement is the finding. Worth keeping: the
finding's own phrasing (``the difference is $O(e^{-\delta t})$ by the loss
gap, hence superPoly-invisible'') was already the correct proof outline, and
the deliberation only had to pin the split constant and the linearity
altitude. Also catalogued for \texttt{CLAUDE.md}: \texttt{positivity} does
not see through \texttt{set}, and implicit constants of eventual-bound
lemmas need explicit instantiation under \texttt{refine}.
