\begin{abstract}
A soundness review of \texttt{Laplace/OneD/AnharmonicPartitionDerivGeneralH.lean}: the file that
defines the weighted partition family $G_n(h)=\int(-(tx))^n e^{-t(L+hx)}$ and proves the
moment-ladder identity $\mathrm{iteratedDeriv}\,k\,(G_0)=G_k$ on $|h|<1$, with the $Z''(0),Z'''(0)$
corollaries. Result: \textbf{a clean fidelity pass with no edits.} The general-$h$
dominated-differentiation step and the induction are correct, and both \texttt{open}s are
load-bearing.
\end{abstract}

\section{Setting}
This is the keystone of the anharmonic cumulant machinery: it upgrades the $h=0$ derivative step of
\reviewref[AnharmonicPartitionHigherDeriv]{2026-06-05-review-laplace-oned-anharmonicpartitionhigherderiv}
to a general-$h$ statement, then promotes the single derivative to the full iterated-derivative
identity $\mathrm{iteratedDeriv}\,k\,Z=G_k$ --- which is what makes the cumulant files
(\reviewref[AnharmonicFourthCumulant]{2026-06-05-review-laplace-oned-anharmonicfourthcumulant})
genuinely about derivatives of $Z$, not just of integral expressions that happen to coincide.

\section{What was reviewed}
The definition \texttt{weightedPartition} and eight declarations: the bare-exponential domination,
integrand integrability, \texttt{weightedPartition\_hasDerivAt} ($G_n'=G_{n+1}$ at general
$|h|<1$), \texttt{weightedPartition\_deriv}, the induction
\texttt{iteratedDeriv\_weightedPartition\_zero} ($\mathrm{iteratedDeriv}\,k\,(G_0)=G_k$), and the
$Z''(0)=\int(tx)^2 e^{-tL}$, $Z'''(0)=-\!\int(tx)^3 e^{-tL}$ corollaries.

\section{Fidelity / soundness findings}
\textbf{Sound; checked end to end.} The definition is $G_n$. The general-$h$ step is the right
neighbourhood upgrade of the $h=0$ argument: coercivity data, \texttt{ball 0 1}$\in\mathcal N\,h_0$,
AE-measurability of the family and the derivative, an integrable majorant, the uniform derivative
bound on the ball, pointwise \texttt{HasDerivAt}, and
\texttt{hasDerivAt\_integral\_of\_dominated\_loc\_of\_deriv\_le}. The induction is the standard
germ-locality move: $\mathrm{iteratedDeriv}(k+1)(G_0)=\mathrm{deriv}(\mathrm{iteratedDeriv}\,k\,(G_0))
=\mathrm{deriv}(G_k)=G_{k+1}$, the middle step via $=_{\!\!\mathfrak n}[\mathcal N h]$ from the
induction hypothesis on the ball. $Z''(0)=G_2(0)=\int(tx)^2$, $Z'''(0)=G_3(0)=-\!\int(tx)^3$.
The reviewer confirmed all five points. Crucially, because the file proves
$\mathrm{iteratedDeriv}\,k\,Z=G_k$, the corollary titles ``second/third $h$-derivative of $Z$'' are
fully warranted here --- closing the report-only caveat noted on the $h=0$ sibling.

\section{Simplifications applied}
\textbf{None --- the bucket was empty.} \texttt{open MeasureTheory} (bare $\int$ and measure API)
and \texttt{open Topology} (bare $\mathcal N$ and $=_{\!\!\mathfrak n}$) are both load-bearing;
there is no \texttt{open Real}; the single import is used; and the dense

None.

\section{Lessons}
\begin{itemize}[noitemsep]
  \item \textbf{Proving the iterated-derivative identity is what licenses the ``$Z^{(k)}$'' prose.}
    The $h=0$ sibling proved derivatives of integral \emph{expressions} and had to hedge its
    ``$Z''$'' wording; this file proves $\mathrm{iteratedDeriv}\,k\,Z=G_k$ outright, so the same
    wording is exact. When a docstring says ``the $k$-th derivative of $Z$'', the licensing fact is
    precisely an in-scope \texttt{iteratedDeriv} identity --- check for it before flagging the prose
    either way.
  \item \textbf{Two integral-side files in a row, both \texttt{MeasureTheory}-load-bearing.} The
    measure-vs-algebra split continues to hold per-file: the differentiation-under-the-integral base
    and this general-$h$/iterated promotion both carry bare $\int$, in contrast to the pure cumulant
    algebra above them.
\end{itemize}
