\begin{abstract}
The inverse-direction programme's one-theorem form. From the note's
prose data --- two globally smooth losses, analytic at their unknown
minima, vanishing recentred gradients, diagonal-matched
positive-definite matrices, and one physical data premise (superPoly
agreement of the localized Gibbs moment families, over every pair of
ball localizations, on smooth compactly supported tests in a common
region containing balls around both minima) --- the theorem concludes
that the minima coincide and the germs agree there modulo the
additive constant. Every package hypothesis of the located capstone
is discharged internally by the constructors of the two preceding
tides. The single fix round taught a lesson worth keeping: a
\code{have}-bound constructor is opaque, so its postconditions
cannot be recovered by \code{rfl} --- consume them through the
structure's own fields (here, the region \emph{is} the ball of
radius \code{delta} by construction, and positivity is the
\code{delta\_pos} field), not by re-deriving the internal choice
terms.
\end{abstract}

\section{Setting}

With the radial Taylor bound merged, the chain from prose to
conclusion existed but took two compositions at every call site.
This tide packages it, choosing the radius-quantified form of the
data premise --- ``for every pair of ball localizations'' --- which
is the ball specialization of the common-region option the
grand-composition consult recommended, and which instantiates
cleanly at the constructed packages' radii.

\section{Where progress stalled}

One round. Binding the families with \code{have} made them opaque,
and the planned \code{rfl} extraction of the region failed with
metavariable noise; re-deriving the constructor's internal
\code{choose} chains by hand would have been both fragile and
futile (classical choice terms are opaque to definitional
reduction even when spelled identically). The repair --- inline
applications plus field access --- is the general pattern:
constructor postconditions that callers need should be read off
structure fields.
