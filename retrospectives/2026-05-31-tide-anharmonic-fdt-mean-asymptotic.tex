\begin{abstract}
The companion to the cross-susceptibility asymptote, completing both $t\to\infty$
limits the FDT-capstone tide deferred: the first-cumulant fluctuation--%
dissipation limit
\[
  \frac{\partial}{\partial h}\Big|_0\langle x\rangle_h = -t\,\mathrm{Var}_0(x)
    \;\longrightarrow\; -\frac{1}{\lambda}.
\]
The FDT identity is phrased with the abstract \texttt{Threepoint} covariance
while the asymptotic input uses the concrete \texttt{Laplace} covariance; the
tide also lands the small definitional bridge between them. New file
\texttt{AnharmonicFDTMeanAsymptotic.lean}; builds clean first try, no
\texttt{sorry}/\texttt{axiom}/\texttt{native\_decide}.
\end{abstract}

\section{Setting}

The cross-susceptibility asymptote tide\footnote{\tideref{2026-05-31-tide-anharmonic-fdt-asymptotic}.}
closed one of the two limits deferred by the FDT capstone, the one that
composed cleanly because both factors used \texttt{Threepoint.kappa3}. The
\emph{mean} limit did not: the FDT identity gives
$\partial_h\langle x\rangle_h|_0 = -t\cdot\texttt{Threepoint.gibbsCov}\cdots0$,
but the available asymptotic, \texttt{cov\_self\_anharmonic\_asymptotic}, is
stated for \texttt{Laplace.gibbsCov}. This tide supplies the missing
definitional bridge and then closes the limit.

\section{The thing formalised}

\begin{quote}\itshape
For the anharmonic potential, $\deriv\big(h\mapsto\langle x\rangle_h\big)(0)\to
-1/\lambda$ as $t\to\infty$; and, as reusable infrastructure, the abstract
\texttt{Threepoint} Gibbs expectation/covariance at $h=0$ (with $A=\mathrm{id}$,
against Lebesgue) equals the concrete \texttt{Laplace} one.
\end{quote}

\section{Proof strategy}

\emph{Bridge.} \texttt{Threepoint.gibbsExp} at $h=0$ unfolds to
$(\int\varphi\,e^{-t(L+0\cdot x)})/(\int e^{-t(L+0\cdot x)})$ and
\texttt{Laplace.gibbsExpectation} to $(\int\varphi\,e^{-tL})/\texttt{partitionFunction}$;
\texttt{simp [zero\_mul, add\_zero]} collapses the $0\cdot x$ and the two
coincide. The covariance bridge is then three rewrites of the expectation
bridge. \emph{Limit.} As in the cross-susceptibility tide:
\texttt{cov\_self\_anharmonic\_asymptotic} gives $t\cdot\mathrm{Var}\to1/\lambda$,
so its negation $-(t\cdot\mathrm{Var})\to-1/\lambda$; on $t>0$ the FDT identity
(after the covariance bridge) rewrites $\deriv(\langle x\rangle_h)(0)$ to
$-t\cdot\texttt{Laplace.gibbsCov}$, and \texttt{Tendsto.congr'} transfers the
limit.

\section{Roadblocks and resolutions}

None --- first-try build. The only subtlety was anticipated: the FDT identity
and the asymptotic live in different covariance namespaces, so the bridge had
to be proved first. It is a pure \texttt{simp [zero\_mul, add\_zero]} after
unfolding, because the two definitions agree once the perturbation term
vanishes at $h=0$.

\section{What was Mathlib, what was new}

\textbf{Mathlib.} The \texttt{Tendsto} combinators and atTop
helper\footnote{\texttt{Filter.Tendsto.neg}, \texttt{Filter.Tendsto.congr'},
\texttt{eventually\_gt\_atTop}.}, plus \texttt{HasDerivAt.deriv}, \texttt{simp},
\texttt{ring}.

\textbf{Seabed (reused).} The unconditional first-cumulant FDT identity and the
covariance asymptotic.\footnote{\texttt{cov\_self\_anharmonic\_asymptotic}.}

\textbf{New.} The \texttt{Threepoint}-to-\texttt{Laplace} expectation/covariance
bridge (reusable) and the mean asymptote. About 50 lines.

\section{Lessons}

\begin{itemize}[itemsep=3pt]
\item \textbf{A namespace bridge is often the whole tide.} Two formalisations of
  ``the same'' object (here the unperturbed Gibbs covariance) can block a clean
  composition; the bridge is a short \texttt{unfold} + \texttt{simp} once the
  defining expressions are made to coincide.
\item \textbf{The bridge is reusable.} Any future result that needs the abstract
  \texttt{Threepoint} machinery's $h=0$ specialisation to meet the concrete
  \texttt{Laplace} asymptotics can now cite it.
\end{itemize}
