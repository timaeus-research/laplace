\begin{abstract}
A soundness review of \texttt{Laplace/TwoD/QuarticSexticOddMoments.lean}: the two
parity-completion theorems stating that a mixed quartic--sextic 2D moment vanishes when at least one
exponent is odd. Result: \textbf{a clean fidelity pass with no edits.} With this file, the entire 2D
separable-lift family --- the \texttt{AddSeparable} root, the generic kthkth trio and odd companion,
and the $(2,3)$ \texttt{QuarticSextic*} specialisations and cores --- is reviewed end-to-end.
\end{abstract}

\section{Setting}
The $(2,3)$ analogue of the generic
\reviewref[KthKthOddMoments]{2026-06-05-review-laplace-twod-kthkthoddmoments}, and the odd-moment
companion to the \reviewref[QuarticSextic core]{2026-06-05-review-laplace-twod-quarticsextic}. It is
the last unreviewed file of the \texttt{TwoD/} lift family.

\section{What was reviewed}
Two theorems: \texttt{gibbsExpectation\_quarticSextic\_odd\_pow\_pow\_eq\_zero}
($\langle x^{2j+1}y^n\rangle=0$ for any $n$) and
\texttt{gibbsExpectation\_quarticSextic\_pow\_odd\_pow\_eq\_zero}
($\langle x^m y^{2k+1}\rangle=0$ for any $m$). Each factorises via the core's
\texttt{gibbsExpectation\_quarticSextic\_factor} and kills the odd 1D factor with
\texttt{quartic\_expected\_value\_odd} (resp.\ \texttt{sextic\_expected\_value\_odd}); the product
is zero by \texttt{zero\_mul}/\texttt{mul\_zero}.

\section{Fidelity / soundness findings}
\textbf{Sound; no mismatch.} As in the generic companion, the non-odd factor needs only
integrability --- supplied by \texttt{quartic\_integrable\_pow\_pot}/%
\texttt{sextic\_integrable\_pow\_pot} for every natural exponent --- so each theorem holds for
arbitrary $n$ (resp.\ $m$), and the two cover every ``at least one odd'' case including odd--odd.
The docstrings are accurate.

\section{Simplifications applied}
\textbf{None --- the bucket was empty.} The file has no \texttt{open} statements, its single import
is load-bearing, and the closing \texttt{simp} does real work (\texttt{zero\_mul}/\texttt{mul\_zero}).
The reviewer's only suggestion --- merging the \texttt{rw} and \texttt{simp} into one \texttt{simpa}
--- is cosmetic proof-golf, declined. No worktree was created.

\section{Disagreements and open questions}
None.

\section{Lessons}
\begin{itemize}[noitemsep]
  \item \textbf{The 2D lift family is internally consistent end-to-end.} Reviewing the root, the
    generic trio$+$odd companion, and the $(2,3)$ specialisations and cores in dependency order
    surfaced exactly one genuine fidelity drift (the partition-asymptotic doc bullet naming the
    wrong theorem), a scatter of dead \texttt{open}s (Real always, MeasureTheory only in the
    def-stated files), two ill-typed/mislabelled docstrings, and three clean empty-bucket passes ---
    and no mathematical error anywhere. The closed-form constants all trace back to the 1D
    \texttt{quartic\_partition}/\texttt{sextic\_partition} and \texttt{*\_expected\_value\_*} lemmas.
\end{itemize}
