\begin{abstract}
A fidelity-focused Tide-review of the eleven files that landed in the germbij
arc of the \texttt{laplace} seabed on 2026-08-09/10 (PRs \#93--\#103): the
three-file completion of the inverse half of the note's Theorem~3.1 in the
note's own ``same asymptotics'' data language, and the eight landed stages of
the forward-expansion programme~A. The reviewer (GPT-5.6 Sol, two consults)
was given the note's TeX, the upstream Lean definitions, and the files. The
\emph{forward arc came back clean}: all eight targeted checks --- exponent-split
indexing, the graded-exponential truncation, the growth exponents $j+2N$ and
$(N{+}2)(N{+}1)$, the unrestricted exponential remainder, the $\lambda/4$
Gaussian budget, the $T_2$/qform bridge, and the two truncation conventions ---
verified consistent, independently agreeing with the session's own hand
checks. The \emph{inverse arc} produced four fidelity findings, none a false
statement, all of the form ``the Lean statement is weaker than, or the
docstring broader than, the note's claim'': the headline assumes the matched
$0$--$2$ jet (and a shared Hessian matrix) that the note derives; equality of
the additive constant the note declares lost is assumed at $j=0$; the
$C_c^\infty$-observable premise is bridged to bare-monomial data only
semantically, not by a lemma; and one module docstring claims a
translated-integral identity that was dropped during its tide. All four are
escalated to the human; per the review discipline (and two forward tides in
flight on these files) nothing was changed --- the sixteen proof-maintenance
simplifications GPT suggested are recorded as follow-ups.
\end{abstract}

\section{Setting}

The germbij note asks when the map $\Phi_L$, sending a loss to the asymptotic
expansions of its localized posterior expectations $\langle\phi\rangle_t$ over
all observables, is injective, and its Theorem~3.1 answers the nondegenerate
case: $\Phi_L$ determines, and is determined by, the pair $(w^*, \{D^k
L(w^*)\}_{k\ge2})$. On 2026-08-09 a full audit of the merged corpus against
the note found the recovery theorems consuming $o(q^{k-2})$ moment-\emph{rate}
hypotheses where the note's premise is equality of expansion \emph{families}
(differences $o(t^{-\infty})$), and identified the missing pieces; the
resulting programme landed as PRs \#93--\#95 (items B, D, C: the expansion
bridge, location recovery, the analytic germ corollary --- ``the inverse half
of Theorem 3.1 is complete'') and opened the six-stage forward programme~A,
of which stages 1 through 5c-i (PRs \#96--\#103) had landed by the time of
this review. The user asked for a review of exactly this new content,
focusing on fidelity of the formalised statements to the text. Eleven files
at \texttt{laplace} commit \texttt{b7566bb}; this is the widest-scoped review
tide so far, run as one review with the independent consult split per arc.

\section{Fidelity findings}

The forward arc returned \textbf{no fidelity findings}: every targeted check
came back consistent, with the reviewer re-deriving the indexing ($V_{s+1} =
T_{s+3}$ carrying weight $q^{s+1} = q^{k-2}$, degrees $3,\dots,N{+}2$
exactly), the truncation argument (the exponent polynomial has zero constant
term, so outer-exponential terms $i\ge N{+}1$ touch only coefficients beyond
$N$), the budget arithmetic ($-T_2 + |S| + |R| \le -(\lambda/2 - \lambda/8 -
\lambda/8)\|z\|^2 = -(\lambda/4)\|z\|^2$), and the reconciliation of the two
Taylor-truncation conventions through \texttt{remConst}. The session's
independent hand checks, done before the consult returned, agreed on every
point --- a genuinely convergent double verification.

The inverse arc produced four findings. None says a proved statement is
false; all four say the formalisation is \emph{narrower} than the prose
around it.

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2em]
\item \textbf{The matched base case (largest).} The headline
theorem\footnote{\texttt{smooth\_jet\_recovery\_of\_superPoly\_moments},
\texttt{ExpansionBridge.lean:192}.} takes both losses in
\texttt{HigherLaplaceDomain} packages sharing the \emph{same} matrix $H$, and
assumes \texttt{hbase}: equality of iterated derivatives at orders $0,1,2$.
The note's Theorem~3.1 \emph{derives} the Hessian from the data ($t\,
\Cov_t[w_i,w_j] \to (H^{-1})_{ij}$). That Hessian step exists in the merged
corpus in rate language (\texttt{hessian\_recovery}, via the covariance
limit \texttt{tendsto\_normalized\_\allowbreak covariance}) but is not
composed into the superPoly-language
headline: there is no $k=2$ analogue of the expansion bridge for covariance
data. So the theorem formalises the higher-order induction \emph{conditional
on} an already-matched $2$-jet, and the ``inverse half complete'' gloss (and
the note's answer-(i) \verb|\leanref|) reads broader than this one statement
warrants without its uncomposed companions.
\item \textbf{The assumed constant.} \texttt{hbase} at $j=0$ assumes
$L_1(0)=L_2(0)$ --- exactly the additive constant the note proves is
\emph{lost} ($\Phi_{L+c}=\Phi_L$). A pair $L_2 = L_1 + c$ has identical
normalized moment data yet is excluded by hypothesis, so the composed
corollary is strictly weaker than Corollary~3.2's inverse direction. The
analytic step's \emph{conclusion} ($L_1 y - L_1 0 = L_2 y - L_2 0$ near $0$)
is correctly phrased modulo constants; the overreach is confined to the
hypotheses, and a shift-normalization wrapper would remove it.
\item \textbf{The observable class.} The note's premise quantifies over
$\phi \in C_c^\infty(U)$; the Lean data is bare monomials and coordinates
against the $U$-restricted Gibbs measure. Semantically a faithful weakening:
the difference to any admissible $w^\alpha\chi(w)$ is supported away from the
minimum and is $O(e^{-\delta t})$ by the loss gap, hence invisible beyond all
orders. But that cutoff-removal implication is \emph{not formalised}, so the
headline cannot yet be applied to the note's literal premise without an
additional lemma.
\item \textbf{A docstring overclaim.} \texttt{LocationRecovery.lean}'s module
docstring says the located-moment/translated-loss identity is ``recorded via
translation invariance of Lebesgue measure''. No such lemma is in the file;
the tide log records it was dropped mid-tide (a \texttt{PiLp}
instance-synthesis failure) as non-load-bearing. \texttt{locatedMoment} is a
faithful \emph{model} by construction --- the reviewer confirmed the
change-of-variables reading --- but the docstring describes a theorem that
does not exist.
\end{enumerate}

Worth recording as explicit non-findings: \texttt{SuperPoly} is equivalent to
the note's $o(t^{-\infty})$ convention; the junk values of
\texttt{posteriorMomentT} at $t\le0$ are harmless under \texttt{atTop}
filters; monomial-only test data is justified by the upstream polarization
theorems; the \texttt{IsSymm} hypotheses are implied by the packages' own
\texttt{ContDiff} fields (formalization debt, not a wrong assumption); and
\texttt{AnalyticAt} at the minimum is correctly weaker than ``analytic on
$U$''.

\gptbox{They do not yet constitute the full inverse half of Theorem 3.1 and
Corollary 3.2 from the note's literal premises, principally because: Hessian
equality is assumed rather than recovered; equality of the lost additive
constant is assumed; compactly supported $\Phi_L$-data have not formally been
bridged to bare monomial/coordinate moments; translated physical posterior
integrals have not formally been identified with \texttt{locatedMoment}.}

\section{Simplifications applied}

\textbf{None --- deliberately.} The user scoped this review to fidelity, and
two forward tides (the 5c-ii numerator and packaging stages) were in flight
importing these files at review time; proof-body churn would have invited
rebuild and merge races for zero statement-level gain. The sixteen
suggestions from the two consults --- all proof-maintenance: dead code,
duplicated ray-path and mesoscopic-scale derivations, a posterior-linearity
helper kit, a \texttt{SuperPoly}$\to$\texttt{Tendsto} helper, internal
derivation of the symmetry hypotheses, splitting the window-majorant
telescope into private lemmas --- are recorded in the review log for a
dedicated simplification pass once programme~A lands. The signature
fingerprint of all eleven files is unchanged (no edits), and the build was
not touched.

\section{Disagreements and open questions}

No Claude/GPT disagreements: the consult's four findings coincide with the
four candidate findings recorded in the review log \emph{before} the response
arrived, and its clean forward verdict matched the hand checks. What is
escalated to the human is the findings themselves, with a suggested reading:

\begin{itemize}[nosep]
\item Findings 1--3 are most naturally read not as errors but as the
\emph{remaining perimeter} of the inverse half: a $k=2$ superPoly bridge for
covariance data (composing \texttt{hessian\_recovery} into the headline), a
shift-normalization wrapper discharging the $j=0$ assumption, and a
cutoff-removal lemma connecting $C_c^\infty$ data to the monomial data. Each
looks like one small forward tide; together they would let the three
\verb|\leanref| anchors on answer~(i) carry their weight literally.
\item Finding 4 is a one-line docstring fix (or the dropped lemma, if the
\texttt{PiLp} instance issue is worth revisiting).
\item Until then, the honest gloss of the landed inverse half is: \emph{given
two localized nondegenerate losses with matched value, gradient and Hessian
at the minimum, superpolynomially-equal monomial moment families force equal
jets at all orders, and equal germs modulo the constant in the analytic
case} --- with location recovery as a separate, unconditional statement.
\end{itemize}

\section{Lessons}

The audit consult that scoped PRs \#93--\#95 flagged its own caveat honestly
(``B+C alone would NOT justify claiming Theorem 3.1 formalised''), and the
per-tide logs preserved it --- but the caveat eroded between artefact layers:
by the time the claim reached the completion banner in the tide log, the
seabed description, and the \verb|\leanref| tags, the conditional nature of
the base case had dropped out. Reviews of ``completion'' tides should
routinely diff the headline theorem's \emph{hypothesis list} against the
prose claim, which is where this class of drift lives; the statement itself
compiled, was proved, and is true. Second lesson: a docstring narrating a
lemma that was dropped during iteration (the translated-integral form) is a
specific, checkable failure mode --- when a tide drops a planned lemma, the
module docstring needs the same edit.
