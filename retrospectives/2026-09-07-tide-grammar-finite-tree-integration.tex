\begin{abstract}
Astra~\#10 rank~1. An abstract lemma with no analytic content: if for a.e.\ parameter $w$
$|Z(w)-\sum_{\alpha\in P}N^{-\alpha}(A_\alpha(w)\log N+B_\alpha(w))|\le KR$ with a.e.-bounded,
a.e.-strongly-measurable coefficients and a nonnegative integrable weight $\varrho$, then
$|\int Z\varrho-\sum_{\alpha\in P}N^{-\alpha}((\int A_\alpha\varrho)\log N+\int B_\alpha\varrho)|\le KR\int\varrho$
(\texttt{integral\_finite\_expansion}); no separate integrability of $Z$ is required. Specialised to
a measurable family of analytic amplitudes with a common envelope, the averaged chart integral has
the SAME finite Taylor tree with averaged canonical coefficients and the remainder constant
multiplied by the mass of the weight (\texttt{twoD\_taylor\_tree\_integrated}), and for a
probability measure the expectation $E[Z_N]$ has the Taylor tree with expected coefficients
(\texttt{twoD\_taylor\_tree\_expectation}). Measurability in the parameter is a hypothesis here.
Zero \texttt{sorry}/\texttt{axiom}.
\end{abstract}

\section{Mathematics}

Write $E(w)=Z(w)-\sum_\alpha N^{-\alpha}(A_\alpha(w)\log N+B_\alpha(w))$. The a.e.\ bounds make
$E\varrho$, $A_\alpha\varrho$, $B_\alpha\varrho$ integrable (bounded measurable times integrable),
hence $Z\varrho$ too, and linearity of the integral gives
$\int Z\varrho=\int E\varrho+\sum_\alpha N^{-\alpha}(\log N\int A_\alpha\varrho+\int B_\alpha\varrho)$;
finally $|\int E\varrho|\le\int|E|\varrho\le KR\int\varrho$. The specialisation feeds the uniform
Taylor tree of unit~135 (remainder bound for every $w$) and the envelope bounds of unit~134 on the
canonical coefficients (a.e.\ bounds, here everywhere).

\section{Paper-facing meaning}

Astra~\#10's paper-facing message: the Taylor tree is uniform on bounded analytic families
(unit~135) and commutes with finite-mass averaging, so the per-stratum integral
$\int Z_w(N)\rho_I(w)\,dw$ over the noncritical coordinates, and the expectation over a random
amplitude $\eta e^{\beta s\xi_n}$ with a common envelope, have the same finite expansion with
averaged/expected canonical coefficients. What is NOT yet covered: measurability of the chart
integral and of the canonical coefficients in the parameter (Astra~\#10 ranks~2--3, via partial sums
and the series formulas), and anything about $E[\log Z]$ or the fluctuation function, which need
lower-tail control beyond this deterministic bound.

\section{Lean notes}

\begin{itemize}
\item \texttt{Integrable.bdd\_mul (hg : Integrable g) (hf : AEStronglyMeasurable f) (∀ᵐ x, ‖f x‖ ≤
c)} is the a.e.-bounded-times-integrable lemma; it lets one avoid any integrability hypothesis on
$Z$ itself.
\item \texttt{Finset.aestronglyMeasurable\_sum} produces the Pi-sum \texttt{∑ i ∈ s, f i}; there is
no primed pointwise variant on this pin, so transfer with \texttt{.congr} and
\texttt{Finset.sum\_apply}.
\item \texttt{norm\_integral\_le\_of\_norm\_le (hg : Integrable g) (∀ᵐ x, ‖f x‖ ≤ g x)} gives
$|\int f|\le\int g$ after \texttt{← Real.norm\_eq\_abs}.
\item For a probability measure \texttt{simp} proves \texttt{∫ \_, (1 : ℝ) ∂μ = 1}.
\end{itemize}
