\begin{abstract}
A soundness review of \texttt{Laplace/TwoD/QuarticSextic.lean}: the closed-form core that
specialises the generic additively-separable factorisation theorems to the
$(\mathrm{quartic},\mathrm{sextic})$ pair, $L(x,y)=x^4/24+y^6/720$, and that the quartic--sextic
asymptotic-packaging files consume. Result: \textbf{a clean fidelity pass with two edits} --- a dead
\texttt{open Real} and a docstring that wrote a two-variable potential as a one-variable lambda.
\end{abstract}

\section{Setting}
This file is the bridge between the reviewed generic root
(\reviewref[AddSeparable]{2026-06-05-review-laplace-twod-addseparable}) and the
$(2,3)$ asymptotic specialisations
(\reviewref[partition]{2026-06-05-review-laplace-twod-quarticsexticpartition},
\reviewref[moment]{2026-06-05-review-laplace-twod-quarticsexticmoment}): it discharges the
integrability side-conditions for the quartic and sextic Boltzmann weights and instantiates the
three generic theorems, then substitutes the 1D closed forms to give the explicit
$Z_{2D}$ value.

\section{What was reviewed}
Two private integrability lemmas plus five public theorems: the partition factorisation
$Z_{2D}=Z_qZ_s$, its closed-form value
$\tfrac12(24/t)^{1/4}\Gamma(1/4)\cdot\tfrac13(720/t)^{1/6}\Gamma(1/6)$, strict positivity, the
separable-observable moment factorisation, and the mixed-coordinate covariance vanishing.

\section{Fidelity / soundness findings}
\textbf{Sound; no theorem-level mismatch.} The closed form matches the product of the 1D
\texttt{quartic\_partition}/\texttt{sextic\_partition} values, positivity follows from the 1D
factors, and the factorisation/covariance theorems are the expected specialisations of the reviewed
generic add-separable theorems, with the right nonzero-partition and integrability hypotheses.
\textbf{One docstring inaccuracy, fixed.} The \texttt{partitionFunction\_quarticSextic\_factor}
docstring described the potential as ``$x \mapsto x^4/24 + y^6/720$'' --- an ill-typed
one-variable lambda whose body mentions $y$. Corrected to $(x,y)\mapsto x^4/24+y^6/720$
(comment-only).

\section{Simplifications applied}
\textbf{One dead \texttt{open}.} \texttt{open Real} is dead (\texttt{Real.exp}/\texttt{Real.Gamma}
qualified, bare $(24/t)\,\hat{}\,(\text{real})$ is \texttt{rpow}); \texttt{open MeasureTheory} stays
--- bare \texttt{Integrable} appears in the two private lemmas and the \texttt{hf}/\texttt{hg}
hypotheses. Module green at $2701$ jobs, no warnings, signatures byte-identical. GPT affirmed.

\section{Disagreements and open questions}
None. The reviewer's proposal to inline the single-use \texttt{have} in the two private lemmas (and
drop a likely-redundant \texttt{one\_mul} from the \texttt{simpa} set) was declined as proof-golf.

\section{Lessons}
\begin{itemize}[noitemsep]
  \item \textbf{Ill-typed informal notation is a fidelity nit worth fixing.} ``$x\mapsto
    x^4/24+y^6/720$'' parses (to a reader) as a one-argument function, but the body is binary; it is
    the documentation analogue of a type error. Cheap to fix, and it removes a genuine
    point of confusion about the potential's arity.
  \item \textbf{The closed-form core is where the explicit constants enter.} Reviewing it after the
    asymptotic packagers (which only repackage) confirmed that the $24$, $720$, $\Gamma(1/4)$,
    $\Gamma(1/6)$ all trace to the 1D \texttt{quartic\_partition}/\texttt{sextic\_partition} ---
    closing the provenance of every constant the $(2,3)$ arc uses.
\end{itemize}
