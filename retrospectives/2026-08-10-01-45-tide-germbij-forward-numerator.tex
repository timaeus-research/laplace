\begin{abstract}
The analytic heart of the numerator expansion: on the mesoscopic
window, the normalized remainder integral tends to zero,
\[
  \int_{W_q} P(z)\,
  \frac{e^{-(L(qz)-L(0))/q^2}
    - e^{-T_2(z)}\sum_{j\le N} P_j(z)\,q^j}{q^N}\,dz
  \;\longrightarrow\; 0 \qquad (q \to 0^+),
\]
by the filter-indexed dominated convergence theorem with the window
indicator folded into the integrand, exactly as the architecture
consult prescribed. The dominator is the previous tide's $q$-uniform
majorant times the observable's growth; the pointwise limit at fixed
$z$ is the scalar graded expansion, activated by the vanishing scaled
remainder and switched on by eventual window membership. The tide
also defines the expansion's coefficient integrals
$a_j = \int P\, e^{-T_2} P_j$ and proves them integrable. The two
outer tails and the final packaging were deliberately cut to the next
tide: they are a full tide of set-integral comparisons, and a clean
cut beats a half-done state.
\end{abstract}

\section{Setting}

\begin{sloppypar}
Stage 5c-ii-a of the forward-expansion programme (the consult's item
7, with items 8--9 deferred). The consult's DCT guidance was followed
literally: integral over all of $\R^d$ with the varying window as an
indicator (set integrals with a varying set leave one unfolding
restrictions manually), dominated convergence via the filter-indexed
theorem, and measurability in $z$ for each fixed $q$ only.
\end{sloppypar}

\section{The thing formalised}

\begin{sloppypar}
\code{boltzmann\_factor\_eq} extracts the majorant tide's factoring
of the true Boltzmann factor through the exponent split as a

here). \code{numeratorCoeff} and
\code{integrable\_coeff\_integrand} give the coefficient integrals,
dominated by $(1+\|z\|)^{n+j+2N} e^{-(\lambda/2)\|z\|^2}$ through the

\code{tendsto\_integral\_window\_remainder} is the dominated
convergence: eventual measurability mirrors the seabed's rescaled-DCT
block (the loss is only measurable, everything else continuous);
eventual domination is the 5c-i majorant times the observable bound
$|P| \le 2C_P(1+\|z\|)^n$, with the $q^N$ cancelling the
normalization; the dominator integrates by the binomial-Gaussian
helper; and the pointwise limit composes
\code{exp\_graded\_expansion} (at $a_s = V_s(z)$, $\rho$ the scaled
remainder) with \code{IsLittleO.tendsto\_div\_nhds\_zero}, a constant
multiplication, and an eventual-equality transfer through
\code{boltzmann\_factor\_eq} and eventual window membership.
\end{sloppypar}

\section{Where progress stalled}

Two rounds. Rewriting the target with $0 = \int 0$ also rewrote the
zero inside the filter $\mathcal N[>](0)$ --- state the DCT
conclusion with $\int 0$ and transfer by \code{simpa} instead of
rewriting the goal. And \code{positivity} cannot see
$0 < \lambda/4$ for an opaque domain field (pass
\code{linarith [D.lambda\_pos]}). A third, self-inflicted round: a
scripted re-indentation of the four DCT bullets mangled the nested
case split; rewriting the block wholesale was faster than repairing
it.
