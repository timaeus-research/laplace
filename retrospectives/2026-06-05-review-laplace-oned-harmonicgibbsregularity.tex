\begin{abstract}
A soundness review of \texttt{Laplace/OneD/HarmonicGibbsRegularity.lean}: the
\texttt{Threepoint.GibbsRegularity} witness for the harmonic Gibbs measure (the harmonic analogue
of the anharmonic regularity file, and the witness the harmonic cross-susceptibility capstone
consumes). Result: \textbf{a clean fidelity pass with six edits} --- three dead \texttt{open}s, a
dead import, and two docstring corrections.
\end{abstract}

\section{Setting}
The harmonic counterpart of
\reviewref[AnharmonicGibbsRegularity]{2026-06-05-review-laplace-oned-anharmonicgibbsregularity}: it
proves the regularity data for $L=(\lambda/2)x^2$, $A=\mathrm{id}$ via the \emph{closed form} of the
perturbed partition (square completion $+$ Lebesgue translation $+$ \texttt{integral\_gaussian})
rather than dominated convergence, and is the substrate the
\reviewref[harmonic cross-susc capstone]{2026-06-05-review-laplace-oned-harmoniccrosssuscderiv}
builds on.

\section{What was reviewed}
The perturbed partition closed form $\int e^{-t((\lambda/2)x^2+hx)}=e^{th^2/2\lambda}\sqrt{2\pi/
\lambda t}$, its $h=0$ specialisation, $Z>0$, the first-moment odd-symmetry vanishing
$\int(-tx)e^{-(t\lambda/2)x^2}=0$, and the \texttt{harmonic\_id\_gibbsRegularity} assembly.

\section{Fidelity / soundness findings}
\textbf{Sound; no defects.} Square completion $(\lambda/2)x^2+hx=(\lambda/2)(x+h/\lambda)^2-h^2/2
\lambda$ gives the closed form via translation invariance and \texttt{integral\_gaussian} at
$b=t\lambda/2$; $Z>0$ via \texttt{Real.sqrt\_pos}; the first moment vanishes by odd symmetry
($x\mapsto-x$ invariance); and the three \texttt{GibbsRegularity} fields assemble correctly. The
reviewer confirmed all four (noting, harmlessly, that the odd-vanishing lemma is stated for all
$\lambda,t$ because Lean totalises the Bochner integral --- true as written, used under
$\lambda,t>0$).

\textbf{Two docstring corrections.} The file header wrote the potential as ``$((\lambda/2)\cdot)$''
--- linear scaling, not the quadratic $(\lambda/2)x^2$ (the \emph{headline} bullet already had it
right); corrected to match. And the helpers bullet advertised
\texttt{harmonic\_integral\_x\_exp\_neg\_eq\_zero} (wrong name, and its formula dropped the $-t$),
while the actual theorem is \texttt{harmonic\_integral\_neg\_t\_x\_exp\_neg\_eq\_zero}; corrected
name and formula.

\section{Simplifications applied}
\textbf{Three dead opens and a dead import.} \texttt{open Real} (all \texttt{Real.*} qualified,
bare $\sqrt{}$/$\pi$ only in docstrings), \texttt{open Filter} (all \texttt{Filter.*} qualified, no
\texttt{filter\_upwards}), and \texttt{open Topology} (uses root-level \texttt{nhds}, never $\mathcal
N$) are all dead; \texttt{open MeasureTheory} stays. And \texttt{import
\dots IntervalIntegral.Basic} is dead --- no interval-integral notation or lemmas are used
(\texttt{integral\_gaussian} comes from \texttt{GaussianIntegral}); a full build confirmed it is not
transitively load-bearing. Signatures byte-identical, no warnings. GPT affirmed.

\section{Disagreements and open questions}
None. The reviewer's proof-golf (\texttt{show}$\to$\texttt{change}, a \texttt{simpa}-collapse of
\texttt{harmonic\_partition\_eq}) was declined.

\section{Lessons}
\begin{itemize}[noitemsep]
  \item \textbf{The headline bullet and the file header can disagree --- the header was the wrong
    one.} The \texttt{\#\# Headline} section already wrote the potential as
    a \texttt{fun x => \dots} mapping $x\mapsto(\lambda/2)x^2$; only the top-of-file summary had the
    lossy ``$((\lambda/2)\cdot)$''.
    When two docstring sites state the same object, cross-check them against each other (and the
    actual signature), not just against intuition.
  \item \textbf{A four-\texttt{open} header in a closed-form-Gaussian file is mostly dead.} Three of
    the four opens (\texttt{Real}, \texttt{Filter}, \texttt{Topology}) carried nothing --- the
    bare-name greps called each before the build confirmed. Headers accrete opens from the template;
    only \texttt{MeasureTheory} (the bare $\int$) was load-bearing.
\end{itemize}
