\section{Setting}

Stage 2 of the weighted-jet programme: the finite-jet potential
$L(x) = a\,x^{2k} + \sum_{r=1}^R c_r x^{2k+r}$, its integrability
envelope, and the exact scaling identity that every later stage
manipulates. The stage's design question --- which envelope --- was
the scope trap the programme consult had flagged, and it was resolved
by a dedicated consult \emph{before} any Lean was written.

\section{The envelope decision}

The consult rejected the drafted ``even positive top coefficient''
envelope with a decisive counterexample. The factorization
\[
L_q(u) \;=\; u^{2k}\,P(qu),
\qquad P(y) = a + \sum_{r=1}^R c_r y^r,
\]
shows that small $q$ never tames the perturbation: $qu$ ranges over
all of $\mathbb{R}$ regardless. For $P(y) = 1 - 3y + y^2$ (positive
even top!) one has $P(1) = -1$, so $L_q(1/q) = -q^{-2k}$ and the
Gibbs weight carries a remote peak of size $e^{q^{-2k}}$: integrable
at each fixed $q$, but nowhere near the reference measure as
$q \to 0$. The correct envelope is a \emph{uniformly positive
profile}: $\rho \le P(y)$ for all $y$, some $\rho > 0$. This yields
the clean global inequality $\rho\,u^{2k} \le L_q(u)$ for every real
$q$, hence a $q$-independent dominating function and, later,
dominated differentiation at $q = 0$. Arbitrary target jets are
recovered by an even stabilizer $d\,y^M$ with $M > R$, invisible to
extraction through order $R$.

\section{The thing formalised}

Fifteen declarations in \texttt{Laplace/OneD/JetScaling.lean},
sorry-free with zero warnings: the jet definitions and profile
factorization; the envelope predicate
\texttt{HasPositiveJetProfile} with its lower bound and
$q$-independent domination; integrability and positivity of all
polynomial moments of the jet Gibbs weight; and the exact scaling
identity\footnote{\texttt{normalized\_polynomialJet\_scale},
\texttt{gibbsExpectation\_polynomialJet\_scale}.}: for $q > 0$ with
$t q^{2k} = 1$,
\[
\Big\langle x^s \Big\rangle_t
 \;=\; q^s \cdot
 \frac{\int u^s\, e^{-L_q(u)}\,du}{\int e^{-L_q(u)}\,du}.
\]
The $R = 2$ certificate\footnote{\texttt{hasPositiveJetProfile\_two}.}
($c_2 > 0$, $c_1^2 < 4ac_2$, $\rho = a - c_1^2/(4c_2)$) is the
quadratic-profile analogue of the anharmonic discriminant
$\alpha^2 < 3\lambda\gamma$ and admits unrestricted odd $c_1$.

A deliberate API choice: the core theorems take the algebraic
hypotheses $q > 0$ and $t q^{2k} = 1$ rather than a let-bound
$t^{-1/(2k)}$, keeping \texttt{rpow} plumbing out of the analytic
layer entirely.

\section{Roadblocks and resolutions}

Build iteration surfaced one new catalogue entry: equality goals
produced by \texttt{Integrable.congr} can arrive
\emph{beta-unreduced}, so a targeted \texttt{rw [pow\_zero]} finds no
pattern where a plain \texttt{simp} (which beta-reduces first)
closes the goal. The rest were known classes: dead tactics after
closing \texttt{field\_simp}/\texttt{norm\_num}, a redundant bridging
\texttt{have} whose \texttt{rw} closed everything (signalled by the
``unsolved goals + no goals'' error pair), and atom-matching for
\texttt{linarith} handled by normalising exponents first. The
per-term substitution algebra inside \texttt{Finset.sum\_congr}
discharged cleanly by \texttt{linear\_combination} against
$t q^{2k} = 1$.

\section{The deliberation}

Consult archived verbatim in the tide log.\footnote{%
\texttt{tide-log/gpt56\_jet\_scaling\_envelope\_v1.md}.} Its central
contribution was negative and mathematical, not tactical: the
counterexample killing the drafted envelope before a single line
depended on it. The numerical check confirmed both the counterexample
($P(1) = -1$) and the certificate ($\min P = 0.75 = \rho$ exactly at
$(1, -1, 1)$), and verified the scaling identity to machine precision
($10^{-15}$ over $t \in \{5, 40\}$, $s \in \{1,2,3\}$), as befits an
exact identity.

\section{Mathlib vs new}

Mathlib lookup: the linear-substitution integral identity, dominated
integrability with a continuous majorant, and integral positivity via
support.\footnote{These are
\texttt{Measure.}\allowbreak\texttt{integral\_comp\_mul\_right},
\texttt{Integrable.mono'}, and
\texttt{integral\_pos\_iff\_support\_of\_nonneg}.} Seabed reuse: the
\texttt{kth\_integrable\_pow} family as the dominating integrable.
New: the profile viewpoint and everything downstream.

\section{What the next tide inherits}

Stage 3 of the programme: the weighted exponential polynomial ---
expanding $\exp(-\sum_r c_r q^r u^{2k+r})$ in powers of $q$ with
coefficient polynomials $P_n(u)$ and the triangular decomposition
$P_n(u) = -c_n u^{2k+n} + \widetilde{P}_n(u; c_1, \dots, c_{n-1})$,
where the new coefficient enters exactly once and linearly. The
envelope's $q$-independent domination is what will upgrade the formal
expansion to an integrated asymptotic one in stage 4.
