\begin{abstract}
Unit 208 closes programme~B2 by feeding the conditional assembly theorem of unit~207 with the
phase-dressed chart integrals of Headlines~XIII--XIV. For a finite family of charts $a\in\iota$,
each with its own dimension, exponents $(h_a,k_a)$, minimum ratio $\lambda_a$ ($p_a=2\lambda_a$),
multiplicity $m_a$, continuous phase $\xi_a$, observable $\varphi_a$ and density $c_a>0$ on the
closed cube, all at the same $\beta$,
\[
\frac{\sum_a\int_{(0,1]^{d_a}}\varphi_ac_a\,u^{h_a}e^{-\beta N^2u^{2k_a}+\beta Nu^{k_a}\xi_a}}
     {\sum_a\int_{(0,1]^{d_a}}c_a\,u^{h_a}e^{-\beta N^2u^{2k_a}+\beta Nu^{k_a}\xi_a}}
\;\longrightarrow\;
\frac{\sum_{a\in\mathrm{sel}}\mathrm{phaseCoeff}_a[\varphi_ac_a]}{\sum_{a\in\mathrm{sel}}\mathrm{phaseCoeff}_a[c_a]},
\]
where $\mathrm{sel}$ is the set of charts with $p_a=\min_bp_b$ and $m_a=\max\{m_b:p_b=\min\}$
(\texttt{headline\_assembled\_posterior}). The denominator is positive since each selected
$\mathrm{phaseCoeff}_a[c_a]>0$ and the selection is nonempty. Zero \texttt{sorry}/\texttt{axiom};
9085 jobs.
\end{abstract}

\section{Mathematics and scope}
Nothing new analytically: \texttt{assembly\_ratio\_tendsto} with $I_a$ the chart integrals,
$p_a=2\lambda_a$, $m_a=$ \texttt{multCount}, limits from \texttt{phase\_leading\_tendsto}, and
positivity of the selected denominator sum from \texttt{phaseCoeff\_pos} and
\texttt{exists\_leadMult}. Dependent types carry the per-chart dimensions
(\texttt{h k : (a : ι) → Fin (d a + 1) → ℕ}).

What the theorem says: \emph{given} that the posterior numerator and denominator are these finite
sums of chart integrals, the leading quotient is the selected-chart face average. What it does not
say: that the resolution, Jacobians, partition of unity and tangential integrations produce such a
sum (the paper-level input), nor anything about random phases. Astra~\#22's gates are met:
minimum exponent then \emph{maximum} multiplicity; numerator and denominator assembled separately;
denominator positivity via an explicit interface (here $c_a>0$; Astra suggested allowing vanishing
partition amplitudes with positivity of the assembled coefficient instead --- a one-line weakening
of the hypothesis \texttt{hpos} in unit~207's \texttt{assembly\_ratio\_tendsto}, already available).

\section{Lean notes}
\begin{itemize}
\item A family of charts with varying dimension is a dependent function
  \texttt{h : (a : ι) → Fin (d a + 1) → ℕ}; all downstream lemmas apply pointwise in \texttt{a}
  without friction.
\item The selection predicate must be spelled out identically in the statement and in the
  \texttt{Finset.filter} of \texttt{assembly\_ratio\_tendsto} for \texttt{exact} to unify; using
  \texttt{fun b => 2 * l b} for \texttt{p} and \texttt{fun b => multCount …} for \texttt{m}
  throughout makes this syntactic.
\end{itemize}
