\begin{abstract}
Unit 214 (Astra~\#23 programme~G, step~1) provides the probabilistic glue for assembling finitely many
charts with jointly random phases: a deterministic convergent sequence converges in measure; finitely
many sequences converging to $0$ in measure give a vector converging to $0$ (sup norm); Slutsky
pairing of a sequence converging in distribution with a deterministic convergent sequence (a wrapper
around Mathlib's \texttt{TendstoInDistribution.prodMk\_of\_tendstoInMeasure\_const}); and the
\emph{joint vector form of Headline~XVI}: for charts $a\in\kappa$ (finite) with their own dimensions
and exponents, jointly convergent random phases $X_n\Rightarrow Z$ in
$\prod_aC([0,1]^{d_a+1})$ and fixed amplitudes $g_a$, the vector
$(F^{(a)}_{N_n}(X_n^a,g_a))_a$ converges in distribution to $(F^{(a)}(Z^a,g_a))_a$
(\texttt{tendstoInDistribution\_normChart\_vec}). Zero \texttt{sorry}/\texttt{axiom}; 9091 jobs.
\end{abstract}

\section{Mathematics}
Nothing new analytically: the vector statement is Headline~XVI componentwise, with the errors
collected by the union bound (\texttt{measure\_iUnion\_fintype\_le} behind
\texttt{pi\_norm\_lt\_iff}) and the limits by continuous mapping of the phase vector. The
deterministic-coefficient Slutsky lemma is what will turn the per-chart normalised integrals into
the assembled sums $\sum_aI_a/S_*=\sum_a(S_a/S_*)\,(I_a/S_a)$ with $S_a/S_*\to e_a\in\{0,1\}$.

\section{Lean notes}
\begin{itemize}
\item Mathlib's \texttt{prodMk\_of\_tendstoInMeasure\_const} needs the first space to be a
  \texttt{SeminormedAddCommGroup} with \texttt{BorelSpace} and \texttt{SecondCountableTopology};
  a wrapper stated with a bare \texttt{TopologicalSpace} does not typecheck.
\item Composing \texttt{continuous\_apply a} inside \texttt{continuous\_comp} leaves the Pi type
  metavariable unresolved; state the composite continuity as a typed \texttt{have} first.
\item The Borel σ-algebra on the factors gives the product σ-algebra on
  \texttt{PhaseSpaces κ d} for free (\texttt{measurable\_pi\_iff}, \texttt{measurable\_pi\_apply}).
\end{itemize}
