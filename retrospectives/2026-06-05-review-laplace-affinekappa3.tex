\begin{abstract}
A soundness review of \texttt{Laplace/AffineKappa3.lean}: the potential-agnostic factored third-cumulant
identity $\kappa_3(L,a_2x+b_2,t,a_1x+b_1,a_3x+b_3)=a_1a_2a_3\,\kappa_3(L,x,t,x,x)$ --- the general
result the harmonic-vanishing and anharmonic-asymptotic $\kappa_3$ files specialise. Result: a clean
fidelity pass with two small edits (a dead equation name and a misleading-docstring clarification).
\end{abstract}

\section{Setting}
This is the abstraction beneath
\reviewref[HarmonicAffineKappa3]{2026-06-05-review-laplace-oned-harmonicaffinekappa3} and
\reviewref[AnharmonicKappa3Affine]{2026-05-31-review-laplace-oned-anharmonickappa3affine}: rather than
re-derive the affine factoring per potential, it proves once, for an arbitrary potential $L$ (given
$Z\ne0$ and the degree-$\le3$ moment integrabilities), that shifting and scaling the three observables
of $\kappa_3$ multiplies the all-identity cumulant by $a_1a_2a_3$ and drops the additive constants.

\section{What was reviewed}
The Threepoint$\to$Laplace bridge \texttt{threepoint\_gibbsExp\_volume\_zero\_eq}; the five-term unfold
\texttt{kappa3\_volume\_unfold}; the affine and cubic expectation reductions
\texttt{gibbsExpectation\_affine} and \texttt{gibbsExp\_cubic\_eq}; and the headline
\texttt{kappa3\_volume\_shifted\_affine\_eq\_smul} --- checked for correct cumulant signs, polynomial
coefficients, and the genuine cancellation of the $b$-constants.

\section{Fidelity / soundness findings}
\textbf{Sound; no defect.} The reviewer confirmed the five-term unfold's signs ($+\,-\,-\,-\,+$), every
coefficient in the seven product-term expansions, and the headline $a_1a_2a_3$, and then verified the
\emph{cancellation} directly: after substituting the moments the left side collapses to
$a_1a_2a_3\,(m_3-3m_2m_1+2m_1^3)=a_1a_2a_3\,\kappa_3(x,x,x)$, with every $b$-dependent contribution ---
the one-$b$ $m_2$ terms, the two-$b$ $m_1$ terms, the one-$b$ $m_1^2$ terms, and the pure constant
$b_1b_2b_3$ (whose coefficients sum $+1-1-1-1+2=0$) --- summing to zero. ``No mathematical/algebraic
fidelity defects.''

\section{Simplifications and documentation fixes applied}
\textbf{A dead equation name and a misleading docstring.} The first-moment
\texttt{set \dots with hm1} carried an equation name \texttt{hm1} that --- unlike \texttt{hm2}/
\texttt{hm3}, which are rewritten with by name --- is never used; dropped (the abbreviation it names
stays). And \texttt{kappa3\_volume\_unfold}'s
docstring claimed the unfolded expression is ``independent of the perturbation direction $A$,'' which
reads false: $A$ is plainly all over the right-hand side as an \emph{observable} ($\varphi A B$, $AB$,
$A$). The accurate point --- which the fix now states --- is that $A$ drops only from the Gibbs
\emph{weight} at $h=0$ (the perturbation $h\cdot A$ leaves the integrand), which is what lets the unfold
go through the \emph{unperturbed} \texttt{gibbsExpectation}; $A$ survives as an observable. Build green,
no warnings, signatures byte-identical; the reviewer affirmed both.

\section{Disagreements and open questions}
None. The file opens only \texttt{MeasureTheory} (no dead \texttt{open Real} to shed --- already
minimal). The \texttt{import \dots Bochner.Basic} is redundant (\texttt{Laplace.Gibbs} supplies it
transitively) but its symbols \texttt{Integrable}/\texttt{Measure}/\texttt{volume} are used directly, so
it was kept --- redundant-but-used is not dead code (the same call made for the redundant
\texttt{Anharmonic} import in the
\reviewref[IntegralRemainder review]{2026-06-05-review-laplace-oned-integralremainder}).

\section{Lessons}
\begin{itemize}[noitemsep]
  \item \textbf{An ``independent of $X$'' docstring is worth a second look when $X$ is visibly in the
    statement.} The claim was true of the Gibbs \emph{weight} but false of the \emph{expression}; the
    fix is to name which object is actually $X$-independent. A reader who took the docstring literally
    would have been misled about what the unfold says.
  \item \textbf{A potential-agnostic lemma is the right place to verify the cancellation once.} Because
    this file proves the affine factoring for arbitrary $L$, confirming the $b$-drop here certifies it
    for every specialisation (harmonic, anharmonic, \dots) at once --- the review analogue of the
    abstraction the file itself provides.
\end{itemize}
