\begin{abstract}
Completion of Astra~\#10's rank~5. The amplitude coefficient map $(x,y)\mapsto c(x,y)(s)$ of the
§4.2 cutoff integrand, $c_k(s)=e^{\beta s x_{00}}\,(y*E_{\bar x}(\beta s))_k$, is locally Lipschitz
in the weighted norm $\|\cdot\|_\rho$:
\[
\|c(x,y)(s)-c(x',y')(s)\|_\rho\le e^{2|\beta s|M_x}\big(\|y-y'\|_\rho+2M_y|\beta s|\|x-x'\|_\rho\big)
\]
for $\|x\|_\rho,\|x'\|_\rho\le M_x$, $\|y'\|_\rho\le M_y$ (\texttt{ampCoeff\_lipschitz}), with the
pointwise form $|c_k(x,y)(s)-c_k(x',y')(s)|\rho^{|k|}\le$ the same
(\texttt{ampCoeff\_sub\_abs\_le}). Zero \texttt{sorry}/\texttt{axiom}.
\end{abstract}

\section{Mathematics}
Write $u=\beta s x_{00}$, $E=E_{\bar x}(\beta s)$ with $\bar x=$ \texttt{dropConst}$\,x$, and
primes for the second data set. Then
\[
c-c'=e^{u}\,(y-y')*E\;+\;e^{u}\,y'*(E-E')\;+\;(e^{u}-e^{u'})\,y'*E'.
\]
The norms are controlled by $\|E\|_\rho\le e^{|t|\|\bar x\|_\rho}\le e^{|t|M_x}$ (unit~118 and
$\|\bar x\|_\rho\le\|x\|_\rho$), $\|E-E'\|_\rho\le|t|e^{|t|M_x}\|\bar x-\bar x'\|_\rho\le
|t|e^{|t|M_x}\|x-x'\|_\rho$ (unit~142, using $\overline{x-x'}=\bar x-\bar x'$), $e^{u}\le
e^{|t|M_x}$ since $|x_{00}|\le\|x\|_\rho$ (the $k=(0,0)$ term with weight $\rho^0=1$), and
$|e^u-e^{u'}|\le|u-u'|e^{\max(u,u')}\le e^{|t|M_x}|t|\|x-x'\|_\rho$. Adding the three pieces gives
$e^{2|t|M_x}(\|y-y'\|_\rho+2M_y|t|\|x-x'\|_\rho)$. Only $y'$ needs a norm bound; $y$ enters through
$y-y'$ alone.

\section{Paper-facing meaning}
Astra~\#10 listed ``$c_s(x,y)$ is locally Lipschitz in the Banach-algebra norm'' as the last piece of
the coefficient-closure programme: with parameter measurability (units~138--139), the weighted
bounds (140) and linearity (141), it makes the map from analytic data $(\xi,\eta)$ to Taylor-tree
coefficients a continuous (indeed locally Lipschitz) map on the $\rho$-ball, which is what one
needs to move expectations and limits past the coefficient extraction in §4.3. The estimate is
uniform on $s\in[0,S]$ for each fixed $S$, with the Gaussian-type growth $e^{2|\beta s|M_x}$ in $s$
that the envelope class of unit~140 absorbs.
