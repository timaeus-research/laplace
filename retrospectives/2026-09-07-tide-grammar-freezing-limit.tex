\begin{abstract}
The right-hand side of the freezing inequality (unit~77) is normalised by $N^p/\log^{d_0+1}N$: the strip
terms vanish because the mixed-block envelope loses one logarithm, the corner term converges to
$\omega C_4$ by the frozen coefficient theorem, and uniform continuity of $\xi,\eta$ on the closed box
makes $\omega$ arbitrarily small. Hence $N^p(Z(N)-Z_0(N))/\log^{d_0+1}N\to0$: the leading coefficient
of a block of multiplicity $m=d_0+2\ge2$ depends on $\xi,\eta$ only through their values on the minimal
face $u=0$. Zero \texttt{sorry}/\texttt{axiom}.
\end{abstract}

\section{Proof strategy}
Strip terms: on the open box the shifted block is bounded by
$K(Nv^{k'})^{-p}(1+\log_+(Nv^{k'}B))^{d_0}$, hence the normalised integrand by
$K(2+\log_+(B'B))^{d_0}\,v^{h'}(v^{k'})^{-p}/\log N$; \texttt{integral\_mono\_of\_nonneg} needs only
integrability of the dominating side, and \texttt{squeeze\_zero'} against $C/\log N$ finishes.
Freezing limit: given $\varepsilon$, choose $\omega=\varepsilon/(2C_2(|C_4|+1))$, get $\delta$ from
uniform continuity (\texttt{IsCompact.uniformContinuousOn\_of\_continuous},
\texttt{Metric.uniformContinuousOn\_iff}, with $\mathrm{dist}((u,v),(0,v))=\max_i|u_i|$ via
\texttt{Prod.dist\_eq} and \texttt{dist\_pi\_le\_iff}), then eventually the corner term is
$<\varepsilon/2$ and the strip sum is $<\varepsilon/2$.

\section{Roadblocks and resolutions}
\begin{itemize}
\item \texttt{Tendsto.congr'} pointwise goals are beta redexes: \texttt{beta\_reduce} before
\texttt{rw}.
\item Rewriting the frozen inner integral with \texttt{← integral\_const\_mul} is ambiguous (two
matching products); state the integrand identity by \texttt{integral\_congr\_ae} and rewrite forward.
\item \texttt{field\_simp} closes the two scalar identities outright; a trailing \texttt{ring} errors
with ``no goals''.
\item \texttt{tendsto\_finset\_sum} is deprecated for \texttt{tendsto\_finsetSum}.
\end{itemize}

\section{Outcome}
Astra target~5 is essentially complete: the general multiplicity-$m$ leading term reduces to the
frozen one. Next: assemble the main theorem $Z\sim C\,N^{-p}\log^{d_0+1}N$ with
$C=\frac{1}{(d_0+1)!\prod k_i}\int v^{h'}(v^{k'})^{-p}A_{p-1}(\xi(0,v))\eta(0,v)\,dv$, positivity when
$\eta(0,\cdot)>0$, the $\log n$ form, and the Fubini bridge to the full box.
