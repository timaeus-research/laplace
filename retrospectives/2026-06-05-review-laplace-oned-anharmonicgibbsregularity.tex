\begin{abstract}
A soundness review of \texttt{Laplace/OneD/AnharmonicGibbsRegularity.lean}: the
\texttt{Threepoint.GibbsRegularity} witness for the anharmonic Gibbs measure (the regularity data
the FDT review consumes), with its integrability, positivity, and the perturbed pointwise
bound/derivative lemmas. Result: \textbf{a clean fidelity pass with one docstring fix} --- a
limit-vs-asymptotic notation correction. A candidate dead import was \emph{rejected} by the build,
and a suspected stale cross-reference turned out correct.
\end{abstract}

\section{Setting}
This is the regularity factory at the base of the anharmonic FDT/cumulant stack: it proves
$\int e^{-tL}<\infty$, $Z>0$, the perturbed pointwise bound (via AM--GM and coercivity), the
pointwise \texttt{HasDerivAt}, and assembles them into the
\reviewref[FDT review]{2026-06-05-review-laplace-oned-anharmonicfdt}'s
\texttt{anharmonic\_id\_gibbsRegularity}.

\section{What was reviewed}
Six theorems plus the \texttt{GibbsRegularity} witness: integrability of $e^{-tL}$ and $x\,e^{-tL}$,
$Z>0$, the perturbed pointwise \texttt{HasDerivAt} and bound, the private bound-integrability, and
the headline instance.

\section{Fidelity / soundness findings}
\textbf{Sound; no defects.} The integrability and positivity are the standard coercive-domination
arguments; the perturbed pointwise bound is correct, with the $e^{t/(2c)}$ factor arising from
$-thx\le t|x|\le \tfrac{tc}{2}x^2+\tfrac{t}{2c}\le\tfrac t2 L+\tfrac t{2c}$ (AM--GM plus coercivity
$cx^2\le L$, $|h|<1$); the pointwise \texttt{HasDerivAt} is the chain rule; and the
\texttt{GibbsRegularity} assembly feeds the dominated-differentiation theorem the right package. The
reviewer confirmed all of this.

\textbf{One docstring fix.} The module doc wrote ``$\partial_h\mathrm{Cov}_h(x,x)|_0\to
\alpha/(\lambda^3 t)$ as $t\to\infty$'' --- but the right-hand side is $t$-dependent (it tends to
$0$), so ``$\to$'' (a finite limit) is the wrong relation. Corrected to the asymptotic
``$\sim\alpha/(\lambda^3 t)$'' (equivalently $t\,\partial_h\mathrm{Cov}\to\alpha/\lambda^3$); the
value is right (the cited empirical asymptote $0.005$ is $\alpha/(\lambda^3 t)$ at
$(\lambda,\alpha,\gamma,t)=(1,0.5,1,100)$).

\section{Simplifications applied}
\textbf{Only the docstring fix --- the candidate import removal was rejected.}
\texttt{import Laplace.OneD.AnharmonicKappa3Affine} is referenced by no symbol (only in comments,
which note a kappa3 lemma is ``private there and re-derived here''), so it looked dead. But removing
it makes \texttt{integrable\_abs\_pow\_mul\_exp\_neg\_mul\_sq} an unknown identifier --- that import
is the \emph{only} transitive path to the Mathlib Gaussian lemma here. Kept. \texttt{open
MeasureTheory}/\texttt{Topology} and the other three imports are load-bearing.

\section{Disagreements and open questions}
None standing. Two reviewer items were resolved against the reviewer by checking: the import is
load-bearing (build), and the ``\texttt{AnharmonicKappa3}-vs-\texttt{AnharmonicKappa3Affine}''
cross-reference the reviewer thought stale is in fact correct ---
\texttt{kappa3\_anharmonic\_id\_id\_id\_asymptotic} genuinely lives in \texttt{AnharmonicKappa3.lean}
(a distinct file from the imported \texttt{Affine} one, which serves the integrability lemma).

\section{Lessons}
\begin{itemize}[noitemsep]
  \item \textbf{The same transitive lemma, opposite verdicts in adjacent files.}
    \texttt{integrable\_abs\_pow\_mul\_exp\_neg\_mul\_sq} was the symbol behind a removable import in
    the \reviewref[observable-monomials review]{2026-06-05-review-laplace-oned-anharmonicgibbsobservablemonomials}
    (reachable elsewhere) but behind a \emph{kept} one here (the sole path). ``Is this import dead?''
    is a per-file build question even for the identical underlying lemma --- never carry the verdict
    across files.
  \item \textbf{Check a ``stale cross-reference'' before fixing it.} The docstring named
    \texttt{AnharmonicKappa3} while the file imports \texttt{AnharmonicKappa3Affine}; the reviewer
    read that as drift, but the named lemma really is in the non-\texttt{Affine} file. A one-line
    grep distinguished a correct cross-file pointer from a typo.
  \item \textbf{``$\to$'' demands a constant right-hand side.} An asymptote with a surviving $t$ is
    an equivalence ($\sim$) or an $o(\cdot)$ statement, not a limit; the docstring's ``$\to
    \alpha/(\lambda^3 t)$'' silently asserted a limit that is really $0$.
\end{itemize}
