\begin{abstract}
Paper-facing wrappers for the tangentially integrated normal block (unit~189). For a compact
tangential set $K$ with an integrable signed density $q$ and a continuous amplitude $\eta(v,u)$: the
mixed-ratio face-supported coefficient spelled out
(\texttt{headline\_tangential\_normal\_moment}); the equal-ratio \emph{local leading-density}
formula
\[
\frac{\int_Kq(v)\int_{(0,1]^{m+1}}\eta(v,u)\,u^he^{-\beta Nu^{2k}}\,du\,dv}{N^{-\lambda}(\log N)^m}
\longrightarrow\frac{\Gamma(\lambda)\beta^{-\lambda}}{m!\prod_i2k_i}\int_Kq(v)\,\eta(v,0)\,dv
\]
(\texttt{headline\_tangential\_normal\_moment\_equal}), the chart-level precursor of the paper's
$\Gamma(\lambda)/(m-1)!\,a_I\int_{S_I}(\phi\circ\pi)c_0|dv|$ with $c_0$ and $\phi\circ\pi$ absorbed in
$q\eta$; and the equivalence form under a nonzero integrated coefficient
(\texttt{headline\_tangential\_normal\_moment\_equiv}). Zero \texttt{sorry}/\texttt{axiom}.
\end{abstract}

\section{Paper-facing meaning}
Astra~\#17's ``leading-density corollary'': at the level of one normal block in fixed coordinates,
the paper's leading coefficient structure --- a Gamma/Jacobian constant times an integral over the
tangential variables of the amplitude restricted to the normal origin --- is now a theorem when all
normal ratios are equal, and is replaced by the face-supported integral when they are mixed. The
identification of $\int_Kq(v)\,\cdot\,dv$ with $\int_{S_I}c_0\,|dv|$ (chart, density, partition of
unity) is outside the formalised boundary and is not claimed.
