\begin{abstract}
\noindent Two losses on $\mathbb{R}^d$, each $C^2$ with a
positive-definite Hessian at its minimum, whose posterior covariance
data agree to $o(q^2)$, have the same Hessian
(\texttt{hessian\_recovery}): uniqueness of limits identifies the
inverse Hessians entrywise through the H5 covariance limit, and
positive-definiteness makes inversion injective. This closes the
multivariate H-recovery milestone --- the germbij note's Hessian step
in arbitrary finite dimension, formalised end to end across eight
stages in a single day's arc. This retrospective doubles as the arc
synthesis.
\end{abstract}

\section{The arc}

The scoping consult staged Hessian-first recovery as H0--H6 and made
four structural rulings that all held: \texttt{EuclideanSpace}
throughout; one dilation wrapper with the orientation fixed once;
whitening rather than diagonalization for the Gaussian package; and
no Wick/Isserlis anywhere. The stages landed as: H1 dilation
(PR~\#66); H2a the standard Gaussian package and H2b whitening
(\#67--\#68, with a dedicated shape consult that split the flagged
``largest API spike'' in two); H3a fixed-ray rescaling and H3b the
uniform quadratic Peano remainder with coercivity (\#69--\#70, its
own consult); H4 one generic dominated-convergence theorem (\#71);
H5 the normalization bookkeeping (\#72); and H6 this tide. Every
consult prediction about where the difficulty lay was correct; the
one difficulty no consult predicted (the mean-value-inequality
unification bomb in H3b) was tactical, not mathematical.

\section{The thing formalised (arc statement)}

For a loss $L$ of class $C^2$ near its minimum at the origin with
$DL(0) = 0$ and $H = D^2L(0)$ positive definite, integrated over a
domain $U \supseteq B_\delta(0)$ carrying the rescaled lower bound:
\[
q^{-1}\,\mathbb{E}_q[w_i] \to 0, \qquad
q^{-2}\,\mathbb{E}_q[w_i w_j] \to (H^{-1})_{ij}, \qquad
q^{-2}\,\mathrm{Cov}_q(w_i, w_j) \to (H^{-1})_{ij},
\]
and if two such losses produce covariance data agreeing to $o(q^2)$,
then $H_1 = H_2$.

\section{How H6 works}

A dozen lines on top of H5: the difference of the two normalized
covariances tends both to $H_1^{-1}{}_{ij} - H_2^{-1}{}_{ij}$ (by the
two H5 limits) and to $0$ (by the data hypothesis divided through),
so the entries agree; \texttt{Matrix.ext} and double nonsingular
inversion finish. It was the arc's first zero-repair file --- the
interfaces built by the previous seven tides fit without friction.

\section{Process notes (arc level)}

Eight tides, three consults (scoping, H2 shape, H3/H4 shape), all
archived verbatim in the tide logs; one executed numerical check
(the $d = 2$ whitened moments, eight decimals) covering the H2, H5,
and H6 targets; stacked PRs with chained merge-on-green watchers.
The error catalogue gained five entries, the sharpest being the
H3b discovery that Mathlib's two mean-value-inequality variants
differ catastrophically in elaboration cost when handed
explicit-derivative hypotheses. Two process incidents, both caught
and reverted within minutes: a wrong-cwd commit that briefly landed
Lean notes on the SRI main branch, and a stray \texttt{pdflatex}
that surfaced (and led to removing) a \texttt{texput.log} committed
by accident months earlier.

\section{What this opens}

The germbij multivariate commissions beyond the Hessian: degree-$k$
tensor recovery by the pairwise-difference route (the scoping
consult's covariance-Gram injectivity plan: full support plus
variance, no Isserlis), and the degenerate strata of \S7.4 beyond
dimension one. Both need fresh scoping consults; the H2--H6
infrastructure (Gaussian package, whitening, the
\texttt{LocalQuadraticApprox}/\texttt{LocalLaplaceDomain}
interfaces, the generic dominated convergence) is exactly the seabed
they will launch from.
