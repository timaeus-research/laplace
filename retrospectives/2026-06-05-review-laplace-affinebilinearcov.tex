\begin{abstract}
A soundness review of \texttt{Laplace/AffineBilinearCov.lean}: the affine-bilinear specialisation of
the Gibbs covariance, $\mathrm{Cov}_t[a_1\varphi+b_1,a_2\psi+b_2]=a_1a_2\,\mathrm{Cov}_t[\varphi,\psi]$,
the first consumer of the covariance algebra from \texttt{Laplace.Gibbs}. Result: a clean fidelity
pass with a single dead-\texttt{open} removal.
\end{abstract}

\section{Setting}
The immediate downstream of \reviewref[the Gibbs foundation]{2026-06-05-review-laplace-gibbs}: it
composes \texttt{gibbsCov\_add\_left/right}, \texttt{gibbsCov\_smul\_left/right}, and
\texttt{gibbsCov\_const\_left/right} into the affine-input reduction --- additive constants drop
(covariance with a constant is zero), scalars factor out.

\section{What was reviewed}
\texttt{gibbsCov\_affine\_left} ($\mathrm{Cov}[a\varphi+b,\psi]=a\,\mathrm{Cov}[\varphi,\psi]$),
\texttt{gibbsCov\_affine\_right} (its symmetric twin), and the headline
\texttt{gibbsCov\_affine\_affine} ($a_1a_2\,\mathrm{Cov}$), checked against the affine-bilinear law and
for correct, minimal integrability hypotheses.

\section{Fidelity / soundness findings}
\textbf{Sound; no defect.} The reviewer confirmed all three statements: the headline coefficient is the
correct product $a_1a_2$, both additive constants disappear, and the four base integrability hypotheses
($h_{\exp},h_\varphi,h_\psi,h_{\varphi\psi}$) are exactly the compositional ones needed to derive
integrability of the scaled/offset observables before applying the base algebra --- all used, none
spurious. ``No statement-level mismatches found.''

\section{Simplifications applied}
\textbf{One dead open.} \texttt{open Real} carried nothing (every \texttt{Real.exp} is qualified; the
only bare \texttt{exp} is in the module docstring); \texttt{open MeasureTheory} stays (bare
\texttt{Integrable} in every hypothesis), and \texttt{import Laplace.Gibbs} is the load-bearing source
of the algebra. Build green, no warnings, signatures byte-identical; the reviewer affirmed. No dead
locals and no droppable hypotheses --- a tight composition file.

\section{Disagreements and open questions}
None. The reviewer's proof-golf (inline the syntactic \texttt{eq}/\texttt{change} rewrites; prove
\texttt{affine\_right} directly from the right-slot algebra to drop a derived local) was declined as
proof-shape-only.

\section{Lessons}
\begin{itemize}[noitemsep]
  \item \textbf{A pure composition file inherits its parent's cleanliness.} Having just reviewed the
    Gibbs algebra it consumes, this file's review reduced to confirming the composition is faithful and
    the hypotheses minimal --- exactly the cheap, high-confidence pass that reviewing a consumer right
    after its foundation buys (the same economy seen lifting the TwoD trio right after their
    \texttt{AddSeparable} root).
  \item \textbf{The dead \texttt{open Real} recurs once more.} Across the laplace seabed the single most
    common landable is a templated \texttt{open Real} that the file never uses unqualified; the
    foundational and composition files (\texttt{Gibbs}, \texttt{AffineBilinearCov}) are no exception.
\end{itemize}
