\begin{abstract}
For the amplitude $F(u,s)=\eta(u)e^{\beta s\xi(u)}$ with $\xi,\eta$ admitting first-order Taylor
expansions at $0$ with quadratic remainders on $[0,b]$, we prove
$|F(u,s)-F(0,s)-u\,\partial_uF(0,s)|\le C\,u^2(1+s)^2e^{3\beta Ls}$ for $u\in[0,b]$, $s\ge0$, with
$\partial_uF(0,s)=e^{\beta sa}(\eta_1+\beta s\eta_0a_1)$ and an explicit $C$. This is the input for the
second-order asymptotics of the chart integral (Astra's step~3). Zero \texttt{sorry}/\texttt{axiom}.
\end{abstract}

\section{Proof strategy}
Split $F(u,s)-F(0,s)-u\partial_uF(0,s)=(\eta-\eta_0-u\eta_1)e^{\beta s\xi}
+u\eta_1(e^{\beta s\xi}-e^{\beta sa})+\eta_0\big(e^{\beta s\xi}-e^{\beta sa}-\beta sua_1e^{\beta sa}\big)$
and write $e^{\beta s\xi(u)}=e^{\beta sa}e^{t}$ with $t=\beta s(\xi(u)-a)$, $|t|\le\beta sXu$,
$X=L_1+Kb$. The third piece is $e^{\beta sa}(e^t-1-t)+e^{\beta sa}\beta s(\xi-a-ua_1)$; the elementary
bounds $|e^t-1|\le|t|e^{|t|}$ (from the unit-76 mean-value bound) and $|e^t-1-t|\le3t^2e^{|t|}$ (Mathlib's
bound for $|t|\le1$, trivial otherwise) together with $e^{\beta sa}e^{|t|}\le e^{3\beta Ls}$ give the
result; finally $1,s,s^2\le(1+s)^2$.

\section{Roadblocks and resolutions}
\begin{itemize}
\item \texttt{gcongr} on $3t^2\cdot(E_0e^{|t|})\le3(\beta sXu)^2\cdot E_x$ tries to prove $0\le t$; use
\texttt{sq\_abs} and \texttt{pow\_le\_pow\_left₀} on $|t|$ explicitly.
\item In a large context \texttt{nlinarith} for $s^2\le(1+s)^2$ hits the heartbeat limit; prove such
facts by the dedicated lemmas (\texttt{one\_le\_pow₀}, \texttt{le\_self\_pow₀},
\texttt{pow\_le\_pow\_left₀}) and feed \texttt{linarith} the four product inequalities explicitly.
\end{itemize}

\section{Outcome}
The kernel Taylor bound is ready to be fed into the density-transfer theorem. Next: the one-dimensional
second-order theorem $Z(n)=C_0n^{-p/2}+C_1n^{-q/2}+O(n^{-(h+3)/(2k)})$ with
$C_1=\frac1k[\eta'(0)S_q(a)+\beta\eta(0)\xi'(0)S_{q+1}(a)]$.
