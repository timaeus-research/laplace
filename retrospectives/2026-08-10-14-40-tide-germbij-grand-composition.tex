\begin{abstract}
The located moments of \code{LocationRecovery} --- the ``$w^*$
clause'' of the note's Theorem 3.1 --- were a definition-level model:
\code{locatedMoment} was \emph{defined} as the anchored moment of a
translated observable, with the identification against the posterior
integral of the actual translated loss $w \mapsto L(w - p)$ on the
translated region left as an unformalised change of variables. This
tide closes that gap with a small generic layer: the unrescaled
region integral and normalized moment of an arbitrary loss over an
arbitrary region (of which the package moments are instances by
\code{rfl}), the translated region $\{w : w - p \in U\}$, and the
Lebesgue-substitution identities showing the located moment is
exactly the physical Gibbs moment of the translated loss --- at every
scale $q$ without any denominator control (numerator and denominator
are individually equal), and at every positive temperature in the
literal $e^{-t\Lambda}$ form. Constant-shift invariance of the
normalized moment comes along for free, and a bonus theorem
discharges the recovery headlines' symmetry hypotheses at analytic
points: every iterated derivative of an analytic function is
permutation-symmetric, by the $\omega$-regularity API. The file
passed its first full check with zero fix rounds.
\end{abstract}

\section{Setting}

The perimeter review identified the remaining distance between the
merged inverse-direction corpus and a literal Theorem 3.1 statement:
location recovery uncomposed, packages and symmetry assumed, the
observable data centred. The deliberation (archived in the tide log)
sharpened the plan into four tides; this is the first. The decisive
simplification, surfaced by reading \code{HessianMoments} before
consulting: \code{posteriorIntegral} is \emph{already} the unrescaled
$U$-restricted integral $\int \mathbf{1}_U\, f\, e^{-L/q^2}$ --- the
$q^d e^{-L(0)/q^2}$ prefactor form is a separate dilation identity
--- so translation never interacts with the rescaling at all.

\section{The thing formalised}

\begin{sloppypar}
\code{regionIntegralQ}, \code{regionMomentQ}, \code{regionMomentT}:
the $V$-restricted Gibbs integral and normalized moment of a loss
$\Lambda$, at scale $q$ and in the physical temperature form. The
package moments are these verbatim
(\code{posteriorMoment\_eq\_regionMomentQ} is \code{rfl}).
\code{regionIntegralQ\_translate} is one application of
\code{integral\_add\_left\_eq\_self}: the indicator of the translated
region evaluated at $p + y$ collapses to the indicator of $U$ at $y$,
and $\Lambda((p+y)-p) = \Lambda(y)$. Both quotient identities follow
componentwise, so they hold for \emph{every} $q$, junk cases
included. The temperature bridge
(\code{regionMomentT\_eq\_regionMomentQ}) holds for $0 < t$ --- below
zero the corpus's $(\sqrt{t})^{-1}$ parametrization has junk
semantics and the physical form genuinely differs, exactly as the
consult warned. \code{regionMomentQ\_sub\_const} factors
$e^{a/q^2}$ out of numerator and denominator and cancels it by
\code{mul\_div\_mul\_left}. The bonus
\code{AnalyticAt.iteratedFDeriv\_isSymm} is three lines:
\code{AnalyticAt.contDiffAt} at grade $\omega$ feeds
\code{ContDiffAt.iteratedFDeriv\_comp\_perm}, whose conclusion is the
corpus's \code{IsSymm} definitionally.
\end{sloppypar}

\section{Where progress stalled}

It did not --- zero fix rounds, a first for this programme. The
gotcha catalogue accounts for it: \code{beta\_reduce} before
indicator rewrites inside \code{integral\_congr\_ae}, the
function-first argument order of \code{integral\_add\_left\_eq\_self}
(caught at writing time; part 2 of the singular arc paid for that
lesson), \code{open scoped ContDiff} before naming $\omega$, and the
exponential-factor cancellation idiom. The consult's design
corrections (raw loss in the definition, shift invariance as a
separate lemma, translated region as a set-builder rather than
\code{+\textsubscript{v}} notation) each preempted a class of
friction.
