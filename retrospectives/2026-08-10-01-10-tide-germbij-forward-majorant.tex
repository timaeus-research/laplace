\begin{abstract}
The architecture consult called this theorem the numerator stage's
``key hidden dependency'': everything the dominated-convergence step
needs about the integrand, in one statement. On the mesoscopic
window, eventually in $q$,
\[
  \Big| e^{-(L(qz)-L(0))/q^2}
    - e^{-T_2(z)} \sum_{j=0}^{N} P_j(z)\, q^j \Big|
  \;\le\;
  C\,(1+\|z\|)^{(N+2)(N+1)}\; q^N\; e^{-(\lambda/4)\|z\|^2},
\]
with $C$ explicit: the domain's Taylor-window constant, plus
$M^{N+1}/(N+1)!$ from the exponential remainder, plus the tail
coefficient sum through degree $N^2$. The proof factors the true
Boltzmann factor through the exponent split --- whose critical-point
hypothesis is now the bridge theorem of the previous tide, not an
assumption --- and telescopes the difference into the three scalar
pieces prepared in 5b: the perturbation strip carrying
$|q^N\rho_q(z)|$, the unrestricted exponential Taylor remainder at
$-A(q,z)$ with the multinomial extraction
$|A|^{N+1} \le q^{N+1}(\sum_s |V_s(z)|)^{N+1}$, and the graded
polynomial tail carrying $q^{N+1}$. Every exponential factor absorbs
into the weakened Gaussian of 5c-pre.
\end{abstract}

\section{Setting}

Stage 5c-i of the forward-expansion programme. The four preceding
tides were built so that this one would be assembly: 5a supplied the
coefficient growth, 5b the three scalar estimates, 5c-pre the bridge
and the absorption. The assembly is still the longest single proof of
the programme (one theorem, $\sim$260 lines), but it compiled on the
second build attempt with six local fixes and no restructuring ---
the decomposition did its job.

\section{The thing formalised}

\begin{sloppypar}
\code{sum\_range\_shift\_eq\_sum\_Icc} aligns the exponent split's
$\sum_{s \in \mathrm{range}\,N} q^{s+1} V_{s+1}$ with the graded
expansion's $\sum_{s \in \mathrm{Icc}\,1\,N} V_s q^s$. The majorant
\code{normalized\_window\_remainder\_bound} then: obtains the
absorption, ball-landing, and $q \in (0,1)$ facts eventually;
factors via \code{exponent\_split} discharged by
\code{taylorHomogeneousTerm\_one\_eq\_zero}; sets $S$ (the
homogeneous correction), $R$ (the scaled perturbation), and the
graded polynomial $G$; and bounds the telescope
$|e^{-(S+R)} - \Sigma| \le |e^{-(S+R)} - e^{-S}| +
|e^{-S} - G(q)| + |G(q) - \Sigma|$ piece by piece. The pieces reuse,
in order: \code{abs\_exp\_neg\_add\_sub\_exp\_neg\_le} with
\code{abs\_scaledRem\_le}; \code{abs\_exp\_sub\_sum\_le} with
\code{abs\_exponent\_sum\_le} and the growth bound $M(1+\|z\|)^{N+2}$
on $\sum_s|V_s|$; and \code{gradedExpPoly\_tail\_bound} with the 5a
coefficient growth. Absorption closes each piece at rate $\lambda/4$.
\end{sloppypar}

\section{Where progress stalled}

\begin{sloppypar}
Six small fixes on one round, all catalogued classes or new
catalogue entries: \code{add\_le\_add\_right} adds on the left in
this Mathlib (use \code{add\_le\_add h le\_rfl});
\code{positivity} cannot see nonnegativity of opaque structure
fields (derive a local fact for \code{remConst} once, thread it);
\code{Metric.mem\_ball\_zero\_iff} does not exist on this pin
(rewrite through \code{Metric.mem\_ball} and
\code{dist\_zero\_right}); a \code{gcongr} discharged its own side
goal; and --- the one genuinely structural point --- the
\code{set ... with} folding of $R$ must happen \emph{after} the
absorption instance is obtained, so the fold reaches into that
hypothesis and \code{linarith}-style steps see one atom.
\end{sloppypar}

\section{Mathlib lookup versus new mathematics}

Lookup: \code{Finset.sum\_Icc\_succ\_top}, \code{Metric.mem\_ball},
\code{abs\_sub\_le}, and the mono/absolute-value toolkit. Everything
else was this programme's own five preceding stages. New mathematics:
none --- the theorem is the disciplined sum of its parts, which was
the design.
