\begin{abstract}
The divisor density of unit~70 is measurable and integrable on the box, and positive when
$\eta(0,\cdot)>0$, so its integral $C(\xi,\eta)=\frac1{k_0}\int v^{h'}(v^{k'})^{-p}A_{p-1}(\xi(0,v))\eta(0,v)\,dv$
is a positive constant and the state integral satisfies $Z(n)\sim C(\xi,\eta)\,n^{-p/2}$. Along the
way: the fluctuation mass $A_\gamma(a)$ is monotone, hence measurable, in $a$. Zero
\texttt{sorry}/\texttt{axiom}.
\end{abstract}

\section{Proof strategy}
Monotonicity of $a\mapsto A_\gamma(a)$ is pointwise monotonicity of $e^{\beta a t}$; measurability
follows. The density is dominated by $K\prod_iv_i^{h'_i}(v_i^{k'_i})^{-p}$ with $K$ built from the
compact bounds on $\xi(0,\cdot),\eta(0,\cdot)$ and the monotonicity of $A$; the weight is a product of
single-coordinate integrable functions. Positivity is \texttt{integral\_pos\_iff\_support\_of\_nonneg\_ae}
with the whole box inside the support and $\texttt{boxMeasure}(\text{box})=(\mathrm{ofReal}\,b)^d>0$
(\texttt{Measure.pi\_pi}). The equivalence multiplies the limit of $n^{p/2}Z$ by $n^{-p/2}$.

\section{Roadblocks and resolutions}
\begin{itemize}
\item A bare \texttt{by} block as the left argument of \texttt{.trans} has no expected type; state
the intermediate fact with \texttt{have}.
\item The \texttt{rpow} exponent identity closes with \texttt{ring\_nf} rather than \texttt{congr 1; ring}.
\end{itemize}

\section{Outcome}
For a unique minimal coordinate the state-density coefficient is positive and gives a sharp asymptotic
equivalence. Remaining in Programme~A: the Fubini bridge to the $(d+1)$-dimensional box; then Programme~B
(several minimal coordinates).
