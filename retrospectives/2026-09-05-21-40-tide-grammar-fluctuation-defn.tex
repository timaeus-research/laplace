\begin{abstract}
The first unit of the Stage-1 autoformalisation of the grammar paper (\emph{Expectations and the
Exceptional Divisor}) \S4. We define Watanabe's fluctuation function
$S_\lambda(a)=\int_0^\infty t^{\lambda-1}e^{-\beta t+\beta a\sqrt t}\,dt$, prove it integrable for
every $a$, and evaluate the base case $S_\lambda(0)=\beta^{-\lambda}\Gamma(\lambda)$. All three land
first-attempt against existing Mathlib Gamma-integral machinery; zero \texttt{sorry}/\texttt{axiom}.
\end{abstract}

\section{Setting}
The grammar paper's headline asymptotic
$Z_n[\phi]\sim\sum_{\mu,m}C_{\mu,m}\,n^{-\mu}(\log n)^{m-1}$ (\S4) is assembled from derivatives of
the \emph{fluctuation function} (Watanabe, Def.~5.8), which packages the interaction between Gaussian
fluctuations and the power-law singularity of the resolved phase. The \texttt{laplace} seabed already
carries a deep 1D/2D Laplace-expansion body, but \S4 was untouched: no fluctuation, Weber, or Hermite
material anywhere. This unit opens that block with the object everything else in \S4 is built from.
It is the minimal foundational step: the definition plus the two facts (well-definedness and the base
value) that every subsequent property presupposes.

\section{The thing formalised}
For $\beta,\lambda>0$,
\[
  S_\lambda(a) \;=\; \int_0^\infty t^{\lambda-1}\,e^{-\beta t+\beta a\sqrt t}\,dt ,
  \qquad
  S_\lambda(0)=\beta^{-\lambda}\Gamma(\lambda).
\]
Lean signatures (namespace \texttt{Laplace.Grammar}, file \texttt{Laplace/Grammar/Fluctuation.lean}):
\begin{verbatim}
noncomputable def fluctuation (β lam a : ℝ) : ℝ :=
  ∫ t in Set.Ioi (0:ℝ), t ^ (lam - 1) * Real.exp (-β * t + β * a * Real.sqrt t)

theorem fluctuation_integrableOn (β lam : ℝ) (hβ : 0 < β) (hlam : 0 < lam) (a : ℝ) :
    IntegrableOn (fun t => t ^ (lam - 1) *
      Real.exp (-β * t + β * a * Real.sqrt t)) (Set.Ioi 0)

theorem fluctuation_zero (β lam : ℝ) (hβ : 0 < β) (hlam : 0 < lam) :
    fluctuation β lam 0 = β ^ (-lam) * Real.Gamma lam
\end{verbatim}

\section{Proof strategy}
\emph{Base value.} At $a=0$ the phase collapses to $-\beta t$, and the integrand is exactly the
integrand of Mathlib's \texttt{integral\_rpow\_mul\_exp\_neg\_mul\_rpow} at $p=1$, $q=\lambda-1$,
$b=\beta$, whose value $b^{-(q+1)/p}(1/p)\,\Gamma((q+1)/p)$ specialises to $\beta^{-\lambda}\Gamma(\lambda)$.
A \texttt{setIntegral\_congr\_fun} rewrites $\beta\cdot 0\cdot\sqrt t$ away and $-\beta t$ into
$-\beta\,t^{1}$ (\texttt{rpow\_one}) to match the lemma verbatim.

\emph{Integrability.} Completing the square gives the AM--GM bound $a\sqrt t\le t/2+a^2/2$, i.e.
$\tfrac12(\sqrt t-a)^2\ge 0$, so $e^{-\beta t+\beta a\sqrt t}\le e^{\beta a^2/2}e^{-(\beta/2)t}$. Hence
the integrand is dominated by $e^{\beta a^2/2}\,t^{\lambda-1}e^{-(\beta/2)t}$, which is Gamma-integrable
(\texttt{integrableOn\_rpow\_mul\_exp\_neg\_mul\_rpow} at $p=1$, $b=\beta/2$, times a constant). The
assembly is \texttt{Integrable.mono'}: the majorant, \texttt{AEStronglyMeasurable} of the integrand
(\texttt{ContinuousOn} on $(0,\infty)$ --- \texttt{rpow\_const} off zero times a continuous exponential),
and the a.e.\ pointwise bound, the last closed by
\texttt{nlinarith [sq\_nonneg (√t - a), Real.sq\_sqrt ht0.le]}.

\section{What was Mathlib, what was new}
Entirely a Mathlib assembly: the two Gamma-integral lemmas (value and integrability) are the whole
analytic content; the ``new'' part is only the AM--GM domination and the base-value bookkeeping. This
is exactly the intended character of a Stage-1 autoformalisation unit --- evaluate existing machinery
on the paper's specific integrand.

\section{Lessons}
The \texttt{laplace} Gamma-integral toolkit (\texttt{integral\_rpow\_mul\_exp\_neg\_mul\_rpow} and its
integrability companion, both at $p=1$) is the natural substrate for the entire fluctuation-function
family: every $\lambda\mapsto\lambda+\tfrac12$ shift in \S4 is a re-indexing of the same Gamma
integral. Recorded for the next units.
