\begin{abstract}
Deterministic half of Astra~\#11 rank~4 (the ordered-remainder statement of
thm:strataempiricalexpansion). Given $Z(N)=\sum_{\gamma\in P}N^{-\gamma}(A_\gamma\log N+B_\gamma)+R(N)$
with $|R(N)|\le KN^{-E}(1+\log N)$, bounded coefficients $|A_\gamma|,|B_\gamma|\le K_c$ and a gap
$\delta>0$ above $\alpha\in P$, the two normalised remainders satisfy for $N\ge e$
\[
\Big|\frac{Z(N)-L_{<\alpha}(N)}{N^{-\alpha}\log N}-A_\alpha\Big|\le\mathrm{errA}(N),\qquad
\Big|\frac{Z(N)-L_{<\alpha}(N)-N^{-\alpha}A_\alpha\log N}{N^{-\alpha}}-B_\alpha\Big|\le\mathrm{errB}(N),
\]
with explicit rates $\mathrm{errA}(N)=2(KN^{\alpha-E}+|P|K_cN^{-\delta})+K_c/\log N$ and
$\mathrm{errB}(N)=(KN^{\alpha-E}+|P|K_cN^{-\delta})(1+\log N)$, both tending to $0$
(\texttt{normalised\_A\_le}, \texttt{normalised\_B\_le}, \texttt{tendsto\_errA}, \texttt{tendsto\_errB}).
Zero \texttt{sorry}/\texttt{axiom}.
\end{abstract}

\section{Mathematics}
Split the finite expansion at $\alpha$: $\sum_P=L_{<\alpha}+N^{-\alpha}(A_\alpha\log N+B_\alpha)+U_{>\alpha}$.
The upper part is bounded by $|P|\,N^{-(\alpha+\delta)}K_c(1+\log N)$ because every exponent above
$\alpha$ is at least $\alpha+\delta$ and $N\ge1$. Then
$(Z-L_{<\alpha})/(N^{-\alpha}\log N)-A_\alpha=(R+U)/(N^{-\alpha}\log N)+B_\alpha/\log N$, and for
$\log N\ge1$ the factor $(1+\log N)/\log N\le2$ gives $\mathrm{errA}$. The B-slot has no
$1/\log N$ term but keeps the $(1+\log N)$ growth, harmless against $N^{-\delta}$. The rates tend to
zero by $N^{-a}\to0$ and $\log N=o(N^a)$ (Mathlib \texttt{isLittleO\_log\_rpow\_atTop}).

\section{Paper-facing meaning}
The paper's second conclusion in thm:strataempiricalexpansion is that the normalised remainder
converges in distribution to $C_{\mu',m'}(G)$. Astra~\#11 stressed two points that this unit makes
precise: the two slots ($\log N$ coefficient and constant) must be treated in order, and the
B-slot must subtract the CURRENT random coefficient $A_\alpha(\xi_n)$, not its limit. With the
uniform Taylor tree (unit~135) supplying $K$ independent of the data on a bounded family and the
envelope bounds (unit~134) supplying $K_c$, the deterministic error is uniform over the family, so
the probabilistic statement reduces to the continuous-mapping transfer (unit~147) plus a
deterministic $o(1)$ perturbation (Slutsky: \texttt{TendstoInDistribution.add\_of\_tendstoInMeasure\_const}).
That wrapper needs the measurability of $\omega\mapsto Z_n(\omega)$, i.e.\ joint continuity of the
amplitude coefficients in $(a,s)$ on \texttt{CoeffPair}~$\times\mathbb R$: the next unit.

\section{Lean notes}
\begin{itemize}
\item Splitting a \texttt{Finset} sum at a member: \texttt{Finset.add\_sum\_erase} then
\texttt{Finset.sum\_filter\_add\_sum\_filter\_not} on the erased set, with two small
\texttt{Finset.ext} identifications of the filters.
\item \texttt{Real.add\_one\_le\_exp 0} does not give $1\le e$ in a usable form; use
\texttt{Real.one\_le\_exp zero\_le\_one}. $1\le\log N\iff e\le N$ is
\texttt{Real.le\_log\_iff\_exp\_le}.
\item Powers: prove \texttt{N\^{}(α−E) * N\^{}(−α) = N\^{}(−E)} once via \texttt{← Real.rpow\_add} and
rewrite backwards (\texttt{rw [← hE1, ← hE2]; ring}) to pass from $N^{-E}$ to $N^{\alpha-E}N^{-\alpha}$.
\item \texttt{div\_le\_iff₀} turns the normalised bound into a product bound; \texttt{field\_simp; ring}
closes the two algebraic identities for the normalised remainders.
\item \texttt{Real.tendsto\_log\_atTop.inv\_tendsto\_atTop.const\_mul Kc} with
\texttt{simpa [div\_eq\_mul\_inv]} gives $K_c/\log N\to0$.
\end{itemize}
