\begin{abstract}
Completion of Astra~\#11 rank~4. For measurable random Taylor data $X_n\colon\Omega\to\texttt{CoeffPair}$
with $\|X_n\|\le M$, converging in distribution to $Z$, and a scale $N_n\to\infty$, the ordered
normalised remainders of the chart integral converge in distribution to the limiting canonical
coefficients:
\[
\frac{Z_{N_n}(X_n)-L_{<\alpha}}{N_n^{-\alpha}\log N_n}\Rightarrow A_\alpha(Z),\qquad
\frac{Z_{N_n}(X_n)-L_{<\alpha}-N_n^{-\alpha}A_\alpha(X_n)\log N_n}{N_n^{-\alpha}}\Rightarrow B_\alpha(Z)
\]
(\texttt{tendstoInDistribution\_normA}, \texttt{tendstoInDistribution\_normB}), for every retained
exponent $\alpha$ of the Taylor tree. This is the $d=2$ chart-level form, for bounded analytic
families, of the second conclusion of thm:strataempiricalexpansion. Zero \texttt{sorry}/\texttt{axiom}.
\end{abstract}

\section{Mathematics}
Write the normalised remainder as $A_\alpha(X_n)+\varepsilon_n$. On the ball $\|a\|\le M$ the uniform
Taylor tree (unit~149) gives the remainder bound with the data-independent constant
$K=|\texttt{uniformTreeConst}|$, the envelope bounds (unit~134) give a uniform coefficient bound $K_c$
(a sum of absolute values of the per-exponent envelope constants, so that each term is dominated
without a separate nonnegativity argument), and the exponent set has a gap $\delta$ above $\alpha$;
so unit~148's deterministic bound gives $|\varepsilon_n(\omega)|\le\mathrm{errA}(N_n)\to0$ uniformly
in $\omega$. Hence $\varepsilon_n\to0$ in probability (indeed the exceptional set is eventually
empty), $A_\alpha(X_n)\Rightarrow A_\alpha(Z)$ by the continuous mapping theorem (unit~147), and
Slutsky's theorem gives the claim. The B-slot is identical with the current $A_\alpha(X_n)$ removed
first, exactly as Astra~\#11 insisted.

\section{Paper-facing meaning}
thm:strataempiricalexpansion states that
\[
\frac{Z_n^0-\sum_{(\mu,m)<(\mu',m')}C_{\mu,m}(\xi_n)n^{-\mu}(\log n)^{m-1}}{n^{-\mu'}(\log n)^{m'-1}}
\to C_{\mu',m'}(G)
\]
in distribution. For $d=2$, one chart, and Taylor data confined to a norm ball,
this is now a theorem: the two slots $(\alpha,2)$ (the $\log$ coefficient) and $(\alpha,1)$ (the
constant) are handled in order. Limitations, to be stated in staging: bounded families (the natural
upgrade localises by $\Pr(\|X_n\|>M)$); a common sample space (Mathlib's Slutsky is stated for a
fixed $\Omega$); the functional CLT $\xi_n\Rightarrow G$ and the resolution/chart assembly are not
formalised.

\section{Lean notes}
\begin{itemize}
\item \texttt{Finset.exists\_min\_image} on the filter $\{\gamma\in P:\alpha<\gamma\}$ gives the gap;
the empty case takes $\delta=1$.
\item No nonnegativity lemmas exist for the envelope constants \texttt{canonUEnv} etc.; wrapping
each per-exponent bound in $|\cdot|$ and summing makes \texttt{Finset.single\_le\_sum} applicable
with \texttt{abs\_nonneg}. Likewise $K=|\texttt{uniformTreeConst}|$ avoids proving the constant
nonnegative.
\item \texttt{tendstoInMeasure\_iff\_norm} turns convergence in measure into statements about
$\mu\{\varepsilon\le\|f_n-g\|\}$; when $|f_n|\le e_n<\varepsilon$ everywhere the set is empty and
\texttt{tendsto\_const\_nhds.congr'} finishes. \texttt{Set.mem\_setOf\_eq} is deprecated for
\texttt{Set.mem\_ofPred\_eq}.
\item \texttt{TendstoInDistribution.add\_of\_tendstoInMeasure\_const} needs
\texttt{[l.IsCountablyGenerated]} and a fixed sample space; the final \texttt{congr} step is
\texttt{simp only [Pi.add\_apply, Function.comp]; ring}.
\end{itemize}
