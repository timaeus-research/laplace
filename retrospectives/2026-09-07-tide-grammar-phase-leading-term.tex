\begin{abstract}
Unit 202 closes the deterministic core of programme~A. For continuous phase $\xi$ and amplitude
$\eta$ on $\mathbb R^d$, exponents $h,k$ with $\lambda=\min_i(h_i+1)/(2k_i)$, minimiser set $J$,
$m=|J|$, $p=2\lambda$, and $s=Nu^k$,
\begin{align*}
\frac{\int_{(0,1]^d}\eta(u)\,u^h\,e^{-\beta s^2+\beta s\,\xi(u)}\,du}{N^{-p}(\log N)^{m-1}}
\;\longrightarrow\;
&2^{m-1}\cdot2\cdot\frac{1}{(m-1)!\prod_{i\in J}2k_i}\\
&\quad\times\int_{(0,1]^d}\eta(\pi u)\,S^{(\beta)}_{p/2}(\xi(\pi u))\prod_{i\notin J}u_i^{h_i-2k_i\lambda}\,du
\end{align*}
(\texttt{phase\_leading\_tendsto}, limit \texttt{phaseCoeff}), where $\pi$ sets the minimising
coordinates to zero and $S^{(\beta)}_{p/2}(a)=\int_0^\infty s^{p-1}e^{-\beta s^2+\beta as}\,ds$. The
proof is the Taylor-in-phase argument of units 199--201 assembled with \texttt{tendsto\_of\_approx}:
the $2K$-term Taylor approximants converge by the shifted-exponent theorem; the $N$-side remainder is
bounded by $M_\eta\,\tau_K\int u^he^{-(\beta/4)N^2u^{2k}}$, whose normalised version converges to a
$K$-independent constant; and the distance of \texttt{phaseCoeff} to the finite coefficient sum is
bounded by the same $\tau_K$ against the limiting face measure. Numerical check ($d=2$,
$h=(0,1)$, $k=(1,1)$, $\lambda=\tfrac12$, $J=\{0\}$, $\xi(u)=u_1$, $\eta=1$, $\beta=1$):
$N\cdot I_N=1.2332,\ 1.2378$ at $N=10^2,10^3$ against $\int_0^1S(v)\,dv=1.2383$. Zero
\texttt{sorry}/\texttt{axiom}; 9079 jobs.
\end{abstract}

\section{Mathematics}
Three bookkeeping facts make the assembly exact. (i) $u^{h+jk}=u^h(u^k)^j$ and $N^j(u^k)^j=s^j$, so the
$j$-th Taylor term of $e^{\beta s\xi}$ is literally the shifted-exponent integrand with amplitude
$\xi^j\eta$ (\texttt{taylor\_term\_eq}). (ii) The Gaussian moments
$\int_0^\infty s^{p-1}s^je^{-\beta s^2}ds=\tfrac12\Gamma(\lambda+j/2)\beta^{-\lambda-j/2}$ turn the
finite coefficient sum $\sum_j\frac{\beta^j}{j!}2^{m-1}\,\mathrm{amplitudeCoeff}(h+jk,\dots)(\xi^j\eta)$
into $2^{m-1}\cdot2\cdot\mathrm{faceNorm}\int\eta(\pi u)\bigl[\sum_j\frac{(\beta\xi(\pi u))^j}{j!}\tfrac12\Gamma\beta^{-}\bigr]w(u)\,du$,
the finite Taylor approximant of $S$ under the face measure. (iii) Both remainders are controlled
by the one tail constant $\tau_K$ of unit~199, uniformly because $|\beta\xi|\le\beta\|\xi\|_\infty$
on the cube; the $N$-side normaliser $\int u^he^{-(\beta/4)N^2u^{2k}}/(N^{-2\lambda}(\log N)^{m-1})$
converges (bare monomial theorem at $\beta/4$, square-parameter transport) and is eventually
$\le L+1$. Given $\varepsilon$, choose $K$ with $\tau_K\max(C_1,C_2)\le\varepsilon$ and apply
\texttt{tendsto\_of\_approx}. No infinite series, no Cauchy limit, no $\Sigma/\!\int$ interchange
beyond finite sums, as Astra~\#21 recommended.

\section{Lean notes}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Single-step \texttt{calc} followed by a dedented \texttt{have}}: after a one-step
  \texttt{calc a ≤ b := by …}, the calc-steps parser saves its position at the \emph{next token},
  so a following \texttt{have h := e} at any column is parsed as a further calc step (a term-level
  \texttt{have}) and the parser dies at the next line with ``expected \texttt{:=}'' — here on the
  \texttt{at} of \texttt{rw [...] at h}. The symptom is a parse error two lines below the real
  culprit plus ``unknown identifier'' for everything the \texttt{have} defined. Fix: do not end a
  tactic block with a single-step calc (use \texttt{gcongr} directly), or give the calc two steps.
\item \texttt{have h := …} silently shadows the exponent vector \texttt{h}; the next
  \texttt{faceProj h k l} then fails with a Prop-vs-\texttt{Fin d → ℕ} mismatch. Name auxiliary
  facts \texttt{hnorm}.
\item \texttt{gcongr} discharges the side goal \texttt{0 ≤ Mη} itself when it is an assumption;
  the remaining goal is only the main inequality \texttt{|η x| ≤ Mη}.
\item Passing \texttt{\_} for the continuous function in
  \texttt{integrableOn\_face\_mul … \_ (by fun\_prop)} leaves a metavariable that
  higher-order unification (\texttt{?f (faceProj u)}) cannot solve; give \texttt{f} explicitly.
\item \texttt{sed 's/\textbackslash bshiftExp\textbackslash b/…/'} does not rename
  \texttt{prod\_pow\_shiftExp}: \texttt{\_} is a word character. Unit~200's lemmas are now
  \texttt{*\_phaseShift} (renamed in this commit).
\item \texttt{simp only [mul\_sub, sub\_mul, Finset.mul\_sum, Finset.sum\_mul]} closes the
  distributivity identity between a difference of products and a sum of products without
  \texttt{ring} (which cannot see inside \texttt{Finset.sum}).
\end{itemize}
