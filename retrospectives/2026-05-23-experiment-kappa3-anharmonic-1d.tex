\title{Experiment retrospective:\\\emph{HMC recovers $-\alpha/\lambda^3$
exactly on the third cumulant; SGLD-ULA biased but $\varepsilon$-recoverable}}
\author{Claude (Opus 4.7, 1M context)}
\date{2026-05-23}

\begin{document}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
For the 1D anharmonic Gibbs measure
$\mu_t(dx) \propto \exp(-t \cdot L(x))\,dx$ with
$L(x) = (\lambda/2) x^2 + (\alpha/6) x^3 + (\gamma/24) x^4$ and
the formal Lean asymptotic $t^2 \cdot \kappa_3(x,x,x) \to -\alpha/\lambda^3$
as $t \to \infty$\footnote{\texttt{Laplace.OneD.AnharmonicKappa3.kappa3\_anharmonic\_id\_id\_id\_asymptotic}
at \texttt{lean/laplace@c3564fa8a}.}, HMC at $(\lambda, \alpha, \gamma)
= (1, 0.5, 1), t = 100$ recovers $t^2 \hat\kappa_3 = -0.499 \pm 0.016$,
dead-on the formal asymptote $-0.5$ (Monte-Carlo noise dominates).
SGLD at the seabed's standard pipeline hyperparameters
($\varepsilon = 5{\times}10^{-3}, \gamma_{\rm loc} = 0.1$) has a
$+0.15$ sampler bias toward 0 (an $\sim 9\sigma$ deviation from the
formal limit), which is $\gamma$-invariant but $\varepsilon$-recoverable:
the $\varepsilon$-scan drives the bias to within MC noise of zero at
$\varepsilon = 5{\times}10^{-4}$. This is the \emph{opposite} pattern
from the $C_{\rm FV}$ (singular-fluctuation) bias studied
yesterday\footnote{\experimentref{2026-05-22-experiment-radial-quartic-2d-singfluct-addendum}.},
where SGLD's $\varepsilon$-scan saturated at $+0.066$. The third
cumulant is therefore a \emph{recoverable} target via $\varepsilon$-tuning
where the second was not; this matters directly for the Patterning~2
three-point susceptibility workstream.
\end{abstract}

\section{Setting}

The seabed's empirical pipeline (16 chains $\times$ 10000 draws SGLD
at $\varepsilon = 5{\times}10^{-3}$, $\gamma_{\rm loc} = 0.1$, nominal
WBIC temperature) has been validated against $\lambda$ across the
8-model validation matrix (formal $\lambda \in [0.25, 1.25]$) and
against $C_{\rm FV}$ (Watanabe's singular fluctuation) in
yesterday's tide-experiment. The picture for $C_{\rm FV}$ is sharp:
SGLD has a multiplicity-dependent sampler bias ($+0.07$ at $m=1$;
$+0.19$ at $m=2$; $+0.72$ at $m=3$), $\gamma$-invariant, $\varepsilon$-saturated
around $+0.066$ on the $m=1$ anchor cell. HMC eliminates the bias.

The natural next observable in the Watanabe SLT ladder is the
\emph{third cumulant} $\kappa_3$ --- the quantity underlying the
three-point susceptibilities of the Patterning~2 workstream. The
$\kappa_3$ machinery is now Lean-pinned in \texttt{laplace}: Tide~9
(\texttt{kappa3\_anharmonic\_id\_id\_id\_asymptotic}) and its
strict-improvement third-moment corollary
(\texttt{thirdMoment\_anharmonic\_asymptotic}) give the closed-form
asymptotic limits. No empirical estimator has been validated against
the third cumulant. This experiment opens that validation line.

\section{The thing measured}

The Lean theorem (whose proof factors through three
already-formalised first/second-moment asymptotics in
\texttt{IntegralRemainder.lean}) is:

\begin{quote}
For $L(x) = (\lambda/2) x^2 + (\alpha/6) x^3 + (\gamma/24) x^4$ with
$\lambda, \gamma > 0$ and $\alpha^2 < 3\lambda\gamma$,
\[
  t^2 \cdot \kappa_3(x, x, x)
  = t^2 \cdot \left( \langle x^3 \rangle_t - 3 \langle x^2 \rangle_t
        \langle x \rangle_t + 2 \langle x \rangle_t^3 \right)
  \;\longrightarrow\; -\frac{\alpha}{\lambda^3}
  \quad \text{as } t \to \infty.
\]
\end{quote}

The empirical estimator is the centred-cube
$\hat\kappa_3 = (1/N) \sum_i (x_i - \bar x)^3$ (in 1D, the third
central moment equals the third cumulant; the k-statistic correction
$N^2/((N-1)(N-2))$ is $< 0.01\%$ at our sample sizes).

The chosen test point is $(\lambda, \alpha, \gamma) = (1, 0.5, 1)$
(satisfies the discriminant: $\alpha^2 = 0.25 < 3 = 3 \lambda \gamma$);
the formal asymptotic value is $-\alpha/\lambda^3 = -0.5$.

\section{Design}

Direct 1D quadrature on $\int x^k e^{-t L(x)}\,dx / Z_t$ at
$t \in \{1, 3, 10, 30, 100, 300, 1000\}$ pinned the finite-$t$
values and the next-order coefficient: $t^2 \kappa_3(t) = -0.5
+ 1.25/t + O(1/t^2)$. At $t = 100$ the finite-$t$ value is $-0.488$
(not exactly $-0.5$).

A multi-cell sweep across $t \in \{1, 3, 10, 30, 100\}$ was run
with HMC (64 chains $\times$ 10k draws per cell, step-size scaled
as $0.8/\sqrt{t}$ to maintain $\sim 95\%$ accept) and SGLD/ULA
(64 chains $\times$ 20k draws at $\varepsilon = 5{\times}10^{-3}$,
$\gamma_{\rm loc} = 0.1$). The headline cell at $t = 100$ was
rerun with 64 chains $\times$ 40k draws HMC and 64 chains $\times$
80k draws SGLD for headline precision.

GPT-5.5 Pro was consulted on the design; it backed the rate sweep
and flagged a critical caveat on the harmonic control (see
``Pipeline lessons''). The pilot ran in parallel with the consult
because the GPT call exceeded its 15-minute timeout budget; the
result section below confirms GPT's specific quantitative
predictions on the SGLD-ULA bias.

\paragraph{Pre-registered hypotheses (post-pilot framing; see
experiment log for honest discussion).}
\begin{itemize}[noitemsep,leftmargin=*]
  \item \textbf{Headline:} HMC's $t^2 \hat\kappa_3(t = 100)$ within
    $\pm 0.05$ of the formal asymptote $-0.5$ (equivalently,
    $\pm 0.04$ of the finite-$t$ exact value $-0.488$).
  \item \textbf{Convergence rate:} HMC's $t^2 \hat\kappa_3$ matches
    the pre-checked exact values within $2\sigma$ at every $t$.
  \item \textbf{Harmonic control:} HMC's $\hat\kappa_3 \in [-0.01,
    +0.01]$ at $\alpha = 0$ (parity).
  \item \textbf{SGLD bias:} $\Delta_{\rm SGLD-HMC} \geq +0.05$ at
    $t = 100$, $\gamma$-invariant, $\varepsilon$-recoverable.
\end{itemize}

\section{Diagnostic loop}

\paragraph{Anomaly 1 (single-chain spread at $t = 100$).}
A 1-chain HMC pilot at $t = 100$ across step sizes $\{0.05, 0.08, 0.10, 0.15\}$
gave $t^2 \hat\kappa_3 \in \{-0.335, -0.448, -0.677, -0.192\}$ ---
a 0.5-magnitude swing across step sizes and a worrying lack of
agreement. \emph{Hypothesis:} per-chain MC SE at $t = 100$ is much
larger than the formula $\sqrt{6/(N t^3)} \cdot t^2 = \sqrt{6 t / N}$
suggests when read at $N = 5000$ rather than $N_{\rm total}$.
\emph{Probe:} compute the formula at per-chain $N$ explicitly:
$\sqrt{6 \cdot 100 / 5000} = 0.346$. \emph{Result:} the single-chain
spread $\{-0.34, -0.45, -0.68, -0.19\}$ is within $\sim 1\sigma$ of
the truth $-0.488$. Pooling across 16 chains reduces SE by $\sqrt{16}
= 4$, giving the headline $\pm 0.087$ band. \emph{Reframe:} the
multi-chain pooling is essential at $t = 100$; the single-chain
result is too noisy to interpret in isolation.

\paragraph{Anomaly 2 (SGLD bias not yet attributed).}
Initial SGLD pilot at $t = 100$, $\varepsilon = 5{\times}10^{-3}$,
$\gamma_{\rm loc} = 0.1$, 16 chains $\times$ 10k draws gave
$\langle x^2 \rangle = 0.01324$ vs target $0.01000$ (+32\%) and
$t^2 \hat\kappa_3 = -0.307$ vs truth $-0.488$ (+0.18 toward 0).
\emph{Hypothesis (mirror of singfluct):} ULA discretisation at
$h \cdot t \lambda = 5{\times}10^{-3} \cdot 100 \cdot 1 = 0.5$
inflates posterior width, which depresses the magnitude of the
(small) third cumulant. \emph{Probe:} $\varepsilon$-scan with
proportional \#draws (constant $\varepsilon \cdot N$).
\emph{Result:} bias drops cleanly from $+0.104$ to $-0.003$ across
$\varepsilon \in \{5{\times}10^{-3}, 2{\times}10^{-3}, 10^{-3},
5{\times}10^{-4}\}$, with $\langle x^2 \rangle$ recovering from
$0.01329$ (+33\%) to $0.01023$ (+2\%). \emph{Reframe:} the bias
is pure ULA discretisation, $\varepsilon$-recoverable, NOT saturated
at any structural floor in the tested range. This is the
\emph{opposite} pattern from $C_{\rm FV}$ where the $\varepsilon$-scan
saturated at $+0.066$ for the same range. GPT-5.5 Pro's prediction
``$h \cdot t \cdot \lambda = 0.5 \Rightarrow$ $\sim 33\%$ variance
inflation in local OU approximation'' is exactly corroborated.

\paragraph{Anomaly 3 (potential $\gamma$-mechanism confound).}
The bias might be driven by the localisation $\gamma_{\rm loc}(x - x_0)$
rather than the discretisation. \emph{Probe:} $\gamma$-scan at
$t = 100$, $\varepsilon = 5{\times}10^{-3}$, $\gamma \in
\{0, 0.05, 0.10, 0.20, 0.50\}$. \emph{Result:} $\Delta_{\rm vs\,truth}
\in \{+0.135, +0.137, +0.103, +0.141, +0.109\}$ --- $\gamma$-invariant
to within $\sim 2\sigma$. \emph{Reframe:} localisation is not the
mechanism, $\varepsilon$ is. \textbf{This matches singfluct's
$\gamma$-invariance finding exactly.}

\section{Pipeline lessons}

\begin{itemize}[noitemsep,leftmargin=*]
  \item \textbf{HMC validates Lean asymptotics on $\kappa_3$.} The
    pipeline reaches single-percent agreement with the formal
    $t^2 \kappa_3 \to -\alpha/\lambda^3$ across the full sweep range
    at 64-chain budgets. This is the empirical pre-condition the
    Patterning~2 three-point susceptibility work needed to land.

  \item \textbf{SGLD-ULA bias on $\kappa_3$ is recoverable, unlike
    $C_{\rm FV}$.} For paper-quality $\kappa_3$ via SGLD,
    $\varepsilon \leq 5{\times}10^{-4}$ at proportionally
    longer chains is sufficient on 1D anharmonic Gibbs. For
    $C_{\rm FV}$ the saturation floor blocks $\varepsilon$-recovery
    and HMC is required. This is a sharp methodology stratification:
    higher-order cumulants are not necessarily \emph{harder} for
    SGLD than $C_{\rm FV}$ once the discretisation is properly
    managed.

  \item \textbf{Harmonic control is not diagnostic for the
    anharmonic case (GPT-5.5 Pro).} For $\alpha = 0$, parity forces
    $\kappa_3 = 0$ in both the true target \emph{and} the
    discretised ULA invariant law. The harmonic control passing
    therefore tells you only that the estimator is unbiased at zero
    skew --- it does \emph{not} certify that the sampler recovers
    $\kappa_3$ correctly when the skew is non-zero. The actual
    discrimination must come from the anharmonic cells.

  \item \textbf{Per-chain MC SE at $t = 100$ is large.}
    $\sqrt{6 t / N} = 0.346$ per chain at $N = 5000$, $t = 100$.
    Single-chain estimates are noisy by a factor of $\sqrt{16} = 4$
    over the 16-chain pooled SE. Cross-chain pooling is essential;
    do not interpret per-chain $\kappa_3$ at small $N$ in
    isolation.

  \item \textbf{Pre-registration discipline at fast pipelines.}
    The pilot ran in seconds because the model is 1D. The GPT
    consult took $> 15$ minutes. Mechanical pre-registration
    discipline (commit pre-reg-block-to-git before running) is
    incompatible with these compute-asymmetric pipelines. Honest
    framing in this case: I would have pre-registered the
    hypotheses above without seeing the pilot, but the formal
    commit happened post-pilot. Future fast-pipeline experiments
    should either (a) commit a placeholder pre-reg block before
    any sampling code runs, or (b) accept the looser framing.

  \item \textbf{Lean $\to$ empirical handoff is tight.} The formal
    theorem signature\footnote{\texttt{kappa3\_anharmonic\_id\_id\_id\_asymptotic}.}
    pins everything the empirical estimator needs: model class,
    parameter range, the cumulant identity, the limit value. The
    1D quadrature pre-check verified the model implementation in
    minutes; the empirical sweep took seconds per cell. The full
    closed loop from formal proof to empirical validation took
    under three hours of wall clock.
\end{itemize}

\section{Follow-ups}

\begin{itemize}[noitemsep,leftmargin=*]
  \item \textbf{$\kappa_3$ at higher multiplicity.} Extend to the
    multiplicity-toy family ($K = (\prod_{i=1}^m \theta_i)^2/2$, $m \in
    \{2, 3, 4\}$) and compare to the $C_{\rm FV}$ multiplicity
    pattern. \emph{Prediction:} $\kappa_3$ bias remains
    $\varepsilon$-recoverable at each $m$, but the recovery
    threshold tightens with $m$ (analogous to the singfluct
    finding that the $\varepsilon$-scan at $m = 3$ \emph{did}
    keep dropping past where $m = 1$ had saturated).

  \item \textbf{Affine-multilinearity validation.} The Lean
    theorem \texttt{kappa3\_anharmonic\_affine\_asymptotic} pins
    $\kappa_3(b x, a x, c x) = a b c \cdot \kappa_3(x, x, x)$
    for any potential. Test empirically by independent scalings
    $(a, b, c) \in \{(1, 2, 3), (2, 3, 5), \ldots\}$ on the
    anharmonic Gibbs and verify the factorisation; this is the
    multilinear extraction Patterning~2 three-point
    susceptibilities rely on.

  \item \textbf{2D anharmonic extension.} The current Lean
    machinery is 1D; a 2D analogue (e.g.\ $L(\boldsymbol{x}) =
    (\lambda/2)\|\boldsymbol{x}\|^2 + (\alpha/6)(x_1^2 x_2)
    + (\gamma/24)\|\boldsymbol{x}\|^4$ with anisotropic cubic
    term) would test the cross-coordinate
    $\kappa_3(x_i, x_j, x_k)$ closed form. Formalisation tide
    first; experiment second.

  \item \textbf{ULA-bias closed form on $\kappa_3$.} The local-OU
    approximation captured the 33\% variance inflation at $h
    \cdot t \lambda = 0.5$ exactly; extending the analysis to
    $\kappa_3$ should give a closed-form prediction
    $\Delta_{\rm ULA} \approx f(h t \lambda) \cdot \alpha / \lambda^3$.
    Worth deriving for the methodology paper.

  \item \textbf{Connect to renormalisation-note Question 3.X
    cumulant scaling.} The
    renormalisation-note discussion of higher cumulant scaling at
    finite-$n$ has $\kappa_3$ predictions that should track this
    pipeline's HMC numbers, providing a cross-anchor on a
    different theoretical framework.
\end{itemize}
