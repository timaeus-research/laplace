\begin{abstract}
The divide-and-integrate tower on a primitive $G(x)=\int_0^x g$ is the log-weighted integral
$H_j(y)=\frac1{j!}\int_0^y\log^j(y/t)\,g(t)\,dt$ (Fubini on the triangle $0<s\le t\le y$). For the
weighted kernel $g(t)=t^{p-1}e^{-\beta t^2+\beta at}$ this turns the exact tower reduction of unit~50
into the product-density formula for an equal-exponent chart block,
$Z_{d+1}(c)=\big(\prod_{i\le d}k_i^{-1}\big)c^{-p}\frac1{d!}\int_0^{cB}\log^d(cB/t)\,t^{p-1}
e^{-\beta t^2+\beta at}\,dt$, valid at every scale $c>0$. Integrability of $|\log t|^it^\gamma
e^{-\beta t^2+\beta at}$ on $(0,\infty)$ is recorded for the domination arguments to come.
Zero \texttt{sorry}/\texttt{axiom}.
\end{abstract}

\section{Proof strategy}
The triangle integrand $f(t,s)=\mathbf 1_{s\le t}(\log t-\log s)^jg(s)/t$ is measurable
(\texttt{Measurable.ite} on $\{s\le t\}$). Its $s$-section over $(0,y]$ is the inner integral over
$(0,t]$ (\texttt{setIntegral\_indicator} with $\mathrm{Iic}\,t$); its $t$-section is
$g(s)\int_s^y(\log t-\log s)^j/t\,dt=g(s)\log^{j+1}(y/s)/(j+1)$ by the fundamental theorem of calculus.
\texttt{integrable\_prod\_iff'} reduces integrability on the square to that of
$\log^{j+1}(y/s)|g(s)|$, and \texttt{integral\_integral\_swap} finishes the induction.
For the kernel, $|\log t|\le t^{-\varepsilon}/\varepsilon$ on $(0,1]$ and $|\log t|\le t$ on $[1,\infty)$
dominate $|\log t|^i t^\gamma$ by integrable weighted kernels; the binomial expansion of
$(|\log y|+|\log t|)^j$ gives the $(0,y]$ version.

\section{Roadblocks and resolutions}
\begin{itemize}
\item \texttt{positivity} cannot see $0\le t^\gamma\,\mathrm{quadKernel}$ (opaque definition): derive
the fact once from \texttt{quadKernel\_pos} and thread it.
\item \texttt{add\_pow} expands $(x+y)^n$ as $\sum x^m y^{n-m}\binom nm$; the dominating sum has to be
written in that orientation.
\item After \texttt{congr 1} on set integrals the goal may be a measure equality; rewrite the set
identity before, not after.
\item \texttt{Integrable.congr} hands the pointwise goal as a beta redex; use \texttt{simp only}
rather than \texttt{rw}.
\end{itemize}

\section{Outcome}
Astra target~1 (product-density identity) is done in the form best adapted to the existing tower.
