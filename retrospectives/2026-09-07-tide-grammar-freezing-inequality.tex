\begin{abstract}
For the block state integral with general jointly continuous $\xi,\eta$ (block $u\in(0,b]^m$,
noncritical $v\in(0,b]^{d'}$) and its frozen version with $\xi(0,v),\eta(0,v)$, the mean-value bound
and the corner/strip split of unit~76 give, at every scale $N\ge0$,
$|Z(N)-Z_0(N)|\le C_2\big(\omega\,Z^{\beta/2,2L}_h(N)+\tfrac{2M+2L}{\delta}\sum_iZ^{\beta/2,2L}_{h+e_i}(N)\big)$,
where $\omega$ bounds the oscillation of $\xi,\eta$ on the corner $\{u_i\le\delta\}$ and the right-hand
side consists of constant-$\xi$ state integrals. Zero \texttt{sorry}/\texttt{axiom}.
\end{abstract}

\section{Proof strategy}
Everything is done on the product box measure, where all integrands are continuous on the compact
closed box, hence integrable (\texttt{integrable\_box\_prod\_of\_continuous}); \texttt{integral\_prod}
converts to the iterated forms. On the open product box (almost everywhere) the integrand difference
is $v^{h'}u^h\,|\eta f_\xi-\eta_0f_{\xi_0}|(s)$, bounded by
$v^{h'}u^hC_2D\,e^{-(\beta/2)s^2+\beta Ls}$ and then, via the corner/strip split, by
$C_2\big(\omega+\tfrac{D_{\max}}{\delta}\sum_iu_i\big)v^{h'}u^he^{-(\beta/2)s^2+\beta Ls}$; the terms
$u^hu_i=u^{h+e_i}$ are recognised as shifted-exponent integrands. Then
$\|\int(G-G_0)\|\le\int\|G-G_0\|\le\int H$ and $\int H$ is expanded by linearity into the constant-$\xi$
state integrals.

\section{Roadblocks and resolutions}
\begin{itemize}
\item \texttt{ae\_restrict\_mem} on a product of pi measures needs both \texttt{volume}s and the set
given explicitly; otherwise the measurable-space instance is left as a metavariable.
\item \texttt{integral\_congr\_ae} pointwise goals are beta redexes; \texttt{beta\_reduce} before
\texttt{rw}.
\item The unifier cannot recover \texttt{fun \_ v => ξ 0 v} from a continuity proof; pass the frozen
functions explicitly to \texttt{blockIntegrand\_continuous}.
\item The bound $|\xi(0,v)|\le L$ needs $(0,v)$ in the closed box, hence $0\le b$: add
\texttt{hb : 0 < b}.
\end{itemize}

\section{Outcome}
The freezing inequality reduces the general multiplicity-$m$ leading term to the constant-$\xi$ theory
already formalised. Next: the limit argument ($N^p\log^{-d}N\cdot$strip terms $\to0$, then an
$\varepsilon$-argument with uniform continuity on the closed box) and the main theorem
$Z\sim C\,N^{-p}\log^dN$.
