\begin{abstract}
A soundness review of \texttt{Laplace/OneD/AnharmonicFDTMeanAsymptotic.lean}: the large-$t$ first-cumulant FDT
asymptote $\partial_h\langle x\rangle_h|_0\to-1/\lambda$, plus two definitional bridges identifying the
abstract \texttt{Threepoint} Gibbs expectation/covariance at $h=0$ with the concrete \texttt{Laplace} ones.
Result: \textbf{a clean fidelity pass, no edits} --- the bridges, the sign chain, and the value all check out.
\end{abstract}

\section{Setting}
The FDT capstone proved $\partial_h\langle x\rangle_h|_0=-t\,\mathrm{Cov}_0(x,x)$ for the anharmonic Gibbs
measure. Its $t\to\infty$ asymptote needs the variance asymptotic $t\,\mathrm{Cov}_0(x,x)\to1/\lambda$ --- but
that asymptotic is phrased with the concrete \texttt{Laplace.gibbsCov} while the FDT identity uses the
abstract \texttt{Threepoint.gibbsCov}. This file supplies the bridge and lands the asymptote.

\section{What was reviewed}
Three public theorems: the expectation bridge, the covariance bridge, and the headline asymptote.

\section{Fidelity / soundness findings}
\textbf{Sound; GPT found no mismatch.}
\begin{itemize}[noitemsep]
  \item \textbf{Bridge (expectation)}: at $h=0$ with $A=\mathrm{id}$, the perturbation $h\!\cdot\!A=0$, so both
    \texttt{Threepoint.gibbsExp} and \texttt{Laplace.gibbsExpectation} reduce to the same normalised Lebesgue
    integral $\int\varphi\,e^{-tL}/\int e^{-tL}$ (\texttt{unfold} + \texttt{simp [zero\_mul, add\_zero]}).
  \item \textbf{Bridge (covariance)}: $\mathrm{Cov}=E[\varphi\psi]-E[\varphi]E[\psi]$, so rewriting the three
    \texttt{gibbsExp} occurrences by the expectation bridge reduces the \texttt{Threepoint} covariance at
    $h=0$ to the \texttt{Laplace} one.
  \item \textbf{Asymptote}: the sign chain $t\,\mathrm{Cov}\to1/\lambda\Rightarrow-(t\,\mathrm{Cov})\to-1/\lambda$
    (\texttt{.neg} + $-(1/\lambda)=-1/\lambda$), composed with the FDT identity
    ($\partial_h\langle x\rangle|_0=-t\,\mathrm{Cov}$, via \texttt{.deriv} and the covariance bridge), gives
    $\partial_h\langle x\rangle|_0\to-1/\lambda$ --- the harmonic FDT slope.
\end{itemize}

\section{Lessons}
\begin{itemize}[noitemsep]
  \item \textbf{A cross-library bridge at the unperturbed point is a clean \texttt{unfold}+\texttt{simp}.}
    Two formalisations of the same Gibbs object (an abstract perturbed \texttt{Threepoint.gibbsCov} and a
    concrete \texttt{Laplace.gibbsCov}) coincide at $h=0$ because the perturbation term vanishes; the bridge is
    a one-line definitional reduction, and it is exactly what lets an asymptotic phrased in one library be
    composed with an identity phrased in the other. Such bridges are low-risk and high-leverage --- worth a
    quick independent confirmation that the $h=0$ specialisation really collapses both sides identically.
\end{itemize}
