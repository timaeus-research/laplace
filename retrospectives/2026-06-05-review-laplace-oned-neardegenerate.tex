\begin{abstract}
A soundness review of \texttt{Laplace/OneD/NearDegenerate.lean}: the exact universal-scaling
identity $\langle x^{2n}\rangle_{L^{nd}_\varepsilon,t}=\varepsilon^n\langle y^{2n}\rangle_{V,\varepsilon^2t}$
relating the near-degenerate potential $L^{nd}_\varepsilon(x)=\tfrac\varepsilon2 x^2+\tfrac{x^4}{24}$
to the universal $V(y)=\tfrac{y^2}2+\tfrac{y^4}{24}$ via $x=\sqrt\varepsilon\,y$. Result:
\textbf{a clean fidelity pass with one dead-open removal} --- the substantive math (an exact
change-of-variables transport) checked out on all four load-bearing steps.
\end{abstract}

\section{Setting}
Unlike the templated \texttt{TwoD/} lift files, this is a genuine change-of-variables result: the
substitution $x=\sqrt\varepsilon\,y$ sends $t\,L^{nd}_\varepsilon$ to $\varepsilon^2 t\,V$
\emph{exactly}, so every near-degenerate Gibbs moment factors through the universal posterior at
effective inverse temperature $T=\varepsilon^2 t$. It is the formal core of the
near-degenerate-crossover note.

\section{What was reviewed}
Two potential abbreviations, an algebraic core lemma, and four theorems: the integral substitution
($\int f\,e^{-tL^{nd}}=\sqrt\varepsilon\int f(\sqrt\varepsilon\,\cdot)\,e^{-\varepsilon^2tV}$), the
partition scaling ($Z_{L^{nd}_\varepsilon}=\sqrt\varepsilon\,Z_V(\varepsilon^2t)$), the
Gibbs-expectation transport, and the headline monomial scaling.

\section{Fidelity / soundness findings}
\textbf{Sound; checked four ways.} (1) The algebraic core
$L^{nd}_\varepsilon(\sqrt\varepsilon\,y)=\varepsilon^2V(y)$ expands correctly
($\tfrac\varepsilon2(\varepsilon y^2)+\tfrac{\varepsilon^2y^4}{24}=\varepsilon^2(\tfrac{y^2}2+\tfrac{y^4}{24})$).
(2) The Jacobian is in the right direction: $x=\sqrt\varepsilon\,y\Rightarrow dx=\sqrt\varepsilon\,dy$,
matching the $\sqrt\varepsilon$ prefactor (the proof rearranges Mathlib's
\texttt{integral\_comp\_mul\_right} output). (3) The $\sqrt\varepsilon$ prefactors of numerator and
partition cancel in the expectation transport. (4) The headline factor is
$(\sqrt\varepsilon)^{2n}=\varepsilon^n$. The reviewer independently confirmed all four.

One report-only scope note: the module docstring's prose about the $T\ll1$ and $T\gg1$ regimes
(``governed by the pure-quartic core'', ``tracks the standard Laplace asymptotics'') is
\emph{contextual} --- this file proves the exact transport/scaling identity, not those asymptotic
regime statements. Left intact as expository scope-setting, not gutted (the same treatment as other
research-context framing).

\section{Simplifications applied}
\textbf{One dead \texttt{open}.} \texttt{open Real} is dead (\texttt{Real.sqrt}/\texttt{Real.exp}/%
\texttt{Real.sq\_sqrt}/\texttt{Real.sqrt\_pos} all qualified); \texttt{open MeasureTheory} stays
(bare $\int$ throughout). Module green at $2697$ jobs, no warnings, signatures byte-identical. GPT
affirmed.

\section{Disagreements and open questions}
None. The reviewer's several \texttt{show}$\to$\texttt{change} and \texttt{simpa}-compression
suggestions were declined as proof-golf.

\section{Lessons}
\begin{itemize}[noitemsep]
  \item \textbf{A genuine change-of-variables result rewards a four-point fidelity check.} Unlike a
    templated repackaging, this file's correctness hinges on four independently-falsifiable facts
    (algebraic identity, Jacobian direction, prefactor cancellation, exponent count). Enumerating
    them --- rather than trusting the build --- is what a fidelity review adds over a green
    \texttt{lake build}.
  \item \textbf{Contextual regime prose is expository, not a proof obligation.} A docstring that
    situates an exact identity within a $T\ll1$/$T\gg1$ crossover story is scope-setting; reviewing
    it means confirming the file does not \emph{claim} to prove the regimes, not flagging their
    absence.
\end{itemize}
