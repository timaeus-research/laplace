\begin{abstract}
Step~0 of the general-$d$ equal-ratio monomial milestone (Astra~\#12~(c)). The pushforward of
$\prod_it_i^{\lambda-1}dt$ on $(0,1]^d$ under $t\mapsto\prod_it_i$ has density
$z^{\lambda-1}(-\log z)^{d-1}/(d-1)!$; Astra identified the one-dimensional identity that drives the
induction, $\int_z^1(-\log(z/t))^{d-1}/t\,dt=(-\log z)^d/d$. It is proved here in the form
$\int_z^1(\log t-\log z)^d/t\,dt=(-\log z)^{d+1}/(d+1)$ for $0<z\le1$
(\texttt{integral\_log\_sub\_pow\_div}) by the fundamental theorem of calculus with antiderivative
$(\log t-\log z)^{d+1}/(d+1)$. Zero \texttt{sorry}/\texttt{axiom}.
\end{abstract}

\section{The things formalised}
{\small
\begin{verbatim}
theorem hasDerivAt_log_sub_pow_div (z t) (ht : 0 < t) (d : ℕ) :
    HasDerivAt (fun t => (log t - log z)^(d+1) / (d + 1)) ((log t - log z)^d / t) t
theorem integral_log_sub_pow_div (z) (hz : 0 < z) (hz1 : z ≤ 1) (d : ℕ) :
    ∫ t in z..1, (log t - log z)^d / t = (-log z)^(d+1) / (d + 1)
\end{verbatim}
}

\section{Lean notes}
\begin{itemize}
\item \texttt{intervalIntegral.integral\_eq\_sub\_of\_hasDerivAt} needs the derivative on
\texttt{uIcc z 1} (\texttt{uIcc\_of\_le}) and interval integrability, supplied by
\texttt{ContinuousOn.intervalIntegrable} for the quotient of continuous functions with nonvanishing
denominator on $[z,1]$.
\item \texttt{HasDerivAt.congr\_deriv} with \texttt{field\_simp} matches the derivative of the
antiderivative to the integrand; \texttt{volume} needs \texttt{open MeasureTheory}.
\item The evaluation at the endpoints is \texttt{simp [Real.log\_one]} ($0^{d+1}=0$ at $t=z$).
\end{itemize}
