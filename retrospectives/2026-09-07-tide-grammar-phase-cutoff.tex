\begin{abstract}
Unit 204: the transport-only part of Astra~\#20's programme~D, taken because it is what the paper's
tubular fibres $(0,\varepsilon]^d$ actually need. The dilation $u=bx$ turns the phase-dressed integral
on $(0,b]^d$ into $b^{\sum_i(h_i+1)}$ times the unit-box integral at parameter $Nb^{\sum_ik_i}$
with phase and amplitude composed with the dilation (\texttt{phaseBoxCutoff\_eq}); since
$(\log Nc/\log N)^{m-1}\to1$, the leading coefficient acquires exactly
$b^{\sum_i(h_i+1)-2\lambda\sum_ik_i}=\prod_{i\notin J}b^{h_i+1-2k_i\lambda}$
(\texttt{phaseBoxCutoff\_tendsto}), the $b$-dependence of the paper's eq.~\texttt{a\_minus\_m\_explicit},
while the face integral becomes $\int\eta(b\pi u)J_p(\xi(b\pi u))\prod_{i\notin J}u_i^{h_i-pk_i}\,du$,
i.e.\ the amplitude and phase are read on the face of the box $(0,b]^d$
(\texttt{headline\_phase\_leading\_cutoff}). Review v9 (units 198--203) reported no statement-level
defects; its should-fix (write $J^{(\beta)}_p$, never $S$, for \texttt{phaseMoment} in the
documentation) is applied in this branch. Zero \texttt{sorry}/\texttt{axiom}; 9081 jobs.
\end{abstract}

\section{Mathematics}
Pure transport: \texttt{setIntegral\_comp\_smul\_of\_pos} for the dilation of the box, the scaling
$N\mapsto Nb^{\sum k_i}$ inside the kernel (the phase argument $s=Nu^k$ scales exactly, so the
dressed kernel is unchanged in form), and the two limits
$I(Nc)/((Nc)^{-p}(\log Nc)^{m-1})\to C$ and $(\log Nc/\log N)^{m-1}\to1$ multiplied together, as in
unit~183. Nothing new is estimated. Fixed \emph{rectangular} cutoffs $\prod(0,a_i]$ would need the
coordinatewise dilation (a diagonal linear map) and give $\prod_ia_i^{h_i+1-pk_i}$; they are not used
by the paper, whose fibres are cubes, so they are left out.
