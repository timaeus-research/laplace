\begin{abstract}
Astra~\#9 R5, step~2. Every step of the $d=2$ cutoff assembly is now quantitative for $N\ge1$:
the face transfers carry the explicit constant $\mathrm{faceConst}$ (an envelope moment plus tail
moments of the face coefficients, read off \texttt{face\_transfer\_bound}), the mixed remainder
carries the Gaussian-moment constant of unit~130, and a bound at a larger exponent is a bound at the
target exponent for $N\ge1$. Summing,
\[
|Z(N)-S^{\mathrm{merged}}(N)|\le\mathrm{cutoffConst}\cdot N^{-E}(1+\log N),\qquad
E=\min\Bigl(p_1+\frac{M_1}{k_1},\,p_2+\frac{M_2}{k_2}\Bigr),
\]
for every $N\ge1$ and arbitrary starting exponents (\texttt{twoD\_cutoff\_general\_explicit}),
with $\mathrm{cutoffConst}$ the finite sum of the face, corner and mixed constants. Zero
\texttt{sorry}/\texttt{axiom}.
\end{abstract}

\section{Mathematics}

The decomposition $Z-S^{\mathrm{merged}}=\sum_iF^A_i+\sum_jF^B_j-\sum_{ij}F^C_{ij}+F^R$ is the
rectangular identity of unit~106 combined with the over-complete-to-merged identity of unit~127
(at the standard corner order). Each face piece is bounded by \texttt{face\_transfer\_bound} at its
own exponent ($p_2+M_2/k_2$ for $u$-faces, $p_1+M_1/k_1$ for $v$-faces,
$p_1+(M_1+k_1M_2+i)/k_1$ for corners), the mixed piece by unit~130 at
$\min(p_1+M_1/k_1,p_2+M_2/k_2)$; all exponents are $\ge E$, and for $N\ge1$ a bound
$|X|\le KN^{-q}(1+\log N)$ with $q\ge E$ implies $|X|\le KN^{-E}(1+\log N)$ (the constant is
nonnegative because the right side dominates $|X|\ge0$ with a positive factor). The triangle
inequality over the finite sums gives the sum of constants.

\section{Paper-facing meaning}

With unit~130 this is the ``explicit cutoff bound'' half of Astra~\#9's R5: the remainder in
\texttt{thm:TaylorTree} ($d=2$) is bounded by a named finite expression for every $N\ge1$, not only
eventually. For the uniform-family theorem the face constants must still be bounded in terms of the
envelope data alone: $\mathrm{faceConst}$ involves the tail moments of the actual face
coefficients ($k_1^{-1}\mathrm{FP}$, monomial coefficients, log coefficient), which the envelope
constructor of unit~110 bounds by $(1+s)^De^{\beta sa'}$ envelopes; turning those pointwise bounds
into moment bounds is the next step.

\section{Lean notes}

\begin{itemize}
\item \texttt{face\_transfer\_bound} is already stated for the swapped amplitude, so only the goal
needs the \texttt{twoDAmp\_pow\_absorb}/\texttt{twoDAmp\_swap} rewrite, not the hypothesis.
\item \texttt{set A := N \^{} (-E) * (1 + log N)} does not fold later-appearing copies; rewrite
with \texttt{hAdef} and \texttt{← mul\_assoc} to line up \texttt{K * A} with the bounds' shape
\texttt{K * N \^{} (-E) * (1 + log N)}.
\item Nonnegativity of an explicit constant is cheapest to extract from the bound it appears in:
\texttt{le\_of\_mul\_le\_mul\_right} with \texttt{0 * A ≤ K * A}.
\item \texttt{Finset.sum\_mul} then \texttt{Finset.abs\_sum\_le\_sum\_abs} and
\texttt{Finset.sum\_le\_sum} turn termwise bounds \texttt{|F i| ≤ K i * A} into a bound on the
absolute value of the sum.
\end{itemize}
