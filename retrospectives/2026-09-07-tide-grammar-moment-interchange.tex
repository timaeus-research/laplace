\begin{abstract}
The paper's fluctuation coefficients are series indexed by tree types, integrated against the
Gaussian log weight $w(s)=s^{\alpha-1}(\log s)^\ell e^{-\beta s^2}$. This unit exposes the
interchange API for such series: if the weighted $L^1$ norms $\int|w f_t|$ are summable then
$\mathcal M(\sum_tf_t)=\sum_t\mathcal M(f_t)$ with absolute convergence
(\texttt{hasSum\_logMoment}); a normal-convergence hypothesis $\sum_t|f_t(s)|\le G(s)$ with $|w|G$
integrable supplies both hypotheses through a Tonelli argument in \texttt{ENNReal}
(\texttt{weighted\_summable\_of\_dominated}); and the envelope $C(1+s)^De^{\beta sa'}$ is
integrable against $|w|$ for $\alpha>0$ (\texttt{hasSum\_logMoment\_of\_envelope}). This is
Astra~\#4's rank-3 unit and the tool for the first genuinely infinite coefficient identity (the
axis series for the $d=2$ constant coefficient $B$). Zero \texttt{sorry}/\texttt{axiom}.
\end{abstract}

\section{Mathematics}

The core is Mathlib's \texttt{hasSum\_integral\_of\_summable\_integral\_norm}: for integrable
$F_t$ with $\sum_t\|F_t\|_{L^1}<\infty$, $\sum_t\int F_t=\int\sum_tF_t$ as a \texttt{HasSum}. With
$F_t=wf_t$ the left side is $\sum_t\mathcal M(f_t)$ and the integrand on the right is
$w\sum_tf_t$ by \texttt{tsum\_mul\_left} (unconditional in a division ring).

The domination step converts $\sum_t|f_t|\le G$ into the two hypotheses by working in
\texttt{ENNReal}: $\sum_t\int^-\|wf_t\|_e=\int^-\sum_t\|wf_t\|_e$ (\texttt{lintegral\_tsum}, no
summability needed) and pointwise
$\sum_t\|wf_t\|_e=\mathrm{ofReal}|w|\cdot\mathrm{ofReal}\sum_t|f_t|\le\|\,|w|G\|_e$
(\texttt{ENNReal.ofReal\_tsum\_of\_nonneg} uses the pointwise summability). Finiteness of
$\int^-\||w|G\|_e$ is the \texttt{HasFiniteIntegral} half of the integrability of $|w|G$. Then each
$\int^-\|wf_t\|_e$ is finite (integrability) and
$\int|wf_t|=(\int^-\|wf_t\|_e)_{\mathrm{toReal}}$ is summable by \texttt{ENNReal.summable\_toReal}.

For the envelope, $|w(s)|\,C(1+s)^De^{\beta sa'}\le s^{\alpha-1}(1+|\log s|)^\ell e^{-\beta s^2}
\cdot C(1+s)^De^{\beta sa'}$, integrable by unit~96's
\texttt{moment\_integrableOn\_of\_envelope} for $\alpha>0$.

\section{Paper-facing meaning}

This is the interchange lemma Astra~\#3(c) asked for, in the form recommended in Astra~\#4(c): an
arbitrary measurable weight first, the Gaussian log weight as a specialisation, with the
normal-convergence corollary. Its first application will be the absolutely convergent series for the
axis integral $I_\Psi(s)=\sum_{i\ge1}\Psi_i(s)b^i/i$ and hence for the $d=2$ constant coefficient
$B$ when $\Phi$ has convergent Taylor series on a strictly larger disc. No convergence of the
inverse-$N$ expansion is implied.

\section{Lean notes}

\begin{itemize}
\item \texttt{hasSum\_integral\_of\_summable\_integral\_norm} with explicit
\texttt{(μ := volume.restrict (Ioi 0))} left \texttt{NormedSpace ℝ ?E} stuck (the \texttt{0} in
\texttt{Ioi 0} has no type there). Give the \texttt{have} an explicit \texttt{HasSum} type and
let unification fill everything.
\item \texttt{Measurable.abs} does not exist; use
\texttt{continuous\_abs.measurable.comp}.
\item \texttt{Real.enorm\_eq\_ofReal\_abs}, \texttt{ENNReal.ofReal\_mul},
\texttt{ENNReal.tsum\_mul\_left}, \texttt{ENNReal.ofReal\_tsum\_of\_nonneg} chain the pointwise
\texttt{ENNReal} identity; \texttt{gcongr} handles the final \texttt{ofReal} monotonicity.
\item \texttt{integral\_norm\_eq\_lintegral\_enorm} converts the real $L^1$ norm to the
\texttt{toReal} of the lower integral, matching \texttt{ENNReal.summable\_toReal}.
\item An envelope coefficient must be assumed nonnegative (\texttt{hC : 0 ≤ C}) for
\texttt{positivity}; a bound of the form $|c|\le A(\dots)$ with $A<0$ is vacuous anyway.
\end{itemize}
