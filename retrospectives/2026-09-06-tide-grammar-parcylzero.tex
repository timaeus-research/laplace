\begin{abstract}
Unit 12 of the grammar-paper \S4 autoformalisation: the value of the parabolic cylinder function at the
origin, $D_{-\nu}(0)=\sqrt\pi/(2^{\nu/2}\Gamma(\tfrac{\nu+1}{2}))$, as displayed in the proof of
\texttt{cor:fluctuation\_closed\_form}. A short reconciliation of the already-proven
\texttt{parCylNeg\_zero} ($=2^{\nu/2-1}\Gamma(\nu/2)/\Gamma(\nu)$, from the $\Gamma$-integral) with the
paper's form, using Legendre's duplication formula. Zero \texttt{sorry}/\texttt{axiom}.
\end{abstract}

\section{Setting}
The \texttt{ParabolicCylinder} module (unit~6) evaluated $D_{-\nu}(0)$ directly from the DLMF integral as
$2^{\nu/2-1}\Gamma(\nu/2)/\Gamma(\nu)$. The paper states the equivalent Legendre-normalised form
$\sqrt\pi/(2^{\nu/2}\Gamma(\tfrac{\nu+1}{2}))$; this unit records that the two agree.

\section{The thing formalised}
{\small
\begin{verbatim}
theorem parCylNeg_zero_paper (ν : ℝ) (hν : 0 < ν) :
    parCylNeg ν 0 = Real.sqrt π / (2 ^ (ν / 2) * Real.Gamma ((ν + 1) / 2))
\end{verbatim}
}

\section{Proof strategy}
Legendre duplication at $s=\nu/2$ (\texttt{Real.Gamma\_mul\_Gamma\_add\_half}) gives
$\Gamma(\nu/2)\Gamma(\tfrac{\nu+1}{2})=\Gamma(\nu)\,2^{1-\nu}\sqrt\pi$. Cross-multiplying the goal
(\texttt{div\_eq\_div\_iff}, with $\Gamma(\nu),\Gamma(\tfrac{\nu+1}{2})>0$) reduces it to
$(2^{\nu/2-1}2^{\nu/2})\bigl(\Gamma(\nu/2)\Gamma(\tfrac{\nu+1}{2})\bigr)=\sqrt\pi\,\Gamma(\nu)$;
substituting the duplication identity and collapsing the powers $2^{\nu/2-1}2^{\nu/2}=2^{\nu-1}$ and
$2^{\nu-1}2^{1-\nu}=1$ (\texttt{Real.rpow\_add}) closes it by \texttt{ring}.

\section{Roadblocks and resolutions}
None of substance --- a short algebraic reconciliation. The only care is grouping the $2^{\bullet}$
factors before \texttt{Real.rpow\_add} (which does not combine powers buried in a larger product) and
supplying the positivity side conditions for \texttt{div\_eq\_div\_iff}.

\section{What was Mathlib, what was new}
Mathlib supplied Legendre duplication (\texttt{Real.Gamma\_mul\_Gamma\_add\_half}) and the $\Gamma$
positivity/rpow lemmas. New is only the identification of the two closed forms for $D_{-\nu}(0)$.

\section{Lessons}
When two closed forms of a special-function value differ by a $\Gamma$-duplication, the reconciliation
is a cross-multiplication plus one duplication rewrite; group the power factors explicitly so
\texttt{Real.rpow\_add} can fire.

\section{Follow-ups}
The remaining \S4 items (general-$k$ order-derivative and the log-insertion identities
\texttt{prop:LogODE}/\texttt{lem:log\_shift}/\texttt{lem:log\_generating}; the ladder-operator commutator
$[b,b^\dagger]=1$) each need substantial new infrastructure --- iterated parametric differentiation or a
Weyl-algebra framework.
