\begin{abstract}
A small \emph{strict-improvement} tide of Tide~5
(\texttt{kappa3\_harmonic\_id\_id\_id\_eq\_zero}): generalise from the
identity-observable case to an arbitrary triple of affine
observables. For the 1D harmonic potential
$L(x) = (\lambda/2) x^2$ with $\lambda, t > 0$,
\[
  \kappa_3\bigl(b_1 x + c_1,\; b_2 x + c_2,\; b_3 x + c_3\bigr) = 0
\]
under the harmonic Gibbs measure on $\mathbb{R}$. The proof goes by
\emph{moment expansion} (per the GPT-5.5~Pro consult): each of the
seven Gibbs expectations in the cumulant formula reduces, after
polynomial expansion of the affine integrands, to an instance of a
workhorse polynomial-degree-3 lemma whose closed form is
$a_2/(\lambda t) + a_0$ (the odd-power moments
$m_1, m_3$ vanish by parity, and $m_2 = 1/(\lambda t)$). The
constant and $m_2$-coefficient terms in $\kappa_3$ then cancel
\emph{algebraically} by \texttt{ring}. Single session, 323~lines in
one new file (\texttt{Laplace/OneD/HarmonicAffineKappa3.lean}), zero
\texttt{sorry}, zero \texttt{axiom}, zero \texttt{native\_decide}.

The deliberation step converged on Candidate~B (direct moment
expansion, harmonic-specific) over Candidate~A or~C (both of which
would have required factoring $\kappa_3$ multilinearity through a
\emph{Threepoint}-side perturbation-direction-independence lemma we
don't yet have). GPT-5.5~Pro's flag of that API gap was the decisive
input.
\end{abstract}

\section{Setting}

\section{The thing formalised}

The headline theorem is
\begin{quote}
\texttt{Laplace.OneD.kappa3\_harmonic\_affine\_eq\_zero}\,:
\end{quote}
% NOTE: the verbatim block below produces an unavoidable overfull-hbox
% warning. The theorem signature is the load-bearing artefact of this
% retrospective; abbreviating it would defeat the purpose. Recorded
% explicitly here so a future reader knows it was considered.
\begin{verbatim}
theorem kappa3_harmonic_affine_eq_zero
    {lam t : ℝ} (hlam : 0 < lam) (ht : 0 < t)
    (b₁ c₁ b₂ c₂ b₃ c₃ : ℝ) :
    Threepoint.kappa3 (volume : Measure ℝ)
      (harmonicPotential lam)
      (fun x : ℝ => b₂ * x + c₂)
      t
      (fun x : ℝ => b₁ * x + c₁)
      (fun x : ℝ => b₃ * x + c₃) = 0
\end{verbatim}

\noindent
\texttt{Threepoint.kappa3} is the third cumulant under a
$(L, A, t, h)$-perturbed Gibbs measure, evaluated at $h = 0$
(\texttt{CrossSusceptibility.lean:49}); its argument order
$(\mu, L, A, t, \varphi, B)$ is the convention from the threepoint
paper, with $A$ in the second observable position because $A$ is also
the \emph{perturbation direction} of \texttt{gibbsExp}. At $h = 0$
that perturbation collapses, so mathematically
$\kappa_3$ is fully symmetric in the three observable slots; in the
Lean API only the swap $(\varphi \leftrightarrow B)$ is free
(\texttt{kappa3\_swap\_$\varphi$B}).

The \emph{informal} statement is the multilinearity strict-improvement
of Tide~5: $\kappa_3$ vanishes on any triple of affine observables,
not just on the three identities. This is the cumulant-level
expression of the fact that all Gaussians are determined by their
first two moments (so cumulants of order three or higher vanish), and
moreover that affine maps of a Gaussian are still Gaussian.

\section{Proof strategy}

The deliberation step considered three approaches:
\begin{description}[leftmargin=2em]
\item[Candidate~A (multilinearity reduction).] Introduce
$\kappa_3$-multilinearity lemmas in each slot, then specialise to
$b_1 b_2 b_3 \cdot \kappa_3(\mathrm{id}, \mathrm{id}, \mathrm{id}) = 0$
via Tide~5. Reusable infrastructure but $\sim$150--250 lines.

\item[Candidate~B (direct moment expansion).] Unfold $\kappa_3$,
bridge to \texttt{Laplace.gibbsExpectation} (taking advantage of I2's
algebra), expand each affine product as a degree-3 polynomial in
$x$, substitute the harmonic moments, finish by \texttt{ring}.

\item[Candidate~C (private multilinearity).] Same as A but the
multilinearity helpers are kept private to the new file. Same line
count as A.
\end{description}

\noindent
GPT-5.5~Pro voted Candidate~B and flagged a structural reason: in
\texttt{Threepoint.kappa3}, slot $A$ is also the perturbation
direction of \texttt{gibbsExp}, so a true ``$\kappa_3$ is symmetric
across all three slots'' lemma in Lean would require an
\texttt{h=0}-perturbation-independence step that doesn't yet exist
in the \texttt{Threepoint} repo. Candidates A/C
\emph{appear} cheaper than they are because that hidden API
obligation was not folded into their estimates. Candidate B sidesteps
the issue entirely: it works at the integrand level, where the
permutation symmetry is mathematical (factoring through the
\texttt{Threepoint}--\texttt{Laplace} bridge) rather than API-level.

The agreed proof is the moment-expansion route, with three layers of
helpers in the same file:

\begin{description}[leftmargin=2em]
\item[Bridge.] \texttt{threepoint\_gibbsExp\_volume\_zero\_eq}
(private): for $\mu = \texttt{volume}$ on $\mathbb{R}$,
$\texttt{gibbsExp}\,L\,A\,t\,0\,\varphi$ reduces to
$\texttt{Laplace.gibbsExpectation}\,L\,t\,\varphi$. Same private
helper as in \texttt{AnharmonicKappa3.lean}; restated here to keep
the file self-contained.

\item[Moment closed forms.] Three private wrapper lemmas computing
$\langle x \rangle_t = 0$, $\langle x^2 \rangle_t = 1/(\lambda t)$,
$\langle x^3 \rangle_t = 0$ from the existing
\texttt{gibbsExpectation\_harmonic\_pow\_*} lemmas in
\texttt{OneD/Harmonic.lean}.

\item[Polynomial-expectation workhorse.]
\texttt{gibbsExp\_poly\_le3\_harmonic} (private): for any degree-3
polynomial coefficients $(a_3, a_2, a_1, a_0)$, the harmonic Gibbs
expectation of $a_3 x^3 + a_2 x^2 + a_1 x + a_0$ equals
$a_2 / (\lambda t) + a_0$. Path: \texttt{gibbsExpectation\_add} three
times (with type-ascribed integrability witnesses for each tail
sum), \texttt{gibbsExpectation\_smul} three times,
\texttt{gibbsExpectation\_const} once, then substitute the three
moments.
\end{description}

\noindent
The headline theorem is then a direct application of the workhorse
to each affine product in the $\kappa_3$ expansion:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=2em]
\item For each pair-product $(b_i x + c_i)(b_j x + c_j)$, rewrite
as $0 \cdot x^3 + b_i b_j \cdot x^2 + (b_i c_j + c_i b_j) \cdot x +
c_i c_j$ via \texttt{funext}, then apply the workhorse to get
$b_i b_j / (\lambda t) + c_i c_j$.

\item For the triple-product
$(b_1 x + c_1)(b_2 x + c_2)(b_3 x + c_3)$, rewrite as
$b_1 b_2 b_3 \cdot x^3 + (\sum_{\mathrm{sym}} b_i b_j c_k) \cdot x^2 +
(\sum_{\mathrm{sym}} b_i c_j c_k) \cdot x + c_1 c_2 c_3$ and apply
the workhorse to get
$(\sum_{\mathrm{sym}} b_i b_j c_k) / (\lambda t) + c_1 c_2 c_3$.

\item For the three single-affine expectations, apply
\texttt{gibbsExp\_affine\_harmonic} to get the value $c_i$ (since
$\langle x \rangle = 0$).
\end{itemize}

\noindent
The seven closed forms then plug into the cumulant formula, and
\texttt{ring} closes the equality. The cancellation pattern is the
one identified in the numerical sanity check:
the $1/(\lambda t)$-coefficient
$(b_1 b_2 c_3 + b_1 c_2 b_3 + c_1 b_2 b_3)
- b_1 b_2 c_3 - b_1 b_3 c_2 - b_2 b_3 c_1$
is identically zero by symmetry of multiplication, and the
constant-coefficient
$c_1 c_2 c_3 - 3 c_1 c_2 c_3 + 2 c_1 c_2 c_3$
also vanishes.

\section{Roadblocks and resolutions}

Two minor frictions, both anticipated by the laplace
\texttt{CLAUDE.md}'s ``Mathlib gotchas'' section.

\paragraph{Pi.add vs single-lambda mismatch.}
\texttt{Integrable.add} returns its conclusion as
$f + g$ in the \texttt{Pi.add} encoding, not as a single lambda
$x \mapsto f(x) + g(x)$. Subsequent \texttt{Integrable.congr} matches
fail because the goal sees \texttt{Pi.add} not the lambda. The fix
is the standard idiom from \texttt{CLAUDE.md}:
\begin{verbatim}
filter_upwards with x
simp only [Pi.add_apply]; ring
\end{verbatim}
i.e., unfold the \texttt{Pi.add} explicitly via
\texttt{simp only [Pi.add\_apply]} before \texttt{ring}.
The first attempt (without the \texttt{simp}) hit
\emph{`typeclass instance problem is stuck} and \emph{`show` tactic
failed to match} — typical signatures of this issue.

\paragraph{\texttt{convert ... using 1; ring} on a closed goal.}
A `\texttt{convert}' that lands a perfectly-aligned goal (because
\texttt{simp} had already done the alignment) leaves the
\texttt{ring} call with no goals. Replaced with \texttt{exact h}.
Tiny but worth recording: when the simp normal form already matches,
\texttt{convert} is wrong; \texttt{exact} is right.

No GPT consult was needed during Step~3: the deliberation in
Step~2 was load-bearing enough that the proof skeleton was clear
from the start. The implementation rolled through in a single pass
once the integrability discipline (\texttt{Pi.add\_apply}
unfolding) was in place.

\section{What was Mathlib, what was new}

\paragraph{Mathlib lookups (load-bearing).}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=2em]
\item \texttt{Integrable.const\_mul}: scaling preserves integrability.
\item \texttt{Integrable.add}: sum of integrables (used through
\texttt{Pi.add\_apply}-folded integrability witnesses).
\item \texttt{Pi.add\_apply}: the simp lemma that unfolds
\texttt{Pi.add} to pointwise sum.
\item \texttt{filter\_upwards} for almost-everywhere equality
witnesses passed to \texttt{Integrable.congr}.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{laplace-side existing material reused.}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=2em]
\item I2's algebra: \texttt{Laplace.gibbsExpectation\_smul},
\texttt{\_add}, \texttt{\_const} (\texttt{Laplace/Gibbs.lean},
post-\texttt{41a7b41}). Without I2, this proof would have had to
unfold \texttt{gibbsExpectation} manually to ratios of integrals
and re-do the linearity by hand.
\item Partition-function positivity for the harmonic
case\footnote{\texttt{Laplace.OneD.harmonic\_partition\_pos} in

\item Harmonic moment closed forms\footnote{\texttt{Laplace.OneD.gibbsExpectation\_harmonic\_pow\_even}
and \texttt{\_pow\_odd} in \texttt{Laplace/OneD/Harmonic.lean}.}:
direct $m_1 = m_3 = 0$ and $m_2 = 1/(\lambda t)$.

\section{Lessons}

\paragraph{The ``API symmetry vs.\ mathematical symmetry'' distinction.}
GPT-5.5~Pro's flag on Candidates~A/C was \emph{the} decisive
deliberation input. Both candidates' line-count estimates
($\sim$150--250) were predicated on $\kappa_3$ being fully symmetric
in $(\varphi, A, B)$ at the API level — which it is mathematically,
but \emph{not} at the Lean signature level, because slot $A$ is
load-bearing for the \texttt{gibbsExp} perturbation direction. Adding
the missing \texttt{h=0}-perturbation-independence lemma is genuinely
useful future infrastructure, but it would have been an unbudgeted
sub-tide. Worth carrying forward into other deliberation
checklists: \emph{when a candidate's proof sketch quietly invokes a
mathematical symmetry, check whether the corresponding API symmetry
is already formalised}.

\paragraph{Moment expansion plus I2 algebra is a clean pattern.}
The seven Gibbs expectations in the $\kappa_3$ expansion all reduce
to instances of a single polynomial-in-$x$ workhorse lemma. The path
\texttt{gibbsExpectation\_add} ($\times 3$) +
\texttt{gibbsExpectation\_smul} ($\times 3$) +
\texttt{gibbsExpectation\_const} ($\times 1$) +
\texttt{ring} is mechanical once the integrability bookkeeping is
discharged. Subsequent strict-improvements of harmonic-Gibbs
identities (e.g.\ G2 anharmonic affine $\kappa_3$, future affine
$\kappa_4$) can lift the workhorse pattern wholesale, replacing
only the per-potential moment lemmas. Worth promoting the
polynomial-degree-$n$ workhorse to a \texttt{lean-common} (or
laplace-side) helper once the \emph{second} consumer materialises
(G2 is the natural candidate).

\paragraph{Build-cache sharing across worktrees.}
A practical infrastructure note: the new worktree's
\texttt{.lake/packages} pointed initially at the canonical
\texttt{lean/laplace/} clone, which had unbuilt Mathlib /
Threepoint dependencies (because \texttt{lake exe cache get} had
failed offline at \texttt{tide-worktree create} time). Redirecting
the symlink to a sibling worktree's \texttt{.lake/packages} (in this
case the \texttt{tide/gibbscov-algebra} worktree, which had a clean
build) gave instant green builds. This is safe so long as the two
worktrees agree on Mathlib / Threepoint pin (\texttt{lakefile.toml}
and \texttt{lake-manifest.json}); a sibling worktree branched off
the same \texttt{main} should always agree. Worth adding to the
\texttt{tide-worktree create} script as a fallback when
\texttt{cache get} fails.

\section{Follow-ups}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=2em]
\item \emph{G2 — anharmonic affine $\kappa_3$.} The
mirror tide for the anharmonic potential
$L(x) = (\lambda/2)x^2 + (\alpha/6)x^3$. Same proof skeleton with
the harmonic moment lemmas swapped for their anharmonic
counterparts; the workhorse polynomial-expectation lemma generalises
cleanly. Estimated $\sim$80 lines after the workhorse promotion.

\item \emph{Promote the polynomial-degree-$n$ workhorse to
\texttt{lean-common}.} Once G2 lands and demonstrates the second
consumer, lift \texttt{gibbsExp\_poly\_le3\_harmonic} to a generic
\texttt{gibbsExp\_poly\_le3} (or \texttt{\_le\_n}) parameterised by
the moment closed forms. Reusable for any 1D Gibbs measure with
explicit moments. $\sim$80 lines.

\item \emph{The factored-identity formulation
\texttt{kappa3\_affine\_eq\_factored}.} GPT noted in the
deliberation that the factored statement
$\kappa_3(b_i x + c_i)_{i=1,2,3}
= b_1 b_2 b_3 \cdot \kappa_3(x, x, x)$
is the natural \emph{cumulant content} (constants drop, scalings
factor) and is valid for any 1D Gibbs measure with appropriate
integrability — not just the harmonic case. Promoting it to a
standalone theorem in \texttt{Laplace.Gibbs} (or in a new
\texttt{Threepoint}-side helper file) would be the natural setup for
G2 and any future affine-$\kappa_3$ tides.

\item \emph{Affine $\kappa_4$ for the harmonic Gibbs.} Once a
\texttt{kappa4} definition lands in \texttt{Threepoint}, the
mirror affine-vanishing theorem follows the same workhorse pattern
with the polynomial-expectation lemma extended to degree~4
(closed-form moments $m_4 = 3/(\lambda t)^2$ from the existing
even-power harmonic lemma).

\item \emph{$h=0$-perturbation-independence for
\texttt{Threepoint.gibbsExp}.} The lemma flagged by GPT as missing
during deliberation. Statement: \texttt{gibbsExp $\mu$ L $A_1$ t 0
$\varphi$ = gibbsExp $\mu$ L $A_2$ t 0 $\varphi$} for any two
perturbation directions $A_1, A_2$. Mathematically a one-liner
(the perturbation factor collapses at $h=0$), but in Lean would
require an \texttt{integrand\_eq}-style argument since the integrand
literally contains $A$. Defer until a downstream tide actually
needs the symmetry that the existing \texttt{kappa3\_swap\_$\varphi$B}
doesn't cover.
\end{itemize}
