\begin{abstract}
A second optimization pass on the laplace seabed, stacked on the first
(\tideref[the first round]{2026-08-24-16-07-tide-optimize-build}), which had taken the
forced rebuild from 402\,s to 137\,s and left the critical path on a
22-hop chain of small modules. Three findings, each a few lines of edit:
a 16\,s module (\texttt{ScalarBounds}) whose entire cost was one
higher-order unification --- \texttt{summable\_nat\_add\_iff} inventing
its function argument --- cured by naming the function explicitly; six
\texttt{positivity} calls at 1.4\,s each in a 25\,s proof, slow because
their atoms were \texttt{set}-bound constants that positivity looks up by
scanning a 60-hypothesis context, replaced by explicit
\texttt{mul\_nonneg} terms; and the discovery that 15 of the chain's 20
remaining \texttt{import Mathlib} umbrellas were fully redundant (their
Laplace imports already cover every constant used), plus two cuttable
chain edges. Full rebuild $137.9 \to 126.5$\,s ($402 \to 126.5$\,s
cumulative, $-69\%$); CPU $910 \to 882$\,s; fingerprint byte-identical,
0 sorry/axiom. The path is now the three covariance monoliths
($21 + 29 + 52$\,s of a 122\,s path), which need proof restructuring
rather than tactic flags.
\end{abstract}

\section{Setting}

Round one left a forced rebuild at 137\,s with the critical path on the
forward/recovery arc: 21 hops of 4--16\,s each, most barely above the
$\sim$4.4\,s it costs to load the full \texttt{import Mathlib} closure.
Two hops were fat (\texttt{ScalarBounds} 16\,s, \texttt{WindowMajorant}
13\,s); six files had been reverted to umbrella imports after
\texttt{shake} false positives. This round worked in a worktree stacked
on that branch, under the same statement-invariance gate, and --- per
feedback on round one --- used the \texttt{lean-state} LSP daemon for
goal inspection and per-edit checks, reserving real compiles for timing.

\section{What changed}

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2em]
\item \textbf{A unification stall.} \texttt{-Dtrace.profiler} put all
16.8\,s of \texttt{ScalarBounds} on a single \texttt{have}:
\texttt{(summable\_nat\_add\_iff (N + 1)).mpr hsum}. The lemma
relates \texttt{Summable (fun n ↦ f (n + k))} to \texttt{Summable f},
and matching the concrete summand $x^{i+(N+1)}/(i+(N+1))!$ against the
pattern \texttt{f (n + k)} is a higher-order problem Lean solves by
brute unfolding. Supplying \texttt{(f := fun n ↦ x\^{}n / n!)} makes it
first-order: module $16.8 \to 4.9$\,s, essentially the import load. The
\texttt{maxHeartbeats 1600000} budget on that theorem was the tell.
\item \textbf{positivity's context scan.} The 25\,s
remainder$\times$remainder bound in \texttt{Covariance} spent 8\,s in
six \texttt{positivity} calls. Each goal's atoms were \texttt{set}-bound
constants ($K_\varphi', K_\psi', C_\varphi, C_\psi, M_0, M_N$), for
which positivity has no structural rule and falls back to scanning every
hypothesis in a 60-hypothesis context. Explicit terms
(\texttt{mul\_nonneg (mul\_nonneg hKφ'\_nn …) …}) built from the
already-present nonnegativity facts: module $26 \to 17.6$\,s. Two more
context-taxed \texttt{nlinarith} in \texttt{WindowMajorant} got
\texttt{only} lists ($13 \to 9.4$\,s).
\item \textbf{Two chain edges.} Name-usage checks showed
\texttt{ScalarBounds} uses nothing from \texttt{CoeffFn} (it needs
\texttt{ExpGraded}) and \texttt{LocalRateDCT} nothing from
\texttt{HessianMoments} (it needs \texttt{RescaledDCT}) --- the very
edges \texttt{shake} had proposed in round one before its other
suggestions for those files broke them. Cut by hand, with
\texttt{WindowMajorant} and \texttt{GaussAbsorb} taking the direct
imports they had been getting transitively.
\item \textbf{The import floor.} A targeted import set loads in
$\sim$2.5\,s against 4.4\,s for the umbrella. \texttt{\#min\_imports},
run through \texttt{lean-state check} on a temporary trailing command,
reported that 15 of the 20 umbrella imports on the chain were redundant
outright and gave 1--4 targeted modules for the other five. Applied in
chain order and validated by a per-module \texttt{lake build --no-cache}
walk (which re-derives imports against the freshly narrowed upstream on
failure) --- all 20 built first time.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Lessons}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=2em]
\item A \texttt{maxHeartbeats} bump on a short theorem is a profiling
lead, not a fix: here it hid a 16\,s unification that one named argument
removes.
\item \texttt{positivity} is not context-free: atoms it cannot decompose
are resolved by scanning hypotheses, so in fat contexts it pays the same
kind of tax as \texttt{linarith}. Bound constants introduced with
\texttt{set} are the typical trigger; hand it the nonnegativity facts.
\item \texttt{\#min\_imports} through the LSP daemon is a cheap, precise
complement to \texttt{shake}: it answers per file in seconds, and
``redundant umbrella'' is by far its most common verdict in this repo.
Validate against freshly built upstream, not stale oleans --- a green
\texttt{check} of a narrowed file proves nothing about what its
dependents will see after rebuild.
\item Text-replacing a tactic line is unsafe in files with repeated
proof skeletons (one edit landed in a sibling lemma with an identical
\texttt{have} line); edit by line number, bottom-up.
\end{itemize}

\section{Follow-ups}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=2em]
\item Hoist the \texttt{have hpt} pointwise-bound sub-proofs of the
Covariance and CovarianceSharp monoliths into \texttt{private} lemmas
(parallel elaboration; est.\ 10--15\,s off the path together).
\item CovarianceExplicit's five $\sim$33\,s tails: 87 plain
\texttt{linarith} sites (context tax $\sim$8\,s per tail) and the
\texttt{field\_simp} chains in \texttt{abs\_bulkErr\_local\_le}
(10.7\,s); or, if the invariance gate is relaxed to allow additive
public helpers, split the file's Gaussian-contraction sections off.
\item Five files still carry the umbrella after the round-one
reverts\footnote{\texttt{SeparableRecovery}, \texttt{SingularSmooth},
\texttt{AnharmonicFourthCumulant}, \texttt{LocalRateDCT},
\texttt{ScalarBounds}; \texttt{AsymptoticDivision} was narrowed this
round.} --- none on the current path, so left alone.
\end{itemize}
