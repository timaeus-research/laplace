\begin{abstract}
The exponential jets $E_n(s)=\sum_{m<R}\frac{(\beta s)^m}{m!}[X^n](g_R^m)$ of unit~94 satisfy
\[
nE_n(s)=\beta s\sum_{i=1}^{n}i\,x_i\,E_{n-i}(s),\qquad 1\le n<R,
\]
the computational interface recommended in Astra~\#3/\#4 (the paper's tree recursion in disguise).
The proof is finite polynomial algebra: with $T_R=\sum_{m<R}\frac{(\beta s)^m}{m!}g_R^m$,
$T_R'-\beta sg_R'T_R=-\frac{(\beta s)^R}{(R-1)!}g_R'g_R^{R-1}$ is divisible by $X^{R-1}$ because
$g_R$ is divisible by $X$, so the coefficients below $R-1$ of $T_R'$ and $\beta sg_R'T_R$ agree. No
power-series exponential is needed. Zero \texttt{sorry}/\texttt{axiom}.
\end{abstract}

\section{Mathematics}

Differentiating $T_R$ termwise, $(g^m)'=mg^{m-1}g'$ and $\frac{(\beta s)^m}{m!}m=\beta s\frac{(\beta s)^{m-1}}{(m-1)!}$
reindex the sum to $\beta sg'\,T_{R-1}$, i.e. $\beta sg'(T_R-\frac{(\beta s)^{R-1}}{(R-1)!}g^{R-1})$.
Comparing the coefficient of $X^{n-1}$: on the left it is $nE_n$ (\texttt{coeff\_derivative}); on
the right, $\beta s\sum_{i+k=n-1}[X^i]g'\,[X^k]T_R=\beta s\sum_{i<n}(i+1)x_{i+1}E_{n-1-i}$ by the
Cauchy product and $[X^i]g'=(i+1)x_{i+1}$, while the correction $g'g^{R-1}$ is a multiple of
$X^{R-1}$ (\texttt{pow\_dvd\_pow\_of\_dvd} from $X\mid g$) and contributes nothing below $R-1$.

\section{Paper-facing meaning}

Together with the derivative identification of unit~102 this gives the one-dimensional jet
coefficients three equivalent descriptions: the closed truncated exponential formula, the normalised
derivatives, and the recursion $nE_n=\beta s\sum i\,x_iE_{n-i}$ that generates them from the Taylor
data of $\xi$ alone. The recursion is the natural bridge to the partition formula and to the
paper's tree indexing (Astra~\#4(f)), which remain the only unproved steps of the coefficient
identification.

\section{Lean notes}

\begin{itemize}
\item \texttt{Polynomial.derivative} lives in \texttt{Mathlib.Algebra.Polynomial.Derivative}, not in
the project's import closure; import it explicitly.
\item Identities mixing \texttt{C a * p} with products of polynomials defeat \texttt{ring}
(\texttt{C} of a product is not syntactically a product). Convert to scalar actions with
\texttt{simp only [C\_mul', smul\_smul, smul\_mul\_assoc, mul\_smul\_comm]}, then \texttt{congr 1}
separates the scalar identity (\texttt{field\_simp; ring}) from the polynomial one.
\item \texttt{coeff\_smul} + \texttt{coeff\_X\_pow\_mul'} kill the coefficients of an $X^{R-1}$-multiple
below $R-1$ in one \texttt{rw}.
\item \texttt{simp} normalises \texttt{n' + 1 - 1} to \texttt{n'} in the goal but not in a
hypothesis introduced later; normalise both (\texttt{simp only [Nat.add\_sub\_cancel] at hkey ⊢})
before \texttt{linear\_combination}.
\end{itemize}
