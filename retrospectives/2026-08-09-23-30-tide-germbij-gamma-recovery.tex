\section{Setting}

The gamma-rung programme's finale. Five stages built the analytic
ladder --- the signed exponential remainder, the second-order rescaled
expansions, the moment rates, and the fourth-cumulant limit
$t^3\kappa_4 \to 3\alpha^2/\lambda^5 - \gamma/\lambda^4$. What remained
was the recovery reading.

\section{The thing formalised}

Two declarations in \texttt{Laplace/OneD/RecoveryAnharmonicFull.lean},
sorry-free with zero warnings: the observed fourth-cumulant combination
and the theorem\footnote{\texttt{anharmonic\_jet\_recovery}.}: if the
mean susceptibilities, the rescaled covariance susceptibilities, and
the fourth-cumulant data of two anharmonic potentials
$\tfrac{\lambda}{2}x^2 + \tfrac{\alpha}{6}x^3 + \tfrac{\gamma}{24}x^4$
eventually agree, then $\lambda_1 = \lambda_2$, $\alpha_1 = \alpha_2$,
and $\gamma_1 = \gamma_2$. The germbij note's Theorem~3.1 triangular
induction, carried through its third rung: the complete jet of the
quartic-truncated potential is determined by observable data, each
coefficient read off at its own order.

\section{Proof strategy}

Composition: the merged two-rung recovery pins $(\lambda, \alpha)$;
after substitution, the two fourth-cumulant limits share their
$3\alpha^2/\lambda^5$ term, and uniqueness of limits along the eventual
equality forces $\gamma_1/\lambda^4 = \gamma_2/\lambda^4$. Fifteen
lines of proof for the programme's headline.

\section{Roadblocks and resolutions}

None: a first-attempt clean build, the programme's cheapest tide. The
uniqueness-of-limits recovery pattern, in its fifth use, has zero
marginal friction.

\section{What was Mathlib, what was new}

Mathlib: uniqueness of limits. Seabed: the entire programme. New: the
composition --- which is the point. A six-stage analytic programme,
scoped with a numerically pinned target and a symbolically verified
route, ends in a fifteen-line corollary because every hard step was
placed where the plan said it would be.

\section{Lessons}

The gamma rung cost seven tides end to end (stage 2 split at its seam),
with the numerical target pinned before stage 1 and never revised. The
per-stage pattern --- inherit the deliberation, execute against the
catalogue, checkpoint WIP commits on the big files --- held from the
elementary remainder to the finale. Programme-level scoping is the
single highest-leverage act: it converted an open-ended ``extend
Stage 2 to second order'' into a sequence of bounded tides.
