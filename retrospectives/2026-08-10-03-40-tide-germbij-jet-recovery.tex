\section{Setting}

The programme's closing tide. The pairwise difference limit of the
previous stage reduces the germbij \S7.4 recovery to bookkeeping: a
quotient rule, a variance-positivity bridge, a strong induction, and
a change of variables. This tide executes those four stages ---
and, in the process, discovered and repaired a serious process
failure in the previous tide.

\section{The incident}

At this tide's first build, the previous tide's file failed with
fifteen errors --- a file whose tide log and retrospective claimed a
clean build with zero warnings. The claim was false: that tide never
added its file to \texttt{Laplace.lean}'s imports, so its
\texttt{lake build} succeeded vacuously (the file was outside the
import closure), local audits saw nothing, and PR~\#55's CI passed
for the same reason, merging an uncompiled file to \texttt{main}.
The errors were all ordinary first-build classes (renames,
beta-unreduced congruence goals, implicit-argument inference, dead
tactics) and were repaired here; the mathematics was unaffected. The
prior retrospective now carries an erratum, the tide log records the
incident, and the repository \texttt{CLAUDE.md} carries the new rule:
\emph{a build result may be reported only after verifying both the
import line and the new \texttt{.olean}}. The deeper lesson is that
the build gate silently weakens to a no-op exactly when a step is
forgotten, so the gate itself must be checked, not just its exit
code.

\section{The thing formalised}

Six declarations in \texttt{Laplace/OneD/JetRecovery.lean},
sorry-free with zero warnings (verified via import and
\texttt{.olean} this time). The chain: the base-point limit
$J_s(q) \to A_s$; the generic quotient-difference lemma (limits of
scaled numerator and denominator differences give the scaled limit
of the quotient difference, over any filter, with Lean's
division-by-zero convention handling the scale degenerately); the
variance bridge $A_{2m}A_0 - A_m^2 > 0$ from stage 1 at
$t = a\,(2k)!$; the one-rung recovery; the strong induction; and the
headline\footnote{\texttt{polynomialJet\_recovery}.}:
\begin{quote}
For two finite jets $a\,x^{2k} + \sum_{r=1}^R c^\nu_r x^{2k+r}$ with
uniformly positive profiles, if the Gibbs expectations of
$x^{2k+r}$, $1 \le r \le R$, eventually agree as $t \to \infty$,
then $c^1 = c^2$.
\end{quote}
With the pure-monomial recovery of $(k, a)$ from the earlier
recovery thread, this completes the constructive dimension-one
programme of germbij \S7.4(a) for finite jets: every coefficient of
the potential is pinned by moment asymptotics, each at its own order,
with the injectivity supplied by the variance of the matching
observable.

\section{Proof notes}

At rung $r$ with $m = 2k + r$, the quotient-difference lemma applied
to $J_m$ and $Z$ produces the limit
$-\delta_r (A_{2m}A_0 - A_m^2)/A_0^2$; uniqueness of limits against
the data hypothesis and the variance bridge force $\delta_r = 0$. The
transfer to $t$-data composes the stage-2 exact scaling identity with
the observation that $q \mapsto (q^{2k})^{-1}$ carries
$\mathcal{N}[>]\,0$ to \texttt{atTop}, so eventual equality of
$t$-moments becomes eventual vanishing of the scaled $q$-difference.
Three tactical patterns recurred often enough to promote:
\texttt{set} folds only occurrences existing at the time (hypotheses
created later arrive unfolded --- refold with the definitional
equation); \texttt{Tendsto.congr} produces beta-unreduced lambda
applications (prove the function equality by \texttt{funext} and
\texttt{rwa} instead); and \texttt{div\_eq\_zero\_iff} with
\texttt{resolve\_right} cleanly clears a positive denominator from an
equation to zero.

\section{The deliberation}

No fresh consult: stages 3D--3G were specified with proof sketches in
the route consult, whose prediction (``once the unnormalized limit is

comparatively routine'') held --- the mathematics went through
essentially as sketched, with the incident being process rather than
mathematics. The one addition was the base-point limit, implicit in
the consult's quotient rule.
