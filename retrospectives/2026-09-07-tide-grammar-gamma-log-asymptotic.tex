\begin{abstract}
Step~3 of the general-$d$ equal-ratio monomial milestone (Astra~\#14). The one-dimensional
integral produced by the product density of unit~172 satisfies
\[
\int_0^1 z^{\lambda-1}(-\log z)^m e^{-\beta Nz}\,dz
\;\sim\;\Gamma(\lambda)\,\beta^{-\lambda}\,N^{-\lambda}(\log N)^m
\qquad(N\to\infty),
\]
for every $\lambda>0$, $\beta>0$, $m\in\mathbb N$ (\texttt{tendsto\_gammaLogIntegral\_div},
\texttt{gammaLogIntegral\_isEquivalent}). A Lebesgue-integral bridge
(\texttt{lintegral\_logDensity\_mul\_exp}) connects the real-valued statement to the
$[0,\infty]$-valued product-density integrand. Zero \texttt{sorry}/\texttt{axiom}.
\end{abstract}

\section{Mathematics}
Substituting $t=Nz$ gives
$N^{-\lambda}\int_0^N t^{\lambda-1}(\log N-\log t)^m e^{-\beta t}\,dt$. Expanding
$(\log N-\log t)^m=\sum_j\binom mj(\log N)^j(-\log t)^{m-j}$ and dividing by
$N^{-\lambda}(\log N)^m$ leaves
$\sum_j\binom mj(\log N)^{j-m}J_{m-j}(N)$ with
$J_i(N)=\int_0^N t^{\lambda-1}(-\log t)^ie^{-\beta t}\,dt$. Each $J_i$ is bounded uniformly in $N$
by $\int_0^\infty|\log t|^it^{\lambda-1}e^{-\beta t}\,dt<\infty$, so every term with $j<m$ tends to
zero, while $J_0(N)\to\int_0^\infty t^{\lambda-1}e^{-\beta t}\,dt=\Gamma(\lambda)\beta^{-\lambda}$.
The plan recorded after unit~172 was dominated convergence; the binomial split is shorter because the
envelope integrability was already in the seabed (\texttt{log\_pow\_fluctuation\_integrableOn} with
$a=0$), and it produces the bounded-tail lemma as a reusable by-product.

\section{Paper-facing meaning}
Combined with unit~172 this gives, for the equal-ratio monomial $\prod_i x_i^{2k_i}$ with
$(h_i+1)/(2k_i)=\lambda$ in $d$ variables, the leading behaviour
$N^{-\lambda}(\log N)^{d-1}$ with explicit constant $\Gamma(\lambda)\beta^{-\lambda}/(d-1)!$ times the
Jacobian factors $\prod_i 1/(2k_i)$ from unit~170. Remaining: the \texttt{Fin d} box bridge
(unit~174) and the assembly (unit~175).

\section{Lean notes}
\begin{itemize}
\item \texttt{rw [sub\_eq\_add\_neg]} rewrites the \emph{first} subtraction, which here was the
exponent $\lambda-1$, not $\log N-\log t$; use an explicit \texttt{show ... by ring} rewrite.
\item \texttt{pow\_sub₀} produces $a^m\cdot(a^n)^{-1}$, not a division; close with
\texttt{mul\_inv, inv\_inv, div\_eq\_mul\_inv, mul\_comm}.
\item \texttt{Asymptotics.isEquivalent\_of\_tendsto\_one} on this pin takes only the ratio limit
(no eventual-vanishing side condition); the \texttt{\textasciitilde[l]} notation needs
\texttt{open Asymptotics}.
\item \texttt{integral\_finset\_sum}/\texttt{tendsto\_finset\_sum} are deprecated for
\texttt{integral\_finsetSum}/\texttt{tendsto\_finsetSum}.
\item \texttt{Tendsto.zero\_mul\_isBoundedUnder\_le} with \texttt{isBoundedUnder\_of} accepts an

\end{itemize}
