\begin{abstract}
Unit~66's asymptotic for $\xi=a+u^k\tilde J$, $\eta=\eta_0+u^k\tilde\eta$ combined with the logarithm
lemma of unit~61 gives $-\log Z(\xi,\eta)=\tfrac p2\log n-m\log\log n+F_0+o(1)$ for $\eta_0>0$, with
$F_0=-\log(\eta_0C)$: the perturbations move only the constant term of the chart free energy. Zero
\texttt{sorry}/\texttt{axiom}.
\end{abstract}

\section{The things formalised}
{\small
\begin{verbatim}
theorem boxIntegralXiEta_freeEnergy ... (hη₀ : 0 < η₀) :
    ∃ (p : ℝ) (m : ℕ) (F₀ : ℝ), (∀ i, p ≤ finExp k h i) ∧
      m + 1 = (Finset.univ.filter fun i => finExp k h i = p).card ∧
      Tendsto (fun n => -Real.log (boxIntegralXiEta β a b (Real.sqrt n) k h
          (fun u => (∏ i, u i ^ k i) * J u) (fun u => η₀ + (∏ i, u i ^ k i) * η u))
        - (p / 2 * Real.log n - m * Real.log (Real.log n))) atTop (𝓝 F₀)
\end{verbatim}
}

\section{Proof strategy}
\texttt{tendsto\_log\_of\_isEquivalent\_powLog} applied to unit~66's equivalence with constant
$\eta_0C>0$, negated and rearranged.

\section{Roadblocks and resolutions}
\begin{itemize}
\item None: first-pass compile.
\end{itemize}

\section{Outcome}
The chart free energy has the Watanabe form $\lambda\log n-(m-1)\log\log n+O(1)$ for the whole class
of divisible perturbations of $\xi$ and $\eta$, with $\lambda$ and $m$ read off from $(k,h)$.
