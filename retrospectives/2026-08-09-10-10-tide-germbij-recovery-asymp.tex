\section{Setting}

The previous tide\footnote{\tideref{2026-08-09-09-00-tide-germbij-recovery-1d}.}
recovered the degree and scale of a pure even-monomial potential from
\emph{exact} eventual equality of partition functions. The germbij note's
Section~7.4 hypothesis is weaker: agreement of asymptotic
\emph{expansions}. This tide closes that gap, so the recovery thread's
hypotheses now match the note's.

\section{The thing formalised}

Two new theorems and a refactor in \texttt{Laplace/OneD/Recovery.lean},
sorry-free with zero warnings. Uniqueness of power-law
asymptotes\footnote{\texttt{power\_asymptote\_unique}.}: if
$\alpha_1 t^{\beta_1} \sim \alpha_2 t^{\beta_2}$ at $t \to \infty$ with
$\alpha_i > 0$, then $\beta_1 = \beta_2$ and $\alpha_1 = \alpha_2$. And
the asymptotic recovery
theorem\footnote{\texttt{kth\_partitionFunction\_recovery\_of\_isEquivalent}.}:
asymptotic equivalence of the partition functions of
$a_i\, x^{2k_i}/(2k_i)!$ already forces $k_1 = k_2$ and $a_1 = a_2$. Per
the consult's advice, the closed-form power representation, the
coefficient positivity, and the final algebraic extraction were factored
out of the exact-equality theorem into three shared
lemmas\footnote{\texttt{partitionFunction\_kth\_smul\_rpow},
\texttt{kth\_coeff\_pos}, \texttt{kth\_recovery\_of\_data}.}, so the
exact and asymptotic recovery proofs are each now a few lines over a
common tail.

\section{Proof strategy}

The equivalence unfolds to the ratio tending to $1$
(\texttt{isEquivalent\_iff\_tendsto\_one}; the denominator is eventually
nonzero since $t$ is eventually positive). The ratio is eventually the
function $(\alpha_1/\alpha_2)\, t^{\beta_1 - \beta_2}$, and trichotomy on
$\beta_1 - \beta_2$ finishes: a positive gap sends the ratio to infinity
(contradicting a finite limit), a negative gap sends it to zero
(contradicting $1 = 0$ by uniqueness of limits), and a zero gap makes the
ratio the constant $\alpha_1/\alpha_2$, which uniqueness of limits pins
to $1$. The corollary transports the equivalence along the eventual
equality with the closed forms
(\texttt{IsEquivalent.congr\_left}/\texttt{congr\_right}) and reuses the
shared extraction.

\section{Roadblocks and resolutions}

Three build iterations, all elaboration-level. The ratio that
\texttt{isEquivalent\_iff\_tendsto\_one} produces is a \texttt{Pi.div} of
lambdas,
which \texttt{field\_simp} will not see through until
\texttt{Pi.div\_apply} is unfolded --- the same class as the repo's
documented \texttt{Pi.add} gotcha, now observed for division. The
\texttt{rpow} limit lemmas are top-level constants, not in the
\texttt{Real} namespace. And \texttt{tendsto\_nhds\_unique} against a
constant function needs the constant-limit witness stated with an
explicit type ascription, or the elaborator unifies the function the
wrong way.

\section{What was Mathlib, what was new}

Mathlib: the equivalence-to-ratio characterisation, the \texttt{rpow}
limits at infinity, uniqueness of limits, and the congruence lemmas for
\texttt{IsEquivalent}. New: only the trichotomy packaging and the
factoring; the seabed's closed form continues to carry the content.

\section{Lessons}

\texttt{Pi.div} joins \texttt{Pi.add} in the unfold-before-simp gotcha
class; promoted to the repo \texttt{CLAUDE.md} alongside it. When a
deliberated proof plan names limit lemmas, checking their namespaces in
the same probe as their existence costs nothing and saves an iteration.

\section{Follow-ups}

\begin{itemize}
\item The genuine next depth in the recovery thread: subleading
coefficients. The note recovers each $a_j$ of
$a x^{2k} + \sum_{j>2k} a_j x^j$ inductively from corrections at order

case $a x^{2k} + b x^{2k+2}$, where the seabed's moment closed forms
should express the first correction coefficient explicitly in $b$.
