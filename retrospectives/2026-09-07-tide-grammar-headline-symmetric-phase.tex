\begin{abstract}
Unit 217 closes Astra~\#23's programme~D2: interior coordinates with phase. On the orthant
$x=\sigma\cdot u$ of $(-1,1]^d$ one has $x^{2k}=u^{2k}$, $x^h=\chi_h(\sigma)u^h$ and
$x^k\xi(x)=\varepsilon_\sigma u^k\xi(\sigma u)$ with $\chi_h(\sigma)=\prod_i\sigma_i^{h_i}$,
$\varepsilon_\sigma=\prod_i\sigma_i^{k_i}$; by the generic reflection identity of unit~216 the
symmetric-box dressed integral is the sum over orthants of unit-box chart integrals with phase
$\varepsilon_\sigma\,\xi\circ\sigma$ and amplitude $\chi_h(\sigma)\,\eta\circ\sigma$, and
Headline~XIII on each orthant gives
\[
\frac{\int_{(-1,1]^d}\eta\,x^he^{-\beta N^2x^{2k}+\beta Nx^k\xi(x)}\,dx}{N^{-p}(\log N)^{m-1}}
\longrightarrow\sum_{\sigma\in\{\pm1\}^d}\mathrm{phaseCoeff}\bigl(\varepsilon_\sigma\xi\circ\sigma,\ \chi_h(\sigma)\eta\circ\sigma\bigr)
\]
(\texttt{headline\_symmetric\_phase\_leading}); for the absolute density $|x|^h$ the sign
$\chi_h(\sigma)$ disappears (\texttt{headline\_symmetric\_abs\_phase\_leading}). Odd $k_i$ flip the
phase on the corresponding reflection ($J_p(a)$ against $J_p(-a)$): no parity cancellation is
asserted in general, as Astra~\#22--23 warned; the zero-phase parity rule of unit~197 is the case
$\xi=0$. Zero \texttt{sorry}/\texttt{axiom}; 9094 jobs.
\end{abstract}

\section{Mathematics}
Purely algebraic on top of units 216 and 202: $(\sigma_iu_i)^{h_i}=\sigma_i^{h_i}u_i^{h_i}$,
$|\sigma_iu_i|^{h_i}=u_i^{h_i}$ on the positive orthant, and $e^{-\beta(\varepsilon s)^2+\beta a(\varepsilon s)}
=e^{-\beta s^2+\beta(\varepsilon a)s}$ for $\varepsilon^2=1$ move all signs into the phase and the
amplitude. Each orthant is then a Headline~XIII chart with continuous inputs (reflection is
continuous), and finite sums of limits add.
