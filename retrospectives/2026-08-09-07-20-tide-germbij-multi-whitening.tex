\begin{abstract}
\noindent For positive-definite $H$ on $\mathbb{R}^d$, the Gaussian
kernel $K_H(x) = e^{-\langle x, Hx\rangle/2}$ has positive finite
mass, vanishing first moments, and normalized second moments equal
to $(H^{-1})_{ij}$ --- the quadratic Gaussian package that every later
stage of the multivariate programme consumes. The proof whitens: with
$B = \sqrt H$ (continuous functional calculus), the forward identity
$\langle x, Hx\rangle = \|Bx\|^2$ turns $K_H$ into the standard
kernel composed with $B$, one change of variables with a deliberately
opaque Jacobian transports the standard package, and the Jacobian
cancels in every normalized statement. Together with its companion
tide (the standard package), this closes stage H2, which the scoping
consult had flagged as the largest API spike of the programme.
\end{abstract}

\section{Setting}

The germbij multivariate programme is staged H0--H6; H2 is the pure
quadratic Gaussian package. A dedicated shape consult split it: H2a
proved everything for the standard isotropic kernel (all Fubini and
one-dimensional work), and ruled that H2b --- this tide --- should
contain no Fubini at all: only linear algebra and measure transport.
The consult fixed the representation ($\texttt{qform}\,H\,x =
\langle x, H\cdot x\rangle$ via the matrix-to-operator equivalence),
the direction of the whitening identity (forward, never inverting
inside the form), and the treatment of the Jacobian (opaque
nonzeroness, no transport to matrix determinants).

\section{The thing formalised}

For $H$ positive definite, with $Z_0 = (2\pi)^{d/2}$ and
$\mathrm{jacInv}\,H = |\det B|^{-1} > 0$ kept opaque:
\begin{gather*}
\int K_H = \mathrm{jacInv}\,H \cdot Z_0 > 0, \qquad
\int \|x\|^n K_H(x)\,dx < \infty \quad (\forall n),\\
\int x_i\, K_H(x)\,dx = 0, \qquad
\frac{\int x_i x_j\, K_H(x)\,dx}{\int K_H(x)\,dx} = (H^{-1})_{ij}.
\end{gather*}

\section{How the proof works}

$B = \texttt{toEuclideanCLM}\,(\texttt{CFC.sqrt}\,H)$ is
self-adjoint (the square root of a positive-definite matrix is
Hermitian) and injective (if $Bx = 0$ then
$\langle x, Bx\rangle = 0$, contradicting positive-definiteness), so
its determinant is a unit. The forward identity
\[
\langle x, Hx\rangle
= \langle x, B(Bx)\rangle
= \langle Bx, Bx\rangle = \|Bx\|^2
\]
gives $K_H = k_0 \circ B$ pointwise. Mathlib's Haar-measure lemma
for linear maps turns the pushforward of volume under $B$ into
$\mathrm{jacInv}\,H$ times volume, packaged once as
$\int \varphi(Bx)\,dx = \mathrm{jacInv}\,H \int \varphi$ for any
almost-everywhere strongly measurable $\varphi$ (no integrability
needed: both sides scale consistently even for junk values).
Everything then transports from the standard package. For the
moments, the observable is rewritten through the inverse map
$C = (\sqrt H)^{-1}$ (matrix nonsingular inverse, $C \circ B =
\mathrm{id}$), whose coordinates are finite sums
$(Cy)_i = \sum_a C_{ia} y_a$; the H2a delta-form second moment
contracts the double sum to
$Z_0 \sum_a C_{ia} C_{ja} = Z_0 (CC^{\mathsf T})_{ij}$, and the Gram
step $CC^{\mathsf T} = C^2 = (B^2)^{-1} = H^{-1}$ is three rewrites
($C$ symmetric, product-inverse reversal, $\sqrt H \cdot \sqrt H =
H$). In the normalized statement the opaque Jacobian and $Z_0$
cancel by \texttt{field\_simp}, exactly as the consult predicted.

\section{Where it stalled and how it moved}

No stalls; two genuinely new error classes for the catalogue. First,
the class-level \texttt{map\_star} lemma fails on
\texttt{Matrix.toEuclideanCLM} --- instance synthesis cannot find a
\texttt{StarHomClass} for its star-algebra-equivalence type --- but
the structure's own field \texttt{.map\_star'} applies directly, and
term-level definitional unfolding accepts it where tactic-level
rewriting stumbles on the raw \texttt{toFun}. Second, a \texttt{rfl}
bridging $(B \cdot B)\,x$ to the def-wrapped double application hit
a deterministic whnf heartbeat timeout; folding the composition into
a named equation (`$H$-operator equals \texttt{whitening} squared')
and rewriting with \texttt{ContinuousLinearMap.mul\_apply} avoids
whnf entirely. A recurring third: the matrix partial order behind
\texttt{CFC.sqrt} is \emph{scoped} (\texttt{open scoped
MatrixOrder}), and its absence surfaces as PartialOrder synthesis
failures at every use site, not as a missing-import hint.

\section{Mathlib lookup versus new mathematics}

Lookup: the matrix-to-operator star-algebra equivalence and its
inner-product bridge, the square root from the continuous functional
calculus with positive-definiteness transport, the unit-determinant
equivalence, the Haar pushforward under linear maps, and the
integral-under-map lemmas.\footnote{The pushforward lemma is
\texttt{Measure.map\_linearMap\_addHaar\_eq\_smul\_addHaar}; the
others are \texttt{Matrix.toEuclideanCLM} with
\texttt{Matrix.inner\_toEuclideanCLM}, \texttt{CFC.sqrt} with
\texttt{CFC.sqrt\_mul\_sqrt\_self} and
\texttt{IsStrictlyPositive.sqrt},
\texttt{LinearMap.isUnit\_iff\_isUnit\_det}, and
\texttt{integral\_map} / \texttt{integrable\_map\_measure} /
\texttt{integral\_smul\_measure}.} New (project-level): the forward
whitening identity as the single bridge between the quadratic and
standard kernels, the opaque-Jacobian change-of-variables interface,
the coordinate contraction of the inverse map against the delta
moments, and the Gram collapse to $H^{-1}$.

\section{What this opens}

Stage H3: local quadratic rescaling of a smooth loss
($({L(qx) - L(0)})/{q^2} \to \tfrac12\langle x, Hx\rangle$ with a
nondegenerate lower bound on the rescaled domain). Stage H4:
dominated convergence on expanding domains against the
$(1+\|x\|^2)e^{-c\|x\|^2}$ majorant this tide provides. Then H5
(normalization and covariance) and H6 (pairwise Hessian recovery),
the multivariate analogue of \texttt{base\_recovery}.
