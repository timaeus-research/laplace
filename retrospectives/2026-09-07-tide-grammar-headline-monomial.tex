\begin{abstract}
Paper-facing wrappers for the general-$d$ equal-ratio monomial milestone (units 165, 170--175),
stated directly on the Bochner box integral
$M(N)=\int_{(0,1]^{m+1}}\prod_ix_i^{h_i}e^{-\beta N\prod_ix_i^{2k_i}}\,dx$ (the paper's bare normal
moment integral with cutoff $b=1$): the product density, the Gamma/log asymptotic, the exact
reduction $M(N)=\prod_i\frac1{2k_i}\cdot\frac1{m!}\int_0^1z^{\lambda-1}(-\log z)^me^{-\beta Nz}\,dz$,
and the asymptotic $M(N)\sim\Gamma(\lambda)\beta^{-\lambda}/(m!\prod_i2k_i)\,N^{-\lambda}(\log N)^m$
in ratio and paper forms. Zero \texttt{sorry}/\texttt{axiom}.
\end{abstract}

\section{Paper-facing meaning}
The paper's zeta-function analysis of the bare normal moment integral (\S\,``Standard form and the
normal moment integrals'') predicts the leading exponent $\lambda=\min_i(h_i+1)/(2k_i)$ with
multiplicity $|J|$, $J$ the set of coordinates realising the minimum, and the constant
$\Gamma(\lambda)a_{-|J|}/(|J|-1)!$ with $a_{-|J|}=\prod_{i\in J}\frac1{2k_i}$ when $b=1$ and $J=I$.
The wrappers formalise exactly this equal-ratio boundary case, for arbitrary $\beta>0$, without any
Taylor-tree or quadratic-chart intermediary. Not claimed: unequal ratios, cutoffs $b\neq1$, interior
coordinates with parity factors.

\section{Lean notes}
The wrappers unfold \texttt{monomialBoxReal}, \texttt{gammaLogIntegral} and \texttt{logDensity}
definitionally, so each is a one-line \texttt{exact} of the unit-175 theorem; no
\texttt{show}/\texttt{change} was needed because the definitions are reducible enough for
\texttt{exact}-level unification of the integrands.
