\begin{abstract}
A soundness review of \texttt{Laplace/OneD/AnharmonicThirdCumulant.lean}: the third connected cumulant
$\kappa_3=\partial_h^2\langle u\rangle_h|_0=(G_3G_0^2-3G_1G_2G_0+2G_1^3)/G_0^3=\langle u^3\rangle_0-3\langle u^2\rangle_0\langle u\rangle_0+2\langle u\rangle_0^3$
($u=-(t x)$). Result: \textbf{a clean fidelity pass, no edits}, plus one precise report-only note --- a
dead \texttt{open MeasureTheory}, which the reviewer flagged \emph{together with} a load-bearing
\texttt{open Topology} ($\mathcal N$ notation), so ``both unused'' would have broken the build.
\end{abstract}

\section{Setting}
The $\kappa_3$ rung of the anharmonic cumulant ladder: the second $h$-derivative of the perturbed mean
$M(h)=G_1(h)/G_0(h)$, sibling of the \reviewref[$\kappa_2$ susceptibility]{2026-06-05-review-laplace-anharmonic-second-cumulant}.

\section{What was reviewed}
The single public theorem \texttt{anharmonic\_third\_cumulant}.

\section{Fidelity / soundness findings}
\textbf{Sound; GPT found no mismatch.} The proof differentiates the susceptibility $S=(G_2G_0-G_1^2)/G_0^2$
once more (a compound quotient rule on the partition derivatives). With $N=G_2G_0-G_1^2$, $D=G_0^2$,
$N'=G_3G_0-G_1G_2$ (using $\partial_h G_n=G_{n+1}$), $D'=2G_0G_1$:
\[
  \frac{N'D-ND'}{D^2}
  =\frac{(G_3G_0-G_1G_2)G_0^2-(G_2G_0-G_1^2)\,2G_0G_1}{G_0^4}
  =\frac{G_3G_0^2-3G_1G_2G_0+2G_1^3}{G_0^3},
\]
so the $-3$ and $+2$ coefficients are right. The first derivative of the mean is the susceptibility
(\texttt{anharmonic\_mean\_hasDerivAt\_general}, eventually on \texttt{ball 0 1}); the
\texttt{iteratedDeriv\_succ}/\texttt{iteratedDeriv\_one}/\texttt{hev.deriv\_eq}/\texttt{hS.deriv} chain takes
$\mathtt{iteratedDeriv}\,2\,M\,0$ to $\kappa_3$; and the docstring's $\langle u^3\rangle-3\langle u^2\rangle\langle u\rangle+2\langle u\rangle^3$
is the standard third cumulant, equal to the $G_n$ form by moment-normalisation $\langle u^n\rangle=G_n/G_0$.

\section{Simplifications applied}
None. GPT's (B) findings were proof golf (the \texttt{hball}/\texttt{hh} ball-is-a-neighbourhood boilerplate,
an inlinable \texttt{hden\_ne}, a $G_n$ abbreviation, a tighter \texttt{hS} algebra) --- declined --- plus one
dead-open flag that was \emph{half wrong}: GPT reported ``\texttt{open MeasureTheory Topology} unused'', but a
grep shows \texttt{Topology} is load-bearing ($\mathcal N$ appears in \texttt{hev}'s $=^f[\mathcal N\,0]$). Only
\texttt{open MeasureTheory} is genuinely dead. The safe edit is \texttt{open MeasureTheory Topology}
$\to$ \texttt{open Topology}, not removing both. This single cosmetic one-off dead-open was \emph{not landed}
(a worktree + affirmation is not worth one dead word; cf.\ the qd dead-opens deferred to a batch) --- it is a
precise report-only note for a hygiene pass. File left byte-identical.

\section{Disagreements and open questions}
None on the mathematics. The dead-open is report-only.

\section{Lessons}
\begin{itemize}[noitemsep]
  \item \textbf{A multi-name \texttt{open} line needs per-name grep verification --- a reviewer's ``these
    opens are unused'' can be half right.} GPT lumped a load-bearing \texttt{open Topology} (it supplies the
    $\mathcal N$ neighbourhood notation used in the eventual-equality step) with a genuinely-dead
    \texttt{open MeasureTheory} on the same line. Acting on ``both unused'' would have removed $\mathcal N$ and
    broken the build. This is the \texttt{GaussianIntegral} symbol-grep lesson generalised to a shared
    \texttt{open} line: confirm each name's usage independently (and the build is the final arbiter) before
    trimming.
\end{itemize}
