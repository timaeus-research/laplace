\section{Setting}

This tide opens the weighted-jet programme: the germbij note's
\S7.4 inductive recovery, in which each coefficient $c_r$ of a
degenerate one-dimensional potential
$L = a\,x^{2k} + \sum_r c_r x^{2k+r}$ is read off the graded
correction at $t^{-r/(2k)}$ by a covariance pairing. The programme
was scoped against three alternatives (the quintic cumulant rung, the
general-order nondegenerate induction, and the multivariate first
rung); the consult ranked the weighted jet first --- smallest
infrastructure delta per stage, closed forms at every rung, and its
normalized-series machinery de-risks the general-order induction
later. The named scope trap: the bare finite jet need not be globally
integrable (the top term may be odd or negative), so the analytic
envelope must be fixed before the combinatorics.

The seabed survey collapsed the consult's stage 1: the
generalized-Gaussian moment API (partition function, even and odd
moments, Gibbs expectations, integrability) already exists in
\texttt{MonomialPotential.lean} from the recovery thread. What was
missing is exactly the injectivity input every recovery rung will
consume: strict positivity of the variance of a monomial observable
against the reference weight.

\section{The thing formalised}

Three theorems in \texttt{Laplace/OneD/MonomialVariance.lean},
sorry-free with zero warnings, for the reference weight
$e^{-t\,x^{2k}/(2k)!}$ with $k \ge 1$, $t > 0$: the Gamma closed
forms of $\mathrm{Var}(x^n)$ for odd $n$ (the mean vanishes by
symmetry, so the variance is the even
moment)\footnote{\texttt{monomial\_variance\_odd}.} and even
$n$ (a difference of Gamma ratios under the common power of
$(2k)!/t$)\footnote{\texttt{monomial\_variance\_even}.}, and the
programme's load-bearing
fact\footnote{\texttt{monomial\_variance\_pos}.}:
\[
\mathrm{Var}_{\mu_{t,k}}(x^n) \;>\; 0
\qquad (n \ge 1).
\]

\section{Proof strategy}

The positivity proof formalises the note's own injectivity argument
--- $\mathrm{Var}[Q] = 0$ would force $Q$ constant against a measure
of full support. Concretely: with $M = \langle x^n \rangle$ and $Z$
the partition function,
\[
\mathrm{Var}(x^n) = \frac{1}{Z}\int (x^n - M)^2\, e^{-t L_k}\,dx,
\]
and the centered integrand is continuous, nonnegative, and not
identically zero, so its support is open and nonempty
(witness $x = 1$ when $M = 0$, else $x = 0$), hence of positive
Lebesgue measure, and Mathlib's
\texttt{integral\_pos\_iff\_support\_of\_nonneg} gives positivity;
dividing by $Z > 0$ finishes. The variance identity itself is the
usual expansion $\int (x^n - M)^2 w = \int x^{2n} w - 2M \int x^n w +
M^2 Z$ with the middle term collapsed by $\int x^n w = MZ$.

The numerical check (executed first) validated both the tide and the
programme: the Gamma-form variances match quadrature to six decimals
($k \in \{1,2\}$, $n \le 4$), and --- the consult's prescribed
programme-level check --- the $q^r$ coefficient of
$\mathbb{E}_q[u^{2k+r}]$ responds to $c_r$ with slope
$-\mathrm{Var}(u^{2k+r})$ independent of the lower coefficients
(measured/predicted ratios $1.001$--$1.006$ at $q = 0.05$, both
parities of $2k+r$ included).

\section{Roadblocks and resolutions}

Three small ones, two of them new catalogue entries. First,
\texttt{ring} \emph{does} normalise symbolic exponent arithmetic:
$x^{2n}$ versus $(x^n)^2$ with $n$ a free natural closes by plain
\texttt{ring}, and the pre-planned \texttt{two\_mul}/\texttt{pow\_add}
rewrites were actively harmful (\texttt{two\_mul} matched the
constant $2M$ first). Second, \texttt{simp only [defName]} does not
unfold a definition --- \texttt{unfold} does, after which
\texttt{fun\_prop} dispatches continuity of the monomial potential.
Third, \texttt{positivity} cannot conclude $\ne 0$ through $(-M)^2$
without $M \ne 0$ in a form it consumes; explicit
\texttt{mul\_ne\_zero} chains are cleaner for support witnesses.

\section{The deliberation}

The programme-level consult is archived verbatim in the tide
log.\footnote{\texttt{tide-log/gpt56\_weighted\_jet\_scoping\_v1.md}.}
Both parties voted for the weighted-jet programme. Its stage list assigns this tide the
reference moment API; the seabed survey showed most of that stage
already merged, so the tide contracted to the variance layer --- the
kind of adjustment the survey step exists to catch. The consult's
warning that partition data alone would lose odd perturbations at
linear order (symmetry) is precisely why the variance against the
\emph{matching moment observable} $u^{2k+r}$ is the right injectivity
quantity, and why this tide is stage 1 rather than an afterthought.

\section{Mathlib vs new}

Mathlib lookup: \texttt{integral\_pos\_iff\_support\_of\_nonneg},
open-set measure positivity, preimage openness of the support of a
continuous function. Seabed reuse: the entire moment API. New: the
variance identity assembly and the support witnesses.

\section{What the next tide inherits}

Stage 2 of the programme: the exact scaling identity
$t^{s/(2k)}\langle x^s \rangle_t$ as a normalized perturbation of the
reference measure for the finite jet, with the analytic envelope
chosen explicitly (the consult's trap warning). The variance layer
here is consumed at stage 6--7, where the triangular covariance lemma
pins each $c_r$ with denominator $\mathrm{Var}_{\mu}(u^{2k+r}) > 0$.
