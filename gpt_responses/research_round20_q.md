What is the cleanest way to get differentiability of the term constant in the weight: (i) differentiate under the integral directly in the exponential form (`d/dε exp(−B U_{a+εv} ∏u^κ) = −B R_v ∏u^κ exp(…)`, bounded by the profile itself times a polynomial) — which gives the derivative as `−∫ B R_v ∏u^κ · (density)`, an integral against the term measure of a function of the scaled coordinate — or (ii) first integrate out the scaled coordinate to get the `U^{-β}` face form and then apply the face calculus?
