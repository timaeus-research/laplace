## The remaining hard lemma (your round-72 rank 1)
We want, for every `M ∈ P = conv V`: an exposed face `F_M = P ∩ {⟨u,·⟩ = β}` (with `⟨u,v⟩ ≤ β` on `V`) such that
`M ∈ intrinsicInterior ℝ F_M` and `F_M` is the minimal face (`F_M ⊆ G` for every extreme `G ∋ M`, equivalently
`F_M = conv {v ∈ V | a_v(M) > 0}`). Then everything else (essential support `q_M ∼ ν|_{S∈F_M}`, the uniform bound
`dq_M/dν ≤ 1/m_*`, the face order `q_M ≪ q_N ↔ F_M ⊆ F_N`, `L^p` continuity) is routine given `momentBody_faceMeasure_eq_of_exposed`,
`compl_eq_zero_of_mean_face`, domination and the boundary-ray identification.
