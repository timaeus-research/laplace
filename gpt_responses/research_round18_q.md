# germbij formalisation — research consult, round 18: the response map over the data manifold

New direction from the project lead. The germbij note studies the map from a loss `L` to the family of posterior expectation values `Φ_L(φ)(t) = ∫ φ e^{-tL} / ∫ e^{-tL}` (asymptotics in `t`, identifiability, singular structure through resolution). The loss is itself a function of the data distribution: for a model with per-sample loss `ℓ(w, x)` and a distribution `q` on data, `L_q(w) = E_q[ℓ(w, ·)]`. The lead wants the **core features of how the posterior expectation values change with the data distribution**, so as to "map" the space of responses across the data manifold — ideally along paths from the featureless distribution of maximal entropy `q₀` all the way to the actual data distribution `q₁` — and asks: *what would it take to do this with maximum beauty and depth?*

## What exists in the seabed (Lean 4 + Mathlib, laplace repo; all sorry-free)

- Temperature derivatives and susceptibilities: `d/dt ⟨φ⟩_t = −Cov_t(φ, L)`, `covK` to second order, Wick/Gaussian machinery at nondegenerate minima; the whole germbij singular layer (resolution charts, LP certificates, active-truth faces, boundary regimes, pointwise chart-independence).
- Loss-direction tilts (`TiltInterpolation`): for `L_u = L + uR` with a compact window, `d/du ⟨f⟩_u = −t Cov_u(f, R)` (`hasDerivAt_tiltExp`), Cauchy–Schwarz bounds, linearity of tilted expectations.
- Mixture truths (`PatternAttenuation`): `q_θ = (1−θ) q_base + θ q_P` has `L_θ = (1−θ) L_base + θ L_P`; separable attenuation identity `Z(t) = Z_f(t) Z_g(θt)`; the pattern's crossover in `θt`; rigidity: with a common minimiser the leading exponent and log-multiplicity are the same for all `θ ∈ (0,1)` (`mixture_isTheta`); jumps only where a mixture weight vanishes.
- Bayesian influence functions (separate seabed `bif`): `BIF = −Cov`.
- Structural duality seabed: CGF identities, Esscher tilts, data-variety polynomials.
- Pencil identity `e^{-tL₁} − e^{-tL₂}` integrated along the segment; the singular identifiability theorem.

## Observations to be checked

1. Along a **mixture path** `q_s = (1−s) q₀ + s q₁` the loss is affine, `L_s = L₀ + s Δ`, `Δ = L₁ − L₀`, so the posterior family is an exponential tilt of the base posterior by `Δ`: `⟨φ⟩_{t,s} = ⟨φ e^{-tsΔ}⟩_{t,0} / ⟨e^{-tsΔ}⟩_{t,0}`; all responses along the path are determined by the joint law of `(φ, Δ)` under the base posterior; `F(s) = −log Z(s)` is concave/convex (log-CGF) with `F'' = −t² Var_s(Δ)`; the derivative is the fluctuation–response identity; the second derivative the third cumulant.
2. If `q₀` is **featureless** in the sense that `L_{q₀}` is constant in `w` (the data carry no information about the parameter), the base posterior is the prior and the mixture path from `q₀` is exactly the **temperature path** `⟨φ⟩_{ts, q₁}`: the `t`-asymptotics of the note ARE the mixture geodesic from maximal entropy. Is that the right reading of "featureless", and for which models is `L_{q₀}` constant (e.g. label noise uniform over outputs)? When it is not constant, `L₀` is a "nuisance loss" and the path is a genuine tilt.
3. The **e-geodesic** (exponential family path `q_s ∝ q₀^{1−s} q₁^s`) gives a non-affine `L_s`; the duality between m- and e-paths (Amari) should have a counterpart on the response side.
4. The **differential** of the response map at `q` in a direction `δq` (signed measure, zero mass) is `dΦ_q(δq)(φ) = −t Cov_q(φ, E_{δq}[ℓ])`: a linear map from the tangent space of the data manifold to responses — the influence field; the pullback of the covariance structure gives a metric on the data manifold ("how much the posterior responds to a data perturbation"), presumably related to the Fisher metric of the posterior family in the `q`-direction, `t² Var_q(E_{δq}ℓ)`.
5. In the **singular layer** (`t → ∞`) the leading order `λ(q)` and coefficient measure `μ(q)` are functions on the data manifold; along a path the zero locus of `L_s` and the resolution can change, so the response map is stratified (equisingularity strata), analytic within a stratum (units vary) and jumping across strata: a **phase diagram over the data manifold**. The active-truth machinery with moving parameters (`σ(t)`, uniform-in-parameter theorems) is the first instance; `mixture_isTheta` (rigidity of the exponent along mixture paths with a common minimiser) is another.

## Questions

1. What is the most beautiful and deep formulation of "the response map across the data manifold": which objects (the map `q ↦ Φ_q`; its differential; the free-energy functional `F(q) = −log Z_q`; its convexity on mixture lines; its Legendre structure; the induced metric; the stratification of the singular layer), which theorems are the load-bearing ones, and how they organise into a programme comparable to the germbij note (exact layer at finite `t`, asymptotic layer, singular layer)?
2. Is the reading "featureless = the loss is constant in the parameter, so the mixture path from maximal entropy is the temperature path" correct and useful, or is the right notion of featureless different (e.g. a product/independence structure, maximal entropy under moment constraints, the reference measure of the exponential family)? What is the natural coordinate on the data manifold along which the response should be tracked (mixture weight, e-geodesic parameter, an entropy-like coordinate `H(q)`, KL to the truth)?
3. Which results are cheap to formalise now on top of the existing tilt/attenuation/pencil layers, and which are genuinely new mathematics: e.g. (a) the exact tilt representation along mixture paths and its cumulant expansion; (b) convexity of `F` on mixture lines and its consequences (a variational principle for the data distribution?); (c) the influence field and its metric; (d) the e-geodesic responses; (e) the phase diagram of the singular layer along a path with a fixed zero locus but varying exponents/units — in the germbij chart language, how do the exponent data and the LP move with `q`, and what is the exact statement of "the leading order is piecewise-constant/piecewise-linear along the path with jumps at ties"; (f) the "map from featureless to data" as a homotopy of posteriors: what invariants are preserved along it and what is created (features/patterns appearing at crossovers `θt ~ scale`, as in `PatternAttenuation`)?
4. Give a ranked list of the first five theorems to prove (Lean-level statements, hypotheses, the cheapest first step), and name the single deepest statement the programme should aim at.
