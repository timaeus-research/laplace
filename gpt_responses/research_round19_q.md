## Questions

1. **The singular layer over the data manifold.** The coupled example is a toy. What is the right general statement, in the language of the seabed's chart LPs, of "the coupled-limit exponent is piecewise affine in the valuation coordinates of the data path"? Concretely: for a family `L_q = ∑_j c_j(q) x^{α_j}` in a chart with fixed exponents `α_j` and coefficients `c_j(q(t)) ≍ t^{-σ_j}`, the value `λ(σ) = min_r {b·r : α_j·r + σ_j ≥ 1}` is a parametric LP; what is the cleanest theorem that (a) the leading order of `Z_t(q(t))` is `t^{-λ(σ)}` up to log factors, (b) `λ` is concave piecewise affine in `σ` with active faces changing across polyhedral walls, (c) the log multiplicity is controlled by the optimal face? Which hypotheses (positivity of coefficients, compact chart, unit functions) make (a) a consequence of the existing dominant-scale/term machinery with a moving `σ(t)`, and which part is genuinely new (e.g. the tie regime where two monomials scale together and the profile integral changes, as in the coupled example)?
2. **Uniformity in the mixture weight of the coefficient measure.** The interior rigidity gives `(λ,m)` constant on compact subsets of the open simplex but says nothing about the coefficient measure `μ_q` or the limiting posterior. For a mixture `L_a = ∑ aᵢ fᵢ` with a common zero and a common resolution (the `fᵢ` monomial in the same charts with positive units), is the leading coefficient measure `μ_a` a continuous (analytic?) function of `a` on the open simplex, and what is the cleanest formulation — e.g. the leading constant `C(a)` of `Z_{L_a}` as an explicit function of `a` through the face integrals? Is there a formula like `C(a) = ∫_F (∑ aᵢ uᵢ(x))^{-β} ...`? What would a Lean statement look like on top of `TermData`?
3. **The variational and information-geometric layer.** Is the Gibbs variational principle `F_t(q) = inf_ρ {t E_ρ L_q + KL(ρ‖π)}` worth formalising here (Mathlib has `klDiv`), and does it give anything the convexity results don't (e.g. a dual characterisation of the response map, or the concavity of `F` in `q` for arbitrary — non-mixture — paths)? What about thermodynamic length `∫ √g(q̇,q̇) ds` along data paths and its degeneration `~ √t` at regular points?
4. **The all-orders structure.** `d^n/ds^n ⟨φ⟩_s = (−t)^n κ_{n+1}(φ, Δ, …, Δ)` on mixture lines: is there a slick formalisation via the generating function `s ↦ log ∫ e^{-t(L₀ + sΔ) + Jφ}π` (joint CGF in `(s, J)`) that makes the cumulant expansion a statement about mixed partials of one analytic function, rather than an induction with explicit cumulant polynomials? What is the cleanest Lean-level target?
